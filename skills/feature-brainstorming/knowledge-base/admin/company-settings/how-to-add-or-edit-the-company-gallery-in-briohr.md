# How to Add or Edit the Company Gallery in BrioHR

*This article explains how to add or edit the company gallery under Settings > Company.*

Once you have logged in to your BrioHR account, click on **Settings > Company**. In the Company tab, you will see the **Company Information** section.


To add to or edit the Company Gallery, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the **Edit **icon.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48168027465/original/BnKX9edBV3uUWsB9o7SGNFZ-6La_uHm3QQ.png?1638866608)[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48167354829/original/brmgginx1s165xYNKweRFnLGMfjjJPs9Mg.png?1638775276)


2.  Scroll down to the **Company Gallery** column and click on the **+Choose** button. Select the image(s) you wish to upload, and click **Open**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48167356818/original/TDJae_doTIgkvG1ndQBCmBDm1u8l_wKMcg.png?1638775445)


3.  Click the **Upload **button to confirm the upload. If you do not wish to proceed, click **Cancel **to discard any changes made.
(To delete an image, click on the **'x'** icon next to the uploaded image.)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-29-2025-08-54-56-7642-AM.png?width=1490&height=514&name=image-png-Jul-29-2025-08-54-56-7642-AM.png)


4.  Click **Save **to complete the action. Otherwise, click **Cancel **to discard any changes made.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48167360056/original/ljCCtRgxXBAE9IuloQMBbi8FW2hyRf590w.png?1638775781)


#### Image Dimension Guide:


- **The suggested ratios are 4:3 or 16:9, with a maximum file size of 1MB.**
- **Examples of ratio and pixel dimensions:**
**A.  Ratio 4:3: 1000 x 750 px**
**B.  Ratio 16:9: 1000 x 563 px**
- **Please use the same ratio for all uploaded images to ensure that the thumbnails displayed are aligned. For instance, if you choose the 4:3 ratio, all images uploaded must use the 4:3 ratio as well.**
