# How to Add or Edit Your Company Name in BrioHR

*This article explains how to add or edit your company name under Settings > Company.*

Once logged in to your BrioHR account, locate and click the **Settings** icon in the top right corner of the page.


Next, navigate to the **Company** tab to access the **Company Information** section.


To add or edit the Company Name, follow these steps:


1. Click on the **Edit** icon.
2. Enter the desired Company Name in the **Name** field.
3. Click **Save** to apply the changes. To discard any modifications, click **Cancel.**
