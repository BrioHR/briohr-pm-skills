# How to Add or Edit Your Company's 'About Us' Information on BrioHR

*This article explains how to add or edit your company's About Us information under Settings > Company.*

Once you have logged into your BrioHR account, navigate to **Settings > Company**. On the Company tab, you will find the **Company Information** section.

To add or edit your company's 'About Us' information, follow these steps:


1.  Click the **Edit **icon.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48168027465/original/BnKX9edBV3uUWsB9o7SGNFZ-6La_uHm3QQ.png?1638866608)


2.  In the **'About Us'** column, add or edit the company's description.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48168078044/original/PT8Cxw8e2TFEw1MieSeZ41p41NvLdu3cVg.png?1638871250)


3.  Click **Save **to complete the action, or click **Cancel **to discard any changes.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48168079302/original/hs5PynpSjqLPn5cWdLK2irqPsT9_vtPJog.png?1638871366)
