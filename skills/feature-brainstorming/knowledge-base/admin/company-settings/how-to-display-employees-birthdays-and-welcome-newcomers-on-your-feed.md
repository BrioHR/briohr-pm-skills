# How to Display Employees' Birthdays and Welcome Newcomers on Your Feed

*This article explains how to display employee birthdays and newcomer welcome messages on the company feed.*

In the Feed Settings, you can now choose to 'Display Employees' Birthdays' and 'Display Newcomers Welcome Messages' on the feed.


These settings are enabled by default. However, if you disable them, the system will hide all related posts, including those from the past.


If you want to display welcome messages on the feed again, simply re-enable the setting, and the system will unhide all previous posts.


---


Follow the steps below to configure these settings:


1.  In the main settings (top right corner of the screen), click on **Company **then click on **Edit**

2.  At **Feed Settings**, enable or disable the settings as needed, and click **Save** once you've made the changes.
