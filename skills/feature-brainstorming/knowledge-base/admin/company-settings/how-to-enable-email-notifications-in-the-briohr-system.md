# How to Enable Email Notifications in the BrioHR System

*This article explains how to enable email notifications from the BrioHR system under Settings > Company.*

Once you have logged in to your BrioHR account, click on **Settings > Company**. In the Company tab, you will be able to view the **Company Information** section.

To receive email notifications from the BrioHR system, follow these steps:


**NOTE:**


**If you do not enable this function, you will not receive any email notifications from the BrioHR system. These email notification include emails sent out by the system for leave applications, approvals and also tasks to be completed. **


1.  Click on the **Edit **icon.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48167929215/original/xenW5EbyXQy3l6JyiA7z5Ax5QgJL-UsIZQ.png?1638857139)


2.  Check the **email **checkbox next to the **Subscriptions **column.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48167931137/original/3NR6xz1Ap6v2zsvYE5t3PbNDBb6jHtbu3A.png?1638857309)


3.  Click **Save **to complete the action. Otherwise, click **Cancel **to discard any changes.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48167931331/original/yi9Wgg_hshOsBXOrQSIX9HZbUZlhWxu3Bw.png?1638857430)
