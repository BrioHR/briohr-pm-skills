# How to Assign a Head of Department for the Training Module Approval Process

*Assigning a Head of Department is critical for managing training approvals and workflows in the Training Management Module.*

### Introduction


Assigning a Head of Department is an essential step to managing the Training Management module approvals effectively and maintaining a smooth flow. This guide walks you through the importance of assigning a department head.


### Why Assigning a Department Head is Essential


The **Head of Department (HOD)** plays a key role in the **training management module's approval workflow**. When the HOD role is included in the approval process, all related approval tasks will be automatically assigned to the employee designated as the Head of Department. This person will be responsible for approving:


- Training enrollment requests
- Training creation requests


These approvals apply to all submissions made by employees within their department. Having a designated HOD ensures accountability and a more structured approval process.

### Step-by-Step Guide: Assign a Head of Department in Admin Settings


#### Step 1 - Log in to BrioHR Account


Log in to your BrioHR account using your registered credentials.


#### Step 2 - Access the Departments Settings


1. Go to **Settings**.


2. Select **Departments**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-10-2025-08-43-23-6008-AM.png?width=2000&height=921&name=image-png-Oct-10-2025-08-43-23-6008-AM.png)


#### Step 3 - Assign the Head of Department


1. Find the relevant department (e.g., Human Resources) and click the **pencil icon**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-10-2025-08-44-50-2945-AM.png?width=2000&height=793&name=image-png-Oct-10-2025-08-44-50-2945-AM.png)


2. In the 'Head of Department' field, select a name from the drop-down list.


3. Click **Save**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-10-2025-08-45-54-4248-AM.png?width=1684&height=973&name=image-png-Oct-10-2025-08-45-54-4248-AM.png)


4. The **'Head of Department'** is now set for the Human Resources department.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-10-2025-08-46-26-9985-AM.png?width=2000&height=785&name=image-png-Oct-10-2025-08-46-26-9985-AM.png)

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


#### 1. Can I assign more than one Head of Department?No, each department can only have one Head of Department assigned.


**2. What happens if the Head of Department changes roles?**
You can update the assignment by editing the department settings and selecting a new employee.


**3. Does the Head of Department get a notification when assigned?**
No, the assigned Head of Department will not receive any notification once the role is updated in the system.


**4. Can the Head of Department approve trainings for other departments?**
No, the approval rights are limited to the department they are assigned to.
