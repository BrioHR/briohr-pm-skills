# Additional Public Holiday in Selangor : 25th October 2026 (Silver Jubilee Celebration of the Reign of DYMM Sultan Selangor)

*With BrioHR, no action is needed in line with this announcement as we’ve updated our system to reflect the additional public holiday!*

The Selangor State Government has declared **25th October 2026** (**Sunday**) as an additional public holiday in conjuction with the Silver Jubilee Celebration of the Reign of DYMM Sultan Selangor. As this Public Holiday falls on a Sunday, the replacement holiday has been added on the next working day, **26th October 2026** (**Monday**).
(Source: [Portal Kerajaan Negeri Selangor](https://www.selangor.gov.my/index.php/pages/view/23?mid=64&fbclid=IwY2xjawQSAaRleHRuA2FlbQIxMABicmlkETFNeTlFRkoxRHM2elhqbzRIc3J0YwZhcHBfaWQQMjIyMDM5MTc4ODIwMDg5MgABHqd1fw_Yez3emf0JZegUe7HNbCeQKVVDSKkhrHsEEyXp0KtfFcvNoNEmoP7-_aem_tronP43SZbP7yFT6HK1Kzw))


In line with this announcement, we've updated our system with the public holiday, "**Silver Jubilee Celebration of the Reign of DYMM Sultan Selangor  & Silver Jubilee Celebration of the Reign of DYMM Sultan Selangor Holiday**", on the **25th October 2026 and 26th October 2026** that has been added for **all offices** in the **Selangor **state.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-06-2026-04-50-25-4485-AM.png?width=473&height=891&name=image-png-Mar-06-2026-04-50-25-4485-AM.png)


**NOTE:**


**This public holiday is automatically added to the system. If you prefer to disable it, please follow these steps:**


1. Go to **Settings** > **Offices**.
2. Select the relevant office and click **Edit** (green pencil icon).
3. In the "Edit an office" window, navigate to the **Public Holidays** tab.
4. Toggle off ** "Silver Jubilee Celebration of the Reign of DYMM Sultan Selangor Holiday"**
5. Click **Submit** to save your changes.
