# How to Add, Edit, and Delete Offices in BrioHR

*BrioHR offers an intuitive way to manage different locations within your company. Whether you're adding a new office, updating office details, or deleting an office that is no longer needed, this guide will walk you through the steps.*

**Adding a new office** to BrioHR is simple and helps streamline employee management based on location. Here's how you can add an office:


Refer to this article if you want to learn how to edit an employee's office in their profile:


[How to Update Employee Employment Details](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-update-employee-employment-details?hsLang=en)


---


### How to Add an Office in BrioHR


Log in to your BrioHR account.


1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Select **Offices.**
3. Click the **Add new office **button.
4. A pop-out window will appear for you to add a new office.


####


#### Setting up Office's Basic Configuration:


- **Office Name**: Enter a unique name for the office.
- **Description (Optional)**: Add additional details to help identify the office.
- **Country & State**: Select the correct country and state to ensure accurate public holiday settings.


**NOTE:**
**Ensure you select the correct country and state, as this will affect the display of public holidays for the new office. **


*For example, if the state chosen is "Kuala Lumpur", the system will follow public holiday for state Kuala Lumpur.*


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-23-2024-01-31-43-6686-PM.png?width=688&height=332&name=image-png-Sep-23-2024-01-31-43-6686-PM.png)


Additional Options:


- **Assign an Office HR Manager**: (Optional) This can be useful for HR oversight at the office level. Learn more about assigning HR Maanager in [this article](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-assign-an-office-hr-manager-to-an-office?hsLang=en).
- **Enable 'Work on Weekends' or 'Work on Holidays'**: (Optional) Useful for offices with non-traditional working days.
- **Customize Working Days**: (Optional) You can customize the working days for this office, which can be useful for non-standard schedules. Learn more on use case of customizing working days in [this article.](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-enable-leave-applications-for-employees-on-friday/saturday-in-johor-kedah-kelantan-terengganu-offices?hsLang=en)
- Click **Submit **to save a new office.


**NOTE:**


**Enabling "Work on Weekend" and "Work on Holiday" will affect leave applications for employees assigned to this office. **


**The system will treat weekends (Saturday/Sunday) and public holidays as working days. If an employee applies for leave on these days, the leave entitlement will be deducted.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-23-2024-01-32-30-1589-PM.png?width=688&height=330&name=image-png-Sep-23-2024-01-32-30-1589-PM.png)


#### Managing Office's Public Holidays:


Public holidays will automatically update once you have selected the country and state (e.g., Bangkok, Thailand).


Click the articles below for more information on:


1. [How to Add or Customize Public Holidays in BrioHR](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-add-or-customize-public-holidays-in-briohr?hsLang=en)
2. [How to Disable Public Holidays in BrioHR](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/disable-public-holidays-briohr-guide?hsLang=en)


**NOTE:**


**Adding or disabling public holidays will impact employees' leave applications.**
**For example:**


- **Adding a custom public holiday on September 23, 2024, will block leave applications on that date.**
- **Disabling a public holiday on December 25, 2024, will allow employees to apply for leave, and their leave entitlement will be deducted.**

### How to Edit an Office in BrioHR


If you need to **update office details** such as changing the office name, location, or work days settings, follow these steps:


Log in to your BrioHR account.


1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Select **Offices.**
3. Find the relevant office (e.g., Kuala Lumpur) and click **the pencil icon.**
4. A pop-out window will appear for you to edit the configuration.


####


---


### How to Delete an Office in BrioHR


Log in to your BrioHR account.


1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Select **Offices**
3. Find the relevant office (e.g., Kuala Lumpur) and click **the dust bin icon.**
4. A pop-out window will confirm your choice.


**WARNING:**
**You cannot delete an office if any employee is assigned to it. You must first unassign all employees from the office before deleting it.**

### Common Issues When Managing Offices in BrioHR


- **Unable to Delete Office**: Ensure no employees are assigned to the office before attempting to delete it. Reassign employees to different offices before attempting to delete.
- **Changes Not Reflecting**: If office edits don’t appear in employee profiles, refresh the system or check user permissions.
- **Incorrect Public Holidays:** Double-check that the correct country and state are selected for the office, as public holidays are tied to these settings.
