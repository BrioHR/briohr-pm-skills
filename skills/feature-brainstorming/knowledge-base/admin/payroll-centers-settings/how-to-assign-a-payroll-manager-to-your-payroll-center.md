# How to Assign a Payroll Manager to Your Payroll Center

*Learn how to assign or remove a Payroll Manager in BrioHR’s Payroll Center. Step-by-step guide for managing payroll access and generating payroll reports.*

#### Why Assign a Payroll Manager to the Payroll Center?


Assigning a **Payroll Manager** in BrioHR ensures proper access control and efficient payroll processing. A Payroll Manager has permission to:


- Access the **Payroll Center** of your account.
- Download **company payroll reports** from the Reports module.
- Manage multiple payroll centers by restricting which payroll processors can access specific centers.


**Note:**
Users with **Admin Settings** access can still access the Payroll Center, even if they are not designated as Payroll Managers. However, only Payroll Managers can generate **employee and company payroll reports**.


### Steps to Assign a Payroll Manager


1.   Login to BrioHR and click on the **Settings > Payroll Centers**


2.  At the Payroll Center you wish to assign the Payroll Manager, click on **Edit **(green pencil  icon).


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-12-2025-07-30-31-8872-AM.png?width=1901&height=722&name=image-png-Sep-12-2025-07-30-31-8872-AM.png)

3.   In the **Edit Payroll Center** pop-up window, select the **Payroll Manager** from the dropdown list of employees.


4.   Click **Submit **to complete the action. If you need to cancel any modifications, click **Cancel**.

### How to Remove a Payroll Manager


To remove a Payroll Manager, follow the same steps as above and simply **untick the name** before clicking **Submit**.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**1. Can an Admin access the Payroll Center without being a Payroll Manager?**
Yes, Admins with **Settings access** can open the Payroll Center. However, they must be assigned as Payroll Managers to generate payroll reports.


**2. Can multiple Payroll Managers be assigned to one Payroll Center?**
Yes. You can assign more than one Payroll Manager to manage access for multiple users.


**3. What happens if I don’t assign a Payroll Manager?**
Without a Payroll Manager, users may be unable to generate **employee or company payroll reports**, limiting payroll management functions.


**4. Can I reassign Payroll Manager access later?**
Yes. You can edit or remove Payroll Managers anytime by updating the Payroll Center settings.
