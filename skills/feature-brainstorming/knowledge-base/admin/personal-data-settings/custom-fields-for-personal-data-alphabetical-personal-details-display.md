# Custom Fields for Personal Data: Alphabetical Personal Details Display

*This article explains the alphabetical display enhancement for custom fields in Personal Data > Personal Details.*

In a recent system update, we have enhanced the '**Personal Data > Personal Details**' field, offering users a more organized and user-friendly experience. This enhancement can be observed in two key areas:


1. **Admin Settings > Personal Data > Personal Details**
2. **Employee's Profile > Personal Details**


Under Admin Settings, the 'Personal Details' section now showcases custom options in alphabetical order.

**This enhancement specifically pertains to the "Field Type" > "Select" feature.**


![bUhJcPOt1emAfehVUfba9IPNElhoXDLUmA.png?1699956544](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/bUhJcPOt1emAfehVUfba9IPNElhoXDLUmA.png%3F1699956544.png?width=395&height=66&name=bUhJcPOt1emAfehVUfba9IPNElhoXDLUmA.png%3F1699956544.png)


For existing clients, accessing these improvements is straightforward. Simply click on the edit icon to open the '**Edit personal detail item**' pop-up. Here, you can:


1. **View existing options.**
2. **Add new options (by pressing ‘Enter’ after inputting).**
3. **Delete saved options (via the X icon next to the option).**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48313531042/original/0FvS6N9uC5ugB5iqMThDODyfGsR2x5aOFg.png?1696918159)To ensure seamless integration, it's crucial to select the '**Assigned to office**' field for the desired offices where you want the custom Personal Details field to appear on employee profiles.


**Keep in mind that employees must be assigned to the selected offices for the field to display on their profiles.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48313531082/original/mBEFihmyEjonKe6QYhA2dM--XhZLnZICAA.png?1696918202)


**Before Update**: Custom options were not arranged in alphabetical order.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48313531187/original/mO_wWUEew5FmD1OCoHmXOZfMrOhXoCSUcA.png?1696918284)


**After Update**: Options are now arranged in alphabetical order.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48313531231/original/XFbmQeFhLzEC-n2b30HY-Y3OSaH5pcJWQQ.png?1696918315)


No changes need to be made to existing options once edits are saved in the '**Edit personal detail item**' pop-up. The process ensures a hassle-free transition to the improved display. Upon completing these steps, the changes, including the alphabetical arrangement of options, will be immediately reflected in the employee's profile.
