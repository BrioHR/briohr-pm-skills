# How to Manage Emergency Contact Relationships Types in BrioHR

*Learn how to add, edit, or delete emergency contact relationship types in BrioHR. This guide explains how to customise relationship options beyond family members and manage them easily in Settings.*

### Introduction


BrioHR allows you to **customise emergency contact relationship types** to better reflect real-life situations. These relationship types are not limited to family members and can include guardians, partners, friends, or any other relevant contacts.


Customising these options ensures accurate employee records and greater flexibility when managing emergency contact information.


---


### Before You Begin


To manage emergency contact relationship types, you must have permission as main account Admin with access to Admin Settings.





**NOTE:**

Changes made here will affect all employees who use the selected relationship type.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Modifying the Relationship Types


#### Step 1 - Log In to BrioHR Account


Log in to your BrioHR account using your registered credentials.


#### Step 2 - Access the Emergency Contact RelationshipsSettings


1. Go to **Settings**, and click on the **Personal Data** tab.


2. Select the tab **Emergency Contact Relationships**.


You will see a list of existing default relationship types.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-19-2026-09-04-55-3334-AM.png?width=2000&height=925&name=image-png-Jan-19-2026-09-04-55-3334-AM.png)


#### Step 3 - Add a New Emergency Contact Relationship Type


1. Click the **+Add **button.


2. A pop out window will appear.


3. Enter the new relationship type (e.g., Guardia, Partner, Neighbour etc)


4. Click **Save.**


The new relationship type will now be available in employee profiles.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-19-2026-09-05-47-5161-AM.png?width=2000&height=921&name=image-png-Jan-19-2026-09-05-47-5161-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-19-2026-09-05-43-6504-AM.png?width=2000&height=789&name=image-png-Jan-19-2026-09-05-43-6504-AM.png)


#### Step 4 - Edit an Existing Emergency Relationship Type


**To update a relationship type:**


- Click on the **Edit **icon.
- Enter the new relationship type.
- Click the **Save **icon to save the changes.


**NOTE:**


When you edit an existing relationship type in the Settings dashboard, it will automatically update the existing relationship type for all employees in their profiles accordingly.


For example:

If you change the relationship type from "Spouse" to "Husband," the emergency contact relationship in the employee's profile > Emergency tab will automatically change from "Spouse" to "Husband."


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-19-2026-09-06-45-9219-AM.png?width=2000&height=765&name=image-png-Jan-19-2026-09-06-45-9219-AM.png)


#### Step 5 - Delete Existing Emergency Relationship Type


**To remove an existing relationship type:**


- Click on the **Bin **icon.
- Click on the **Confirm **icon to complete the action.


**IMPORTANT REMINDER:**

Deleting a relationship type will permanently remove it from the system. T**his action cannot be undone.**

Any employee records using this relationship type will also be affected.
