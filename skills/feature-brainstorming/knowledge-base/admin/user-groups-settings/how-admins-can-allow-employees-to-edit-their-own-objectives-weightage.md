# How Admins Can Allow Employees to Edit Their Own Objectives Weightage

*Learn how Admins can enable employees to edit their own objectives weightage in BrioHR. Step-by-step guide with permissions setup and employee view explained.*

By default, BrioHR does **not allow employees to edit their own objectives weightage**. However, Admins can configure **User Group permissions** to grant employees the ability to add, update, and manage their own objectives weightage in the Performance Module.


This article provides a **step-by-step guide** for enabling this feature and explains how employees can use it once enabled.





### Steps to Enable Employees to Edit Their Own Objectives Weightage


1.    Login to BrioHR and click into **Settings > User Group**


2.   At the **Employee User Group**, click on the **Edit **icon.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-07-2025-10-33-51-9107-AM.png?width=670&height=260&name=image-png-Sep-07-2025-10-33-51-9107-AM.png)


3.   Within the Employee User Group, go to **Employee Modules Permission**.


4.   In the **Performance Module** column, tick the checkbox **“Allow to edit objectives weightage.”**


5.   Click **Save** to apply the changes.


**IMPORTANT :
****If this checkbox is not selected, employees will NOT be able to edit their own objectives weightage.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-07-2025-10-38-05-5095-AM.png?width=670&height=632&name=image-png-Sep-07-2025-10-38-05-5095-AM.png)

### Employee View: Editing Objectives Weightage


Once enabled, employees can manage their own objectives through **My Goals** on their Home page:


1. Navigate to **Home > My Goals**.
2. In the **Objectives column**, click the **+ Add New Objective** icon to add a new objective with weightage.
3. After entering the details, click **Create Objective** to save.


These updates will then be reflected in:


- **Objectives**
- **Appraisal Form > Objectives Feedback**

### Editing Existing Objectives


1. Click the **Edit (pencil) icon** for the objective to be modified.
2. Adjust the weightage as needed.
3. Click **Update Objective** to confirm the changes.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**1. Can I enable this feature only for certain employees?**
No, the permission is applied at the **User Group level**. To give access to selected employees, assign them to a specific User Group.


**2. What happens if I don’t enable this permission?**
Employees will be able to view their objectives but **cannot edit weightage**. Only managers or Admins can make changes.


**3. Can managers still override employee objectives weightage?**
Yes, managers and Admins with higher-level access can still adjust objectives regardless of employee permissions.


**4. Will edits made by employees reflect in performance reviews?**
Yes, any updates to objectives weightage will automatically sync to the **Appraisal Form > Objectives Feedback** section.
