# How to Allow Office HR Managers to Access Selected Payroll Center’s Employees’ Compensation and Payroll Information

*This guide explains how to enable Office HR Managers to view or modify employees’ compensation and payroll information for a specific payroll center in BrioHR. Follow the steps to manage user group permissions effectively.*

#### Prerequisite: Assign Office HR Manager


Before granting permissions, ensure that an **Office HR Manager** has been assigned.
Refer to this guide for detailed steps:
[How to Assign an Office HR Manager to an Office](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-assign-an-office-hr-manager-to-an-office?hsLang=en).


---


#### Enable Compensation and Payroll Access


1. Go to **Settings** > **User Groups**.
2. Click on **Edit** to modify the selected user group.
3. Adjust the permissions under **Profile Sensitive Data**:

  - To enable **viewing/modifying compensation**, tick **"View your scope"** under **Compensation**.
  - To enable **viewing/modifying payroll information**, tick **"View your scope"** under **Payroll Information**.
4. Click on **Save** to save the changes.
