# How to Enable Manager Access to View Team Performance in BrioHR

*Learn how to configure User Group permissions in BrioHR to allow managers to view and evaluate their team’s performance. Step-by-step guide included.*

For managers and appraisers to effectively evaluate and guide their team, they must have access to their direct reports’ performance pages. In BrioHR, this access is managed through **User Group permissions**. By enabling the right permissions, managers can view their team’s performance via the **Team Performance section** on the Team page.


This guide walks you through the steps to enable manager access to team performance in BrioHR.





### Why Managers Need Access to Team Performance


Granting managers access to view their team’s performance is essential for:


- Conducting accurate **performance evaluations**.
- Providing timely **feedback and coaching**.
- Monitoring **employee growth and development**.
- Driving **overall team productivity**.


Without this access, managers are limited in their ability to effectively support and evaluate their team.





### How to Enable Manager Access to Team Performance


1.    Login to BrioHR and click into **Settings > User Group**


2.   At the **Manager User Group**, click on the **Edit **icon.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-07-2025-12-16-43-7908-PM.png?width=1893&height=782&name=image-png-Sep-07-2025-12-16-43-7908-PM.png)


3.   Within the Manager User Group, go to **Manager Modules Permission**.


4.   Under **Manager Modules Permission** (represents the Team page), look for the **Performance module**.


5.   Toggle the permission **ON**.


6.   Select the **Team Access level**, which defines the scope of direct reports a manager can view.


7.   Click **Save** to apply the changes.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-07-2025-12-21-22-9603-PM.png?width=1040&height=808&name=image-png-Sep-07-2025-12-21-22-9603-PM.png)


Once enabled, managers will see the **Team Performance section** on the Team page and can evaluate their direct reports’ performance effectively.


### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**1. Can all managers automatically view their team’s performance?**
No, only managers with the **Performance module permission enabled** in their User Group can access this feature.


**2. What is the “Team Access” level setting?**
The **Team Access level** determines the scope of employees a manager can view, such as only direct reports or extended teams.


**3. Who can update User Group permissions?**
Only the **main account admin** (with access to Settings) can configure and update permissions.


**4. Can employees view their own performance pages without manager permissions?**
Yes, employees can view their **personal performance pages**, but managers need specific permissions to view their team’s performance pages.
