# How to Enable Managers and HR to Perform Expense Claim Actions on Behalf of Others

*Learn how to grant managers and HR permission to perform expense claim actions on behalf of employees in BrioHR. Step-by-step guide for Admins.*

A new enhancement has been made to the Expense Claims module in BrioHR. As an Admin, you can now **give employees or claim assignees permission to perform claim actions, such as creating new claim items or approving claims for non-team members, on behalf of other employees.**


This feature is available if the assignee has access to HR Lounge > Expense Claims.


---


### Steps to Grant Permission:


Login to your BrioHR account.


1.   Go to **Settings**.


2.   Navigate to **User Groups.**


3.   Choose a user group (e.g., Manager). Click the pencil icon to edit feature accessibility.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48329107128/original/0U1oLHFLAKddIYE12AnUDox7Bvmzm3cXJw.png?1709533843)


4.   A form to Edit User Group will appear.


5.   Navigate to **HR Modules Permission**.


6.   Go to the **Claim Module **section.


7.   Tick the box for **Perform actions on behalf of others** to enable it.


8.   Click **Save **once done.

You have now granted permission for employees in the selected user group to perform claim actions on behalf of others in HR Lounge > Expense Claims.
