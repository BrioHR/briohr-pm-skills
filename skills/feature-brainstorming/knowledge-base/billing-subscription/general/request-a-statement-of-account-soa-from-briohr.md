# Request a Statement of Account (SOA) from BrioHR

*Learn what a Statement of Account (SOA) is and how to request it from BrioHR by emailing finance@briohr.com to review invoice history, payment status, and outstanding balances.*

### Audience & Scope


Audience: HR Admin / Company


Module: Invoice/Billing with BrioHR Finance


Country: Global


---


### Introduction


A **Statement of Account (SOA)** is a summary document that shows all financial transactions between a company (client) and a service provider (vendor) over a specific period.


Unlike an **invoice**, which requests payment for a specific charge, an SOA provides an **overall view of your account status**.


In simple terms, an SOA shows:


- What has been billed
- What has been paid (based on invoice payment status)
- What is still outstanding


Requesting an SOA helps you review **invoice records, payment amounts, payment status, and reconciliation details** for your company.


This article explains how to request a **Statement of Account from BrioHR**.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**Q1: Can I request an SOA for a specific date range?**


A1: Yes. You may request an SOA for any specific period, as long as you clearly state the start and end dates in your request.


**Q2: Who should I contact if I notice discrepancies in my SOA?**


A2: If you notice any discrepancies, please contact BrioHR Finance directly at **finance@briohr.com**.
