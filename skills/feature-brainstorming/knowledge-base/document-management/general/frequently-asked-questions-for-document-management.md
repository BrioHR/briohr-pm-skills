# Frequently Asked Questions for Document Management

*This article explains what are some of the frequently asked questions for the Document Management module.*

#### Audience & Scope


Audience: HR Admin / Manager / Employee


Module: Document Management


Country: Global


Pre-requisites: Access to Document Management module





#### Frequently Asked Questions


**Q: What are Manual Fields and Dynamic Fields in a template?**


A: **Manual Fields** are fields that are added during the drafting stage but must be filled in manually when generating the document while **Dynamic Fields** automatically pull employee information from the system based on the data available in the employee’s profile.


**Q: How do I activate a template after creating it?**


A: After saving the template, it will be in **Draft** status by default. Click the **three-dot icon** next to the template and select **Activate Template**, then confirm the action in the pop-up notification.


**Q: Can I update employee information before generating the document?**


A: Yes. If employee information has been updated, you can click **Refresh Employee Data** to capture the latest information before generating the document.


**Q: How can I track the status of document generation?**


A: Once documents are generated, they will initially show an **In Progress** status. You can click **See Details** for updates. When the process is complete, the status will change to **Completed**.


**Q: Can I add more employees after creating the document generation request?**


A: Yes. If additional employees need to be included, you can **add employees to the list** before generating the documents.


**Q: Can I download or delete generated documents?**


A: Yes. Once the document generation is completed, you will be able to **download a copy** of the document or **delete it** if it is no longer required.


**Q: Where will generated documents be stored?**


A: Generated documents will be saved under the **Employee Profile → Document tab**.


**Q: Do employees need additional permissions to sign documents?**


A: Yes. Employees who are required to sign documents must have the **Document Management** permission enabled under **MyHR**. Managers and Heads of Department must also have the **Document Management** permission enabled under **Team Dashboard**.


**Q: Do signatures need to be completed in a specific order?**


A: No. All assigned signers can review and sign the document simultaneously. There is no sequential approval order, allowing the signing process to be completed more efficiently.


**Q: What happens if I enable the Document Workflow but forget to add a signature field?**


A: The template editor will display a warning indicating that a required signature field is missing. The warning will only disappear once a signature field has been added for every role included in the workflow.


**Q: When is a document marked as completed?**


A: A document is automatically marked as **Completed** once all required signers have submitted their signatures.


**Q: Where do users sign their assigned documents?**


A: The signing location depends on the user's role:


- **Employees** sign documents through **MyHR → Document Management**.
- **Managers**, **Heads of Department**, and **Specific Employees** sign documents through **Team Dashboard → Document Management**.
