# How to Request and Submit Cash Advance Claims

*This article explains how to request and submit cash advance claims for business-related needs.*

BrioHR's latest enhancement to its expense claim features is the introduction of the cash advance claim type. This functionality allows you to request a cash advance for various business-related needs, such as business travel, projects, and more.


Once you save a cash advance request claim report, a new report labeled "**Cash Advance Request**" will be created. This label indicates that the claim is a cash advance type and distinguishes it from other types of claims.


You can create this claim report by clicking the "**+Request Cash Advance**" button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48321201565/original/-CKh2Au8tivyGe8Sc2PZU3pwIDlgzcxnfw.png?1703036933)


After settling a cash advance request, a separate claim report labeled "**Cash Advance Settlement**" will appear on your My Claims dashboard.


**NOTE:**


**This report allows you to account for each transaction made using the reimbursed cash advance.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48321202216/original/7Pl__aL7yLWityuGnb-r9h4h0mQLgdXaBg.png?1703038024)
