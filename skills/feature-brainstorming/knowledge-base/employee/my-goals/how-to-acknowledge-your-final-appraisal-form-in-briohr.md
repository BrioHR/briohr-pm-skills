# How to Acknowledge Your Final Appraisal Form in BrioHR

*This article explains how to acknowledge your final appraisal form after your manager releases it.*

Once your manager releases the appraisal form, you will receive a notification to acknowledge your appraisal via:


1. Email
2. The Pending Actions list when you log in to your BrioHR account.


The image below shows a sample of the email notification you will receive when the appraisal has been released.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48168857694/original/iC_4ZhFsZDJ2BPJAomHB4U5rzMqBM97bQg.png?1639016347)


To log in to your BrioHR account:


1. Go to **Home**.
2. Navigate to **My Goals**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48168858088/original/fAbbwYVbLzHoyRxI-yfr2MJ861OjeSdpxA.png?1639016724)


You will be directed to your **Performance page**.


3.    Click on **Appraisal Form**. Here, you'll see a button labeled '**Acknowledge Appraisal**'.


4.    Review the feedback and rating in this section. Once confirmed, click on '**Acknowledge Appraisal**'.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48168858912/original/PaMzTogsJDzMlEx373pAapia8n_kSwDCVg.png?1639017544)


5.    A confirmation pop-up message will appear.


6.    Click **Continue **to submit your acknowledgment.
