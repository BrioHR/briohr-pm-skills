# How to Add and Update Objective Progress in BrioHR

*Learn how to view, add, update, and modify performance objectives in BrioHR using the My Goals dashboard. Step-by-step guide with FAQs and HR tips.*

### Introduction


The** My Goals** feature in BrioHR is your personal P**erformance Appraisal dashboard, **accessible from the** Home page** or **My HR > Goals. **This feature allows you to:


- View the **performance appraisal period timeline**
- See objectives and **add personal KPIs.**
- Review **assigned competencies.**
- Add and track **training or courses.**
- Complete your **self-appraisal form.**
- Fulfill your **360 feedback tasks.**
- Access the **1-on-1 section **with managers and to record notes.


#### In this guide, we will cover how to:


a) View your objectives.


b) Add new personal objectives.


c) Update progress.


d) Modify existing objectives.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Accessing My Goals Page


You can access the My Goals dashboard in two ways:


#### Option 1 - From the Home Page


Log in to your BrioHR account.


1. Click on the** Home** icon.

### A) Viewing Objectives


1. On your performance dashboard, click **Objectives.**


2. You can see the predefined **company and department-level objectives **set by your HR.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-28-2025-10-42-10-5119-AM.png?width=2000&height=842&name=image-png-Sep-28-2025-10-42-10-5119-AM.png)


---


### B) Adding New Personal Objectives (KPIs)


1. Click on **+Add Objectives.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-28-2025-10-45-57-8351-AM.png?width=2000&height=837&name=image-png-Sep-28-2025-10-45-57-8351-AM.png)


2. A pop-out window will appear.  Fill in the relevant fields.


##### What are the Key Result, Qualitative Goals and Contributing Goals?


- **Key Result: **Measurable goals (e.g., *Increase revenue by MYR 100,000*, or *Reduce support tickets by 15%*)
- **Qualitative Goals: **Non-quantifiable goals that support objective achievement (e.g, *Attend APAC Sales Convention for networking and company brand awareness*)
- **Contributing Goals: **Goals assigned to direct reports that contribute to your overall objectives (mainly for managers).


3. Click **Save** to add this new objective.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-28-2025-10-48-05-1907-AM.png?width=2000&height=1119&name=image-png-Sep-28-2025-10-48-05-1907-AM.png)

### C) Updating Progress of Existing Objective


1. Click the** ellipsis icon (...) **> Select** Update Objective Progress.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-28-2025-11-03-56-3228-AM.png?width=2000&height=809&name=image-png-Sep-28-2025-11-03-56-3228-AM.png)


2. Update the progress of **key results.**


3. Tick the **qualitative goal checkbox **once you have completed it.


4. Progress will automatically update as percentages.


5. To leave a comment, tick **Leave comment to key result/qualitative goal **and add your remark.


If adding supporting document to justify your comment is required, click **+Add Attachment.**


You are allowed to upload documents with a **maximum size of 10MB **per file in these supported file types:


- **.jpg**
- **.jpeg**
- **.png**
- **.pdf**
- **.txt**
- **.csv**
- **.xls**
- **.xlsx**
- **.doc**
- **.docx**
- **.ppt**
- **.pptx**

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**Q1. Can I edit my objectives after my manager confirms them?**


A: Yes and No, depending on access permission.


- It's a **Yes** if the permission to edit objective after confirmation is allowed (enabled).
- It's a **No** if the permission is disabled.


**Q2: What is the difference between Key Results and Qualitative Goals?**


A: Key Results are measurable targets (e.g, revenue goals), while Qualitative Goals focus on quality-based outcomes.


**Q3. Can managers set their objectives completion are dependent on their team members' objective achievement?**


A: Yes, through Contributing Goals, which linking team team members' objectives progress to a specific manager's objectives.


**Q4. Can managers add individual objectives on team members' behalf?**


A: Yes, managers can add individual objectives on behalf of their team members. To learn more, please refer to how managers can add team members' objectives.
