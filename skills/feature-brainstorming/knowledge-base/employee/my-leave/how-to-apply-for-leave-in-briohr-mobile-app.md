# How to Apply for Leave in BrioHR Mobile App

*Learn how to apply for leave using the BrioHR mobile app. Step-by-step guide with instructions for full-day, half-day leave, attachments, and leave approval process.*

### Introduction


The **BrioHR Mobile App** allows employees to apply for leave quickly and conveniently. Before submitting a leave request, ensure you have downloaded the app from the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play Store** and logged in with your credentials.


Learn More: [How to Download and Log in to BrioHR Mobile App (iOS and Android Guide)](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/download-and-login-to-briohr-mobile-app-ios-android-guide?hsLang=en)


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Applying for Leave via BrioHR Mobile App


#### Step 1 - Access the Leaves Section


- Log in to the **BrioHR mobile app**.
- From the main dashboard, tap the **Leaves** button.
- In the **Leaves **dashboard, you will see:

  - **Leave Balance:** Your current available leave
  - **Upcoming Leave/Pending Leave: **Requests awaiting approval
  - **Past Leave/Canceled Leave: **History of approved, used, or canceled leave


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-18-2025-09-05-43-1966-AM.png?width=688&height=629&name=image-png-Aug-18-2025-09-05-43-1966-AM.png)
**


#### Step 2 - Apply for a New Leave


- Tap the **+Apply Leave** button.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-18-2025-09-08-59-4149-AM.png?width=315&height=638&name=image-png-Aug-18-2025-09-08-59-4149-AM.png)


- Select the** leave type** (e.g., Annual Leave, Medical Leave).
- Tap **Choose Date** to select your desired leave period:

**A.  Start Date: Select from the calendar**
**B.  End Date: Select from the calendar.**


** If applying for a half-day leave:**


- Check the **Half-Day** box next to the date
- Select either:

  - **Half Day AM**: morning half-day leave (0.5 day)
  - **Half Day PM**: afternoon half-day leave (0.5 day)
- The system will automatically calculate the total leave days


- Tap **Choose (X days)** to confirm your selection.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48337210598/original/gUfGhl0y0xyIBgeqDdCdcPwVMPHUA5arLg.png?1715928826)


- Provide additional details if necessary:

  - **Justification**: Reason for your leave, if applicable.
  - **Attachment**: Upload supporting documents (e.g., medical certificate)
- Tap **Submit Request (X days)** to finalize your leave application.


---


### Step 3 - Confirmation and Approval


- Once submitted, your leave request will be sent to your **approver**.
- An **email notification** will be triggered to notify them.
- You can track the status of your request under **Pending Leave** in the Leaves dashboard.

#### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


**Q1: Can I cancel a leave request after submission?**


A: Yes. If your leave request is still pending approval or it has been approved, as long as the leave date is in a future-date, you can cancel it directly in the leave dashboard.


Related guide: [How to Cancel Leave Applications & Credit Requests (browser guide)](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-cancel-leave-applications-credit-requests?hsLang=en)


**NOTE:**


**If the leave is a future-date and you cannot cancel it, it is because of your access permission. Please contact your HR Admin for further assistance.**


**Q2: Can I apply for multiple leave types at once?**


A: No. Each leave type (e.g., Annual leave, Medical leave) must be submitted as a separate request.
