# How to Cancel Leave Applications & Credit Requests

*This article explains how to cancel your own leave application and credit request. You can cancel for future date leave only.*

### Introduction


In BrioHR, employees can cancel their own future-dated leave applications (e.g, annual leave, or credit replacement leave), provided they have the necessary permissions.


#### Important Notes:


- Always consult your HR Department first to confirm whether you have permission to cancel your own leave.
- If you do not have access, HR will need to cancel the leave application on your behalf.
- **Only future-dated leave applications** can be cancelled by employees.
- Past date leave applications can be cancelled by HR only.


For **HR Admins: **learn [how to permit employees to cancel their own leave applications](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-enable-employees-to-cancel-their-future-leave-applications?hsLang=en).


---


### Understanding "Past Leave"


##### What is considered past leave?


Any leave application that starts from a date in the past up to the current day.


Example:


If today is 23 September 2025, then any leave applied on or before 23 September 2025 is considered as past leave and cannot be cancelled by employees through Leave dashboard.


This guide is divided into two sections:


**A) Cancel Normal Leave (e.g., Annual Leave)**


**B) Cancel Credit Request (e.g., Replacement Leave)**

### Step-by-Step Guide: Cancel a Leave Application & Credit Leave Request


If you have the required permission, follow these steps:


#### Step 1 - Access the My Leave Dashboard


You can access My Leave in two ways:


##### Option 1 - From the Home page


1. Click the** Home** icon.


2. Select **My Leave.**

3. In the **Leave Details** pop-up, click the **Cancel leave** icon.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**Q1: Can I cancel a leave application for a past date?**


A: No. Past leave applications cannot be cancelled via the dashboard. Please contact your HR for assistance.


**Q2: Why I could not see the cancel button for a future date leave application?**


A: This usually means you do not have permission to cancel leave. HR will need to process the cancellation for you.


**Q3: Can managers cancel leave on behalf of employees?**


A: Yes. Managers can cancel leave on behalf of team members if the required permission is enabled. However, managers can only cancel future date leave applications.


**NOTE:**


There is no 'Leave cancellation window' setting for leaves applied in BrioHR. The only available action is:


1. Allow/disallow employee(s) to cancel future-dated leave via User Group Permissions (only accessible by HR admin).
2. HR admin override (cancel) past-dated leave(s) for employees.
