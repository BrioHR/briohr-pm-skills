# How to Check Leave Application Status in BrioHR

*Learn how to check the status of your leave application in BrioHR. View details such as approval status, approver, leave type, dates, justification, and timeline.*

### Introduction


Monitoring your leave application status in BrioHR ensures that your request has been properly reviewed and approved before you go on leave.


This feature also allows you to:


- Confirm whether your leave has been approved or rejected.
- View any additional details or requirements from the leave approver.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Check Leave Application Status


#### Step 1 - Login to BrioHR Account.


Login to your BrioHR Account.


1. Go to **MyHR > Leave**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-12-2025-07-41-14-0772-AM.png?width=336&height=385&name=image-png-Sep-12-2025-07-41-14-0772-AM.png)
**


---


#### Step 2 - Leave Request Status and History


1. Scroll down to the table titled **'Leave Request Status and History'**


2. Locate the specific leave request you want to review.


3. Check the** status **of the leave application directly in the table.


4. Click **See Details** to view more information, including:


- Leave type
- Leave date(s)
- Leave approver
- Justification message
- Attachments
- Activity timeline


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-12-2025-07-42-00-0950-AM.png?width=2000&height=925&name=image-png-Sep-12-2025-07-42-00-0950-AM.png)

### Why Checking Leave Status Matters


By checking your leave status before your actual leave date, you can avoid scheduling conflicts and ensure your request has been approved by the appropriate manager or HR approver.
