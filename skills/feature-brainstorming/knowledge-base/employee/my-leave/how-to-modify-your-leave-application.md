# How to Modify Your Leave Application

*This article explains how to modify your submitted leave application from the My Leave section.*

1.    **Log in to BrioHR:** Once you’ve logged into your BrioHR account, click on the **Home** icon and select **My Leave**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-25-2024-02-33-57-8495-AM.png?width=1903&height=873&name=image-png-Nov-25-2024-02-33-57-8495-AM.png)


2.   **Access Leave Details:** Click on the **See Details** icon under the **Leave Requests Status & History** column.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-25-2024-02-35-03-1359-AM.png?width=1893&height=867&name=image-png-Nov-25-2024-02-35-03-1359-AM.png)


3.    **Modify Your Leave:** In the **Leave Details** pop-up, select the **Modify Leave** icon.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-25-2024-02-35-31-1117-AM.png?width=1896&height=871&name=image-png-Nov-25-2024-02-35-31-1117-AM.png)


4.    **Update Dates**: Re-select the new **Start Date** and **End Date** for your leave.


**NOTE:**


**You cannot modify the year, only the dates.**


**Half-Day Leave: To apply for a half-day leave, tick the Morning Only or Afternoon Only checkbox next to the date. The system will automatically calculate the leave duration based on your selection.**


5.    **Add a Comment:** Input any **comments **at justification field, if applicable.


6.    **Upload Attachments:** Upload any relevant **attachments **by selecting the **Choose File** button.


7.    **Submit Your Changes:** Click the **Submit** button to confirm your modifications. If you do not wish to proceed, click the **X** icon at the top-right corner to cancel.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-25-2024-02-36-45-8322-AM.png?width=688&height=485&name=image-png-Nov-25-2024-02-36-45-8322-AM.png)


**Completion Notification:** After submitting, a pop-up notification will appear in the top-right corner, confirming that your leave modification was successful. An email notification will also be sent to the approver(s) to process your updated leave application.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-25-2024-02-37-48-6944-AM.png?width=666&height=156&name=image-png-Nov-25-2024-02-37-48-6944-AM.png)
