# How to Enroll or Unenroll from a Training Event in BrioHR

*Learn how employees can enroll or unenroll from training events in BrioHR. Follow this step-by-step guide to request enrollment, check approval status, or cancel training requests*

### Introduction


BrioHR enables employees to join training events that match their interests and support their career development. The platform makes it easy to **browse available training programs**, submit enrollment requests, and manage training participation.


This guide will walk you through the steps to **enroll in a training event**, check your enrollment status, and unenroll if needed.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide to Enroll in a Training Event


#### Step 1 - Access the My Training Page


You can access the Trainings page in two ways:


a) From the **Home Page **> click on **My Training**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-18-26-4385-AM.png?width=670&height=203&name=image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-18-26-4385-AM.png)
**


b) From the **My HR** on the navigation bar > click on **My Training**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-18-32-1849-AM.png?width=287&height=335&name=image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-18-32-1849-AM.png)
**


---


#### Step 2 - Browse the Training Catalog


1. On the My Training page, click on **Training Catalog**


2. Browse through the available events and select the training event that interests you.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-19-07-6572-AM.png?width=670&height=328&name=image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-19-07-6572-AM.png)


---


#### Step 3 - Review Event Details


Carefully review the training event details, including the **description, attachments, and links** provided.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-20-24-0218-AM.png?width=670&height=283&name=image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-20-24-0218-AM.png)


---


#### Step 4 - Enroll to the Training Event


1. On the event page, click **Request to Enroll.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-20-38-5597-AM.png?width=670&height=283&name=image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-20-38-5597-AM.png)


2. A confirmation pop-up will appear. Click **Request Enrollment** to proceed.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-21-21-1739-AM.png?width=572&height=292&name=image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-21-21-1739-AM.png)


Once your request is successful, you will see a confirmation message.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-21-50-7830-AM.png?width=379&height=143&name=image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-21-50-7830-AM.png)


---


#### Step 5 - Check Enrollment Status


1. Go to the **Training List.**


2. Search for the training event by name. The status will show as **Submitted** (pending approval).


3. If needed, click **Cancel Request** to cancel your enrollment request.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-22-30-7323-AM.png?width=670&height=282&name=image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-22-30-7323-AM.png)


**Tip:** If you hover over the 'Submitted' status, you will be able to view the pending approval details for the request.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-13-2026-03-32-46-0284-PM.png?width=1252&height=271&name=image-png-Apr-13-2026-03-32-46-0284-PM.png)


---


### How to Unenroll From a Training Event.


If you are already enrolled but wish to withdraw, follow these steps:


1. Go to the **Training List.**


2. Use the **Filter** option and select **Enrolled**.


3. Find the event and click **Unenroll**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-24-16-8436-AM.png?width=670&height=231&name=image-png-Aug-22-2025-09-24-16-8436-AM.png)

**Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**


**Q1. Can I enroll in multiple training events?
A: **Yes, you can submit requests for multiple events. Each request will be reviewed and approved by your HR or manager.


**Q2. How will I know if my enrollment is approved?**
**A: **Once approved, the status in your Training List will update to **Enrolled**, and you may also receive an email notification.


**Q3. Can I re-enroll after I have unenrolled from an event?**
**A:** Yes, as long as the enrollment window is still open, you can resubmit your request.
