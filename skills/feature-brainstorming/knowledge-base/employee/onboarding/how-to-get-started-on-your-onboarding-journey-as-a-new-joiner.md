# How to Get Started on Your Onboarding Journey as a New Joiner

*This article explains how new joiners log in for the first time and get started on their onboarding journey.*

### Congratulations!


Welcome to the team! As a new joiner at our company, you will receive an email titled "BrioHR Login: Account Created."


This email will contain your **username **and a **temporary password** to log in to BrioHR for the first time. Click on the "**Go to BrioHR**" button to log in.


If you wish to change your password, you can do so after logging in. Please refer to this [article](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-change-your-password-in-briohr?hsLang=en)for detailed instructions.
