# How to Download Your Personal IR8A Form from BrioHR

*This article explains how to download your personal IR8A form from Payroll Documents once it is released.*

Login to Your BrioHR Account


- From the **Home **page, click on **Payroll Documents**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48327212748/original/9CsxADBHTc1WEnq4DtuBqdSIm1usoy7vBQ.png?1707991464)


- Go to the **IR8A Form** tab.
- Look for the year 2023 with the status "**RELEASED**". Click on the **Download **button.


A PDF of your IR8A Form will be downloaded to your computer.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48327212758/original/V6rs3C1DLwg_3emN0yXY-IE0S989Yy2vSA.png?1707991471)
