# How to View and Download Your EA Form?

*This article explains how to view and download your EA form from Payroll Documents once HR releases it.*

You can start downloading your latest EA Form once your HR department has released it to you.


### Steps to Download Your EA Form


Login to BrioHR Account:


- On the **Home **page, click on **Payroll Documents**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48277002755/original/74vjAwFUrOJo9iX0y9Gu-b0nCpGJYH4_dw.png?1674459107)


- Click on **EA Form** under Document Type.


- - Choose the year of the EA Form you want to download. For the latest year assessment (e.g., Year 2022), ensure the status is marked as "**Released**".
- Click on the **Download **button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48277002844/original/OWaLzhy9YXiba44WxReI2dgJRqyw--wLTw.png?1674459139)


- A pop-up window will appear. Select the **language **version of the EA Form you prefer: Bahasa Malaysia or English.
- Click **Download **to save the form to your computer. Your EA Form will be downloaded as a PDF.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48277003165/original/7E1z2D8A2axihtj3zCtHsxXcVY0tCKH3sw.png?1674459261)
