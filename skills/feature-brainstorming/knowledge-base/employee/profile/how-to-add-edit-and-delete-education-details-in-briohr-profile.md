# How to Add, Edit and Delete Education Details in BrioHR Profile

*Learn how employees can add, update, or delete education details in BrioHR profiles. Step-by-step guide with permissions explained.*

### Introduction


BrioHR allows employees to keep their **education history updated** in their personal profile. This helps employers maintain accurate employee records for compliance, career development, and reporting purposes.


- **Employees** can add or edit their education details if they have the required permissions.
- **HR Admins** can add, update, or delete employees’ education details on their behalf.


**NOTE:**


If you cannot find the** add/edit/delete** options, it means your account does not have the necessary permissions. In such cases, contact your HR Admin.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Add, Modify and Edit Your Education Details in Profile


#### Step 1 - Access My Profile


Login to your BrioHR account.


1. Go to **Home page **> Click **View my profile.**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-05-54-07-7034-AM.png?width=2000&height=560&name=image-png-Sep-24-2025-05-54-07-7034-AM.png)
**


---


#### Step 2 - Add a New Education Details


Scroll to the Education Details section.


1. Click **+ (plus) icon** to add a new entry.


2. Fill in the required details in the **Education details **form, including:


- Degree
- University
- City & Country
- Start Date & End Date
- Description (optional)


3. Click **Save** to confirm.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-05-57-35-8945-AM.png?width=2000&height=718&name=image-png-Sep-24-2025-05-57-35-8945-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-05-57-29-5495-AM.png?width=2000&height=1051&name=image-png-Sep-24-2025-05-57-29-5495-AM.png)


---


#### Step 3 - Edit or Delete Existing Education Details


**1. Edit Education:**


- Click the pencil icon next to the entry you want to update.
- Make the necessary changes and save.


**2. Delete Education:**


- Click the bin (trash) icon to remove an entry.
- Confirm deletion when prompted.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-05-59-23-4270-AM.png?width=2005&height=726&name=image-png-Sep-24-2025-05-59-23-4270-AM.png)

#### Important Notes


- If the** add/edit/delete buttons** are not visible in your profile, it means you do not have permission to make changes.
- In this case, reach out to your** Admin or HR Personnel **for assistance.

#### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**Q1. Why can't I add or edit my education details?**


A: This usually happens if your HR Admin has not granted permission for you to edit your profile. Please reach out to them for access.


**Q2: Can HR Admins update my education details for me?**


A: Yes, if you do not have access, your HR or Admin can update your education details in your behalf.


**Q3: Can I add multiple education records?**


A: Yes, you can add multiple entries.


**Q4. Are education details visible to my manager?**


A: Yes, managers and HR can view this information depending on access permission settings.
