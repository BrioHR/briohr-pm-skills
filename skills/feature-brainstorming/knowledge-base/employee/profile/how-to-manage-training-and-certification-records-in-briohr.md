# How to Manage Training & Certification Records in BrioHR

*Learn how to add, edit, or delete training and certification records in BrioHR. Step-by-step guide for employees to update their professional development history.*

### Introduction


Keeping your **training and certification records updated in BrioHR** helps you track your professional development and makes it easier to present your skills during performance appraisals. For employers, these records provide valuable insights into employee growth and competency.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Adding, Edit or Delete Training Records in Certifications in BrioHR


**Step 1 - Log In to BrioHR Account**


Log in to your **BrioHR account **using the registered credentials


**Step 2 - Access the Trainings & Certifications Section**


1. On the **Home page**, click **View My Profile**


2. Scroll to the **Training & Certification** section


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-24-24-3628-AM.png?width=2000&height=647&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-24-24-3628-AM.png)


####


---


#### A) Adding a Training or Certification


1. Click the **plus (+) icon**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-24-57-8552-AM.png?width=2000&height=884&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-24-57-8552-AM.png)
**


2. A pop-up window will appear to add details of new training or certification


3. Enter the required details (e.g., training name, institution, completion date, description)


3. Click **Save** to confirm


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-25-51-0424-AM.png?width=2000&height=1067&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-25-51-0424-AM.png)


---


#### B) Editing a Training or Certification


1. Locate the training or certification you want to update


2. Click the** pencil (edit) icon**


3. Modify the information accordingly.


4. Click** Save **to update the record


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-27-07-5664-AM.png?width=2000&height=835&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-27-07-5664-AM.png)


---


#### C) Deleting a Training or Certification


1. Locate the training or certification you want to remove


2. Click the **bin (delete)** icon


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-28-25-7704-AM.png?width=2000&height=811&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-28-25-7704-AM.png)


3. A confirmation pop-out window will appear


4. Click **Confirm **to delete the record


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-28-36-7248-AM.png?width=514&height=343&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-28-36-7248-AM.png)


---


#### Important Notes


- If the **add, edit, or delete buttons are not visible**, it means you do not have permission to perform these actions.
- In such cases, please contact your **Admin or HR personnel** for assistance.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**Q1: Can my manager or HR update trainings and certifications on my behalf?**


A: Yes. HR Admins and manager with permission can add, edit, or delete training and certifications for employees.


**Q2: Can I upload proof of certification?**


A: No, you cannot upload an attachment here. If you want to upload any supporting documents or certifications, you may refer to [how to upload documents in profile.](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/add-download-delete-documents-in-profile-in-briohr?hsLang=en)


**Q3: What should I do if I don’t see the add/edit/delete options?**


A: Contact your HR Admin, as permissions may be restricted for certain user groups.
