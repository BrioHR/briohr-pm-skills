# How to Upload or Change Your Profile Picture in BrioHR

*Learn how to upload or change your profile picture in BrioHR using a computer web browser. Follow this step-by-step guide to update your profile quickly and easily.*

### Introduction


Your profile picture in BrioHR helps colleagues and managers recognize you easily within the system.


**NOTE:**


Currently, this feature is only available when accessing** BrioHR via a computer web browser.**


If you are new to BrioHR. you may also want to check out:


- [How to Update Your Personal Information in BrioHR](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-edit-your-profile-on-briohr?hsLang=en)
- [BrioHR First-Time Login Guide](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/first-time-login-via-activation-email-and-downloading-the-briohr-app?hsLang=en)

### Step-by-Step Guide: Uploading or Changing Your Profile Picture


#### Step 1 - Log in to Your BrioHR Account


Go to the [BrioHR login page](https://app.briohr.com/signin?redirect=%2Fhome) and enter your registered credentials through a computer web browser


#### Step 2 - Navigate to Your Profile


1. From the **Home page**, select **My Profile**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-06-20-24-2265-AM.png?width=670&height=173&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-06-20-24-2265-AM.png)


#### Step 3 - Upload or Change Profile Picture


1. Hover your mouse over the **Profile Picture section**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-06-27-19-1745-AM.png?width=670&height=223&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-06-27-19-1745-AM.png)


2. Click to **upload or change** your photo.


3. Select the desired photo from your computer.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-06-27-32-6328-AM.png?width=670&height=280&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-06-27-32-6328-AM.png)


#### 4. Click Save to complete the process.


####


#### Step 4 - Confirmation


Once uploaded, a **“Success” message** will appear, confirming that your profile has been updated.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-06-29-19-6039-AM.png?width=670&height=180&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-06-29-19-6039-AM.png)

### Best Practices for Profile Pictures in BrioHR


- Use a **clear, professional photo** for better recognition.
- Ensure the photo has good lighting and a plain background.
- Avoid blurry or casual images, as the profile is visible to managers and colleagues.


Learn more: [Troubleshooting Profile Picture Upload Issues in BrioHR](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/troubleshooting-profile-picture-upload-issues-in-briohr?hsLang=en)

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**Q1: Can I upload a profile picture using the BrioHR mobile app?**


A: No, currently this feature is only available on the computer web browser version of BrioHR.


**Q2: What file formats are supported for profile pictures?**


A: Most common image formats like **.jpg, .png, **and **.jpeg** are supported.


**Q3: Why don't I see the "Upload" option from BrioHR web browser login?**


A: Your HR/Admin may have** disabled the permission **to upload profile picture. Please reach out to your HR/Admin for further assistance.
