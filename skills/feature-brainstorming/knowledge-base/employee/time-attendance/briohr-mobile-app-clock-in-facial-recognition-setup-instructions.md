# BrioHR Mobile App Clock-In: Facial Recognition Setup Instructions

*Learn how to set up facial recognition in the BrioHR mobile app for quick, secure clock-ins. Step-by-step guide for first-time users.*

Facial recognition is a configuration enabled in the Time Attendance policy by the HR Admin. Once enabled, the employees must verify their clock-in and clock-out using their facial images via the mobile app.


---


### First-Time Setup Instructions


Login to BrioHR mobile app, then:


1. Tap the** Clock-in** button.


2. Tap **Begin Setup** (this is a one-time setup process).


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-12-2025-03-44-32-4498-AM.png?width=506&height=447&name=image-png-Aug-12-2025-03-44-32-4498-AM.png)


3. A sample and tips written. Tap** I am ready, let's start.**


- The front camera will activate.
- Hold your phone at eye level and keep your face within the frame.
- Stay still for a few seconds while the system processes your face.


**TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS:**


- **Use a well-lit environment.**
- **Remove hats, sunglasses, or face coverings.**
- **Keep your camera lens clean for a clear image.**


4. Once successful, a green check mark will appear.


- To retake the photo, tap **Retake Photo**.


5. Tap **Proceed to Clock In/Out.**

### How to Clock-in Using Facial Recognition


1. Tap on **Clock-in.**


2. Tap **Verify with Facial Recognition,** and take a new picture


3. You are now successfully Clocked-In.
