# How to Clock In Before Starting Work Using the BrioHR Mobile App

*Learn how to easily clock in before starting work using the BrioHR Mobile App. Follow our step-by-step guide to track your attendance effortlessly.*

### Introduction


Clocking in before starting your workday or shift is simple with the **BrioHR Mobile App**. Your **first clock-in of the day marks your attendance**, and the same steps apply when clocking out at the end of your workday.


Employees can clock in and out directly via the **BrioHR Mobile App** or through the [BrioHR web browser.](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-clock-in-and-out-using-briohr-browser?hsLang=en)


Before using the Attendance Clock-in, ensure you have downloaded the app from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store and logged in with your credentials


Learn More: [How to Download and Log in to BrioHR Mobile App (iOS and Android Guide)](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/download-and-login-to-briohr-mobile-app-ios-android-guide?hsLang=en)


---


### How to Clock In using BrioHR Mobile App


#### Step 1 - Access the Clock-In Section


Login to your BrioHR mobile app


1. Look for the** Time Attendance** box > tap on **Clock-In.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-20-2025-07-24-12-4587-AM.png?width=376&height=801&name=image-png-Aug-20-2025-07-24-12-4587-AM.png)


---


#### Step 2 - Start Clocking In


1. The confirmation screen will display:


- **Clock-In Time**:  your current time.
- **Note (optional)**: add remarks or comments if needed.
- **Current Location**: based on GPS tracking.


  - Learn more: [BrioHR Mobile App: Enable Location Detection Detection for Accurate Clock-Ins.](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/enable-location-detection-for-accurate-clock-ins-briohr-mobile-app?hsLang=en)
- **Facial Recognition Request:** if required by your company.

  - Learn more: [BrioHR Mobile App Clock-In: Facial Recognition Setup Instructions](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/setting-up-facial-recognition-clock-in-clock-out-for-the-first-time?hsLang=en)


2. Tap **Confirm Clock-In.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-20-2025-07-24-52-6550-AM.png?width=373&height=739&name=image-png-Aug-20-2025-07-24-52-6550-AM.png)


A pop-out success message will appear. You are now clocked in for your workday/shift, and the countdown timer will start.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-20-2025-07-27-33-4914-AM.png?width=376&height=815&name=image-png-Aug-20-2025-07-27-33-4914-AM.png)

#### Frequently Asked Question (FAQ)


**Q1: Can I clock-in and clock-out outside the restricted location?****A: **No. You cannot clock in or out outside of the designated location set by your HR Admin. If attempted, you will receive an error message.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-20-2025-07-35-32-2740-AM.png?width=332&height=336&name=image-png-Aug-20-2025-07-35-32-2740-AM.png)


**Q2: Can I manually edit my attendance clock-in and clock-out time?**


**A: **No. Attendance entries cannot be edited manually by employees. Only your Manager or HR Admin can make adjustments if necessary.


**Q3: Do I need to enable GPS tracking and facial recognition every time I clock in?**


**A: **No. These settings generally require one-time permission. After the first setup, your device will automatically recognize your location and facial profile during future clock-ins.


**Q4: What happens if I forget to clock in?**


A: If you forget to clock in, your timesheet will show as **"Not Clocked In". **Your HR Admin or manager may manually update your attendance record to ensure accurate timesheet tracking.
