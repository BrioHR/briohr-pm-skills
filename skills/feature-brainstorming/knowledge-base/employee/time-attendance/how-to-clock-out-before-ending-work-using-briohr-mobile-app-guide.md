# How to Clock Out Before Ending Work Using BrioHR (Mobile App Guide)

*Learn how to perform final clock-out using the BrioHR Mobile App. Follow our step-by-step guide to ensure your timesheet is recorded correctly and working hours are accurate.*

### Introduction


Clocking out at the end of your workday or shift is now easier with the **BrioHR Mobile App**. The final clock-out confirms your attendance, generates your daily timesheet, and calculates your total working hours.


You can perform the final clock-out either through the **BrioHR Mobile App** or the **BrioHR web browser**.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Final Clock-Out in BrioHR Mobile App


#### Step 1 - Access the Clock-Out Section


Login to your BrioHR mobile app


1. Look for the** Time Attendance** box > tap on **Clock-Out.**


**

#### Step 2 - Start Clocking Out


1. The confirmation screen will display:


- **Clock-Out Time**:  your current time.
- **Note (optional)**: add remarks or comments if needed.
- **Current Location**: based on GPS tracking.


  - Learn more: [BrioHR Mobile App: Enable Location Detection Detection for Accurate Clock-Ins.](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/enable-location-detection-for-accurate-clock-ins-briohr-mobile-app?hsLang=en)
- **Facial Recognition Request:** if required by your company.

  - Learn more: [BrioHR Mobile App Clock-In: Facial Recognition Setup Instructions](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/setting-up-facial-recognition-clock-in-clock-out-for-the-first-time?hsLang=en)


2. Tap **Final Clock-Out**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-22-2025-07-42-59-2313-AM.png?width=373&height=743&name=image-png-Aug-22-2025-07-42-59-2313-AM.png)
**


---


#### Step 3 - Confirm Clocking Out


You need to confirm that you are performing a final clock out. This will end your work for today.


1. Tap on **Final Clock Out.**


2. A pop-out success message will appear. You are now clocked out for your workday/shift, and your **timesheet for the day **will be generated automatically.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-22-2025-07-44-03-3820-AM.png?width=688&height=619&name=image-png-Aug-22-2025-07-44-03-3820-AM.png)

#### Why Is Final Clock-Out Important?


Performing a **final clock-out** ensures:


- Accurate recording of your **working hours**
- Proper generation of your **daily timesheet**
- Smooth payroll and attendance management


Failing to clock out may result in missing or inaccurate timesheet records.


---


#### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


**Q1: What happens if I forget to do the final clock-out?**


A: If you forget to clock out, your timesheet for the day won’t be created and your working hours may not be recorded correctly. You also won’t be able to clock in for the next day.


To fix this, you can either complete your clock-out or ask your HR/manager to update your timesheet for you.


**Q2. Can I clock out from both mobile and web?**


A: Yes. you can clock out using either the BrioHR Monile App or the BrioHR Web Browser. Both methods will record your final clock out.


**NOTE:**
If your company’s Time Attendance policy **uses location restrictions** or **facial recognition,** you can only clock out using the **BrioHR Mobile App**. Clock-out will not be available on the web browser.


**Q3. Will I get a notification if I forget to clock out?**
A: Currently, BrioHR does not send reminders. It’s recommended to always clock out at the end of your shift.


**Q4. Can my HR edit my clock-out time if I forget?**
A: Yes, HR or managers can manually adjust your final clock-out time to ensure your timesheet reflects the correct working hours.
