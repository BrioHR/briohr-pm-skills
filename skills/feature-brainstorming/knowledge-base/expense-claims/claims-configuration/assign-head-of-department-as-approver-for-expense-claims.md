# Assign Head of Department as Approver for Expense Claims

*This article explains how to set an employee as a Head of Department and assign them as an approver in the approval workflow for expense claims.*

**Audience & Scope**


Audience: HR Admin


Module: Expense Claims


Country: Global


Pre-requisites: User group settings (Access to Admin Settings and HR Admin Permission for Expense Claim Module)





**Step-by-Step Instructions**


**Part 1: Setting the Head of Department**


1. Go to **Admin Settings > Departments**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-14-02-0463-AM.png?width=670&height=300&name=image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-14-02-0463-AM.png)


2. Click the **edit button** for the department you want to add the HOD of


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-14-26-3025-AM.png?width=670&height=297&name=image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-14-26-3025-AM.png)


3. Choose the name of the HoD from the dropdown


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-15-56-9548-AM.png?width=434&height=407&name=image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-15-56-9548-AM.png)


4. Click **Save**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-27-19-0831-AM.png?width=440&height=248&name=image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-27-19-0831-AM.png)


**Part 2: Assign/Add HoD to Claim Approval Workflow**


1. Go to **HR Lounge > Expense Claims > Claim Policies**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-48-58-3555-AM.png?width=670&height=324&name=image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-48-58-3555-AM.png)


2. Click on the '**Approval workflow list**'.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-49-42-7305-AM.png?width=670&height=256&name=image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-49-42-7305-AM.png)


3. You can create a new workflow or edit an existing workflow


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-50-32-8410-AM.png?width=412&height=487&name=image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-50-32-8410-AM.png)


4. When choosing the approver, choose **Position** for the first dropdown, and **Head of Department** for the second dropdown.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-52-26-1384-AM.png?width=661&height=357&name=image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-52-26-1384-AM.png)


**IMPORTANT:**


The approval step where the Head of Department is selected is skipped if:


- An employee's department has no HoD set (refer to Part 1 above)
- The HoD is the submitter of the claim
- the HoD has ended employment (inactive)


5. If needed, check and update the approval workflow for the specific claim policies.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-55-08-8439-AM.png?width=670&height=191&name=image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-55-08-8439-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-55-50-9831-AM.png?width=670&height=317&name=image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-55-50-9831-AM.png)


**Learn more:** For more details on how to create/edit approval workflow in claim policies, refer to the article [here](/knowledge/how-to-create-and-manage-claim-policies?hsLang=en)


**NOTE: **


Changes in the claim approval workflow will only affect future claim submissions. Claims that are submitted will follow the approval workflow at the time of submission.





**Common Issues/FAQ**


**Q1: Can I select multiple people as HoD?**


A1: No. Only one person can be selected as the HoD for each department.


**Q2: Can the same employee be set as HoD of multiple departments?**


A2: Yes. The same employee can be HoD of multiple departments.


**Q3: If the HoD cannot approve/reject a claim due to being out of the office, who can approve on behalf?**


A3: Admins with access to the expense claims module and permission to perform actions on behalf of others will be able to approve/reject on behalf. Refer to the picture below for user group settings that allow this behaviour.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-22-53-4112-AM.png?width=488&height=396&name=image-png-Jun-10-2026-08-22-53-4112-AM.png)
