# Edit, Delete, or Disable Claim Types in BrioHR Expense Claims Module

*Managing claim types in BrioHR is essential for efficient expense claim handling. HR/Admin users can easily edit, delete, or disable claim types. Follow this step-by-step guide to make changes and ensure streamlined claim processes.*

Login to your BrioHR account.


1. At H**R Lounge**, click on **Expense Claims** menu.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-28-2024-09-13-10-4643-AM.png?width=688&height=282&name=image-png-Aug-28-2024-09-13-10-4643-AM.png)





2. Click on **Claim Types tab.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-02-52-2818-AM.png?width=688&height=321&name=image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-02-52-2818-AM.png)


**To edit Claim Types**


3. Click on **pencil icon** to edit respective claim type.


4. You can edit **Claim Title** to rename the name, or you can update any fields that are needed.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-06-25-2852-AM.png?width=688&height=702&name=image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-06-25-2852-AM.png)


5. Click on **Save button** to save the setting.





**To delete Claim Types**


6.  Click on the **trash bin icon** to delete the respective claim type.


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-07-21-7590-AM.png?width=688&height=321&name=image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-07-21-7590-AM.png)
**


7.  Click on **Delete button** to confirm delete the respective claim type, or click on **Cancel button** to cancel the deletion.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-10-15-2173-AM.png?width=440&height=376&name=image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-10-15-2173-AM.png)


8. System will prompt a warning message as below image, if claim type has been used in claim reports. Hence, you are not able to delete the claim type.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-11-40-1715-AM.png?width=414&height=151&name=image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-11-40-1715-AM.png)


However, you can disable the claim types if the claim types no longer available to be claim by staff.





**To disable Claim Types**


9. Click on toggle button to disable claim type.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-13-41-6892-AM.png?width=688&height=321&name=image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-13-41-6892-AM.png)


10. Toggle button will be off and claim type won't be available in the selection drop-down of claim type in claim report.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-16-15-4990-AM.png?width=688&height=283&name=image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-16-15-4990-AM.png)
