# How to Approve or Reject Claims

*This article explains how to approve or reject an employee's expense claim report from the HR Lounge.*

Log in to Your BrioHR Account


1.   Go to the **HR Lounge**


2.   Navigate to **Expense Claims**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48330478700/original/CmLjXJuAESKEVFkIMReID3awgsmyg-dOCQ.png?1710465494)


3.   Access **Claim Requests**


4.   Select an Employee’s Claim Report. Click either "**Approve**" or "**Reject**."


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48151519945/original/q1T2k-5yXGoNxVzIAIphBIoh76dY87-MWw.png?1635141779)


5.   Confirm Your Action


- **Approve**: A confirmation message will pop up. Click "**Approve**" again to finalize.
- **Reject**: A confirmation message will pop up. Enter the reason for rejection in the provided text box. Click "**Reject**" to finalize.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48143073296/original/VoXo6kEpVwat8Kh4tBjhcPS79WPl7Y0Jpg.png?1632365948)


6.   Update Claim Status


The status of the employee’s claim report will change as follows:


- From Submitted to **Approved**
- From Submitted to **Rejected**
- Reports with the status **Submitted **are pending approval or rejection.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48151520070/original/dIkBjZ_PntGEwdISwnE_iiHL7O3VeoVZbA.png?1635141858)
