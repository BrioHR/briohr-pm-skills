# How to Create Expense Claims on Behalf of Other Employees

*This article explains how to create an expense claim on behalf of another employee when HR or Admin has granted you the permission.*

You can create expense claims on behalf of other employees only if your HR or Admin has granted you the necessary permissions.


Steps to Create an Expense Claim on Behalf of Another Employee:


Log in to your BrioHR account.


1.   Go to **HR Lounge.**


2.   Navigate to **Expense Claims**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48330479621/original/Q9qy8zG1Z2XtFl2BNkzNyQszmGkNpLOP7g.png?1710466877)


3.   Select **Claim Request.**


4.   Click **+New Claim.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48151522964/original/8tKYLWrTfsqWcjIERj4AgX5Gy_p1tyfP9Q.png?1635142969)


5.   The **Employee **field will appear as an additional option. Choose the employee for whom you want to create an expense claim from the drop-down menu.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48330479700/original/Cdksk5SPJxL-dEPwRiqMwXKWKdl5QUBymg.png?1710467035)


Fields marked with a **red asterisk (*)** are mandatory.


6.   Click the appropriate button:


- **Cancel**: To cancel the claim.
- **Save**: To save the new claim and close the form.
- **Save & Add Another**: To save the current claim and immediately create a new one.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48330479801/original/ki7GyDU3ifIKStDhQ4JegDPVNKFt_5eKXA.png?1710467194)


7.   You have successfully created a new claim report on behalf of another employee.


- A status of **Open **indicates that the report is not yet submitted.

  - Both you and the employee can add more claims to the same report.
- Click **Submit **once the claim is finalized.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48151523167/original/r1tq2Af3v543w0bk6iCY9Kt8PCvuCQNmRQ.png?1635143043)
