# How to Delete a Claim Policy in BrioHR: Step-by-Step Guide

*Deleting a claim policy in BrioHR is a straightforward process. However, it’s essential to note that you can only delete a policy that has not been assigned to any employees. Follow the step-by-step instructions below to easily delete an unused claim*

Login to your BrioHR account.


1. At H**R Lounge**, click on **Expense Claims** menu.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-28-2024-09-13-10-4643-AM.png?width=688&height=282&name=image-png-Aug-28-2024-09-13-10-4643-AM.png)





2. Click on **Claim Policies tab.**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-33-38-4663-AM.png?width=688&height=304&name=image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-33-38-4663-AM.png)
**


3. Click on **trash bin icon** to delete the respective claim policy.


4.  Click on **Delete button** to confirm delete the respective claim policy, or click on **Cancel button** to cancel the deletion.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-36-12-4457-AM.png?width=439&height=402&name=image-png-Sep-06-2024-07-36-12-4457-AM.png)


**Note: Only claim policy that has not been assigned to anyone can be delete.**
