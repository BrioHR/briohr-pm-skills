# How to Mark Employee Claims as Reimbursed

*This article explains how HR or Finance can change an approved claim's status to Reimbursed after paying the employee.*

After reimbursing an employee's claim, the HR or Finance personnel responsible must change the claim's status from '**Approved**' to '**Reimbursed**.'


**NOTE:**


**Once a claim has been marked as 'Reimbursed,' this action is final, and no changes or reopening of the claim are allowed.**


### Steps to Mark a Claim as Reimbursed:


Log in to your BrioHR account.


- Go to **HR Lounge.**
- Navigate to **Expense Claims**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48330480665/original/MPGLzWaxHEPkCfYia2wntlBfr4CS72o7sw.png?1710468297)


You will be directed to the Expense Claims dashboard.


- Go to the **Claim Request** page.
- Select a claim report that you want to mark as ‘Reimbursed’ by ticking the checkbox.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48153490063/original/3jmcVzSb5jjbZtsW66U9tm1RlrkHtPcCZQ.png?1635738156)


After selecting a claim, a bottom menu will appear with options.


- Choose the ‘**Mark as Reimbursed**’ option. The other available options are ‘Re-open’ and ‘Cancel Claim.’


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48153490492/original/EfXbYB3tb-pShehKXV9FVps-0e0TitZpuQ.png?1635738518)


- A **confirmation **message will pop up.
- Click ‘**Mark as Reimbursed**’ to finalize the process.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48153490500/original/BXqa8iAjz86_VLKFaPI9kNo9TFhHzsLghA.png?1635738529)


Once marked as '**Reimbursed**,' the claim will no longer appear on the Claim Request page.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48153490502/original/MgxROpCn_VGQT554f0J1ClkRkMq205ICXQ.png?1635738535)


The employee's My Claims page will update, showing the claim's status as '**Reimbursed**.'


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48153490520/original/OB8ryEZFo25S6BEVt3IvCffhhanYLMsinA.png?1635738551)
