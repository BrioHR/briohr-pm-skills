# How to Utilize Available Claim Limits

*This article explains how claim limits determine an employee's available balance and how that balance decreases as claims are submitted, approved, or reimbursed.*

When a specific budget is allocated for certain types of claims, a **limit **is often set accordingly. This limit determines the **available balance**, which is reduced not only when claims are **submitted, approved**, or **reimbursed **but also when claims are still **open**.


#### For example:


A yearly limit of RM 400 has been established for the "Furniture and Fittings" claim type. An employee has submitted a claim for RM 100 and has an additional open claim for RM 100. Consequently, these two claims will utilize RM 200 of the limit, leaving a remaining balance of RM 200 (calculated as RM 400 minus RM 200) available for future claims.
