# How to View Cancelled and Reimbursed Claims in the Expense Claims Module

*This article explains how to display cancelled and reimbursed claims on the Claim Request page, which only shows submitted and approved claims by default.*

By default, only submitted and approved claims are displayed on the **Claim Request** page. To view cancelled and reimbursed claims, follow these steps:


1.   In the **HR Lounge,** click on **Expense Claims**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48330480706/original/842BRbPawyBrw5tPTJoJwDTOSefyBlCqKA.png?1710468354)


2.   On the **Claim Request** page, check the box labeled **Show Cancelled and Reimbursed Claims**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48330480737/original/4Mfpkp6gXu0-LFRnIBCKdkHNjC5AcSMZuQ.png?1710468420)


3.   You can also filter claim requests by office by selecting the appropriate option from the **Select Office** dropdown.
