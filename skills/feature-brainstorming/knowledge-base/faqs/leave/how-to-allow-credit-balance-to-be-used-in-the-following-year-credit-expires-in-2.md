# How to Allow Credit Balance to Be Used in the Following Year (Credit Expires in 2025)

*Learn how to enable carry forward settings for credit leave balances in 2025. Avoid forfeiting unused leave by following this step-by-step guide*

An employee has one day of credit balance expiring on **16 January 2025**, but they are unable to use this balance to apply for leave in 2025. The balance does not appear when they try to apply for leave next year.


### Steps to Enable Carry Forward for Next Year's Credit Balance


By enabling the **Carry Forward** setting in the credit leave policy, employees will be able to use the credit balance expiring next year in the following year.

### Troubleshooting: Why Credit Leave Balances Aren’t Carried Over


#### Why Does This Happen?


For any credit balance with an expiry date that extends into the following year, you must enable the **Carry Forward** setting in the credit leave policy.


#### What Happens If I Don’t Enable This Setting?


If you do not enable **Carry Forward 2025** for credit leave, any balance expiring in the following year will not be carried over and will be forfeited on **1 January 2025**.


To avoid this, it is best to enable the **Carry Forward** permission **BEFORE 31 December 2024**, as the system will carry over any unused leave balances to 2025 at midnight on **31 December 2024**.


#### What If I Enable the Carry Forward Setting AFTER 1 January 2025?


If you enable the **Carry Forward** setting after **1 January 2025**, you will need to recompute the balance manually. For detailed instructions, please refer to [this guide](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-enable-and-configure-carry-forward-of-2024-leave-balance-in-2025?hsLang=en).
