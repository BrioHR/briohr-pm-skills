# How Birthday Announcements Work on BrioHR Feed

*Learn how BrioHR automatically posts employee birthday announcements on the company feed to celebrate special days and boost workplace culture.*

### Introduction


Celebrating employees’ birthdays is made simple with the **BrioHR Feed’s built-in birthday announcement feature**. This automated tool enhances your workplace culture by recognizing team members on their special day, helping them feel appreciated and valued.


With birthday posts automatically displayed on the company feed, everyone can join in celebrating the milestones together without any manual setup or reminders.


---


### How the Birthday Announcement Feature Works


#### 1. Automatic Post Timing


BrioHR automatically publishes the birthday post at 09:00 AM on the employee's birthday. This post appears on the company feed for everyone to see and join in the celebration.


#### 2. Post Visibility Duration


Each birthday announcement remains visible on the feed for **15 days** from the posting date. After that, the post is automatically removed to keep your feed current and organized.


#### 3. Fixed Image Design


The birthday post includes a **pre-set image** designed by BrioHR. At present, this image **cannot be customized**. This ensures all birthday posts maintain a consistent look across your company feed.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**1. When do birthday announcements appear on BrioHR Feed?**
Birthday posts automatically appear on the feed at **8:00 AM** on the employee’s birthday.


**2. How long do birthday posts remain visible?**
They stay visible for **15 days** before automatically disappearing.


**3. Can we change the birthday post image?**
No, the birthday post image is currently **fixed and non-editable**.


**4. Who can see the birthday post?**
All employees with access to the **company Feed** can view the birthday announcement.


**5. Can we disable automatic birthday posts?**
Yes, you can disable them by following the steps in this related article:
[How to Display Employees' Birthdays and Welcome Newcomers on Your Feed](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-display-employees-birthdays-and-welcome-newcomers-on-your-feed?hsLang=en)
