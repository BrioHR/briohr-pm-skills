# How to Create, Edit and Manage Posts in BrioHR Feed

*Learn how to create, edit, delete, and pin posts in BrioHR Feed. Discover how to set post visibility, add media, and notify employees easily.*

### Introduction


The **BrioHR Feed** allows you to create and share updates, announcements, or event-related posts for employees to view. You can choose who can see each post - specific employees, departments, or offices.


**NOTE:**

Only users with access permissions can create, edit, or delete posts. If you do not see the post options in your Feed, please contact your **Account Administrator **to grant access permission.


---


### Benefits of Using BrioHR Feed Posts


The post feature in BrioHR Feed is highly flexible and helps improve internal communication. You can:


- Restrict post visibility to specific audiences.
- Add images, video links, or attachments.
- Mark the post as an event (displayed in the **Event Calendar**).
- Pin posts to the top of the Feed for better visibility.
- Send email notifications to post recipients.


**NOTE:**

Post creators will not receive the email notification themselves.





---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Create a New Post in BrioHR Feed


#### Step 1 - Log In to BrioHR Account


Log in to your BrioHR account using your registered credentials.


#### Step 2 - Access the Feed


1. Navigate to **Feed.**


2. Click on the section** "What would you like to share?"**


3. A pop-up window will appear where you can create a new post.


#### Step 3 - Draft Your Post


1. Choose your audience under **Share With.**


2. Add a **Title** for your post.


3. Add your** Post Description.**


4. (*Optional*) Upload media or attachments. You can upload photos and file attachments, but only link videos (video uploads are not supported).


**NOTE:**
Supported file types for attachments are:


`.csv, .xls, .xlsx, .txt, .pdf, .docx, .jpg, .jpeg, .png`


5. Tick **This is an Event** if your post relates to an event.


6. Select **Pin this Post** if you want the post to stay at the top of the Feed.


7. Tick **Notify All Recipients by Email** to send an email notification to your chosen audience.


**NOTE:  **The post creator will not receive this notification.


8. Click **Post **to publish your update.


Your new post will now appear on the Feed for your selected audience.

### Step-by-Step Guide: Edit an Existing Post


1. Go to your **BrioHR Feed** and locate the post you want to edit.


2. Click on the **three-dot icon (⋯)**.


3. Select **Edit**


4. Make your changes, then click **Post** to save the updates.


### Step-by-Step Guide: Delete an Existing Post


1. Locate the post you wish to delete in your **BrioHR Feed**.


2. Click on the **three-dot icon (⋯)**.


3. Select **Delete.**


4. A confirmation window will appear- click **Yes** to confirm or **No** to cancel.


### Step-by-Step Guide: Pin an Existing Post


1. Go to the post you want to pin in your **BrioHR Feed**.


2. Click on the **three-dot icon (⋯)**.


3. Select **Pin This Post**.


The pinned post will now appear under the **Pinned Posts** section on the left side of your Feed.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**1. Who can create posts in BrioHR Feed?**
Only users with the necessary permissions can create posts. Contact your **HR Admin** if you do not see the posting option.


**2. Can I upload videos directly to the Feed?**
No, you can only share video links (for example, from YouTube or Vimeo).


**3. How do I make sure my post appears at the top?**
Tick the **“Pin this Post”** checkbox when creating or editing the post.


**4. Will I receive an email when I post an update?**
No. The email notification goes only to your selected audience, not to the post creator.


**5. Can I unpin a post later?**
Yes, go to the pinned post, click the **three-dot icon**, and select **Unpin**.


**6. Can employees acknowledge a post?**


There is currently no read-acknowledgement or read-tracking feature; feedback on it is passed to the product team.
