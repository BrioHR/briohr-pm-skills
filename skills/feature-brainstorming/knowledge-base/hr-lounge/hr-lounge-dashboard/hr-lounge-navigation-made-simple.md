# HR Lounge Navigation Made Simple

*This article explains how to navigate the HR Lounge dashboard and access each module from the left panel.*

### A. Accessing The Modules


To access the following modules, click on the respective icons on the left panel of your **HR Lounge** dashboard. You will then be navigated to the individual modules where you can review information and perform further actions.


1. **Employee Management**
2. **Leave Module****
**
3. **Expense Claims Module**
4. **Payroll Module**
5. **Performance Module**
6. **Pulse Module**
7. **Onboarding Module**
8. **Recruitment Module**
9. **Time Attendance Module**
10. **Training Module**
11. **Timesheets Module**
12. **Document Management**


**NOTE:**


**Access to each of these modules in the HR Lounge depends on the user's permission settings. If you are unable to view/access a specific module, the account Admin should check the user's permission settings by going to: Settings > User Groups > Edit > HR Modules Permission > Enable/Disable your preferred settings > Save.**

### B. Adding A New Employee in Employee Management


In **HR Lounge > Employee Management**, click on the **+Add Employee** icon and fill in the employee information in the **Add New Joiner** pop-up window. Click **Submit** to complete the action and add the new employee.

### E. Employee List (in Employee Management)


**i. Search for employees by selecting filters such as:**


**a. Name/ID of employees**
**b. Departments**
**c. Offices**
**d. Joined date**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-15-2026-06-08-31-7993-AM.png?width=670&height=223&name=image-png-Jul-15-2026-06-08-31-7993-AM.png)


**ii. Search for inactive employees by ticking the "Include inactive employees" checkbox.**


**NOTE:**


**By default, only active employees will be displayed in the Employee List.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-15-2026-06-09-17-8040-AM.png?width=1723&height=421&name=image-png-Jul-15-2026-06-09-17-8040-AM.png)


**iii. Determine the number of employees to be shown in the Employee List by clicking on the "Rows per page" dropdown menu. You can select up to 50 rows per page.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-15-2026-06-10-53-1839-AM.png?width=1752&height=477&name=image-png-Jul-15-2026-06-10-53-1839-AM.png)


**iv. Copy an employee's email address by hovering your mouse over the email address ("EMAIL" column), then click to copy the information.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48229057370/original/HeXFyDeuuZS3wN5xcw7tyMjfcOCcXe2M8w.png?1653878356)


**v. Hover your mouse over the "..." (More actions) icon for quick actions such as:**


**a. View the employee's Profile**
**b. Edit the employee's Profile**
**c. Start Onboarding for the employee**
**d. Select the employee's End Employment Date**
**e. View the employee's Leave dashboard**
**f. View the employee's latest Timesheet**
**g. View the employee's claim submissions**
**h. View the employee's latest Performance Cycle**


**NOTE:**


**Access to these quick actions will depend on each employee's user permission settings for each module. For example, if "Timesheets" is not enabled for an employee, the "Timesheet" option will be greyed out in the dropdown menu.**
