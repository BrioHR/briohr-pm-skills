# How to Use the Data Templates for Your BrioHR Implementation

*Learn how to complete BrioHR’s data templates for smooth implementation. Step-by-step instructions and tips to ensure accurate employee and company data migration.*

During your **BrioHR implementation**, our team will help migrate your employee and company data into the system. To streamline this process, we provide a set of **standard data templates**. These templates ensure your information is structured correctly and ready for smooth migration.


To make the process easier, you’ll find both **written instructions** and **short video walkthroughs** for each template. You can follow along step by step while filling in the required details.


The five templates you’ll be working with are:


1. **Employee List Template**
2. **Employee Payroll Data**
3. **Company Payroll Data + Payroll History Report**
4. **Expense Claims**
5. **Leave Template**


#### 1. Employee List Template


This template captures your employees’ core information (e.g., name, email, department, job title, hire date). It forms the backbone of your HR system, so accuracy here is essential.

#### Tips for Filling Out Your Templates


- Always use the **provided formats** (e.g., date format YYYY-MM-DD).
- Do **not change the column headers**, as they are mapped directly to the system.
- Double-check for **typos, missing values, or inconsistent employee info**.
- If unsure about certain fields, leave them blank and your implementation manager will clarify during the review.


#### What’s Next?


Once you’ve completed the templates:


1. Upload them to your **shared implementation folder.**
2. Our team will **review your data** and **reach out** if any clarification is required.
3. After verification, your data will be migrated into BrioHR.


For additional support, please contact your **dedicated implementation manager.**
