# How to view the leave type/policies that assign to the specific employee

*This article provides a step-by-step guide on how employees can view assigned leave types, policies, entitlements, leave balances, and approver details using the BrioHR platform. Follow the instructions to easily access your Leave Policies and Leave*

Login to your BrioHR account:


1. At Home, go to **My Profile.**


**

**


2. Navigate to **Leave Policies** section.
