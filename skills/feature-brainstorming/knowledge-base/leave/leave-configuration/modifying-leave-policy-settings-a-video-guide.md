# Modifying Leave Policy Settings: A Video Guide

*This article explains how to modify leave policy settings, including entitlement days, policy availability, and leave approvers.*

As an HR Admin with access to modify leave policy settings in your account, you can make relevant changes from your end.


Some common modifications made to the leave policy settings include:


1. Editing the entitlement days for a specific leave policy
2. Editing policy availability
3. Editing the leave approver (For details, please refer to [this article](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-set-or-edit-the-general-leave-approver-in-briohr?hsLang=en))
4. Editing the proration mode
5. Editing the carry forward setup and enabling/disabling it
6. Custom configuration: customizing the leave policy setting for specific individuals assigned to that specific leave policy
7. And more


This tutorial will show you how to modify the leave policy settings by changing the entitlement days for Maternity Leave. Even though the tutorial shows this for Maternity Leave, the concept works the same for other leave types as well.


To get started with modifying your leave policy settings:


1. Go to **HR Lounge**
2. Choose the **Leave **module
3. Go to **Leave Policies**
4. Find the leave type you want to modify (e.g., Maternity)
5. Click on "**View leave details**"


If you are looking for information on how to do custom configuration for a specific employee within a leave policy, please refer to this article:


[How to Set Up Custom Leave Configuration](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-set-up-custom-leave-configuration-in-hr-lounge?hsLang=en)
