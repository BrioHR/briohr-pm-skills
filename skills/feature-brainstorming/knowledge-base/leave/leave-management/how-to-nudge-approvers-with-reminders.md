# How to Nudge Approvers with Reminders

*This article explains how to use the Nudge Approvers function to remind managers about pending leave approvals.*

Notice a long list of pending leave approvals?


Use our "**Nudge Approvers**" function to remind leave approvers to manage employees' pending leave applications efficiently.


The process begins with the HR Admin using the HR Lounge. Managers then receive a task in their Pending Actions List, redirecting them to the Team Leave Dashboard to take action.


---


Follow the steps below to learn how:


**1.   Access the Nudge Function:**


- Go to **HR Lounge > Leave > Dashboard** and click on the **Nudge Approvers** button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48337203151/original/hdT_04mBP9pf4CcE-DlHkUEHSno750QnjQ.png?1715922957)


**2.   Select Managers:**


- In the **Nudge Approvers** pop-up window, select the relevant **managers **from the dropdown list and click on the **Send **button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48337203214/original/D1LKOqHkKjwCDFtcoGPuSi9tDH5sKpGHdA.png?1715923029)


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48337203224/original/svKvkMrBR5V9gtN3U3SnPYZbvWasttL5mA.png?1715923034)


**3.   Notification to Managers:**


- Managers will receive both an **email **and a **task **notification in their **Pending Actions List**.


Sample **Email **Received by Managers:


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48262188760/original/H58YOElY0NfYu-HNvDbVCV6K2xpiZSdJ_w.png?1667981850)


Sample **Task **Notification in Manager's **Pending Actions List**:


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48262188760/original/H58YOElY0NfYu-HNvDbVCV6K2xpiZSdJ_w.png?1667981850)


**NOTE:**


**Clicking on the View Detail button will redirect the manager to the Team Leave Dashboard.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48262173385/original/tTfT6ZP4FoMdZRihLws7FYxHSh7HGTvXRw.png?1667977346)


**4.   Action on Pending Requests:**


- At the **Team Leave Dashboard**, a list of pending **leave and credit requests** will be displayed. Managers can then take appropriate action on the pending leave requests.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48262173997/original/t7wEVPC1XUsz6RuHkNoJKgEp4qWvXSGg-w.jpg?1667977559)
