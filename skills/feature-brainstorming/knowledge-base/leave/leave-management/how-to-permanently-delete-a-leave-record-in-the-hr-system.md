# How to Permanently Delete a Leave Record in the HR System

*This article explains how HR can permanently delete approved, pending, or cancelled leave records from the system.*

This feature allows an **HR User** to delete records of Approved, Pending, and Cancelled (Rejected) leave applications.


**NOTE:**


**Deleting a leave application will permanently remove the leave record from the system.**


#### Steps to Delete a Leave Record


**Access Leave Details:**


- Click on the "**See details**" icon for the leave application you want to delete.
Initiate Deletion:


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48174781735/original/tVg-D3bZFsBeYBhGewitRmfHpmsV1OFRIg.png?1640926900)


- In the "**Leave Details**" pop-up window, click on the "**Delete leave**" icon.
Confirm Deletion:


![RjCsbKl-kg7Wg0gl_qUXUHuYYgY-uFLNMg.png?1640927128](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/RjCsbKl-kg7Wg0gl_qUXUHuYYgY-uFLNMg.png%3F1640927128.png?width=475&height=610&name=RjCsbKl-kg7Wg0gl_qUXUHuYYgY-uFLNMg.png%3F1640927128.png)


- In the pop-up notification window, click "**Yes**" to confirm and proceed with deleting the leave record.


![Xg1Hgr7Ay2XPCRAZRqdS8QblHwoZzVzSsA.png?1640927399](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/Xg1Hgr7Ay2XPCRAZRqdS8QblHwoZzVzSsA.png%3F1640927399.png?width=333&height=330&name=Xg1Hgr7Ay2XPCRAZRqdS8QblHwoZzVzSsA.png%3F1640927399.png)


---


**NOTE:**


**The differences between Modify Leave, Cancel Leave, and Delete Leave are as follows:**


**Modify Leave:**


Make changes to the leave application. The leave record will remain in the system.


**Cancel Leave:**


Reject a leave application. The leave record will remain in the system, with the status displayed as "Cancelled". [Click [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-cancel-an-approved-past-date-leave-application?hsLang=en)for how to cancel leave]


**Delete Leave:**


Permanently remove a leave record from the system. This action is irreversible.
