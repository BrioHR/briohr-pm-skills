# How to Recompute Initial Balance and Carry Forward in BrioHR Leave

*Learn how to manually recompute employee leave initial balances and carry forward amounts in BrioHR after updating leave policy rules. Step-by-step guide for HR admins.*

### Introduction


Manual recomputation allows HR administrators to update employees’ **initial leave balances** and **carry forward amounts**, especially after changes are made to the previous year’s leave policy settings.


When you recompute leave balances, the system recalculates the **initial balance and carry forward** based on the **latest rules defined in the leave policy**. This feature is particularly useful when you want the current year’s carry forward to reflect newly updated rules from the previous year.


**Learn More:**

[How to Enable and Configure Carry Forward of Previous Year Leave Balance in the New Year](/knowledge/how-to-enable-and-configure-carry-forward-of-previous-year-leave-balance-in-new-year?hsLang=en)


---


### When Should You Recompute Leave Balances?


You should recompute leave balances when:


- Leave policy rules for the previous year have been modified
- Carry-forward rules are updated after the year has started
- Initial balances need to align with the latest policy configuration


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: How to Recompute Initial Balance and Carry Forward


#### Step 1 - Log In to Your BrioHR Account


Login to BrioHR account using your registered credentials.


#### Step 2 - Open the Leave Policy Page


1. Go to **HR Lounge** on the navigation bar and click** Leave.**


2. Select **Leave Types & Policies**.


2. Choose the leave type you want to recompute (*e.g., Annual leave*).


3. Click **View Leave Details.**


4. You will be directed to the selected leave type page.


![open leave policieis](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/open%20leave%20policieis.gif?width=670&height=306&name=open%20leave%20policieis.gif)


#### Step 3 - Access the Initial Balances Section


1.  Navigate to** Tools **>** Initial Balances.**


2. A pop-up window will appear showing:


- Employee names.
- The initial balance amounts.
- Carry forward amounts.
- Edit (pencil icon)
- Recompute (refresh icon)

### Recompute Leave Balances Options


#### a) Recompute for Individual Employees


1. Search for the employee's name.


2. Click on the **Recompute** (refresh) button.


3. The system will recalculate both the** initial balance** and** carry forward **based on the latest leave policy rules.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-31-2025-03-02-40-7696-AM.png?width=2000&height=897&name=image-png-Dec-31-2025-03-02-40-7696-AM.png)





#### b) Recompute Leave Balances in Bulk


Currently, **bulk recomputation** is not available for HR Admin users.


**NOTE:**


To recompute leave balances for all employees at once, please contact the **BrioHR Support Team **via Live Chat for assistance.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**1. What does recomputing leave balances do?
**Recomputing recalculates an employee's initial balance and carry forward based on the latest leave policy rules.**
**


**2. Will recomputation affect leave already taken?
**Recomputation updates balances based on policy rules but does not remove approved leave records.


**3. Can I manually edit leave balances instead of recomputing?**
Yes. You can manually adjust leave balances if needed.


Refer to [How to Adjust Leave Balance or Carry Forward in HR Lounge.](/knowledge/how-to-adjust-leave-balance-or-carry-forward-in-hr-lounge?hsLang=en)


**4. Can HR Admins recompute leave balances in bulk?**
No, bulk recomputation is not currently supported in the system. Please contact BrioHR Support for assistance.


**5. When should I recompute leave balances?**
You should recompute balances after updating carry forward rules or modifying last year’s leave policy settings.
