# How to View Employee Leave Details and Timelines

*This article explains how HR Admins and Managers can view employee leave details and timelines from the Leave dashboard.*

As an HR Admin or Manager, you can view your employees' or team members' leave details and timelines by following these steps:


- In the **HR Lounge** (for HR Admins) or **Team **section (for Managers), click on **Leave**.

- In the **Leave Dashboard under Calendar** Overview, search for the name of the employee or team member and click on their leave application to view their leave details and timeline.

- A "**Leave Details**" pop-up window will appear on the right side of the dashboard, displaying the details of the employee's or team member's leave application.


In the "**Timeline**" section, you can view detailed leave history, including:


a. Leave creation and approval/rejection date and time information


b. Name of the person who approved/rejected the leave application

**NOTE FOR HR ADMINS:**


**Alternatively, HR Admins with the appropriate user permissions can view leave details from an employee's profile by following these steps:**


1.   In the employee's **profile**, click on the **Leave Policies** tab.


**If you cannot view this tab, verify your access with the Admin user at Settings > User Groups (Edit) > Profile Sensitive Data > Leave Policies.**


2.   Click on **See leave balances**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48238779278/original/EuV5UmFB1iQCKemI5ZeAOZKvhahTIKr5fA.png?1657859600)


3.   In the employee's **Leave Requests Status & History** table, click on any leave application ("**See details**") to view the detailed timeline.
