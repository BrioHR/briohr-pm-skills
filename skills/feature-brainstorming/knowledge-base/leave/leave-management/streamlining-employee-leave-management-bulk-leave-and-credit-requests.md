# Streamlining Employee Leave Management: Bulk Leave and Credit Requests

*This article explains how HR/Admin can apply leave and request credit leave in bulk for multiple employees.*

Employee Leave Management allows HR/Admin to apply leave and request credit leave in bulk for multiple employees on their behalf.


On this page, you will learn how to:


A. Apply leave in bulk from the '**Leave Overview**' section


B. Request credit leave in bulk from the '**Credit Leave Overview**' section


---


#### Where to Access Employee Leave Management


1.   Go to the **HR Lounge** and click on **Leave**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48238829399/original/3K13b_Mggpc9Nt7hXJE1HgXuiNXV3yTFrw.png?1657876676)


2.   Click on **Leave Management** to view:


- **Leave Overview**
- **Credit Leave Overview**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48238833035/original/V1PIKfZBtgwIHDrQW9M9p8xVSh_4wTwmrg.png?1657877453)


3.   To apply for leave or request credit on behalf of employees, follow these steps:


**NOTE:**


**Bulk actions can only be performed for the same leave or credit type for all selected employees.**


---


#### A. Applying Leave In Bulk (Leave Overview)


1.   Click on the **+Add Leave** button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48238834376/original/wFlvpQgxFdayfllxSxpmCNj4jtOKlrJCvQ.png?1657877750)


2.   Fill in the necessary information in the "**Apply Leave**" pop-up window.


**Mandatory Fields:**


- Y**ear (MUST BE THE SAME FOR ALL EMPLOYEES)**
- **Leave Type (MUST BE THE SAME FOR ALL EMPLOYEES)**
- **Employees**
- **Date (MUST BE THE SAME FOR ALL EMPLOYEES)**


Check the **Skip Validation** and **Skip Approval** boxes, then click **Next/Create** to complete the bulk leave creation.


a. **Skip Validation:** This option forces leave creation, bypassing the policy rules.


b. **Skip Approval:** This option processes the request as approved, bypassing the approval workflow.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48238840451/original/2q73hE9Tgjf7a0H3BDKRlDGO74MDj8hrrw.png?1657879090)


- **Depending on your leave policy configuration, you may need to fill in additional information after clicking the Next button.**
- **Once you have input the additional information, click Create to finalize the bulk leave creation.**
- **To make changes and return to the previous window, click Previous.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48238840885/original/zO-bEZkWrw7cdfncb1mYCBQvlwFIjajzwQ.png?1657879189)

#### B. Requesting Credit Leave In Bulk (Credit Leave Overview)


1.   Click on the **+Add Credit **button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48238841667/original/vrhsLUb0maO8Z22KMR7aGoJ6gqkBqlIvkw.png?1657879377)


2.   Fill in the necessary information in the "**Create Multiple Credits**" pop-up window.


**Mandatory Fields:**


- **Credit Type (MUST BE THE SAME FOR ALL EMPLOYEES)**
- **Employees**
- **Credit Amount (**MUST BE THE SAME FOR ALL EMPLOYEES**)**
- **Reference Dates (**MUST BE THE SAME FOR ALL EMPLOYEES**)**


Check the **Skip Validation** and **Skip Approval** boxes, then click **Create **to complete the bulk credit request creation.


a. **Skip Validation:** This option forces leave creation, bypassing the policy rules.


b. **Skip Approval:** This option processes the request as approved, bypassing the approval workflow.
