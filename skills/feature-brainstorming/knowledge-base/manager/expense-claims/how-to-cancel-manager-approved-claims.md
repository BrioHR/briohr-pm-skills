# How to Cancel Manager-Approved Claims

*This article explains why managers cannot cancel approved claims and how to request cancellation through HR or Account Admins.*

**NOTE:**


**Managers do NOT have the authority to cancel claims once they have been approved. Only HR or Account Admins have the necessary permissions to do so.**


If a manager needs to cancel an approved claim, they must contact HR or the Account Admin for assistance.


For HR/Admins: Please refer to the following guide for instructions on canceling approved claims: [How to Cancel Approved Claims - HR/Admin Guide](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-cancel-approved-claims-in-briohr?hsLang=en).
