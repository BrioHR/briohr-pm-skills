# How to Generate a Leave Utilization Report for Managers from the Team Dashboard

*This article explains how managers can generate a leave utilization report from the Team dashboard.*

Effectively managing employee leave is crucial for organizations to maintain productivity and ensure smooth operations. To assist managers in making informed decisions and closely monitoring their team's leave patterns, the Leave Utilization Report is now available and easily accessible from the Team dashboard.


How to Generate the Leave Utilization Report


Follow these steps to generate the Leave Utilization Report:


Navigate to the Report


**Go to Team > Team Leave > Leave Management > Leave Overview > Export Report.**


**NOTE:**


**This report is only available for standard leave types, excluding credit-based leave types.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48304105364/original/I5iI1gNhgIL5ChYhKpUawcwrgPc4ofnJ8w.png?1689064430)


#### Option 1: Download the Leave Utilization Report for the Entire Year


**Select the desired year from the dropdown menu. Click Download to generate the report.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48304105506/original/jy3n4rwDEnU6-sSlPOX8xsd1IjeKbkfQpQ.png?1689064517)


#### Option 2: Download the Leave Utilization Report for a Specific Month


**Select the desired year from the dropdown menu. Choose the specific month. Click Download to generate the report.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48304105589/original/3aN-AUwg6gmsrQFAn08zlWSSHHyUWnpepg.png?1689064555)


Accessing the Leave Balance Report


In addition to the Leave Utilization Report, managers can access the **Leave Balance Report** in the **Report **tab:


Navigate to the **Leave Balance Report**


**Go to Team > Team Leave > Report > Export Report (available in CSV/Excel format).**


This report can be customized by selecting various leave types and adjusting the fields and filters to display the necessary information.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48304389858/original/P0im3NRmavbLQUC6ZvatdYIp8HnEcWGObg.png?1689241192)


Understanding the Reports


**Leave Utilization Report**: Tracks the approved leave applications of employees.


**Leave Balance Report**: Displays the remaining leave balances of employees.


In summary, the Leave Utilization Report monitors approved leave, while the Leave Balance Report provides an overview of the remaining leave balances.
