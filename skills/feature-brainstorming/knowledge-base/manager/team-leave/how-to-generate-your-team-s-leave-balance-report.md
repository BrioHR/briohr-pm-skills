# How to Generate Your Team's Leave Balance Report

*This article explains how managers can generate and download the Leave Balance Report for their team members.*

To download the Leave Balance Report for your team members in BrioHR, follow these steps:


- Log in to your BrioHR account. Click on the **Team **icon and select **Team Leave**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48134971542/original/SKLMGu3RKydb72tgfuemM90Zajln7G6lkQ.png?1630302503)


- Navigate to Reports. Click on **Report**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48134971750/original/SOUpUV3kK3MmGrQdKP7KeZRQZBBNYhQswQ.png?1630302657)


- Configure your report settings. Choose the **year **of the leave report you want to generate, the **date **at which you want the leave balance calculated, and the types of leave you wish to include in the report. You can select multiple **leave types** if needed.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48134975437/original/YXG_t2Qi6QGW_RvgWUqekw-ikMCJ_KvLbQ.png?1630304549)


- Export the report. After configuring the settings, click on the** Export Report** icon to download the report.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48134975916/original/FQ62pQgGp5fyuWG2JgEopGngXKJMMx7_bQ.png?1630304695)


- Access your report. The file will be downloaded and ready for viewing.


**NOTE:**


**This function is available only to Managers who need to generate a Leave Balance Report for their respective team members. To download the Leave Balance Report for all employees, only HR/Admin with appropriate access can do so.**


For more information, please refer to the following article:


[For HR/Admin: [How to Download the Leave Balance Report for All Employees in the Company?]](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-download-the-leave-balance-report-for-all-employees-in-your-company?hsLang=en)
