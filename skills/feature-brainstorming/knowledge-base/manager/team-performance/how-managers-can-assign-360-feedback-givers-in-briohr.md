# How Managers Can Assign 360 Feedback Givers in BrioHR

*Learn how to assign feedback givers and view submitted 360 feedback for your direct reports in BrioHR’s performance cycle. Step-by-step guide for managers.*

### Introduction


360 Feedback is a valuable feature in **BrioHR’s performance management system**.


If it is integrated into the performance cycle, managers will receive a task notification in the **Pending Actions panel** to assign feedback givers for their direct reports.

This guide will show you how to:


- Assign feedback givers in BrioHR.
- View submitted 360 feedback and ratings.

### Step-by-Step Guide: Assigning 360 Feedback Givers


[Embedded content](https://www.loom.com/embed/54fbf68b8a9540f8948e64cf4c155983?sid=6db406cf-4448-4db7-863c-cd912d4c4dce)


####


#### Step 1 - Log in to Your BrioHR Account


Sign in to your account using your registered credentials.


#### Step 2 - Navigate to the Team Section


1. Go to **Team** from the navigation bar


2. Click on **Performance.**


#### Step 3 - Access Your Direct Report's Performance Page


1. Select the **employee** you want to assign 360 feedback givers for.


2. Click **See Details.**


#### Step 4 - Go to the 350 Feedback Section


1. Go to the **360 Feedback **section.


2. Click on **Assign feedback givers**.


#### Step 5 - Assign 360 Feedback Givers.


1. Select names from the drop-down menu.


2. Once selected, the chosen feedback givers will receive a notification to provide feedback.


3. Click **Submit** to confirm.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**Q1: How many feedback givers can I assign per employee?**


A: There is no limit, however, it's advisable to assign feedback givers who work closely with your team members during the performance period.


**Q2: Can I edit the feedback givers after submission?**


A: Yes, you can reassign or update the list of feedback givers before one of them completed the feedback tasks.


**Q3: Do feedback givers get notified automatically?**


A: Yes, the system sends them an email notification and task in Pending Actions once they have been assigned as 360 feedback giver.
