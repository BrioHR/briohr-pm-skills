# How to Complete an Appraiser Appraisal Form (Including Second Appraiser)

*Learn how to complete the Appraiser Appraisal Form in Team dashboard, including feedback entry, ratings, and final submission. This guide also explains the role of the Second Appraiser in the performance review process.*

### Steps to Complete an Appraiser Appraisal Form


1. **Access the Team Performance section**


  - Go to the **Team** > **Performance **

![image](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXeKpzntQVufLKV-KX8L2z5W8VD8VU0md3JZkN3UcQy-TGJSRoWhmOlPK2Hr3xZnBHfDwaSiC6ald8Nl2OidDK1BHots1CeMPzL89pRJt12-CIXwrmiOgt5wLxbokUEYYMmIYf2m3fgjE2D18aLAZDhwLj0d?key=TjJFf_e4fdjz7DiK7oP_3g)
  - Locate the list of **Appraisees** and click **See Details** on a specific employee.

![image](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXcmZpHakyH5ZMMe-o_yv82uNuZ8OPsigf3kmG2sfI6buDErHqIY0rdTqH521NOOVrzZmfxHkJC0FBzTyJY4781DbGHm4Sg2sObJlpwHeoSCQzZzldsztsX9XpH41ewuSbYoZ8_xt0I1bxeP6UDG9g6UztDm?key=TjJFf_e4fdjz7DiK7oP_3g)
2. **Navigate to the Appraisal Form**


  - In the **Employee Performance Dashboard**, open the **Appraisal Form**.

![image](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXfdnnIXX0Weify0FzjOYR0OdWtP1TF-wCGDVk1UfRhV7JTR9XRKLOYBmVr9NO3Q252PZ2PAqikgpDWGbNCYa3dZJ50E9hpiv6Ia7Ev2JROBf7E5KnP6dH_T3xxQVl9bU7Rdyuy1XQHS2nvRlTWQqseY4NM?key=TjJFf_e4fdjz7DiK7oP_3g)


### Appraisal Form Sections


#### 1. Questionnaire


- Enter **comments** based on the questions set by the admin.
- This section does **not affect ratings**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-09-2024-02-59-03-7055-AM.png?width=670&height=410&name=image-png-Oct-09-2024-02-59-03-7055-AM.png)


![image](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXfBSN62Nlzyf69b94e7i8auN9pTmb3Jft77lysxg5zo3gzG85hOv20CzgRsbfmKLa5yzJ4BiqTUE6kiHba1WZKR7VmIC8m9VJ9ULxSPCF82bLMAtT8wiiczrzq5uzJT3TlVGbdvlyBMnuzw3kvfxmkADs4?key=TjJFf_e4fdjz7DiK7oP_3g)


#### 2. Objective Feedback


- Affects the **final rating** (percentage weight is shown).
- Hover over **Show Description** for detailed **Objective descriptions**.
- Click **What rate should I put?** to see rating guidelines.
- Enter comments and provide a **star rating** for each objective.
- The **average rating for Objectives** is displayed at the bottom.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-09-2024-03-24-56-7340-AM.png?width=670&height=493&name=image-png-Oct-09-2024-03-24-56-7340-AM.png)


#### 3. Competencies Feedback


- Affects the **final rating** (percentage weight is shown).
- Displays the assigned **Competency Level** for the appraisee.
- Enter comments and provide a **star rating** for each competency.
- The **average rating for Competencies** is displayed at the bottom.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-09-2024-03-39-25-1469-AM.png?width=670&height=424&name=image-png-Oct-09-2024-03-39-25-1469-AM.png)

**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-09-2024-03-46-06-6741-AM.png?width=670&height=491&name=image-png-Oct-09-2024-03-46-06-6741-AM.png)
**


#### 4. Summary


- Provide **overall feedback** on the appraisee’s performance.
- This section does **not affect ratings**.
- If a **Second Appraiser** is assigned, their name and rating will be displayed below the comment box.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-09-2024-03-47-05-4762-AM.png?width=670&height=363&name=image-png-Oct-09-2024-03-47-05-4762-AM.png)


### Finalizing the Appraisal Form


- Review all **comments and ratings** before submission.
- Click **Submit Appraisal** to finalize.


---


### Second Appraiser Responsibilities


1. **Access the Appraisal Form**


  - Go to **Team > Performance.** Click **See Details** on the assigned appraisee.

![image](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXeKpzntQVufLKV-KX8L2z5W8VD8VU0md3JZkN3UcQy-TGJSRoWhmOlPK2Hr3xZnBHfDwaSiC6ald8Nl2OidDK1BHots1CeMPzL89pRJt12-CIXwrmiOgt5wLxbokUEYYMmIYf2m3fgjE2D18aLAZDhwLj0d?key=TjJFf_e4fdjz7DiK7oP_3g)


![image](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXcmZpHakyH5ZMMe-o_yv82uNuZ8OPsigf3kmG2sfI6buDErHqIY0rdTqH521NOOVrzZmfxHkJC0FBzTyJY4781DbGHm4Sg2sObJlpwHeoSCQzZzldsztsX9XpH41ewuSbYoZ8_xt0I1bxeP6UDG9g6UztDm?key=TjJFf_e4fdjz7DiK7oP_3g)
2. **Review First Appraiser’s Feedback**


  - View comments and ratings from both the **Appraisee** and **First Appraiser** for **Questionnaire, Objective Feedback, and Competencies Feedback**.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-09-2024-04-11-36-2743-AM.png?width=670&height=477&name=image-png-Oct-09-2024-04-11-36-2743-AM.png)
3. **Provide Summary Feedback**


  - Enter your **comments and assessment** in the **Summary** section.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-09-2024-04-14-06-2512-AM.png?width=670&height=407&name=image-png-Oct-09-2024-04-14-06-2512-AM.png)
4. **Approve or Deny the Appraisal**


  - **Approve**: Agree with the **First Appraiser’s** ratings and comments.
  - **Deny**: Disagree with the **First Appraiser’s** feedback.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-09-2024-04-14-39-1672-AM.png?width=670&height=417&name=image-png-Oct-09-2024-04-14-39-1672-AM.png)


By following these steps, both **Appraisers** and **Second Appraisers** can ensure accurate and comprehensive performance evaluations.
