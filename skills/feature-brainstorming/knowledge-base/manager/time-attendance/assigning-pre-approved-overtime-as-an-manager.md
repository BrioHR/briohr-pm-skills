# Assigning Pre-Approved Overtime as an Manager

*This article explains how Managers can assign pre-approved overtime in the system using the Time Attendance module.*

#### Audience & Scope


Audience: Manager


Module: Time Attendance


Country: Global


Pre-requisites: Access to Time Attendance module in Teams Dashboard





#### Step-by-Step Instructions for Managers to request Pre-Approved Overtime


1. Hover over **Teams** and click into the **Time Attendance** module

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-07-2026-01-15-12-4169-AM.png?width=1069&height=487&name=image-png-Jul-07-2026-01-15-12-4169-AM.png)
2. Proceed to click into the **Rostering page
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-07-2026-01-15-45-4001-AM.png?width=1902&height=670&name=image-png-Jul-07-2026-01-15-45-4001-AM.png)
**
3. ** **Click on the **+ Assign Another** button

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-07-2026-01-16-30-6284-AM.png?width=772&height=477&name=image-png-Jul-07-2026-01-16-30-6284-AM.png)
4. Then click on **Add Pre Approved Overtime**

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-07-2026-01-17-06-6687-AM.png?width=777&height=480&name=image-png-Jul-07-2026-01-17-06-6687-AM.png)
5. Fill in the** Overtime duration **and then click on **Add OT
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-07-2026-01-18-21-5382-AM.png?width=882&height=607&name=image-png-Jul-07-2026-01-18-21-5382-AM.png)
**
6. A success message appears to indicate the Overtime has been added successfully

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-07-2026-01-19-00-0158-AM.png?width=1811&height=481&name=image-png-Jul-07-2026-01-19-00-0158-AM.png)
7. Next, once the Roster has been published, the Employee will be able to see the Pre-Approved Overtime appearing in their schedules.
