# How Managers Can Manually Assign and Replace Employee Shifts in BrioHR Rostering

*Learn how to manually assign or replace direct reports' shifts in BrioHR’s rostering feature. Follow this step-by-step guide for managers to efficiently manage shift schedules.*

### Introduction


When direct reports are assigned to a work schedule with multiple shifts, their rostering page will appear empty by default. This is not an error - it is designed to give managers flexibility.


Managers can manually decide which shift period each direct report should work for the week or month. This approach allows for adaptable scheduling to match operational needs and direct report availability.


This is also method if the mangers would like to replace, or exchange designated shift periods on direct report's rostering.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Assign Shift Manually in Direct Report's Rostering


#### Step 1 - Log In to BrioHR Account


Log in to your BrioHR account using your registered credentials.


#### Step 2 - Access Direct Report's Rostering Page.


1. Navigate to **Teams > Time Attendance.**


2. Go to **Rostering.**


3. View by **'Week' **or **'Month'.**


4. Search for the direct report's name to open their rostering view.


#### Step 3 - Assign Shift for Each Day


1. Hover over the desired day box.


2. Click the** (+) plus** icon.


3. Choose from the **predefined shifts** based on the employee’s assigned work schedule.


*For example:*


- Shift 1: 09:00 - 18:00
- Shift 2: 13:00 - 22:00


4. Select the shift your direct report should work for each day.


**NOTE:**

You can assign **two shifts on the same day,** as long as the** time periods do not overlap.**


#### If you intend to assign shifts on a rest day or a public holiday, please refer to the articles below for detailed guidance. This ensures that employees' working hours are accurately calculated as Overtime Rest Day or Overtime Public Holiday in the system.


- [How Managers Can Assign Shifts on Rest Day in BrioHR Rostering](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/managers-assign-shifts-on-rest-day-in-rostering?hsLang=en)
- [How Managers Can Assign Shifts on Public Holiday in BrioHR Rostering](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-managers-can-assign-shifts-on-public-holiday-in-briohr-time-attendance-rostering?hsLang=en)


#### Step 4 - Publish the Rostering


Once shifts are assigned, click **Publish Rostering** to apply the changes. This ensures the new shifts appear correctly in the employee’s **Time Attendance** section.

### Step-by-Step Guide: Replace an Existing Shift in Direct Report's Rostering


#### Step 1 - Access Direct Report's Rostering Page


1. Navigate to** Teams > Time Attendance.**


2. Open **Rostering.**


3. View the roster by **'Week'** or **'Month'.**


4. Search for the direct report’s name.


#### Step 2 - Replace Existing Assigned Shift for Each Day


1. Hover over the day box with the assigned shift.


2. Click the** (+) plus** icon.


3. From the predefined list of shifts under the employee’s assigned work schedule, select the new shift to replace the current one.


#### Step 3 - Publish the Rostering


Once shifts are assigned, click **Publish Rostering** to apply the changes. This ensures the new shifts appear correctly in the employee’s **Time Attendance** section.


If rostering has been published prior, you do not need to publish again. The newly replaced shifts will appear immediately from employee's Time Attendance page.

### Update: Create New Shift (Custom Shift)


If you need to customize a shift to have specific clock in/out and break timings and none of the pre-defined shifts (set by Admin in Admin Settings > Schedules & Shifts) work, you can choose to create a shift with custom timing. Now, the custom shift includes additional break settings.


1. Choose the day you want to assign the custom shift, and click **Create new shift.**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-18-2026-09-15-20-7847-AM.png?width=670&height=368&name=image-png-Aug-18-2026-09-15-20-7847-AM.png)
**


2. Enter the **clock-in** and **clock-out** times.


3. Add the **break duration**, **break start period**, choose to **track break lateness**, and choose to **exclude unused break from worked hours. **Hover over the '?' icon to learn more about the setting.


4. Give the **shift a name** and click **Create** to add the new shift.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-18-2026-09-17-24-1602-AM.png?width=670&height=618&name=image-png-Aug-18-2026-09-17-24-1602-AM.png)


**NOTES: **


- The **custom shift cannot be replicated**. Use pre-defined (existing) shifts if a shift will be used often in the roster.
- If the shift you assign has a **break period** configured, the employee will only be able to clock out for break (start break) within that defined time window.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**1. Why is my direct report's rostering page empty?
**This happens when employees are assigned to a work schedule with multiple shifts. Managers must manually assign specific shifts.


**2. Can I assign two shifts in one day?
**Yes, as long as the two shifts do not overlap in time.
**
3. What happens if I forget to publish the roster?
**If you do not publish, the assigned shifts will not reflect in the employee's Time Attendance record. They are not able to view which shift period they need to work on.**
**


**4. Can I view rosters for multiple employees at once?
**Yes, in the Rostering view, you can switch between Week or Month mode to manage multiple direct reports efficiently.
