# Managing Pre-Approval Overtime Submission Requests as a Manager

*This article explains how Managers can manage requests submitted by Employees for Pre-Approval Overtime function in the system using the Time Attendance module.*

**Audience & Scope**


Audience: Manager


Module: Time Attendance


Country: Global


Pre-requisites: Team dashboard access for Time Attendance module enabled





#### Step-by-Step Instructions for Managers to Manage Requests for Pre-Approved Overtime


1. Check within the email inbox for the email notification on the Overtime Pre-Approval submission by the employee
2. Click on **Go to BrioHR**
3. Once logged in, click into the **Pending Actions** list

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-27-2026-03-30-27-5141-AM.png?width=670&height=237&name=image-png-Feb-27-2026-03-30-27-5141-AM.png)
4. Search for the **Approve OT Request** task for the employee
5. Choose to **Review OT, Reject** or check the **Roster **directly
6. If you choose to **Review OT**, you will be able to review the Overtime hours before approving the submission.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-27-2026-03-32-02-9805-AM.png?width=363&height=545&name=image-png-Feb-27-2026-03-32-02-9805-AM.png)
7. If you choose to **Reject**, you will need to provide a reason for rejecting the request.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-27-2026-03-33-30-1529-AM.png?width=366&height=587&name=image-png-Feb-27-2026-03-33-30-1529-AM.png)
8. If you choose to **Go to Roster**, you will be brought to the roster of the employee to check the schedule assigned to the employee.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-27-2026-03-34-18-3855-AM.png?width=670&height=190&name=image-png-Feb-27-2026-03-34-18-3855-AM.png)
9. If the request is approved, it will be seen in the Roster
