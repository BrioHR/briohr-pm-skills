# How to Approve, Reject, and Export Direct Report's Timesheets

*Learn how to approve, reject and export your direct reports timesheet in BrioHR.*

**Audience & Scope**


Audience: Manager


Module: Timesheet


Country: Global


Pre-requisites: Access to Timesheet module in Team Dashboard





This tutorial guides you through the process of approving, rejecting, and exporting your direct reports' submitted timesheets from two locations: the Pending Actions panel and the Team page.

**NOTE:**


Managers can export their direct reports timesheet from the Team > Timesheet > Dashboard > Review Timesheet page.
