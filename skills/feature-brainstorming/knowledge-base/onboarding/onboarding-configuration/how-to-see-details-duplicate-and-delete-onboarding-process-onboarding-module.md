# How to see details, duplicate & delete onboarding process (Onboarding Module)

*This is a step-by-step guide on how to see details, duplicate & delete onboarding process in Onboarding Module*

Login to your BrioHR account:


1. At **HR Lounge**, click on **Onboarding (new)** menu.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-09-2025-08-42-17-0055-AM.png?width=688&height=365&name=image-png-Jan-09-2025-08-42-17-0055-AM.png)


2.  At **Process** page, click on **See details button** to see the whole details and setting for the onboarding process.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-09-2025-09-27-37-1136-AM.png?width=688&height=206&name=image-png-Jan-09-2025-09-27-37-1136-AM.png)


3. You will see the details, and if the onboarding process need to be edit, you can edit from this page as well.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-09-2025-09-31-03-4289-AM.png?width=688&height=361&name=image-png-Jan-09-2025-09-31-03-4289-AM.png)


4. Click on **'X' **to exit from the page and go back to **Process page.**


---


### To duplicate existing Onboarding Process to new process


5. Click on **Duplicate button** to duplicate the process.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-09-2025-09-31-56-4213-AM.png?width=688&height=206&name=image-png-Jan-09-2025-09-31-56-4213-AM.png)


6. Once you click on **Duplicate button,** the new **Onboarding Process** will created following all the setting from the existing Onboarding Process you duplicate. You may edit the **Process Name** and the task by click on **See Details button.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-09-2025-09-34-24-3658-AM.png?width=688&height=217&name=image-png-Jan-09-2025-09-34-24-3658-AM.png)





---


### To delete the Onboarding Process


7. Click on **Delete button** to delete the onboarding process.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-09-2025-09-37-02-3586-AM.png?width=688&height=206&name=image-png-Jan-09-2025-09-37-02-3586-AM.png)


8. Click on **Delete button** to confirm deletion.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-09-2025-09-38-27-9372-AM.png?width=688&height=280&name=image-png-Jan-09-2025-09-38-27-9372-AM.png)
