# Part 1: How to Create and Customize Your Employee Onboarding Process

*This article explains how to create and customize your employee onboarding process by job role, department, and location.*

Before bringing in a new employee, it is crucial to set up your onboarding process.


You can customize the onboarding process based on the employee's job role, department, and location. Additionally, you have the flexibility to create multiple processes, each with unique tasks and requirements.


For detailed instructions on how to add a new hire to the onboarding process, please refer to our [guide here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-add-a-new-employee-to-the-onboarding-process?hsLang=en).
