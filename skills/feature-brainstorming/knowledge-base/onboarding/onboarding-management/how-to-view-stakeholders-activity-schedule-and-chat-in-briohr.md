# How to View Stakeholders, Activity, Schedule, and Chat in BrioHR

*This article explains how to view stakeholders, activity logs, schedules, and chat while tracking an onboarding process.*

BrioHR allows you to track and manage the **onboarding process** efficiently by providing access to **stakeholders, activity logs, schedules, and chat interactions**. This helps ensure a smooth transition for new joiners by keeping all necessary information in one place.


### Steps to View Stakeholders, Activity, Schedule, and Chat


1. Login to **BrioHR**
2. Navigate to** HR Lounge > Onboarding**
3. **Select the applicable Employee’s Onboarding **
4. **Select the Required Section:**

  - **Stakeholders** – View all assigned stakeholders in the onboarding process, such as HR, managers, and department heads.
  - **Schedule** – Check orientation schedules and any planned activities for the new joiner.
  - **Chat** – Communicate directly with the new joiner, HR, manager, or head of department through the chat feature.
  - **Activity** – Track all activity logs related to the onboarding process, including task completions and updates.
