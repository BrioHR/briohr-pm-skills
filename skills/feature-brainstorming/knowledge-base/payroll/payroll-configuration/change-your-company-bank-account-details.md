# Change Your Company Bank Account Details

*This article explains how to update your company bank account details and regenerate payroll bank files so they use the new account.*

** Audience & Scope**

- **Audience:** HR Admin
- **Module:** Payroll
- **Country:** Global
- **Prerequisites:** Access to HR Lounge > Payroll settings
- **Feature available on:** Web
- **When to use this article:** HR Admin needs to change the company bank account used for payroll and wants the bank files to reflect the new details.


**Step-by-Step Instructions**

1. Navigate to **HR Lounge** > **Payroll** > **Settings** > **General**.
2. Update your **company bank account details**.
3. Click **Save**.
4. Return to your payroll cycle.
5. **Generate the bank file again** — it will use the updated bank information.


**Common Issues / FAQ**

- **Q:** I already generated the bank file before changing the account — will it update automatically?
**A:** No. Bank files already generated do not update themselves. Regenerate the bank file after saving the new details.
