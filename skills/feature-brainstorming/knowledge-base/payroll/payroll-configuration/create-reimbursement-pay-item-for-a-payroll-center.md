# Create Reimbursement Pay Item for a Payroll Center

*This article explains how to create a payroll item under the Reimbursement category for a specific payroll center. The reimbursement pay item can be used in payroll processing once created. It can also be selected for payroll connection in the expense claims module.*

**Audience & Scope**


Audience: HR Admin, Claims and Payroll Managers


Module: Payroll


Country: Malaysia & Singapore


Pre-requisites: HR Modules Permission for the Payroll module





**Step-by-Step Instructions**


1. Go to **HR Lounge > Payroll**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-06-2026-01-33-45-7552-PM.png?width=1629&height=835&name=image-png-Aug-06-2026-01-33-45-7552-PM.png)
**


2. Choose your **Payroll Center** (if more than one)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-06-2026-01-34-58-7527-PM.png?width=414&height=328&name=image-png-Aug-06-2026-01-34-58-7527-PM.png)


3. Go to **Settings > Payroll Items**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-06-2026-01-36-00-9822-PM.png?width=1851&height=679&name=image-png-Aug-06-2026-01-36-00-9822-PM.png)
**


4. Click **+ Create custom item**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-06-2026-01-36-49-0403-PM.png?width=1675&height=472&name=image-png-Aug-06-2026-01-36-49-0403-PM.png)
**


5. Give your reimbursement item a name. This name will appear in reports and payslips when you process this item in payroll.


6. Select **Reimbursement** as the **category**. Addition type will be fixed to Not a remuneration once Reimbursement is selected.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-06-2026-01-40-18-5614-PM.png?width=1311&height=850&name=image-png-Aug-06-2026-01-40-18-5614-PM.png)


7. Click **Save**


**TIP: **


View existing Reimbursement items by selecting the 'Reimbursement' category in the filter


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-06-2026-01-24-24-0631-PM.png?width=1746&height=634&name=image-png-Aug-06-2026-01-24-24-0631-PM.png)





**Common Issues/FAQ**


**Q1: Why can't I edit the 'Expense Claims' item?**


A1: That item is a default payroll item in the system. Default items cannot be edited or deleted. You can create a new reimbursement item if needed.


**Q2: Can I delete a reimbursement item if it has been used in payroll?**


A2: No. You will not be able to delete any items once it is used in payroll.
