# How to Schedule Payslip Release in BrioHR (Payroll Admin Guide)

*Learn how to schedule payslip releases in BrioHR. Control when employees can access their payslips, choose immediate or scheduled releases of payslips for employees.*

**Audience & Scope**


Audience: HR Admin


Module: Payroll


Country: Global


Pre-requisites: Payroll Manager access to required payroll center


###


### Overview of the Scheduled Payslip Release Feature


BrioHR introduces a new feature that gives Payroll Admins the flexibility to **schedule the release of payslips** after completing a payroll cycle. This feature ensures that employees only gain access to their payslips at the date set by the Payroll Admin.


With this update, Payroll Admins can:


- **Schedule payslip release dates** for each payroll cycle.
- **Choose immediate release** once payroll is completed.
- **Choose to email payslips** to employees when payslips are available.
- Maintain **better control and flexibility** over payroll processing and communication.





### How to Enable this Function on BrioHR


1.   Login to BrioHR and click into **HR Lounge > Payroll**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-26-2025-04-16-46-7706-AM.png?width=670&height=276&name=image-png-Sep-26-2025-04-16-46-7706-AM.png)
**


2.   At the Payroll Module, click into **Settings > General**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-26-2025-04-18-47-6025-AM.png?width=670&height=224&name=image-png-Sep-26-2025-04-18-47-6025-AM.png)


3.   Scroll down to **Payslips** > **Auto Release Payslip **and click on the toggle to **Disable** the function. Once complete, click **Save** to apply the changes.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-26-2025-04-19-48-1474-AM.png?width=670&height=138&name=image-png-Sep-26-2025-04-19-48-1474-AM.png)


**NOTE:**


This setting is **Enabled by default**, therefore there is **no change** to the existing behaviour and the payslips will be **automatically available** for employees to view once the payroll cycle is completed.


If you wish to **manually select the date** of when the payslip should be available to employees, be sure to **Disable** this function.


**IMPORTANT:**


If **Auto-Release Payslip is enabled**, clicking **Confirm Submission** will immediately make the payslip visible to employees — you do not need to click Complete first.


If **Auto-Release is disabled**, payslips are **only released after Complete** or a manual scheduled release.


###


### How Scheduled Payslip Release Works


#### 1.   Complete the Payroll Cycle


Before releasing payslips, ensure that the payroll cycle has been **completed**. Once the payroll cycle has been completed, click into the **See Details** button.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-26-2025-04-00-24-5029-AM.png?width=670&height=468&name=image-png-Sep-26-2025-04-00-24-5029-AM.png)


For Payroll Months which have **more than one payroll cycle (eg, Default Salary cycle + Commission cycle in a singular month)**, the **Combined cycle payslip** will only be available to be released **once the Payroll Month has been completed** on the system


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-26-2025-09-27-43-4662-AM.png?width=670&height=233&name=image-png-Sep-26-2025-09-27-43-4662-AM.png)


#### 2.   Choose Release Options


When setting up the payslip release, Payroll Admins can:


- **Release now** – Payslips are available to employees right away.
- **Schedule release** – Payslips will only be available to employees on the selected date.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-26-2025-03-53-12-5049-AM.png?width=670&height=240&name=image-png-Sep-26-2025-03-53-12-5049-AM.png)


#### 3.   Control Employee Access


Choose to **Schedule the release** of payslips by choosing the date. Employees can only view the payslips **based on the date chosen here**** by Payroll Admin**. Until then, payslips remain inaccessible.


**NOTE:**
On the selected release date, payslips will automatically be sent out at** 12:00 am (GM+8).**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-26-2025-04-24-12-8203-AM.png?width=670&height=229&name=image-png-Sep-26-2025-04-24-12-8203-AM.png)


####


#### 4.   Available Options for Change


There is also the availability to **change the schedule** of the payslips, in the event that the payslip needs to be **sent right away**, the schedule **date needs to be changed** or even if the **scheduling needs to be cancelled**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-26-2025-04-41-08-8125-AM.png?width=670&height=224&name=image-png-Sep-26-2025-04-41-08-8125-AM.png)





#### 5.   Email Payslips to Employees


Admins can also choose to send the payslip via **email** to employees, ensuring better communication and accessibility.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-26-2025-03-58-21-4097-AM.png?width=670&height=231&name=image-png-Sep-26-2025-03-58-21-4097-AM.png)

### Benefits of Scheduling Payslip Release


- **Flexibility for Admins** – Allows more time to review payroll before releasing payslips.
- **Improved accuracy** – Reduces errors by controlling release timing.
- **Better communication** – Email notifications can be enabled to alert employees.
- **Employee transparency** – Ensures employees receive payslips only when authorized.





### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**1. Can I release payslips immediately after payroll is processed?**
Yes, Payroll Admins can select the **immediate release option**, making payslips accessible to employees right after payroll completion.


**2. Do I need to set a release date for every payroll cycle?**
Yes, a release date must be scheduled for each payroll cycle to ensure proper timing and flexibility.


**3. Can I change the release date after scheduling it?**
Yes, Payroll Admins can adjust the scheduled release date before the payslip is made available.


**4. Can employees receive their payslips via email?**
Yes, the Admins can choose to send payslips via email and the employees will receive an **email notification** to notify them.


**5. Can employees view payslips before the scheduled release date?**
No, employees can only view their payslips once the Payroll Admin releases them.


**6. What time does the system release the payslips?**
The payslips will automatically be released at **12:00 am (GMT+8)** on the selected release date.
