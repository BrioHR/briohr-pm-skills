# Payslip Updates Made Easy: How to Edit Company Name in Payroll (SG & MY)

*Learn how to change your company name on payslips for Singapore and Malaysia payroll centers. Step-by-step guide for quick and accurate updates.*

### Step-by-Step Guide to Edit Company Name in Payroll Center


#### Step 1 - Login to your BrioHR account


1. Access the BrioHR login page


2. Enter your login credentials


#### Step 2 - Go to the Payroll Module


1. Hover over **HR Lounge** on the Navigation bar, click into **Payroll** Module


2. Choose the **payroll center**


#### Step 3 - Go to Payroll Center Settings


1. Click into **Settings** and then into **General**


2. The payroll center name is located at the **General Information** section


#### Step 4 - Edit the Company Name


1. Edit the **Name** of the Payroll Center


2. Scroll down to the end of the page, click **Save**
