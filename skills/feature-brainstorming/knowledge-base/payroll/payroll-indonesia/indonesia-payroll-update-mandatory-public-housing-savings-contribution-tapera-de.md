# Indonesia Payroll Update: Mandatory Public Housing Savings Contribution (TAPERA) Details and Implications

*This article explains Indonesia's mandatory TAPERA public housing savings contribution, its timeline, and its implications for payroll.*

We have an important update for all our **Indonesia-based clients** using our **Payroll Module and Payroll Outsourcing services**. Starting in **2027**, the Indonesian government will enforce a new regulation that could significantly impact your business operations.

### What is TAPERA?


The Tabungan Perumahan Rakyat (TAPERA), or Public Housing Savings, is a government-initiated program designed to provide affordable housing financing to enhance national welfare. This initiative was formalized through Government Regulation No. 21 of 2024, amending the earlier Government Regulation No. 25 of 2020.


### Who is Affected?


The new amendment, introduced in May 2024, mandates savings contributions from both employers and employees who meet the following criteria:


- Age: 20 years and above
- Marital Status: Married
- Income: Above the minimum wage
- Employment Categories:

  - Private Employees
  - Self-Employed Individuals
  - Civil Servants
  - Indonesian National Military (TNI)
  - Civil Service Applicants
  - State-Owned Enterprise (BUMN) Employees
  - Provincially Owned Corporation (BUMD) Employees
  - Privately Owned Enterprise (BUMS) Employees


### Contribution Details


- Employers are required to contribute 0.5% of the employee's salary.
- Employees will have 2.5% of their salary deducted monthly.


### Implementation Timeline


While this regulation is set to take effect in 2027, the Ministry of Public Works and Housing (PUPR) announced in July 2024 that mandatory participation will be phased in to ensure a smooth transition.
