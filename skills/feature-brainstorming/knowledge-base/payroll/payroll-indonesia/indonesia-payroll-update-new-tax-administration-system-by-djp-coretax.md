# Indonesia Payroll Update: New Tax Administration System by DJP (Coretax)

*This article explains Indonesia's new Coretax tax administration system by DJP and its impact on payroll outsourcing clients.*

**IMPORTANT:**
**Indonesia Payroll is supported through our Payroll Outsourcing services in brioHR.**


The Indonesian Directorate General of Taxes (DJP) has launched [Coretax](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2024-11/SP-40%20PEMERINTAH%20TERBITKAN%20ATURAN%20PELAKSANAAN%20SISTEM%20INTI%20ADMINISTRASI%20PERPAJAKAN%20%28CORETAX%29_0.pdf), a new tax administration system designed to streamline tax registration, reporting, and payment processes.


**NOTE:**


**Starting January 1, 2025, DJP Online will no longer be in use for these purposes. **


**Transitioning to Coretax is mandatory for all companies operating in Indonesia to ensure compliance with the latest tax regulations.**


**Key Points:**


1. **Mandatory Registration**:
All companies operating in Indonesia must register on the Coretax system.
2. **Replacement of DJP Online**:
Coretax will fully replace DJP Online for tax-related activities, including registration, reporting, and payments.
3. **Compliance Requirement**:
Registration on Coretax is essential to meet tax compliance requirements and avoid potential penalties.


### Registration Process


Most of the information on the website is available in Indonesian, so please take note when navigating the platform. Below are the steps to register on Coretax:


1. **Learn About Coretax**:
Visit [Coretax Overview](https://www.pajak.go.id/coretaxdjp) to understand the system and its features.
2. **Prepare for Registration**:
Refer to this [Corporate Registration Guide](https://coretaxdjp.pajak.go.id/registration-portal/id-ID/CH001/typeSelection/corporateRegistration) to prepare the necessary documentation and steps.
3. **Register Your Company**:
Complete your registration on Coretax via this [registration link](https://coretaxdjp.pajak.go.id/identityproviderportal/Account/Login?ReturnUrl=%2Fidentityproviderportal%2Fconnect%2Fauthorize%2Fcallback%3Fclient_id%3Dcats-portal-angular-client%26redirect_uri%3Dhttps%253A%252F%252Fcoretaxdjp.pajak.go.id%252Fhome-portal%252Fid-ID%252Fportal-auth-callback%26response_type%3Dcode%26scope%3Dopenid%2520profile%2520catshomeportal-api%26state%3Dfbce8c834a4144d887d68456e0b324fb%26code_challenge%3D9gbEFtm1xBU-QmB2RS1WKGGCpPJNMmBOZ6PQ8MS4Jrk%26code_challenge_method%3DS256%26response_mode%3Dquery%26client%3Dcats-portal-angular-client).
4. **Submit Required Documents**:
Prepare and upload the necessary documents, such as:


  - Taxpayer Identification Number (NPWP)
  - Company profile
  - Identification documents


Please refer to this [document](https://support.briohr.com/hubfs/Materi%20Edukasi%20Coretax%20Umum.pdf?hsLang=en) for further explanation about the Coretax system, including steps to activate and log in.
