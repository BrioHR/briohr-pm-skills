# How to Complete the PERKESO Employee Registration Form (Borang 2)

*This article explains how to complete and download the PERKESO Employee Registration Form (Borang 2) for registering new employees.*

**The PERKESO Employee Registration Form (Borang 2) is essential for employers who need to register new employees with PERKESO. This form is available for download through BrioHR.**


**NOTE:**


**Borang 2 is not for first-time registrations with PERKESO.**


**For first-time registration, please follow these steps:**


- **For Employees:**
Visit the nearest PERKESO branch to obtain your SSN/SSFW.
- **For Employers:**
Log in to the [PERKESO Assist Portal](https://assist.perkeso.gov.my/employer/login) to register under the "**Foreign Worker**" section and generate the SSFW number for the employee. Click [here](https://www.perkeso.gov.my/images/borang2017/Borang_Pendaftaran_Caruman/Pendaftaran_Majikan_Pekerja/Panduan_Borang_2.pdf)to access the PERKESO guide.
- **Updating Employee Information:**
Once you have obtained the SSFW number, update it in the **Employee Profile under Payroll > General Payroll Information > SSFW Number.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48315148143/original/ABYT4DUzOo85pptjQ4FHLCf9QAb718A76w.png?1698202438)


- **Generating Borang 2 in BrioHR:**
After updating the SSFW number, generate Borang 2 in BrioHR to complete the employee registration process.


### Steps to Download Borang 2:


- The HR Manager/Admin should log in to **HR Lounge** and select **Payroll**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48259240715/original/8PjeClUoSIujniyBvr1jAUQ33kIFnR7w0A.jpg?1666773765)


- On the **Payroll Dashboard**, click **Employee Payroll Document**s under **Quick Actions**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48259242178/original/1mrbh9JMEOHLlhtP1BBVLqoAuriF1VNtHA.png?1666774000)


- Select **PERKESO Borang 2** from the document type. By default, text files for the current year will be displayed.


To download the form for a different year, use the dropdown menu to select the desired year.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48317307113/original/dFDugZXV3RHfGExl3Qw5aaku84x4dnn_Uw.png?1699952669)


For more details and to explore other forms available, visit the [PERKESO official website](https://www.perkeso.gov.my/en/about-us/source/forms.html).
