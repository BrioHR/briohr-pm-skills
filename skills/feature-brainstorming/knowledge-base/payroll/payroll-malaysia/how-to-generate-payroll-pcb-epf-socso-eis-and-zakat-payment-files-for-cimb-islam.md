# How to Generate Payroll, PCB, EPF, SOCSO, EIS & Zakat Payment Files for CIMB Islamic Bank Berhad

*This article explains how to set up CIMB Islamic Bank Berhad and generate payroll, PCB, EPF, SOCSO, EIS, and Zakat payment files.*

**NOTE:**


**This guide is specifically for CIMB Bank Berhad and CIMB Islamic Bank Berhad users. If you are using CIMB Bank Berhad, please click [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-set-up-and-download-bank-files-using-hong-leong-bank-berhads-connectfirst-platform?hsLang=en).**


### Step 1: Bank Account Setup


- In* ***HR Lounge**, click on **Payroll**.
- Go to **Settings**, scroll down to the **Bank Account** section, and in the "**Bank Account**" field, select **CIMB Islamic Bank Berhad**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48237436954/original/pbBmbc0naaCNJkgIun5dW4CqK2wlGSh5-Q.png?1657252214)


- Enter the required information in the provided fields (refer to the sample screenshot below) and click **Save at the bottom of the Settings page** to complete the setup.


**NOTE:**


**To successfully download the bank file with complete information, ensure all required fields (highlighted in the screenshot below) are filled out.**


####


### Step 2: Downloading The Bank File


- In **HR Lounge**, click on **Payroll**.
- In the **Payroll History** dashboard, click on the **COMPLETED payroll month** for which you want to process payments.
- Scroll down to the **Bank File** section. Depending on your payment cycle (single or double-cycle), click on the appropriate button: **Download, Mid-cycle, End-cycle, or Combined cycle** (refer to the sample screenshots below) to download the bank file.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48216428902/original/6n7s2mQC_nRvcZBBQfibw5FXfBTAnquB_Q.png?1649994747)


- In the "**Generate bank file for CIMB Islamic Bank Berhad**" pop-up window:

  1. Select the bank file type.
  2. Select the employee(s).
  3. Click **Generate **to download the file.


![oRp6Rpne1mLofLUN_SgasbGjddT85VPtVw.png?1657253514](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/oRp6Rpne1mLofLUN_SgasbGjddT85VPtVw.png%3F1657253514.png?width=450&height=388&name=oRp6Rpne1mLofLUN_SgasbGjddT85VPtVw.png%3F1657253514.png)


- After downloading the bank file, upload it to the bank's portal to process the payment.


**NOTE:**


**If any information is missing, you won’t be able to generate the bank file. A warning prompt will appear (refer to the screenshot below). In this case, enter the missing information and try generating the bank file again.**
