# How to Make HRDF payment

*HRDF (Human Resources Development Fund) payments in Malaysia are mandatory contributions for eligible employers under the Pembangunan Sumber Manusia Berhad (PSMB) Act 2001.*

- Employers with **10 or more Malaysian employees** must contribute **1%** of employees' monthly wages.
- Employers with **5 to 9 Malaysian employees** may opt to contribute **0.5%**.
- The contribution applies to **Malaysian and permanent resident employees only**.





Here’s a step-by-step guide to making HRDF payments:


- Log in to the **HRDF e-TRiS portal**: https://e-tris.hrdf.com.my.
- Go to **Levy > Levy Statement** to view your monthly levy details.
- Download the statement to review the amount due.





You can pay via one of the following methods:


#### A. Online Banking (FPX) - Recommended


1. Log in to **e-Tris**.
2. Go to **Levy > Levy Payment**.
3. Select **FPX Payment**.
4. Choose your **bank**, and proceed with online payment.


#### B. JomPAY


1. Use **JomPAY via your online banking**.
2. Enter:

  - **Biller Code**: 74038 (HRDF)
  - **Ref-1**: Your **Employer Code**.
  - **Ref-2**: Your **Levy Statement Number**.
3. Complete payment.


#### C. Bank Transfer / EFT


- Transfer to **HRD Corp**’s bank account (details available in **e-Tris**).
- Email the proof of payment to **finance@hrdcorp.gov.my**.


#### D. Cheque Payment


- Issue a cheque payable to **"Pembangunan Sumber Manusia Berhad"**.
- Write your **Employer Code** and **Levy Statement Number** at the back.
- Submit the cheque to HRD Corp's office.
