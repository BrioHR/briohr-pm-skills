# How to Prepare for Tax Filing in Malaysia

*Learn how to release EA & PCB II forms to employees, download CP8D and Form E from BrioHR, and submit statutory payroll forms via LHDN MyTax.*

**Audience & Scope**


Audience: HR Admin


Module: Payroll


Country: Malaysia


Pre-requisites: Access to Payroll Center (Malaysia)


---


### Introduction


This article explains how employers can manage EA, PCB II, CP8D and Form E using BrioHR and submit the required documents to Lembaga Hasil Dalam Negeri Malaysia (LHDN).


**Important dates to take note of:**


- **28th February:** Employers must provide the EA Form and PCB II Form to all employees
- **1st March:  **Submission of CP8D and Form E opens on LHDN MyTax Portal


This guide is divided into three parts:


- **Part A: **[Releasing EA & PCB II Forms to Employees](#part-a-release-forms)
- **Part B:**[Downloading CP8D & Form E for Employers](#part-b-download-forms)
- **Part C: **[Submitting CP8D & Form E via LHDN MyTax](#part-c-submit-cp8d-form-e)


---


### Part A: Releasing EA & PCB II Forms To Employees


**IMPORTANT:**


- All employers are required to provide EA and PCB II Forms to their employees by **28th February **each year.
- Since these forms contain statutory payroll information, it is strongly recommended to review and validate the data early before releasing them to employees.


#### How to Access EA & PCB II Forms in BrioHR


For detailed instructions, refer to the following related articles:


1. [Access and Manage Employee Payroll Documents: Payslips, EA Forms, and PCB II Forms](/knowledge/how-to-access-and-manage-employee-payroll-documents-payslips-ea-forms-and-pcb-ii-forms?hsLang=en)
2. [Send EA Forms to Resigned Employee via Email in BrioHR](/knowledge/how-to-send-ea-form-to-resigned-employee-via-email?hsLang=en)
3. [Download EA Form for Employees with Multiple Payroll Centers](/knowledge/how-to-download-ea-form-for-employees-with-2-payroll-centers?hsLang=en)


#### Important Checks Before Releasing the Forms


**1.   Verify Payroll Data**


Ensure all payroll values are accurate. The easiest way to cross-check is by using the Employee Payroll Report: [Download Employee Payroll Report in BrioHR](/knowledge/generate-employee-payroll-report-in-briohr?hsLang=en)


**2.   Confirm Company & Officer Information**


Make sure the following details are completed correctly:


- **Payroll Center > Settings > Registration Information**


  - Officer's name
  - IC/Passport number
  - Position
  - [Update Officer Details (Maklumat Pegawai) in EA Form and Form E](/knowledge/how-to-update-officer-details-maklumat-pegawai-in-ea-form-and-e-form-in-malaysia-payroll?hsLang=en)
- **Payroll Center > Settings > General Information**

  - Name (this is the Company Name of the payroll center)
  - Address (this is the Company Address appearing on EA Form and Form E)


**3.   Review Pay Item Mapping**


- BrioHR default pay items are already mapped correctly to EA Form fields based on statutory requirements.
- If you have created custom pay items, ensure they are mapped to the correct EA Form fields.
- To review or update pay items, go to **Payroll Center > Settings > Payroll Items > Search for Pay Item Name > Edit**

  - Refer here for details:** **[Correct Custom Pay Items Not Reflecting in EA Form](/knowledge/payroll-malaysia-how-pay-items-map-to-ea-form-fields-in-briohr-payroll-module?hsLang=en)

### Part B: Downloading CP8D & Form E For Employers


**IMPORTANT**


**Submission Deadline:**


- **Form E submission deadline**: **31st March** every year.
- **Online filing opens:** **1st March **every year, via LHDN[MyTax Portal](https://mytax.hasil.gov.my/).
- **Submission method: **e-Filing only


#### Steps to Download CP8D & Form E in BrioHR


Navigate to** HR Lounge > Payroll > Quick Actions > Form E/CP8D > Download.**


#### i)   Download CP8D


1. Select the year.
2. Choose "Employees to **exclude **from CP8D and Form E" from the dropdown (if applicable).
3. Download the **CP8D text (.txt) file** for e-Filing submission.
4. Download the** CP8D PDF file **for the company's record.


For more details on the related CP8D column explanation articles:


- [Column F (Employment Status)](/knowledge/understanding-cp8d-employee-status-and-set-as-status-1?hsLang=en)
- [Column G (Retirement Date)](/knowledge/understanding-the-retirement-date-employment-status-fields-in-cp8d-txt-and-pdf-files?hsLang=en)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-26-2026-08-03-26-7703-AM.png?width=670&height=383&name=image-png-Jan-26-2026-08-03-26-7703-AM.png)


**NOTE:**


**In the scenario where you have an employee who resigned and re-joined the company in the same year (2 employee profiles), you can choose to 'combine' the employee so they're listed as one employee (based on MyKad/Passport number) in the CP8D file:**


- **Same MyKad number (regardless of passport number)**
- **Same Passport number (if no record of MyKad number)****
**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-08-2026-05-22-27-2790-AM.png?width=661&height=485&name=image-png-May-08-2026-05-22-27-2790-AM.png)


#### ii)   Download Form E


1. Select the "**Status of employer**" and "**Status of business**."

  1. **Number of new employees (A3)**
  2. **Number of employees who ceased employment/died (A4)**
  3. **Number of employees who ceased employment and left Malaysia (A5)**
  4. Update the following fields if applicable:
2. Enable "**Reported to LHDNM**" if A5 is applicable.
3. Choose **"Return of CP8D (13)": **Defaulted to **option 1 (via Data Prefill)**. If otherwise, please choose another option.
4. Download **Form E.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-26-2026-08-03-55-2856-AM.png?width=670&height=363&name=image-png-Jan-26-2026-08-03-55-2856-AM.png)

### Part C: Submitting CP8D & Form E Via LHDN MyTax


LHDN requires all companies to submit CP8D and Form E via the **MyTax portal.**


It is advisable to prepare all forms and documents in advance to avoid late-submission penalties.


**NOTE:**


The steps below are provided as general guidance and may change. For confirmation or assistance, **please contact LHDN directly.**


### i)   CP8D Submission (Available from 1st March)


1.  Log in to your company’s [MyTax](https://mytax.hasil.gov.my/) account.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48289034642/original/i8WydVs_6Fm_X1c7c6PrRF6oAl7CytbK5Q.png?1679632074)


**NOTE:**


If there are other roles in the MyTax main display, select the appropriate role by clicking on Role Selection.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48289034619/original/GbR7c1RJjW6iOWAHijDWiYCfoOcsvak0DA.png?1679632055)


2.  At the top left, click on **ezHasil Services > e-Data Praisi/e-CP8D**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48289034640/original/Z4g0uw5xItwCx2XGZW28IPegHdjYdsc9IA.png?1679632073)


3.   Enter the **Employer Number** and click **Confirm**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48289034620/original/bMWEyT_tvYk3TR1MbdKXV4ASFeWONtNO8A.png?1679632056)


4.   Under **e-C.P.8D > select Submission of C.P.8D**


5. **   **Choose **Upload C.P.8D** as the submission method.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48323621223/original/HKurZsviS0MSDGatr8qMo--GKKmMhyst4g.png?1705373907)


6.    Verify the '**No. Employer E**' and select the '**Year of Remuneration**.'


7.      Upload the C.P.8D text file downloaded from BrioHR.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48323621245/original/zydLT7txfZoX6U4D3x12h4j1zpuXIHuyYg.png?1705373923)


8.    Review the information, complete the declaration, and click **Confirm & Submit**.


9.     On the **Acceptance Acknowledgement** page, click **Print Confirmation** to save the PDF file for your records.


### ii) Form E Submission (Available from 1st March)


1.    Log in to your company’s [MyTax](https://mytax.hasil.gov.my/) account.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48289034643/original/OkcrHf7N3uIHxO9pWXGw7GyrdBk92w-bXw.png?1679632074)


**NOTE:**


If there are other roles in the MyTax main display, select the appropriate role by clicking on Role Selection.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48289034618/original/r7Z0n_aUrKj6Ud8EWGyEveBDI_C_Zn4KCw.png?1679632054)


2.    At the top left, click on **ezHasil Services > e-Filing.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48289034644/original/Ms5wgM3UYOtdXR3uI4aKj1HtzuGgObGhqQ.png?1679632074)


3.    Click on **e-Form.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48289034629/original/ZGK6nWMTsjjE0i_joWIGSVXYWr1cdafVug.png?1679632070)


4.    Under **e-E,** click on '**Tahun Taksiran**' and select the relevant year of assessment.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48289034632/original/ORLycV5n-CkQIAWjBRQnhLcjr-rMfeqpTA.png?1679632071)


5.    Enter the company's **Income Tax Number** (E number) and click **Proceed**.


**NOTE:**


Please disable any pop-up blockers before accessing the e-Form.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48289034638/original/nkaJ-QYrZane1hIAnY-A2KKKdY30CwSTUw.png?1679632072)


6.   Complete the company particulars.


7.    Under "**Return of C.P.8D** or **Cara pengemukaan C.P.8D**" column, select "**Upload C.P.8D **or **Melalui e-Data Praisi / e-CP8D**" from the dropdown.


8.    Enter the employee information.


9.     Refer to Form E downloaded from BrioHR. Input “0” for fields that are not applicable.


- Enter the information for each director and employee.


10.     Review all information, sign the declaration by clicking **Sign & Send.**


11.    Save the confirmation by clicking:


- **Print Acknowledgement, **
- **Print e-E,** or
- **Print C.P.8D** to save the PDF file for your records.
