# How to Send EA Form to Resigned Employee via Email in BrioHR

*Learn how to easily send EA Forms to resigned employees in BrioHR. This step-by-step guide covers how to locate inactive employees and email their EA Forms securely.*

### Introduction


Employees who have resigned within the year are still required to receive an **EA Form** that includes income from their previous employment.


This income information must be declared during **e-Filing** on the **LHDN (Lembaga Hasil Dalam Negeri Malaysia)** portal, starting **March 1st** each year.


BrioHR provides a dedicated function that allows HR administrators to **send EA Forms directly via email** to resigned employees. This article will guide you through the process step by step.





---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Send EA Form to Resigned Employees via Email


#### Step 1 - Log In to BrioHR Account


Log in to the BrioHR account using your registered credentials.


**NOTE:**

Ensure that you have the appropriate** Payroll Admin** permissions to access the payroll module.


#### Step 2 - Access the Payroll Center


1. From the main menu, go to** HR Lounge > Payroll.**


2. Navigate to the **Employee Payroll List** table.


#### Step 3 - Search for Resigned Employees


1. In the **Status** field, filter by **Inactive.**


- This will display a list of resigned employees who were previously processed in the current payroll center.


2. If an employee has been **transferred to a new payroll center,** you will see an indicator such as:

**"Not in this payroll center since MMMYYYY"


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-10-2025-04-42-48-4669-AM.png?width=2000&height=457&name=image-png-Nov-10-2025-04-42-48-4669-AM.png)

**


3. Use the search bar to find the specific resigned employee's name.


#### Step 4 - Send EA Form via Email


1. Click **Select an Action** next to the employee's record.


2. Choose** Send EA via Email **from the dropdown menu.


3. Enter the required details in the form.


4. Input the resigned employee's** valid email address.**


[Embedded content](https://www.loom.com/embed/c3882e7b57ad49ff89f2e99cece96c8d)





**IMPORTANT NOTE:**

If the email address entered is invalid or inactive, the system will still attempt to send the EA Form. However, the resigned employee **will not receive** the email.


**TIP:**

Always confirm the employee's latest personal email address before sending the EA Form to ensure successful delivery.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**1. When should I send the EA Form to resigned employees?**
EA Forms should be sent latest by February 28th of the following year, as employees are required to declare their income during e-Filing on the LHDN portal starting from March 1st.


**2. Can I resend the EA Form if the resigned employee did not receive it?**
Yes. You can repeat the same steps and resend the EA Form to the employee's updated email address.


**4. Can I download the EA Form instead of emailing it?**
Yes, BrioHR allows you to download the EA Form manually and share it through alternative methods if needed. From the drop-down menu, select the **EA Form **option.
