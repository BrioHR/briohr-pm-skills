# How to Update SOCSO Contributions for Expatriates and Foreign Workers

*This article explains how to update SOCSO contributions for expatriates and foreign workers covered by PERKESO's Invalidity Scheme from July 2024.*

**Effective July 1, 2024, as per the latest announcement by PERKESO (known as SOCSO in English), expatriates and foreign workers will be covered under the Invalidity Scheme. This scheme includes 24-hour protection and funeral repatriation costs.**


To ensure compliance, follow these steps to update SOCSO contributions for expatriates and foreign workers before processing payroll:


Update SOCSO Contributions for Individual Employees


- Log in to Your **BrioHR **Account and Click on the **People **icon.
- Use the Search column to find the **employee** by **Name/ID**.
- Confirm that the employee is an **expatriate or foreign worker with a valid visa/pass** in Malaysia.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48343666654/original/Qep6-JPWsv_NVntRRz1aR6GVHEP9kbS2Lw.png?1721109827)


- Click on the employee's name to be directed to their **profile**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48343666670/original/CGV1F-gOZzyO9NBAiMBSQuyMyAMaupc-qA.png?1721109836)


- In the employee's profile, click on the **Payroll** section.
- Scroll down to the **Statutory Contributions** section and click on the Pencil icon

 to edit.
- In the SOCSO field, select **Insured against invalidity and employment injury**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48343666733/original/otw3u-h7uiW3vlugLP7TOz0OhIBJ4pli-g.png?1721109859)


- Click the Save button [](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48343665464/original/B1Je7q2w4ly5UGQ1NlPttOTrehiGs_Tssw.png?1721109107) to apply the updates.


### Bulk Update SOCSO Contributions


If you need to update contributions for multiple employees, follow these steps:


- Navigate to **HR Lounge > Payroll** and select your **Payroll Center**.
- In the **Dashboard**, scroll down and click on **Bulk Download/Upload.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48343667060/original/hky-iXctD_HYv2UaamPtt0G2iW6mkWBxMA.png?1721110041)


- Click on **Download.xlsx** to download the payroll data file.
- Open the downloaded file and locate **column AL (SOCSO).**
- Update the SOCSO category for affected employees to **Insured against invalidity and employment injury.**
- Save the changes to the Excel file. Upload the updated file back to the system by selecting **Upload ****payroll information** and clicking on Upload Excel File [](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48343666537/original/Dc2xEYUPq259fwBchisoCirBYc90CrspOQ.png?1721109734).


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48343667233/original/bx9IXMzhlpltdaVOb76SJzXEPsiMcJ-_Pw.png?1721110128)


- The data will be successfully uploaded into your BrioHR account.
