# How to Upload SOCSO + EIS Combined Text Files to PERKESO Assist Portal

*This article explains how to download the combined SOCSO and EIS text file from BrioHR and upload it to the PERKESO Assist Portal.*

**Effective July 22, 2022, employers can now use the [PERKESO Assist Portal](https://assist.perkeso.gov.my/employer/login) to submit SOCSO and EIS contribution details and payments in a single transaction.**


Follow these steps to download the combined text file from BrioHR and upload it to the PERKESO Assist Portal:


### Step 1: Downloading The Combined Text File From BrioHR


- In the **HR Lounge**, click on **Payroll**.


![TBVJjM9KohUZXpEn8NG4MX72W1eMoZ7vEg.png?1660879930](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/TBVJjM9KohUZXpEn8NG4MX72W1eMoZ7vEg.png%3F1660879930.png?width=263&height=528&name=TBVJjM9KohUZXpEn8NG4MX72W1eMoZ7vEg.png%3F1660879930.png)


- In the **Payroll Dashboard**, click on the **completed **payroll month you wish to submit payments for.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246212330/original/UGagk9nbaPMTLszaec3445M7oTqNhovZmQ.png?1660880351)


- In the **Statutory Reporting** column, click on **SOCSO & EIS Combined > Text File**. The text file will then be downloaded automatically.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246212613/original/5fO_DQGHju4H6_8jjP0M-efgarnxK2kmEQ.png?1660880636)


### Step 2: Uploading The Combined Text File To The Perkeso Assist Portal


- Log in to your [PERKESO Assist Portal](https://assist.perkeso.gov.my/employer/login) account.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246214340/original/-AXaigK3zHTVo_e4NbFRSEh-AO4cQ2oCig.jpg?1660882167)


- Go to the **My Sites **tab and select **CONTRIBUTION **from the dropdown menu.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246240007/original/Hf0t7Rht1bFM9Fc-ZevhVlr-njCr1Jy-jQ.png?1660895108)


- Ensure the format selected is **SOCSO + EIS**.


![8ECMGrTIhhMBuQ57k-pyWeT99nOXuYxbwQ.png?1660895482](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/8ECMGrTIhhMBuQ57k-pyWeT99nOXuYxbwQ.png%3F1660895482.png?width=520&height=385&name=8ECMGrTIhhMBuQ57k-pyWeT99nOXuYxbwQ.png%3F1660895482.png)


- To confirm, click on the **Contribution **tab and select **SOCSO + EIS**.


![uoIOnzlFozJkGHimCGY_HmtyG6ETh9yp8g.png?1660895498](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/uoIOnzlFozJkGHimCGY_HmtyG6ETh9yp8g.png%3F1660895498.png?width=652&height=384&name=uoIOnzlFozJkGHimCGY_HmtyG6ETh9yp8g.png%3F1660895498.png)


- Scroll to the bottom of the page and click on **TEXT FILE**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246242253/original/-f0Uz3Bsfnrlmk-RAaAAL7XolkqR33vrLA.png?1660895691)


- Upload the combined text file by clicking on **Choose File > UPLOAD.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246243401/original/SF4upH2iszJggvjS6pJJKM0p4YGsmEWHpw.png?1660896014)


- After uploading, the information will appear in the **Contribution Summary** section. Once verified, click **SUBMIT **to complete the file upload.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246244552/original/0rHOEqnRrkJnZ9gqVEw0gUkWlvfVcVtFqQ.png?1660896299)
