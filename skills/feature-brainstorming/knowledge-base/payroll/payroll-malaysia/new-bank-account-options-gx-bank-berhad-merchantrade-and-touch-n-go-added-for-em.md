# New Bank Account Options: GX Bank Berhad, Merchantrade, and Touch n Go Added for Employees

*This article explains the addition of GX Bank Berhad, Merchantrade, and Touch n Go as selectable employee bank account options.*

We are pleased to inform you that three additional bank options—**GX Bank Berhad**, **Merchantrade**, and **Touch n Go**—have been added to the employee bank account list. These options can now be selected as the employee's bank account during setup.





**Note:**


Please note that for payroll purposes, **Touch n' Go** accounts are supported by the bank file formats listed in [Malaysia Payroll Centre: Supported Banks for Payroll, Statutory and Zakat Bank Files in BrioHR](/knowledge/malaysia-payroll-centre-supported-banks-for-payroll-statutory-and-zakat-bank-files-in-briohr?hsLang=en). For GX Bank Berhad and Merchantrade accounts, bank file generation is currently supported only by AmBank (M) Berhad using the AmAccess Biz (Excel) format.





To update an employee's bank account details with these options, please follow the steps below:


1. Navigate to **HR Lounge > Employee Management** and click on the employee's user icon.
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-27-2026-04-03-17-2858-AM.png?width=1499&height=739&name=image-png-Jul-27-2026-04-03-17-2858-AM.png)


2. From the menu on the left-hand side, select **Payroll** and click the **pencil** icon in the **Employee Bank Information** section.
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-27-2026-04-05-46-9121-AM.png?width=1641&height=937&name=image-png-Jul-27-2026-04-05-46-9121-AM.png)


3. Use the **Bank Account** dropdown menu to select the appropriate bank.
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-27-2026-04-06-53-9695-AM.png?width=1567&height=796&name=image-png-Jul-27-2026-04-06-53-9695-AM.png)


4. Enter the **Account Holder Name** and **Account Number**.


5. Click **Save** to confirm the changes.
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-27-2026-04-07-31-2266-AM.png?width=1755&height=595&name=image-png-Jul-27-2026-04-07-31-2266-AM.png)
