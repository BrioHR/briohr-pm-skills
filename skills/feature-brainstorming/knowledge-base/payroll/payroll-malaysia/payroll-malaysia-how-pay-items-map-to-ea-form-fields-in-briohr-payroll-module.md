# Payroll Malaysia: How Pay Items Map to EA Form Fields in BrioHR Payroll Module

*This article explains how to map a payroll item to specific fields in the EA form.*

**Audience & Scope**


Audience: HR Admin & Payroll Manager


Module: Payroll


Country: Malaysia


Pre-requisites: HR Module Permission for the Payroll module and Module Settings


---


**Step-by-Step Instructions**


1. Navigate to **HR Lounge > Payroll > Settings > Payroll Items** — the '**EA Field**' column shows which EA form section each pay item maps to.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-06-2026-12-52-15-9730-AM.png?width=670&height=335&name=image-png-May-06-2026-12-52-15-9730-AM.png)


2. To map the custom payroll items, click the **Edit** (pencil icon) of the specific payroll item, and choose the correct option from the **EA form field** dropdown:


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-06-2026-12-53-56-3700-AM.png?width=670&height=356&name=image-png-May-06-2026-12-53-56-3700-AM.png)


- Select the appropriate section of the EA Form where this item should appear, or choose '**Not applicable to EA form**' if it should not be included in the EA Form.
- Determine the correct section based on your specific needs.
- The amount for this pay item will be reflected in the chosen section of the EA Form.


**NOTE:**


- **The default payroll items cannot be edited. The EA form mapping for default payroll items is fixed. **


**IMPORTANT: **


**For payroll items with unclear statutory treatment, please confirm with LHDN — BrioHR cannot provide statutory advice.**





**Common Issues/FAQ**


Q: Who can make the changes to the payroll item EA form mapping?


A: Any user with access to your payroll module and module settings.


Q: I'm not sure how to map a custom item. What do I do?


A: Please contact LHDN directly for further confirmation. BrioHR cannot provide statutory advice.
