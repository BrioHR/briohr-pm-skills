# Understanding the 'Retirement Date & Employment Status' Fields in CP8D TXT and PDF Files

*This article explains the Retirement Date and Employment Status fields in the CP8D TXT and PDF files and how to generate them.*

As the season for generating CP8D forms approaches, we have a quick update regarding the files that can be generated through our system.


To generate the CP8D file, please follow these steps:


**HR Lounge > Payroll > Dashboard > Quick Actions > Form E/CP8D > Download > Choose file/format of your choice > Download**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48329902588/original/0UzBUntb_MIuBlZXv1JtPbXS31_IYugo8g.png?1710139881)


**NOTE:**


**In the scenario where you have an employee who resigned and re-joined the company in the same year (2 employee profiles), you can choose to 'combine' the employee so they're listed as one employee (based on MyKad/Passport number) in the CP8D file:**


- **Same MyKad number (regardless of passport number)**
- **Same Passport number (if no record of MyKad number)**

However, if you encounter an error message while downloading the form, please be aware that this is related to a recent update to the forms.

**Key Updates:**


**Mandatory Date of Birth Field:**


The **Date of Birth** for employees is now a **required **field, especially for those still employed with the company. The system automatically adds **60 years (the retirement age in Malaysia) to the employee's Date of Birth and populates Column G (Tarikh Persaraan / Tamat Kontrak)** with the calculated retirement date.


**Handling Resigned or Terminated Employees:**


For employees who have **resigned or were terminated** during the assessment year, the system **automatically inputs the End Employment Date into Column G of the CP8D form**.


**NOTE:**


**If an employee is terminated in 2024, the CP8D/Form E generated for 2023 will still capture the employee's Date of Birth, as they were active during 2023.**


**System Data Capture Explanation:**


**Active Employee Example: Date of Birth: 12 Mar 1983**


System Capture: **Date of Birth + 60 years (retirement age) = 12 Mar 2043 in Column G**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48329904084/original/bmizBB6AmRm5rNCqpfbBAHXm-sZgLB19iQ.png?1710140832)


**Resigned Employee Example: Resignation Date: 27 Dec 2023**


System Capture: Resignation Date = **27 Dec 2023 in Column G**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48329904410/original/xW6nBSe67qUTM8nA9HqUOGo_X5M5CE_qWA.png?1710141095)


This update applies to all employees within the payroll centers, and the information for this column is mandatory. If you receive an error message, it will indicate which employees lack the necessary information.


**Updating Employee Information:** If you need to update employee information, follow these steps:


#### For Active Employees:


Path: **Employee Profile > Profile > Personal Details > Edit > Date of Birth > Fill in details > Save**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48329905070/original/r88AZuDBYHRwn75HuN102J9nHive6XRU6w.png?1710141538)


#### For Inactive/Resigned Employees:


Path: **Employee Profile > Three Dots > End Employment > Fill in the termination date > Save**
