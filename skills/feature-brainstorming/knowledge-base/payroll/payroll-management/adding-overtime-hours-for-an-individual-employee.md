# Adding Overtime Hours for an Individual Employee

*This article explains how to add overtime hours for an individual employee during the Adjust Payroll stage.*

1.   Navigate to **HR Lounge > Payroll**.


2.   Click on '**Run Payroll**'.


3.   You will be directed to the '**Adjust Payroll (Step 1)**' page.


4.   Search for or select the employee, then click the '**+ Add Addition**' button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48242245414/original/9ym-t8Gy94o0-n98R5olND2ftN_IhD96Hg.png?1659347749)


5.   In the search bar, type '**Overtime**' and select the relevant overtime payroll item.


![qXQK61uOy-2VCYzZFbMCljcPu3ICo6Wbew.png?1659347869](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/qXQK61uOy-2VCYzZFbMCljcPu3ICo6Wbew.png%3F1659347869.png?width=688&height=167&name=qXQK61uOy-2VCYzZFbMCljcPu3ICo6Wbew.png%3F1659347869.png)


6.   Enter the **total number of overtime hours** in the '**+ Add Hours**' column. The system will automatically calculate the corresponding amount, as illustrated in the sample screenshot below.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48242246775/original/Q42JAidmEBTRMp9iBaZ4Km-1OuoZq_L1rA.png?1659348067)
