# Default Pay Items: Definition and Use

*Default pay items are predefined components built into BrioHR payroll center which comply with statutory regulations for countries Malaysia and Singapore.*

### What Are Default Pay Items?


In payroll management, **default pay items** refer to predefined components built into payroll software, which automatically comply with statutory regulations. In BrioHR, these pay items ensure compliance with local tax laws and statutory contributions for countries Malaysia and Singapore. They help businesses remain compliant while reducing manual payroll management efforts.


---


### How to View Default Pay Items in BrioHR


Follow these steps to access and review default pay items in BrioHR:


Login to **BrioHR.**


1. Navigate to** HR Lounge.**
2. Select **Payroll.**
3. Go to **Settings.**
4. Under **Payroll Items.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-11-2026-05-37-24-5465-AM.png?width=2319&height=1006&name=image-png-May-11-2026-05-37-24-5465-AM.png)


Here, you will see all available pay items for the payroll center.


All default pay items have a square icon next to their name, indicating that it is a default pay item and cannot be edited or deleted.


---


### Default Pay Items for Malaysia Payroll


These include statutory contributions like EPF, SOCSO, EIS, and income tax, among others. These items are preloaded based on local regulations.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-11-2026-05-46-00-4473-AM.png?width=1853&height=1028&name=image-png-May-11-2026-05-46-00-4473-AM.png)

### Default Pay Items for Singapore Payroll


Items such as CPF (Central Provident Fund), SDL (Skills Development Levy), and other statutory contributions are configured to meet Singapore’s payroll requirements.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-11-2026-05-47-01-9026-AM.png?width=1797&height=1040&name=image-png-May-11-2026-05-47-01-9026-AM.png)

### Customizing Pay Items


Default pay items **cannot** be edited, as they are designed to comply with statutory requirements. If your company needs to create a customized pay item, you can do so by setting your own configurations.


For detailed steps on how to create and configure custom pay items, refer to [this article](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-create-a-custom-pay-item-in-briohr?hsLang=en).


---


### Conclusion


By using BrioHR’s default pay items, businesses can streamline their payroll process, ensure compliance, and minimize errors. These built-in items reduce the need for manual configuration and help keep your payroll accurate and legally compliant.
