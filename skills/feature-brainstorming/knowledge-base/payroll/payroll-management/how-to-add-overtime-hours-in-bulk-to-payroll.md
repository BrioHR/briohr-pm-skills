# How to Add Overtime Hours in Bulk to Payroll

*This article explains how to add overtime hours in bulk during the Adjust Payroll stage using the Overtime Multi Rates table or an import file.*

1.   Go to **HR Lounge > Payroll**.


2.   Click on **Run Payroll**.


3.   You will be directed to the **Adjust Payroll (Step 1) **page.


4.   In the Adjust Payroll Stage, click the **Overtime** button to open the Overtime Multi Rates table


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48242240095/original/fyGRed8BZfZcvNdJX6Sj3L0YaX8qQ4oBgQ.png?1659346689)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-27-2026-06-09-35-1961-AM.png?width=670&height=318&name=image-png-May-27-2026-06-09-35-1961-AM.png)


5.   Enter the **total hours** according to the applicable **multiplier.**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-27-2026-06-17-23-5878-AM.png?width=1723&height=691&name=image-png-May-27-2026-06-17-23-5878-AM.png)
**[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48242241866/original/iD7Z580jr2-j4wXyh5qkwdvYUrCaNEKliw.png?1659347014)


6. If needed, the **Import OverTime** function could also be used. You can download an Excel template, include the OT data in the excel, and reupload it to reflect in the payroll process.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-27-2026-06-23-29-0359-AM.png?width=1780&height=886&name=image-png-May-27-2026-06-23-29-0359-AM.png)


7.  After all the hours have been entered correctly, **return to the adjust payroll screen**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-27-2026-06-18-24-3148-AM.png?width=1771&height=585&name=image-png-May-27-2026-06-18-24-3148-AM.png)


8.   Click on any employee to **verify that the amount** has been applied correctly before proceeding to complete the payroll (Steps 2 & 3).


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48242242897/original/rCg7-pZs2ZVYqL3rnzhNPs1fsHZrYFWEQg.png?1659347251)
