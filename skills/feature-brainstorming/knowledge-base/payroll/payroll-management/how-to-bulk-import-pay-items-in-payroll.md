# How to Bulk Import Pay Items in Payroll

*This article explains how to bulk import additions, deductions, and tax relief pay items for multiple employees using Payroll Bulk Actions.*

Are you tired of manually adding pay items to your payroll? Our **Payroll Bulk Actions** feature allows you to effortlessly import various pay items, including additions, deductions, and tax relief items, for multiple employees all at once!


This process is straightforward and convenient. Simply download the appropriate template for the pay item you need to add, input the required amounts, and upload the template. Our system will automatically capture the details, saving you valuable time and effort.


At the end of this article, you'll find a step-by-step **video **guide on how to bulk import pay items using "**Bonus**" as an example.


---


Log in to BrioHR


1.   Navigate to **HR Lounge > Payroll.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48290373921/original/NA1chojeyGosjkRNpJSuFzFG5ZJlelQELg.png?1680249693)

2.   On the **Dashboard**, proceed to **Run Payroll **as usual.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48290374277/original/7-OHRe7bWppKN-gGCpksjwnBOSbBx12TNw.png?1680249793)

3.   After selecting the payroll **cycle**, click on **+Add Items in Bulk**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48290374537/original/lhEqmxzCo7yZH0kF2r9zu_5YTwS_ZQfs9Q.png?1680249874)





4.   Choose the **type **of pay item, then click the **Import **button.


**NOTE:**


**Each pay item has a unique template. Ensure you select the correct type before downloading.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48290375052/original/6kd8IK7mfG06_PTmi8XKpvGGMbIcQHR-OA.png?1680249999)


Example: In the screenshot above, the "Bonus" pay item is selected. To input the bonus amount, click the **Import Bonus** button.


5.   Click the **Download **button to get the bulk import template.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48290377078/original/tlb8ZaO967nkrCPpPF-hSRlE35izSLDr4Q.png?1680250535)


6.   Enter the necessary details under the **Bonus **tab. Save a copy of the completed template for upload.


**Important Guidelines:**


1. **The template cannot be used to modify employee or salary information.**
2. **Only input the pay item amounts.**
3. **Add or edit amounts only for employees who have the pay item.**
4. **Do not edit or delete any columns, headers, or modify the template's format.**
5. **Simply downloading and re-uploading the template with all employees included is not recommended.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48290377254/original/5LB4rB9BEfTZJeUo7ONXzaAKA58yW0MwxA.png?1680250594)


7.   Click **Choose File** to select the completed template, then click **Upload Excel File** to complete the process.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48290377745/original/rz1HU4XJUA2jvDoHzOCemBJ6h8bMNaINCA.png?1680250748)


8.   Within minutes, the import process will be completed! You'll see the amounts displayed in the list for easy tracking and management.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48290378316/original/Y9UvduVfdIkIZsnfPH9bwJmS2Q04uQUZXg.png?1680250910)


9.   Once the import process is finished, you can verify and validate the data for each employee by checking their individual pay item amounts during the payroll run.
