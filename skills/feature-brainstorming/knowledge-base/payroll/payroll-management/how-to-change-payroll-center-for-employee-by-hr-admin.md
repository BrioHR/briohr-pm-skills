# How to change payroll center for employee (by HR/Admin)

*This is a step-by-step guide on how to change payroll center for employee by HR or admin*

**Audience & Scope**


Audience: HR Admin


Module: Employee Management & Admin Settings


Country: Global


Pre-requisites: Access to Payroll Sensitive Information, Access to Admin Settings


---


#### Method 1: Change for an individual employee through Employee Profile


1. At** HR Lounge > Employee Management **or** People > Employee Directory,** find and click on the respective employee to view the profile.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-04-2026-09-55-05-8369-AM.png?width=670&height=353&name=image-png-May-04-2026-09-55-05-8369-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-04-2026-09-55-49-7379-AM.png?width=670&height=317&name=image-png-May-04-2026-09-55-49-7379-AM.png)


2. Navigate to the **Payroll** section.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-14-2024-07-04-30-4114-AM.png?width=670&height=478&name=image-png-Nov-14-2024-07-04-30-4114-AM.png)


3. Click on **edit pencil icon** to change the **Payroll Center.**


4. Choose the **Payroll Center** from the drop-down list.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-14-2024-07-07-32-6537-AM.png?width=670&height=462&name=image-png-Nov-14-2024-07-07-32-6537-AM.png)


5. Click on **Save** button to save the setting.


---


#### Method 2: Change for multiple employees through Payroll Center in Admin Settings


To change/transfer multiple employees between payroll centers, you will need to first unassign them from their current payroll center, then assign them to the new payroll center:


1. Go to **Admin Settings > Payroll Center**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-04-2026-10-09-11-5958-AM.png?width=670&height=366&name=image-png-May-04-2026-10-09-11-5958-AM.png)


2. Click the **Edit** button (pencil icon) of the payroll center you wish to remove the employee(s) from


3. Find the employees you want to change/transfer and **untick the box to unassign** them from the payroll center


4. Click **Submit**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-04-2026-10-34-06-3114-AM.png?width=670&height=354&name=image-png-May-04-2026-10-34-06-3114-AM.png)


5. Click the **Edit** button (pencil icon) of the new payroll center you want to change/transfer the employees to


6. Find the employees you want to change/transfer and **tick the box to assign** them to the payroll center


7. Click **Submit**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-04-2026-10-35-28-3249-AM.png?width=670&height=346&name=image-png-May-04-2026-10-35-28-3249-AM.png)
