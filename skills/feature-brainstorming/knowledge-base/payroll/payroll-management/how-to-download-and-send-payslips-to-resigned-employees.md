# How to Download and Send Payslips to Resigned Employees

*This article explains how to download payslips for resigned employees and send them by email from Payroll Documents.*

1.   Once logged in, navigate to **HR Lounge > Payroll.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48294289510/original/3feCgIWy1QskThQXo3UgLeGis75H0DnCGQ.png?1682413731)


2.   On the Payroll Dashboard, locate the **Quick Actions** column. Click on **Payroll Documents and select Document Type: Payslip.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48294291592/original/ADtm9HE-qeozthOAQkbYxR14iXM_fcBifg.png?1682414302)


3.   Choose the **employee **from the dropdown list and specify the **payroll period (Year, Month, Cycle).**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48294291829/original/ePSdPDmH6BxaZnMeAFUM-9Qi6RVukAC-9w.png?1682414377)


4.   **Download **a copy of the payslip. Since the function to send payslips is only available for active employees, you will need to manually email the payslip to the resigned employee’s address.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48294291953/original/y_Mj3cUQgIDLlDrMiF3bvqpTF38KKuSRvQ.png?1682414419)


5.   The "**send payslip**" function is only available for **active **employees. For resigned or terminated employees, you will need to manually send the payslip to their email addresses.
