# How to Edit or Delete an Employee's Past Compensation

*This article explains how to edit or delete an employee's past compensation and why changes don't affect existing payslips or reports.*

**NOTE:**


**When you edit or delete past compensation, the changes will not be reflected in existing payslips or payroll reports, as the compensation has already been processed in past payrolls.**


### Steps to Edit or Delete Past Compensation:


1.   Go to the employee's **Profile **and click on the **Compensation **tab.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48197798038/original/46HWjyUUO8wppZI2bmAmU0HJqJIY-rH2fQ.png?1646577488)


2.   Hover over the compensation you want to edit or delete, then click on the **Edit **icon.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48197798132/original/hnLlV6D_kpGov5v7y1n2QM7TLT_8JHqi_Q.png?1646577634)


3.   Since the compensation has already been used in a past payroll, you will initially only be able to edit the **Comment **section. To make further modifications, click on **Enable Editing**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48197799595/original/Z2lwFH9iqKAGpwcny3JxbfaJEv8QT76L-A.png?1646577958)


4.   After **enabling editing**, you can perform the following actions:


- Edit the **Effective Date**
- Edit the **Salary Type**
- Edit the **Amount**
- Add or update a **Comment**


After making your changes, click **Save **to apply the updates, or click **Delete **to remove the compensation.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48197801565/original/XjtkbaLmN561PWO0Y9aNTwKfgJ6qSSy1cA.png?1646578493)


5.   The edited compensation will be updated accordingly in the **Compensation **tab.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48197801745/original/uqnvxsI34i9PEjgIc8C_gXu1we5WLjoQ-Q.png?1646578661)





**IMPORTANT:**


After saving the salary change in the compensation tab, be sure to go to the **ongoing** payroll cycle or reopen the relevant payroll cycle and click the **Refresh** button to have the edited salary showing. The change **will not** apply to the payroll cycle automatically.


6.   If the compensation is deleted, the salary record will be removed from the **Compensation **tab accordingly.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48197802377/original/koAWnfaOglcPscOkR7c8A1PXZ24Z3-o7qg.png?1646578985)
