# How to Email Payslips to All Employees at Once

*This article explains how to email payslips to all employees at once using the Employee Payslips quick action in Payroll.*

1.   After logging in to your BrioHR account, click on the **HR Lounge** icon and select "**Payroll**."


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48165720007/original/te3vJ7zuAU_STwQ2aYlygItasuOYWrl7Fw.png?1638329213)


2.   In the **Quick Actions** column, choose "**Employee Payslips**" and then select "**Send Email**."


![04nu3tCpOqyR6Hu7c5RFvwghmVUyxB5WKw.png?1638329537](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/04nu3tCpOqyR6Hu7c5RFvwghmVUyxB5WKw.png%3F1638329537.png?width=276&height=411&name=04nu3tCpOqyR6Hu7c5RFvwghmVUyxB5WKw.png%3F1638329537.png)


3.   From the dropdown list, select the appropriate **Year **and **Month**.


![pcWCWmIIP1CmbCoOFEd3w6x6nH1c3nlNKg.png?1638329737](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/pcWCWmIIP1CmbCoOFEd3w6x6nH1c3nlNKg.png%3F1638329737.png?width=465&height=231&name=pcWCWmIIP1CmbCoOFEd3w6x6nH1c3nlNKg.png%3F1638329737.png)


4.   Choose the **Employees **to whom you want to send the Payslips, and click "**Send Payslips**" to finalize the process.


![9FwxBcu2GZrgshBO-aigq31xLXtMUosvJw.png?1638330450](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/9FwxBcu2GZrgshBO-aigq31xLXtMUosvJw.png%3F1638330450.png?width=451&height=489&name=9FwxBcu2GZrgshBO-aigq31xLXtMUosvJw.png%3F1638330450.png)


5.   The selected employees will receive an** email notification** from BrioHR, as illustrated in the screenshot below.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48165722213/original/NweZDZwOFDmOpspmPANC5EOEixtxtdn-SA.jpg?1638330550)
