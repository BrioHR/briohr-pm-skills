# How to Exclude & Include Employees in an Ongoing Payroll Cycle

*If you need to adjust the list of employees included in an ongoing payroll cycle, follow these steps to either exclude or include employees as needed.*

This feature allows you to customize your payroll processing by selecting only the relevant employees for each specific cycle.


### Editing an Ongoing Payroll Cycle


1. Login to BrioHR
2. Click on **HR Lounge**
3. Select **Payroll**
4. Choose the **Ongoing Month**
5. Select the** Ongoing Pay Cycle**
6. Click on **Edit Cycle**
7. **Select **and **Deselect **Employee
8. Click **Save**
