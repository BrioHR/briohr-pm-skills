# How to Generate Employee Payroll Report in BrioHR

*This article explains how to generate employee payroll report in BrioHR. Learn how to create detailed payroll reports for a specific employee and export them in Excel format for easy access and analysis.*

Payroll reports provide essential insights into employee compensation and financial management. This article explains how you generate payroll reports for Malaysia employee payroll, providing monthly and yearly data efficiently for each individual in an easy-to-export Excel format.


This step-by-step guide simplifies payroll reporting, ensuring accurate and efficient access to essential payroll data.


**NOTE:**


- **[[This guide]](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/create-hrdf-payroll-report-guide?hsLang=en) provides detailed instructions on how to tailor the company payroll report to extract the HRDF amount for each individual employee.**
- **[[This guide]](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-create-a-zakat-deduction-list-report-in-payroll?hsLang=en) details the process of customizing the company payroll report to extract information regarding employees who have zakat deductions, along with their identification details such as MyKad and Passport numbers.**


---


### Steps to Generate Employee Payroll Report


1. Navigate to the **HR Lounge** and select **Reports**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-27-2024-09-22-02-7017-AM.png?width=670&height=369&name=image-png-Dec-27-2024-09-22-02-7017-AM.png)


2. On the **Report Builder** page, locate **Employee Payroll** and click **Create**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-12-2025-12-28-55-7505-PM.png?width=670&height=221&name=image-png-Jan-12-2025-12-28-55-7505-PM.png)


3. Select the specific employee and the period you want to generate the report for.


You can choose a **single month, multiple months**, or **year.**





![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-27-2024-09-27-07-0775-AM.png?width=670&height=446&name=image-png-Dec-27-2024-09-27-07-0775-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-27-2024-09-28-38-0824-AM.png?width=670&height=445&name=image-png-Dec-27-2024-09-28-38-0824-AM.png)


4. Click **Export as Excel** to download the individual employee report.





![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-27-2024-09-29-35-1076-AM.png?width=670&height=449&name=image-png-Dec-27-2024-09-29-35-1076-AM.png)


Below is an example of the report in an Excel file.
