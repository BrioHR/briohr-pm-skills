# How to Update Employee Payroll Details

*Learn how to efficiently update employee payroll details, including bank information and statutory contributions, for both Malaysia and Singapore payroll systems.*

**New Update: The "Touch n Go" option is now available in the employee bank account list, along with other banks. To view and access this information, navigate to Employee Profile > Payroll > Edit Employee Bank Information > Bank Account.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48339954128/original/xQA-adtJG1WyHAt6nLvwI-lcv4txVmP56g.png?1718174823)

If you need to update an employee's personal payroll details, follow these guidelines based on their payroll assignment:


#### 1) Employees Under Malaysia Payroll:


To update an employee's payroll details in Malaysia:


- Add Basic Salary: Update under the 'Compensation' menu. [Refer [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-manage-salary-history-in-an-employees-profile?hsLang=en)for details].
- PCB Number
- SSFW Number: [Refer [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-manage-and-edit-ssfw-numbers-for-foreign-employees?hsLang=en)for details].
- Tax Resident Category: [Refer [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-set-tax-resident-and-non-tax-resident-status-in-malaysia?hsLang=en)for details].
- Marital Status: [Refer [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/tax-resident-marital-status-categories?hsLang=en) for details].
- Child Information: For tax relief purposes.
- Update TP3 Form: For new joiners. [Refer [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/what-is-tp3-form-in-malaysia-payroll?hsLang=en) for details].
- Employee's Bank Information
- Update Statutory Contributions: EPF Category, EPF Number, SOCSO, EIS, HRDF, PRS.


**2) Employees Under Singapore Payroll:**


To update an employee's payroll details in Singapore:


- Add Basic Salary: Update under the 'Compensation' menu. [Refer [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-manage-salary-history-in-an-employees-profile?hsLang=en)for details].
- Tax Resident Category
- Employee's Bank Information
- Update Statutory Contributions: CPF Category, SDL, CDAC, MBMF, SINDA, EFP, FWL.


---


#### Steps to Update Employee Payroll Information:


- Go to **People **in your payroll system.
- Search for the Employee's Name and click on it.

- Go to the **Payroll **Menu and make the necessary updates.
