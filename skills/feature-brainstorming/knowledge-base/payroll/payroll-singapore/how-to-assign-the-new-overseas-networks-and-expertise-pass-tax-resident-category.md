# How to Assign the New "Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass" Tax Resident Category in BrioHR

*This article explains how to assign the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass tax resident category to an employee in BrioHR.*

The Ministry of Manpower (MOM) has introduced a new tax resident category known as the "Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass." For comprehensive information, please refer to the official source [here](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/key-facts).


### How to Assign the New Tax Resident Category in BrioHR


Assigning the "Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass" category in BrioHR is straightforward.


Follow these steps to update an employee’s profile:


1. **Navigate to the Employee Profile**

  1. Go to the employee's **Profile > Payroll **tab.
2. **Edit Payroll Information**

  1. Scroll to the "General Payroll Information" section.
  2. Click the "Edit" button (green pencil icon).
3. **Select the New Category**

  1. In the "Tax resident category" dropdown menu, choose "Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass."
4. **Save Changes**

  1. Click "Save" to finalize the update.

### Important Information


- **CPF Contributions:** Employees under this category are exempt from CPF contributions. However, contributions to other statutory funds, such as SDL, CDAC, and others, may still be required.
- **Reporting:** Once this tax resident category is assigned, it will appear in the following reports:

  - Company Payroll Report
  - Payroll Variance Report
  - Onboarding > Payroll Details
