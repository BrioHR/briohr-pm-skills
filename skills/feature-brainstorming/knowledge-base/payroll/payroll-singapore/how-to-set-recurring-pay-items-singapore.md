# How to Set Recurring Pay Items (Singapore)

*Guide to Creating and Setting Recurring Custom Pay Items in BrioHR (Singapore Payroll)*

In BrioHR, we provide a default list of pay items that comply with local statutory rules and regulations. However, there may be times when you need to create a custom item because the existing default items cannot be edited to fit your company's specific processes. In such cases, your best option is to create a custom pay item to address this need.


***NOTE : Before creating a custom payslip item to be used in the payroll, it is advisable to check if similar items already exist in the Default Payslip Items list prepared by BrioHR.**





Login to the BrioHR account


1. Click on **HR Lounge**


2. Navigate to **Payroll**



![1-Aug-22-2024-04-40-03-4703-PM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/1-Aug-22-2024-04-40-03-4703-PM.png?width=688&height=300&name=1-Aug-22-2024-04-40-03-4703-PM.png)




You'll be directed to the Payroll dashboard page.
3. Click on **Settings**


**
![2-Aug-22-2024-04-41-31-0586-PM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/2-Aug-22-2024-04-41-31-0586-PM.png?width=688&height=294&name=2-Aug-22-2024-04-41-31-0586-PM.png)
**


4. Click on **Salary Allocation & Default Items**


**
![3-Aug-22-2024-04-42-42-7690-PM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/3-Aug-22-2024-04-42-42-7690-PM.png?width=688&height=310&name=3-Aug-22-2024-04-42-42-7690-PM.png)
**


5. Click on **+Create Custom Item**


**
![4-Aug-22-2024-04-44-12-9852-PM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/4-Aug-22-2024-04-44-12-9852-PM.png?width=688&height=272&name=4-Aug-22-2024-04-44-12-9852-PM.png)
**


6. A pop-up (**Create Custom Item**) will appear. Fill in all the relevant fields, and click **Submit** to save your changes.


****NOTE : Kindly remember to select the correct options when creating a custom payslip item. As if the wrong option has been selected, it may affect the payroll calculations.***





***
![5-Aug-22-2024-04-45-53-7021-PM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/5-Aug-22-2024-04-45-53-7021-PM.png?width=397&height=616&name=5-Aug-22-2024-04-45-53-7021-PM.png)
***


7. You may then click on the **See list of Payroll Items** icon, to confirm that the newly created custom payslip item has been added to the Item list accordingly.


![6-Aug-22-2024-04-47-07-4140-PM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/6-Aug-22-2024-04-47-07-4140-PM.png?width=688&height=261&name=6-Aug-22-2024-04-47-07-4140-PM.png)


8. If you want to **edit** your custom pay item, click on the pencil icon and make the changes.


![7-Aug-22-2024-04-48-33-1267-PM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/7-Aug-22-2024-04-48-33-1267-PM.png?width=688&height=227&name=7-Aug-22-2024-04-48-33-1267-PM.png)
