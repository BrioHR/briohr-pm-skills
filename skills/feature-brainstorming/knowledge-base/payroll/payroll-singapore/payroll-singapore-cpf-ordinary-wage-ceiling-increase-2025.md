# Payroll Singapore: CPF Ordinary Wage Ceiling Increase (2025)

*This article explains the 2025 CPF Ordinary Wage ceiling increase from $6,800 to $7,400 and how it affects your BrioHR payroll cycles.*

In 2025, the CPF Ordinary Wage (OW) ceiling will increase from $6,800 to $7,400. This update will affect how CPF contributions are calculated for both you and your employees. In this article, we’ll walk you through what this change means, how it will affect your BrioHR payroll cycles, and the updated CPF Ordinary Wage and annual salary ceilings.

**What’s Changing?**
The CPF Ordinary Wage ceiling will be raised, which will impact the amount of CPF contributions required from you or your employees.

**How This Affects BrioHR Payroll**
Good news! There’s no need to make manual adjustments in your BrioHR account. BrioHR will **automatically** update the CPF Ordinary Wage ceiling during your payroll runs. This means you can continue processing payroll as usual without any extra setup or configuration.


**NOTE:**


**We’ll update the system in advance, so you’ll be able to view and apply these changes when processing the January 2025 payroll.**





**Updated CPF Ordinary Wage and Annual Salary Ceilings**
Here’s a summary of the CPF Ordinary Wage (OW) and annual salary ceilings from 2023 to 2026:
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-20-2024-03-01-29-1314-AM.png?width=688&height=254&name=image-png-Dec-20-2024-03-01-29-1314-AM.png)


- **The CPF annual salary ceiling of $102,000 will remain unchanged. This is the maximum amount of CPF contributions payable for all salaries received in a year, including both Ordinary Wages and Additional Wages.**
- **There will also be no change to the Additional Wage ceiling and CPF Annual Limit. These will stay the same at [$102,000 – Total Ordinary Wage subject to CPF for the year] and $37,740, respectively.**


**Source: **[CPF Contribution Changes from 1 January 2025](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/employer/infohub/news/cpf-related-announcements/new-contribution-rates)

**Tip: What is the Additional Wage (AW) Ceiling?**


The [Additional Wage (AW) ceiling](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/employer/employer-obligations/what-payments-attract-cpf-contributions) determines the amount of Additional Wages that attract CPF contributions. This ceiling is applied on a per-employer, per-calendar-year basis. It is calculated as follows:


**$102,000 – Total Ordinary Wage subject to CPF for the year**


To calculate the CPF payable on Additional Wages, you can use the [Additional Wage Ceiling Calculator](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/employer/tools-and-services/calculators/additional-wage-ceiling-calculator). For more details about [AW](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/what-are-ordinary-wages-ow-and-additional-wages-aw) and the [AW ceiling](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/how-do-i-calculate-the-additional-wage-aw-ceiling-and-the-amount-that-attracts-cpf-contributions), visit the CPF website.


**Source: [CPFB Additional Wage Ceiling](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/what-is-the-additional-wage-aw-ceiling)**
