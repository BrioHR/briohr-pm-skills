# Who Should Be Included in the IR8A Form?

*This article explains which employees must be included in or excluded from Singapore's IR8A form according to IRAS guidelines.*

Employers in Singapore are required to complete Form IR8A, along with Appendix 8A, Appendix 8B, or Form IR8S (if applicable), to report their employees' remuneration.


According to the **Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore's (IRAS)** **Explanatory Notes**, here’s a guide on which employees must be included or excluded in the IR8A Form:


### A. INCLUDE:


- Full-time resident employees
- Part-time resident employees
- Non-resident employees who are based overseas but required to render services in Singapore during the year (excluding employment income details where clearance has been filed)
- Company directors, including non-resident directors
- Board members receiving Board/Committee Member fees
- Pensioners
- Employees who have left the organization but received income in 2022 (e.g., stock options gains)


### B. EXCLUDE:


- Foreigners posted overseas after tax clearance has been filed (i.e., Form IR21) and who did not render any employment service in Singapore for the remainder of the calendar year
- Foreigners contracted by a Singapore employer to be based overseas and who rendered their employment services entirely outside Singapore for the entire calendar year
- Foreigners who have left the organization and for whom filing of [Form IR21](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/employers/tax-clearance-for-foreign-spr-employees-(ir21)) (tax clearance) is required


**Source: [Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) - Tax Forms for Employers](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/employers/auto-inclusion-scheme-(ais)-for-employment-income/reporting-employee-earnings-(ir8a-appendix-8a-appendix-8b-ir8s))**


#### How to View Included or Excluded Employees in BrioHR:


To check which employees are included or excluded in the IR8A Form using BrioHR, follow these steps:


- Go to **HR Lounge** and click on **Payroll**.
- Select the **Payroll Center**.
- Under **Quick Actions**, click on the **Payroll Documents** button.

- Navigate to the **IR8A (Main)** section and click on the **Employee List** button.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-19-2025-03-43-53-8381-AM.png?width=688&height=190&name=image-png-Feb-19-2025-03-43-53-8381-AM.png)


- Use the checkbox to **include or exclude **employees in IR8A.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-19-2025-03-46-42-3904-AM.png?width=688&height=395&name=image-png-Feb-19-2025-03-46-42-3904-AM.png)


**Tips:**


- **Automatic Inclusion:** **Employees with payslips or who were included in the previous year's payroll will be automatically included.**


**>>**  If there are specific reasons for exclusion, the HR Admin must manually exclude these employees before submission by unticking the employee and clicking "Save".
- **Automatic Exclusion: Employees without IR8A data will be automatically excluded and their names will be greyed out from the list.**


**>>**  If there are specific reasons to include these employees, the HR Admin must manually include them before submission by selecting the employee from the "Employees > Select Employee" dropdown. After clicking "Recompute IR8A," ensure that the values are correct before saving.
