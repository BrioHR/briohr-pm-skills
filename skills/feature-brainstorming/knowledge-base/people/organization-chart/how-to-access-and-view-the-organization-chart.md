# How to Access and View the Organization Chart

*This article explains how to access, filter, and print the organization chart in horizontal or compact view.*

The organization chart displays the breakdown structure of your company in a diagram form. You can view it horizontally or in a compact format, apply filters to narrow or widen your search, and print the page.


A short video is attached below, detailing the following features of the Organization Chart:


1. **Enabling access to the Org Chart and accessing it on the system**
2. **Selecting filters to narrow or widen your search**
3. **Printing the Org Chart and zooming in/out**
4. **Changing the view from compact to horizontal**
5. **Expanding and collapsing the Org Chart**
6. **Editing information, viewing employee profiles, and adding direct reports (vacant positions or new employees)**
7. **Managing vacant positions: edit, add, or delete positions**
