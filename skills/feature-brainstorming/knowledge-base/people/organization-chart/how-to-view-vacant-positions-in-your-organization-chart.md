# How to View Vacant Positions in Your Organization Chart

*This article explains how to view the list of vacant positions in your organization chart.*

Login to your BrioHR account:


1.   Go to **People**.


2.   Navigate to **Org Chart**.


3.   Click **Vacant Positions** at the bottom left.
