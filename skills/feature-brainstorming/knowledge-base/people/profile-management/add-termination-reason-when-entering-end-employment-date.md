# Add Termination Reason When Entering End Employment Date

*This article explains how to add a termination reason when an end employment date is entered for an employee. The termination reason can also be included in the employee information report. This can be used to support turnover reporting, compliance documentation, and offboarding workflows.*

**Audience & Scope**


Audience: HR Admin


Module: Core HR (Employee Management)


Country: Global


Prerequisites: User group settings: -


1. Under Profile Sensitive Data - View and Modify permission for Employment end date and Job grade
2. Under HR Modules Permission - Global: Create, import employee





**Part 1: Entering Termination Reason**


1. Go to **HR Lounge > Employee Management**.


2. Search for the employee's Name or ID in the **search **column.


3. Click on the employee's name to navigate to their **profile**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-15-2026-02-26-57-3997-AM.png?width=670&height=298&name=image-png-Jul-15-2026-02-26-57-3997-AM.png)


4. On the resigned employee's **profile**, click the **ellipsis ('...')** icon next to the Edit button.


5. Select '**End Employment**' from the dropdown menu.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-15-2026-02-28-17-1280-AM.png?width=670&height=243&name=image-png-Jul-15-2026-02-28-17-1280-AM.png)


6. Input the **Employment End Date**


7. Choose the **termination reason** from the dropdown


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-26-2026-01-14-14-9922-PM.png?width=607&height=483&name=image-png-Jul-26-2026-01-14-14-9922-PM.png)


**NOTE:**


The 'Reason' dropdown is split between 2 categories, **VOLUNTARY** and **INVOLUNTARY** reasons. Please scroll to the bottom of the list to view all available reasons. The category can be used/included in reports that contain Employment Details.


The options in the resignation ‘Reason’ dropdown are fixed and cannot be customized.


8. Enter in any **Comment** (optional).


9. Click **Save **to complete the action or **Cancel **to abort.





**Part 2: Including the termination reason in employee information report**


1. Go to **Analytics > Report Builder**


2. Click on **Employee Information Report**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-26-2026-01-41-46-2668-PM.png?width=670&height=390&name=image-png-Jul-26-2026-01-41-46-2668-PM.png)


3. Click **Field & Filters**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-27-2026-03-24-37-9412-AM.png?width=670&height=237&name=image-png-Jul-27-2026-03-24-37-9412-AM.png)
**


4. Go to **Employment Details**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-27-2026-03-25-22-5253-AM.png?width=670&height=292&name=image-png-Jul-27-2026-03-25-22-5253-AM.png)
**


5. Scroll down to find the '**Termination Category**', '**Termination Reason**', and '**Termination Comment**'. You may choose to include or exclude the fields and add filters depending on your reporting needs.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-27-2026-03-27-07-5307-AM.png?width=670&height=265&name=image-png-Jul-27-2026-03-27-07-5307-AM.png)


**TIP:**


The termination category, termination reason, and termination comment fields and filters are also available in reports that include employment details.


6. Click **Apply Changes **to apply your chosen fields and filters to the report.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-27-2026-03-28-11-5201-AM.png?width=670&height=248&name=image-png-Jul-27-2026-03-28-11-5201-AM.png)


7. Click '**Save as**' and name your report if you would like to use the same format in the future.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-27-2026-03-28-44-5745-AM.png?width=670&height=236&name=image-png-Jul-27-2026-03-28-44-5745-AM.png)





**Common Issues/FAQ**


**Q1: Why can't I see the 3 dots to enter the employment end date?**


A1: You may not have the necessary permissions. Your user group permissions need to be configured correctly; refer to the 'pre-requisite' section at the beginning of this article.


**Q2: I want my report only to include employees that resign within a certain time period (e.g., June 2026). How do I do this?**


A2: In the Field & Filters, under Employment details, add a date filter for Employment End Date, and click 'Apply Changes'.
