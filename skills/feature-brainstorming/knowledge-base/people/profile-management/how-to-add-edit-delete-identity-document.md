# How to add/edit/delete identity document?

*Manage Employee Identity Documents: Add, Edit, or Delete Records in BrioHR.*

This feature assists you in adding/editing/deleting the employee's identity documents in the system. This will mainly be used when employees have updated their identity documents or left the company.


To find the identity documents, you can go to the employee's profile > Identity document.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-27-2024-12-31-47-7950-PM.png?width=688&height=565&name=image-png-Aug-27-2024-12-31-47-7950-PM.png)


To make any changes to the identity documents, you can click on the green pencil icon on the right of the page.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-27-2024-12-33-54-7648-PM.png?width=688&height=565&name=image-png-Aug-27-2024-12-33-54-7648-PM.png)


Once you have click on the green pencil icon, you will be able to edit the following:


- Identity document number
- Delivery date
- Expiry date
- Removal of the attachment


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-27-2024-12-34-59-4219-PM.png?width=688&height=503&name=image-png-Aug-27-2024-12-34-59-4219-PM.png)


Once you have completed the updates, do remember to click "Save".
