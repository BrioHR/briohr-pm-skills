# How to Change or Remove an Employee's Employment End or Resign Date

*This article explains how to change or remove an employee's employment end or resign date by restoring their profile first.*

You would like to change an employee's employment end or resign date or remove it from their profile. However, you could not find where to change it.


Firstly, you need to restore the employee's profile either on their profile page or from Settings > Users. Once restored, the initial employment end date will be removed, allowing you to change it to a new date or leave it blank.


---


1. To restore an employee's profile, please refer to [this article](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-restore-a-resigned-employees-account-status?hsLang=en).
2. Once the employment end or resign date has been removed, you may set a new date. Refer to [this article](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-set-an-employees-end-date?hsLang=en) for instructions.

**NOTE:**


**If you are unable to restore an employee's account or set an employment end date, it may be due to your User Group access permission settings.**


**Please reach out to your main account Admin to enable the necessary permissions for modifying employees' employment end dates.**
