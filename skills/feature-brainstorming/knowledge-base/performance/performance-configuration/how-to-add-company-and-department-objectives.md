# How to Add Company and Department Objectives

*Learn how to set up company and department objectives step-by-step in BrioHR*

Here’s how you can easily add a company or department objective in BrioHR:


1. Go to **HR Lounge > Performance > Objective**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-27-2025-03-46-57-9566-AM.png?width=1051&height=478&name=image-png-Mar-27-2025-03-46-57-9566-AM.png)
2. Click on **Add Objective** to start creating a new objective.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-27-2025-03-48-00-4967-AM.png?width=1117&height=487&name=image-png-Mar-27-2025-03-48-00-4967-AM.png)
3. Enter all the relevant details in the provided fields.
4. **Set the Objective Type:**


  - **For a Company Objective:**
Leave the **Department** field empty. This will create the objective at the company level.
  - **For a Department Objective:**
Select the specific department from the dropdown list in the **Department** field.
5. Once all the fields are completed, click on **Save** to create the objective.
