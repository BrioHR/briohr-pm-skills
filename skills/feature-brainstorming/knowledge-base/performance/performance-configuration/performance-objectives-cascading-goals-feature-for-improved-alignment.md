# Performance Objectives: Cascading Goals Feature for Improved Alignment

*This article explains the Contributing Goals feature that lets managers cascade objectives to their subordinates.*

We are thrilled to announce our latest feature, "**Enabling Contributing Goals**." This enhancement empowers managers and appraisers to establish direct connections between their objectives and those of their subordinates. This functionality proves particularly beneficial when subordinates play a pivotal role in achieving overarching goals.


**For example:**


The CEO aims to achieve a revenue target of $10 million, with the Sales Department pivotal in this endeavor. Both "Field Sales" and "Online Sales" have individual targets of $5 million each. The CEO can align their objective with the Head of Sales Department (direct report/appraisee only), allowing progress on the Head of Sales' objectives to contribute to the CEO's overarching goal.


**IMPORTANT NOTES:**


1. **If a subordinate resigns, the progress of their objective will still reflect in the objective progress of the manager/appraiser and contribute to their goals. You may remove the contributing goal if the resigned subordinate's progress should not affect the manager/appraiser.**
2. **If a manager/appraiser resigns, it won't affect the objectives of the subordinates.**
3. **Contributors do not see the progress from contributing goals.**
