# How to Close the Current Performance Cycle in BrioHR

*This article explains how to close the current performance cycle for all participating employees.*

There is a way to close the current ongoing performance cycle. Once this cycle is closed, it will be closed for all employees who are participating in the current cycle.


Login to your BrioHR account:


1.   Go to **HR Lounge**.


2.   Navigate to **Employee Performance**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48337202424/original/mLqPv6mVR7u5zYQMJYcMODA481D86Xyayg.png?1715922208)


You will be taken to the **Performance Dashboard**.


3.   Click on the **three dots (...)** next to the View Report button.


4.   Choose **Close Current Cycle** from the drop-down menu.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48337202475/original/oTy5Xzx_rAP6GYIJNbfpuBsHpeNIHl4LuA.png?1715922270)


A confirmation message will appear.


5.   Click **Yes **to continue.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48337202533/original/MfEmeoVmQouN5T_EFvoWp8oCHBa-ZopJlA.png?1715922316)


**NOTE:**


**Once the performance cycle has been closed:**


1. **Employees who haven't submitted their self-appraisal will no longer be able to do so.**
2. **Managers/Appraisers who haven't submitted their appraisals will no longer be able to do so.**
3. **Any appraisal forms that are not yet released to the employees will be automatically released, and employees will be able to see their final appraisal ratings and comments.**
4. **Employees who haven't acknowledged their released appraisal forms will no longer be able to do so.**
5. **HR cannot undo the process of closing the performance cycle.**
6. **HR can start opening a new performance cycle instead.**
