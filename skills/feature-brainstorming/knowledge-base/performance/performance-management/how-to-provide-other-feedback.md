# How to Provide "Other Feedback"

*Step-by-step guide to submitting 'Other Feedback' for employees to support performance appraisals.*

Providing feedback is an important way to support employee development and teamwork. Follow these steps:


1. Go to **People**.
2. Click on the employee to go to the employee's **Profile**
3. Click on **Give Feedback** button in the employee's Profile.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-27-2025-03-51-15-4734-AM.png?width=1063&height=418&name=image-png-Mar-27-2025-03-51-15-4734-AM.png)
4. Insert your feedback or comments.
5. Click **Add **to submit your feedback.


***Note:***
*The feedback is only visible to the employee’s Manager and HR. It will also be used as reference during their performance appraisal.*
