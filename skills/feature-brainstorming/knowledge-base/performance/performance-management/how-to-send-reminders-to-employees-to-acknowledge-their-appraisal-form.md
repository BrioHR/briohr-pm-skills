# How to Send Reminders to Employees to Acknowledge Their Appraisal Form

*Ensure a smooth performance review process by reminding employees to acknowledge their appraisal forms. Follow these simple steps in HR Lounge to send automated reminders and ensure timely completion.*

### Steps to Nudge Employees to Acknowledge Their Appraisal Form


To finalize the appraisal process, employees must acknowledge their appraisal forms. Follow these steps to send them a reminder:


#### 1. Access the Performance Module


- Navigate to **HR Lounge > Performance Module**
- Scroll down to locate the **Appraisal Overview** section to view each appraisal stage’s status, due date, and completion percentage.
- ![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-18-2024-02-54-09-1326-AM.png?width=688&height=375&name=image-png-Oct-18-2024-02-54-09-1326-AM.png)


#### 2. Open the Release Appraisal Section


- Click **See Details** under **Release Appraisal** to view pending acknowledgments.
- ![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-14-2024-06-42-38-2692-AM.png?width=688&height=281&name=image-png-Nov-14-2024-06-42-38-2692-AM.png)


#### 3. Send a Nudge Reminder


- In the **Acknowledge Appraisal** page, click **Nudge** next to the employees who have not yet acknowledged their appraisal form.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-27-2025-04-44-52-3477-AM.png?width=1069&height=685&name=image-png-Mar-27-2025-04-44-52-3477-AM.png)


#### 4. Confirm the Reminder


- Click **Nudge** again to send an automated email reminder to the selected employees.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-14-2024-06-48-28-7536-AM.png?width=688&height=581&name=image-png-Nov-14-2024-06-48-28-7536-AM.png)


#### 5. Employee Notification


- Employees will receive an email notification prompting them to acknowledge their appraisal form.
- ![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-14-2024-06-53-59-2268-AM.png?width=688&height=333&name=image-png-Nov-14-2024-06-53-59-2268-AM.png)


By following these steps, HR can ensure employees complete their appraisal acknowledgments on time, streamlining the performance review process.
