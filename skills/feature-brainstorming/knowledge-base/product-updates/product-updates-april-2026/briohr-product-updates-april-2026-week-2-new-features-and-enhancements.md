*Discover BrioHR’s April 2026 product updates, designed to improve your HR experience. Stay informed on the latest innovations from BrioHR.*

### Introduction


At BrioHR, we are committed to continuously enhancing the user experience. We listen to valuable feedback from our clients to deliver new features, improvements, and system enhancements.


This article provides a summary of all BrioHR product updates released on Week 2 of April 2026.

### Payroll Updates


### Enhanced Payslip Security: Password Protection


To help you further protect sensitive employee data, you can now enable password protection for payslips accessed via email. When activated, employees will be required to enter a password to open their payslip PDF.


**👉 [Learn how to enable password protection here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/enhance-payslip-security-with-password-protected-payslips?utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-_rMDN-FuD5g38nELUkS6-UebdzZkQDjFhalGNRofmHVmkAbqBCzYRnHmaajRO-GSgNceOo&hsLang=en)**


**What this means for you:**


- **Increased Privacy:** Adds a layer of security for sensitive compensation data.
- **Customizable Setup:** Choose between DOB or Identification Number as the password.
- **Easy Setup:** Admins can toggle this feature on or off under **Payroll Settings > General**.


### Enhanced Reporting for Form E (Malaysia)


We’ve upgraded our reporting capabilities to ensure even greater accuracy and ease of reconciliation for your yearly Form E submissions.


**👉 [Read the guide on Enhanced Form E Reporting here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/enhanced-reporting-for-better-accuracy-in-form-e?utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-_rMDN-FuD5g38nELUkS6-UebdzZkQDjFhalGNRofmHVmkAbqBCzYRnHmaajRO-GSgNceOo&hsLang=en)**


**Why This Enhancement Matters:**
This new reporting feature provides greater transparency and control over your Form E submission by:


- Reducing manual effort in validating employee counts
- Minimizing reporting errors
- Helping ensure compliance with LHDN requirements


With clearer visibility into how A3 and A4 figures are calculated, you can submit your Form E with greater confidence.


### Training Module Updates


### View Pending Approval Status


We’ve introduced an update to the Training Module to improve transparency for admins, managers, and employees. Users can now see the pending approval status for training requests they have submitted.


Simply hover on any 'Submitted' or 'Requested' status icon to view the pending approval details.


**What this means for you:**


- **Reduced Manual Inquiries:** Employees no longer need to contact HR to check if their request was received; they can see the status directly in their dashboard.
- **Clearer Visibility:** Provides a real-time view of where a training request sits in the approval workflow.
- **Improved User Experience:** A more intuitive interface for employees managing their professional development.
