### Time Attendance Updates


### Option to Exclude Unused Break Time from Total Work Hours


You can now enable '*exclude unused break from work hours'*  for a specific shift (Time Attendance module)


**Example:**
When this option is ticked for a shift, if an employee only takes a 30-minute break for a shift that allows for 1 hour break time, the system will not count the 30 minutes of unused break time as working time. If the employee clocks in at 9 am and clocks out at 6 pm, with a 30-minute break, the system will still count them as working an 8-hour shift (not 8 hours and 30 minutes) because the 30 minutes of unused break time is not counted as working time.


**👉Read about [how to set it up in your shift settings here](/knowledge/how-to-create-new-work-shifts?hsLang=en).**





### Active/Inactive Filter Added for Assign Schedule Page


Admins can now easily find employees using the added filter when assigning schedules to employees (in Admin Settings > Schedules and Shift).
