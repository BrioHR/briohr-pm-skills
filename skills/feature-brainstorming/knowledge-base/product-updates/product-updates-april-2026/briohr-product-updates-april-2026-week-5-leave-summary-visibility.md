### Leave Module Update


#### Take Control of Leave Summary Visibility


You can now choose to disable the '**Payslip Leave Summary**' which appears in the payslips generated.


By **default**, this feature will be **enabled**, allowing employees to conveniently view their leave balances directly within their payslips. However, we understand that different organisations have different preferences, so you now have the flexibility to turn off this feature if it does not align with your needs.
