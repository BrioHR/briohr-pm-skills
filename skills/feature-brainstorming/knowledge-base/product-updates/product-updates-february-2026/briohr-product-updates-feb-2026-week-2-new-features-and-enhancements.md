# BrioHR Product Updates - Feb 2026, Week 2 | New Features and Enhancements

*Discover BrioHR’s February 2026 product updates, including new features and system enhancements designed to improve your HR experience. Stay informed on the latest innovations from BrioHR.*

### Introduction


At BrioHR, we are committed to continuously enhancing the user experience. We listen to valuable feedback from our clients to deliver new features, improvements, and system enhancements.


This article provides a summary of all BrioHR product updates released on Week 2 of February 2026.


---


### Performance Module Enhancement Ad Hoc Cycle Reports


- [Create and Download Ad-Hoc Performance Cycle Reports for Better Performance Management](/knowledge/create-and-download-ad-hoc-performance-cycle-reports-for-better-performance-management?hsLang=en)


  - You can now download comprehensive reports for Ad Hoc Performance Cycles. Whether it’s a probation review, PIP, or a project-based review, you can now export performance data to track progress more effectively.
  - Are you still unfamiliar with ad hoc performance cycles?


Read this step-by-step guide to get started: [How to Create an Ad-Hoc Performance Appraisal Cycle in BrioHR](/knowledge/how-to-create-an-ad-hoc-performance-appraisal-cycle-in-briohr?hsLang=en)


### Payroll Updates


### Independent Overtime & Unpaid Leave Calculations


- [Latest Update: Income Basis Configuration Split for Overtime and Unpaid Leave](/knowledge/income-basis-configuration-split-for-overtime-and-unpaid-leave?hsLang=en)


  - You now have greater control over your payroll calculations! We’ve split the Income Basis configuration into two separate settings. This allows you to define calculations independently for:


- - - Overtime Calculations
    - Unpaid Leave Deductions


### Zakat (MY): Pay Percentage of PCB as Zakat


- [Setting Zakat as a Percentage (%) of PCB in BrioHR](/knowledge/setting-zakat-as-a-percentage-of-pcb?hsLang=en)


  - You can now choose to pay a percentage of their PCB as Zakat. This automation ensures that religious obligations are met conveniently while ensuring tax rebates are accurately reflected in their payroll, saving time for both HR and employees.
