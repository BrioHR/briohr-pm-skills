### Time Attendance New Feature


### Employee OT Pre-Approval Request


With this enhancement, managers can easily review and approve or reject OT requests in advance. Once approved, the OT will be automatically approved in the system when the actual overtime is performed — ensuring a smoother and more controlled OT management process.

Employees can:


- Submit OT pre-approval requests by duration or timeslot
- View a list of all submitted OT requests
- Receive email notifications when requests are approved or rejected
- See approved OT hours reflected in their schedule and timesheet


Managers can:


- Receive email notifications when an OT pre-approval request is submitted
- View requests in the Pending Action List
- Approve or reject requests directly from the system


This update is designed to streamline overtime management, improve visibility, and enhance approval workflows for both employees and managers.
