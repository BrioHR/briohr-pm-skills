### Import Employee Fixed Remunerations and Deductions in Bulk


Admin users can now efficiently **update fixed remunerations and deductions** for multiple employees at once **using the Bulk Import/Export** feature, ensuring streamlined data management and consistency for companies with high headcounts.

### Manage Multiple Subsidiaries with Ease


Switching between company accounts is now simpler than ever.

With the latest enhancement to the **brioHR Mobile App**, users with access to multiple subsidiary accounts can now switch between their assigned subsidiaries directly from the **Account** tab—without having to log out and log back in.

What's New?


- **Seamlessly switch** between your accessible subsidiary accounts.
- **Return to your main branch** anytime with a single tap.
- **Save time** by eliminating the need to repeatedly log in and out.
- Only subsidiaries you have permission to access will be displayed for a secure and streamlined experience.


Whether you're managing multiple companies or supporting employees across subsidiaries, this enhancement makes navigating between accounts faster and more convenient while you're on the go.


**📲Update your brioHR Mobile App to start using this feature today!**

### Expense Claims Module Update


### Gain Better Visibility into Employee Expense Claims


Keeping track of employee claim balances just got easier.
Introducing the **Expense Claims Balance Report**—a new report designed to give HR teams a complete view of employees' expense claim entitlements, helping you monitor claim utilisation and available balances with greater accuracy.


What's New?


- **View claim balances at a glance**See each employee's claim limit, amount used, and remaining balance in a single report.
- **Generate accurate balances**Calculate claim balances based on your selected effective date using **transaction dates** for more accurate reporting.
- **Filter the data you need**Generate reports by department, claim policy, and claim type to quickly find the information that matters.
- **Break down claim utilisation**
Analyse claim balances by Year, Month, or Transaction for better visibility.
- **Export with ease**
Download your reports in CSV, Excel, or PDF formats for auditing, reconciliation, or further analysis.

### Time Attendance Module Update


### Employee Overtime Approval (Post OT Request) available on Mobile App!


Employees can now conveniently **request overtime approval directly via the Time Attendance module in the brioHR mobile app, **using the new Post OT submission feature.
To ensure this feature works as intended, please note the following:


- First enable **Post OT submission** for your attendance policy.
- It is available for employees using **Daily Overtime Calculation** only.
- Requests can only be submitted for **actual overtime worked on past dates**, up to the number of hours recorded, and while the timesheet is still **Open**.


[👉Read more about the steps to enable this feature within the policy here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/enable-employee-to-submit-overtime-for-approval-as-hr-admin?utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-9xsaXaSFsqpt8zQwYnIRmf0KMTv5-K9lUXhTYUZmG_wMaLexEIrMme5pbN1DrinilTkuZV&hsLang=en)
