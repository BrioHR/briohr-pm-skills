### Additional Public Holiday in Selangor: Silver Jubilee Celebration of the Reign of DYMM Sultan Selangor on 25th October 2026


The Selangor State Government has declared **25 October 2026 (Sunday)** as an additional public holiday in conjunction with the **Silver Jubilee Celebration of the Reign of DYMM Sultan Selangor**.


As the holiday falls on a Sunday, a **replacement public holiday will be observed on 26 October 2026 (Monday)**.


In line with this announcement, our system has been updated to include both public holidays for **all offices located in Selangor**:


- 25 October 2026 – Silver Jubilee Celebration of the Reign of DYMM Sultan Selangor
- 26 October 2026 – Silver Jubilee Celebration of the Reign of DYMM Sultan Selangor Holiday


Read our article below:


- [Additional Public Holiday in Selangor: 25th October 2026 (Sultan of Selangor's Birthday Holiday)](/knowledge/additional-public-holiday-in-selangor-25th-october-2026-sultan-of-selangors-birthday-holiday?hsLang=en)


Want to remove this public holiday from your calendar?
Follow this [step-by-step guide](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/additional-public-holiday-in-selangor-25th-october-2025-silver-jubilee-celebration-of-the-reign-of-dymm-sultan-selangor?utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz--eS_PkhraBk5c_hcFvRvWMe3CDAjWo3TEmmVOC37KRUAIpUpvoLrQnoLr8EWRSMnfzlPAN&hsLang=en) to disable the public holiday in your Office settings.
