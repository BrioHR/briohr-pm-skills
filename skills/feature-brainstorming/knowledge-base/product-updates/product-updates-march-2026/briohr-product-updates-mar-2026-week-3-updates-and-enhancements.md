### Malaysia HR Update


### Additional Public Holiday for Hari Raya (2026)


Following the announcement of Malaysia's Prime Minister on Sunday, 15th March, our system has automatically added Friday, 20th March 2026, as an additional Hari Raya Aidilfitri holiday for all offices located in Malaysia. Aside from this National holiday, we have also added 2 state holidays for Kelantan on the 19th March 2026 and Johor on the 24th March 2026.


Thus, the following days will be available as public holidays in our system for Hari Raya Aidilfitri 2026:


**📌 Hari Raya Aidilfitri Additional Holiday, Thursday, 19 March 2026 - Kelantan**


**📌 Hari Raya Aidilfitri Additional Holiday, Friday, 20 March 2026 - National**


**📌 Hari Raya Aidilfitri, Saturday, 21 March 2026 - National**


**📌 Hari Raya Aidilfitri Holiday, Sunday, 22 March 2026 - National**


**📌 Hari Raya Aidilfitri Holiday, Monday, 23 March 2026 - National, except Johor**


**📌 Hari Raya Aidilfitri Holiday, Tuesday, 24 March 2026 - Johor**


**Please Note:**


- This public holiday is automatically applied for offices with the **Country** set as Malaysia.
- Read our article: [Additional Public Holiday in Malaysia: Hari Raya Aidilfitri Additional Holiday](/knowledge/additional-public-holiday-in-malaysia-20th-march-2026-hari-raya-aidilfitri-additional-holiday?hsLang=en)


**FAQ:**


**What if my employees have already applied for leave on the additional holidays?**


If an employee has already booked leave for the additional holiday, the system will not cancel the leave. To ensure their leave entitlement is credited back, please follow these steps:


- Employee Self-Service: Employees can cancel their own leave requests. Their leave balance will be automatically restored once the cancellation is processed.
- HR Admin Action: An HR Admin can cancel the leave on the employee's behalf to refresh their entitlement balance.


Want to remove this public holiday from your calendar?
Follow this [step-by-step guide](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/additional-public-holiday-in-selangor-25th-october-2025-silver-jubilee-celebration-of-the-reign-of-dymm-sultan-selangor?utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz--eS_PkhraBk5c_hcFvRvWMe3CDAjWo3TEmmVOC37KRUAIpUpvoLrQnoLr8EWRSMnfzlPAN&hsLang=en) to disable the public holiday in your Office settings.


### Time Attendance Enhancement


### TimeTec Device Duplicate Clock Protection


With **Duplicate Clock Protection** on your TimeTec device, you can prevent employees from tapping in or out repeatedly within a short time frame. Once enabled, the system blocks additional taps for a defined period (e.g. 5 minutes), ensuring cleaner and more reliable attendance data.


**Quick setup in 5 simple steps:**


1. Go to *System Settings *on your TimeTec Device
2. Select *Attendance*
3. Tap *Duplicate Punch Period*
4. Choose or define your preferred time range
5. Save your settings


Once activated, employees will see a notification if they attempt to clock in again too soon—helping reinforce proper usage while keeping your records accurate.


Read the article: [How Duplicate Clock Protection can be Enabled in your TimeTec device](/knowledge/how-duplicate-clock-protection-can-be-enabled-in-your-timetec-device?hsLang=en)
