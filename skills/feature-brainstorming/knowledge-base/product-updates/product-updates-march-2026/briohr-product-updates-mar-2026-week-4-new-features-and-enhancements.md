### Payroll Module


### Daily Rate Compensation Now Available


We’re excited to introduce a new enhancement to the Employee Profile that gives you greater flexibility in managing employee salaries — **Daily Rate Compensation** is now available in the Compensation tab.


With this new option, you can now easily set an employee’s salary based on a daily rate. Simply head to **Employee Profile > Compensation > Salary**, click on **+ Add Salary**, and select **Daily Rate** from the dropdown to get started.


Here’s what you need to know:


- The **effective date must be set to the first of the month**
- During payroll, **basic pay will default to 0**
- Payroll processors will need to **add the number of working days** to calculate the total salary


Read our article for the guide: [Setting up Daily Rate Compensation in Employees Profile](/knowledge/daily-rate-compensation-in-employee-profile?hsLang=en)
