# How to Answer the Pulse Survey as a Respondent on BrioHR

*This article explains how employees answer a Pulse Survey from the email invitation or Pending Actions.*

You will receive an email titled '**Share your mood and thoughts*****'***.


To answer the survey, click on '**Answer survey**.' You will be asked to log in to BrioHR.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48301953391/original/kiTk_Tm3G1pFUN0I3WrxLgmEZsOhZ74sWw.png?1687242109)


Log in to BrioHR:


- Click on **Pending Actions.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48301953533/original/FJ10PUmtAEwx-X6yKn2jcKcTyqWZq2tDNg.png?1687242197)


- Search for the '**Pulse**' task and click on it.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48301953584/original/dY18KCExHhdaBP6L6e9lfxhxazbaTTg7ow.png?1687242229)


- You will be directed to another **survey response browser**. You will need to:


  - **Rate your satisfaction** by choosing the available emoticons.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48301953656/original/srLu700LDCKr7aT8peQe7K4CndJhSkUV1A.png?1687242271)


- - **Provide an explanation** for your rating in the text field.
- Once finished, click on the "**Submit**" button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48301953737/original/TxBTnUHIY28CL17DgJQME_V_0j7ZdsUegQ.png?1687242330)


After your response has been recorded, you will receive an acknowledgment on your survey page.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48302088197/original/JIXJWB9YoMjUxgQDYi7K6KpTjwwS1Pz5og.png?1687312680)
