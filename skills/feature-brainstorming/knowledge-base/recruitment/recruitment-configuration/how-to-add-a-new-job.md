# How to add a new job

*Step-by-Step Guide to Adding a New Job in BrioHR Recruitment Module.*

#### Here are the steps to create a job position in the Recruitment module;


1. As an HR admin, go to the Recruitment module via HR Lounge


2. In the Dashboard page, you will see [**Add New Job**]. Click into the [Add New Job] to start creating a job


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-22-2024-09-22-12-5811-AM.png?width=688&height=446&name=image-png-Nov-22-2024-09-22-12-5811-AM.png)


3. Fill in the below details;


- Job Title
- Description - *Reach out to Chat Support Team to enable Job Description AI function*
- Hiring managers
- Recruiter
- Department
- Country
- Specialization
- Job grade
- Interview process - [Choose from the processes you created under the module settings](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-set-up-and-customize-your-interview-process?hsLang=en)
- Pre-Assessment Form - [Choose from the forms you created under the module settings](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-create-a-pre-assessment-question-and-interview-scorecard?hsLang=en)
- Years of work experience - *Maximum & Minimum years *


4. Determine if you want to make the job position visible on the career page by toggling on [**Publish on career page?**]

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-22-2024-10-04-28-0882-AM.png?width=654&height=760&name=image-png-Nov-22-2024-10-04-28-0882-AM.png)



5. Hit [**Submit**] button to save the job
