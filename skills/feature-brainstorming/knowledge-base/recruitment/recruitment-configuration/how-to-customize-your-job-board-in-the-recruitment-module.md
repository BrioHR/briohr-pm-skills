# How to Customize Your Job Board in the Recruitment Module

*This article explains how to customize your job board's theme colors and cover photo in the Recruitment module.*

You can customize the Job Board in the Recruitment Module by selecting theme colors (for buttons and links) or by adding a cover photo. Follow the steps below to customize your Job Board:


- Navigate to the Recruitment Module: In the **HR Lounge**, click on "**Recruitment**."


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48232314348/original/7D0duJQ49QAAyVeyebjqKOs9y4y4Njw4CA.png?1655199888)


- Access the Job Board Settings: Click on the "**Settings**" dashboard. Then, in the **Job Board** section:


**A.  **Select Job Board Theme Color: Click on the dropdown menu under "Theme Color" and **choose your preferred color for buttons and links**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48232316205/original/DN5mPmruMoRJdew1gm_eG4SRIGESWJsWtw.png?1655200219)


**B.  Upload a Cover Photo**: Click on the "**Choose File**" icon, then select the photo you wish to upload.


**NOTE:**


**The recommended cover photo size is 1440 x 350 pixels.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48232323976/original/oO32ZMkkb0etlpM2x0Pnyn6azRu7zhi8Rg.png?1655201798)


**C.  Replace or Remove the Cover Photo**: To replace or delete the current cover photo, click on the "**Remove**" icon.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48232323615/original/c9xvggAGfBYua9lnXGGV8kBlRN6hc1wH8Q.png?1655201711)


For more information on adding or editing company information, click [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-add-or-edit-company-information?hsLang=en).
