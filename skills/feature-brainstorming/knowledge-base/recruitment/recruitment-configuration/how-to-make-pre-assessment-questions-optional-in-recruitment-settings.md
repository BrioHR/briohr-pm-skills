# How to Make Pre-Assessment Questions Optional in Recruitment Settings

*This article explains how to make pre-assessment questions optional in the Recruitment settings.*

1.   Navigate to HR Lounge > Recruitment.


2.   Click on the Settings button (located next to the Dashboard).


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48087107476/original/cLmo9-BLg3s7U9LndCg-hIAoxPDUcfzdaw.png?1613377735)


3.   In the Pre-Assessment Form section, hover over the **pencil **icon and click on it to edit the settings.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48087107578/original/pm8iM8e92-dPeqkDDOHCt4MM2G3AIwOiNA.png?1613377777)


4.   To make a question optional for candidates, switch off the **Mandatory**? button. If desired, you can turn off all mandatory buttons, allowing candidates to submit only their CV, first name, last name, and email address. Once your changes are complete, click on the **Update Form** button.
