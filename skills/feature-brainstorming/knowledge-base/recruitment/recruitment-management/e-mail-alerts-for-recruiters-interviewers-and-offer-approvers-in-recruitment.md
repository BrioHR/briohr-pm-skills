# E-mail Alerts for Recruiters, Interviewers, and Offer Approvers in Recruitment

*This article explains the in-app and email alerts sent to recruiters, interviewers, and offer approvers during recruitment.*

The system sends notifications via in-app and e-mail to:


1. Interviewers – For interview scheduling.
2. Interviewers – For interview cancellations.
3. Recruiters/Specific Users – To assign a user for candidate screening.
4. Specific Users – For internal offer approval requests.


### Part 1: Notifications To Interviewers – Interview Scheduling


At this stage, e-mail notifications **DO NOT** go to the candidates. These notifications are intended to **inform the interviewer** that an interview has been scheduled, prompting them to access the system to use the **Interview Scorecard** for providing post-interview feedback.


The e-mail notification includes a button to add the event to the calendar or download it as an ICS file, allowing export to other digital calendars (such as Microsoft Outlook, Apple Calendar, etc.).


**NOTE:**


**The event calendar DOES NOT include a video call link. Interviewers must create a separate video call link.**

### Part 2: Notifications To Interviewers – Interview Cancellations


When a scheduled interview is canceled, the **interviewer will receive an e-mail notification** informing them of the cancellation.


If the interviewer has already added the event to their calendar, they **must manually remove** it, as the system will not automatically delete the event upon interview cancellation.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48146452942/original/bmWNhSVx79pZuCHKkOUoEHZFls4_pax-cw.png?1633492149)


### Part 3: Notifications To Recruiters/Specific Users – Assigning A User For Candidate Screening


Recruiters or specific users will receive an e-mail notification if they are assigned to screen a candidate. After completing the **screening**, the user must log in to BrioHR to input their **feedback**.


The e-mail notification includes a button to add the event to the calendar or download it as an ICS file, allowing export to other digital calendars (such as Microsoft Outlook, Apple Calendar, etc.).


**NOTE:**


**The event calendar DOES NOT include a video call link. The user must create a separate video call link.**

### Part 4: Notifications To Specific Users – Internal Offer Approval Requests


Using the "**Generate Offer**" button, a request can be made via the system to obtain internal approval before extending an offer to a candidate.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48146456568/original/NREdvW8qju5ilAU4iKAlcn2F2rNvsaLf7A.png?1633495447)


The approver will receive an e-mail notification prompting them to log in to BrioHR to process the approval request.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48146456593/original/S8hisX3gEOTryPFEor_rCPC-sWWgeKOaRg.png?1633495464)


The approver can then approve or reject the offer letter.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48146456737/original/GLBSsKXTNqkzoWHd4PwllEfV7ubLvz5iDg.png?1633495573)


Upon approval, the candidate's application progress page will display the "**Offer**" status.
