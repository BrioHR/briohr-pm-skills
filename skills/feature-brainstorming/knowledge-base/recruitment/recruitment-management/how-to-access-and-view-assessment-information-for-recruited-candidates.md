# How to Access and View Assessment Information for Recruited Candidates

*This article explains how to access a recruited candidate's pre-assessment, scorecard, and assessment information.*

1.   Go to **HR Lounge > Recruitment**.


2.   On the dashboard, click on **All Candidates**.


3.   In the **Status **field, select **Recruited **from the dropdown menu.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48043836690/original/iX7kcOJUonRhnSLswsGJ0qWceV86lH5TRw.png?1591777766)


4.   You can view the recruited candidate's Pre-Assessment, Scorecard, and Resume in the **Assessment **column by clicking the corresponding button.
