# How to Add a Candidate as 'Favorite' or 'Mark as Viewed'

*This article explains how to mark a candidate as Favorite or Mark as Viewed from the All Candidates dashboard.*

### Method 1: HR Admin (HR Lounge > All Candidates)


1.   In **HR Lounge**, click on **Recruitment**.


2.   On the Recruitment **Dashboard**, click on **All Candidates**.


3.   Hover your mouse over the candidate's name to reveal options, then select "**Add to Favorites**" or "**Mark as Viewed**".


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48227902701/original/0kieT0qml9L_VwHeIR4tMCPxMolcBGPYdg.png?1653383803)


4.   The icons will be highlighted as shown in the sample screenshot below.


To deselect, simply click the icons again.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48227905122/original/LcWoyqwApZeFswdTYZsAnj_5JqNe0_OGgA.png?1653384136)


**NOTE:**


**When a candidate is marked as "Favorite" or "Viewed" from HR Lounge > Recruitment module, this information is visible to all users with access to the HR Lounge > Recruitment module.**


### Method 2: HR Admin (HR Lounge > Candidate Profile)


1.   In **HR Loung**e, click on **Recruitment**.


2.   On the Recruitment **Dashboard**, click on **All Candidates**.


3.   Click the "..." icon next to the candidate's name, then select "**See Candidate Profile**".


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48227913604/original/0NZhdge5TWkV8Jv2l36vTc2sjpKaALhWFA.png?1653385658)


4.   Click the icons to "**Add to Favorites**" or "**Mark as Viewed**" as needed.


To deselect, simply click the icons again.


![J3VpT6tMaBB6Nfcbt3frV10cwOe7MiDi1Q.png?1653387762](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/J3VpT6tMaBB6Nfcbt3frV10cwOe7MiDi1Q.png%3F1653387762.png?width=394&height=379&name=J3VpT6tMaBB6Nfcbt3frV10cwOe7MiDi1Q.png%3F1653387762.png)


5.   The information will then be reflected in the **Dashboard > All Candidates** list as well.


To deselect, simply click the icons again.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48227918009/original/0sI-eDc-5E7HjcniiVy13kJ584H8VcjGgA.png?1653386391)


**NOTE:**


**When a candidate is marked as "Favorite" or "Viewed" from HR Lounge > Recruitment module, this information is visible to all users with access to the HR Lounge > Recruitment module.**


### Method 3: Manager (Team > All Candidates)


1.   In **Team**, click on **Recruitment**.


2.   Click on **All Candidates**.


3.   Hover your mouse over the candidate's name to reveal options, then select "**Add to Favorites**" or "**Mark as Viewed**".


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48227921557/original/ooppjbccgpT9D2aiYHu9ww-1YCc_uqrU1A.png?1653386970)


4.  The icons will be highlighted as shown in the sample screenshot below.


To deselect, simply click the icons again.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48227922436/original/_7GVh2uh9W2gr6nIbkO21ohO_ScNjacESA.png?1653387152)


**NOTE:**


**When a candidate is marked as "Favorite" or "Viewed" from Team > Recruitment module, this information is only visible to the Manager in Team. It is not visible in HR Lounge to any other users.**


### Method 4: Manager (Team > Candidate Profile)


1.   In **Team**, click on **Recruitment**.


2.   On the Recruitment **Dashboard**, click on **All Candidates**.


3.   Click the "..." icon next to the candidate's name, then select "**See Candidate Profile**".

4.   Click the icons to "**Add to Favorites**" or "**Mark as Viewed**" as needed.


To deselect, simply click the icons again.
