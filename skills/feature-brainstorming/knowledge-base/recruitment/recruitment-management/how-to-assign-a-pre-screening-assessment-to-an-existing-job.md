# How to Assign a Pre-screening Assessment to an Existing Job

*This article explains how to assign a pre-screening assessment to an existing job that was created without one.*

**(For HR/ Recruiter only)**


Assigning a pre-screening assessment to a job that was previously created without one is a straightforward process. Follow these steps:


Navigate to **HR Lounge > Recruitment.**


1. Click on **Open Jobs**.
2. Locate the job you wish to update and click the
![RDgjta6fhONesVOd4oz9jz8pDEBH717eFA.png?1591171548](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/RDgjta6fhONesVOd4oz9jz8pDEBH717eFA.png%3F1591171548.png?width=21&height=18&name=RDgjta6fhONesVOd4oz9jz8pDEBH717eFA.png%3F1591171548.png)
 icon at the end of the **job title**. Then, select **Edit Details**.
3. In the pop-up window, scroll down to find the **Pre-Assessment Form** section.
4. From the dropdown menu, select the **assessment **you want to assign to this job.
5. Finally, click the
