# How to generate Recruitment Report?

*Step-by-Step Guide to Generating Recruitment Reports in BrioHR.*

To download the Recruitment Report for all the applicants, follow these steps:


1. Go to **Analytics** from the navigation bar, click on **Report Builder**

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-04-2026-03-45-07-5261-AM.png?width=1637&height=843&name=image-png-May-04-2026-03-45-07-5261-AM.png)
2. Look for **Recruitment**, click **Create**

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-22-2024-02-46-17-4055-AM.png?width=670&height=339&name=image-png-Nov-22-2024-02-46-17-4055-AM.png)
3. Configure your report settings. Choose the type of Jobs** **of the Recruitment report you want to generate and the **application period **of the candidates
4. Check out the **Fields & Filters**, it can help you to include or exclude the information (eg. Candidates' details, hiring details, pre-assessment forms, etc.) in the report

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-22-2024-02-48-53-5725-AM.png?width=670&height=232&name=image-png-Nov-22-2024-02-48-53-5725-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-22-2024-02-59-32-8367-AM.png?width=670&height=623&name=image-png-Nov-22-2024-02-59-32-8367-AM.png)
5. Once completed your selections, click **Apply changes**

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-22-2024-09-12-16-8280-AM.png?width=670&height=493&name=image-png-Nov-22-2024-09-12-16-8280-AM.png)
6. Export the report. After configuring the settings, click on the** Export Report** icon to download the report.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-22-2024-09-13-21-6868-AM.png?width=670&height=157&name=image-png-Nov-22-2024-09-13-21-6868-AM.png)
7. The file will be downloaded and ready for viewing.
