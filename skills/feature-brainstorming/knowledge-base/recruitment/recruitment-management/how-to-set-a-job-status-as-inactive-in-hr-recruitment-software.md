# How to Set a Job Status as Inactive in HR Recruitment Software

*This article explains how to change a job status from Active to Inactive in the Recruitment module.*

**NOTE:**


**By default, all job statuses are set to "Active."**


Follow these steps to change a job status to "**Inactive**":


- Access Recruitment: Go to **HR Lounge** and click on **Recruitment**.


![oJ1n1h6QgQhjzSwVhtSfq77X4L7kUxM2bQ.png?1656396012](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/oJ1n1h6QgQhjzSwVhtSfq77X4L7kUxM2bQ.png%3F1656396012.png?width=296&height=518&name=oJ1n1h6QgQhjzSwVhtSfq77X4L7kUxM2bQ.png%3F1656396012.png)


- Locate the Candidate: Navigate to **Dashboard > All Candidates. **Search for the employee's name and click on the three dots ("...") to select **See candidate profile.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48235217813/original/fqf-I_Pj5sSiqY8jBGaLdYeY4bCFdGe2UQ.png?1656409726)


- Change Job Status: In the **JOBS APPLIED** column, click on the green dot to switch the job status from "**Active**" to "**Inactive**."


![-N299mofzd7eyE3Hla1CB3DG8XdCPPCynA.png?1656409919](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/-N299mofzd7eyE3Hla1CB3DG8XdCPPCynA.png%3F1656409919.png?width=506&height=526&name=-N299mofzd7eyE3Hla1CB3DG8XdCPPCynA.png%3F1656409919.png)


- Confirm Status Change: Once the job is set to "**Inactive**," the green dot will turn into a red "**x**" icon, indicating the job is now inactive.


**NOTE:**


**This action can be undone by clicking the "x" icon again, changing the status back to "Active."**


![tP7dYGpPklb8d92b3Vah14h2fgfbEsQFIA.png?1656410063](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/tP7dYGpPklb8d92b3Vah14h2fgfbEsQFIA.png%3F1656410063.png?width=382&height=545&name=tP7dYGpPklb8d92b3Vah14h2fgfbEsQFIA.png%3F1656410063.png)


- Visibility of Inactive Jobs: Jobs marked as "**Inactive**" will no longer appear in the **APPLIED **column under **Dashboard > All Candidates**.


**NOTE:**


**Only jobs with an "Active" status will be visible in the APPLIED column under Dashboard > All Candidates.**


- Example A: When the "**HR Executive**" job status is set to "**Active**," both jobs are visible.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48235222526/original/lcPdQQh4FA_WtWpEENftu_1D01oMLBWxOA.png?1656410631)


- Example B: When the "**HR Executive**" job status is set to "**Inactive**," the job is no longer visible.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48235223137/original/wyHOSgf5l6tffqAwpvZSGt-3UiD90rL3vA.png?1656410753)


- Impact on Open Jobs: If a job is marked as "**Inactive**" in the Candidate's Profile, it won't appear when accessing it from **Dashboard > Open Jobs** **UNLESS** the **Display inactive candidate(s)** advanced filter is enabled.


a.   Before applying the "**Display inactive candidate(s)**" filter: The candidate is not visible.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48235226616/original/5zpbkfeLod7hlKRcrnqr8UvBSoNUvl2KJw.png?1656411434)


b.   After applying the "**Display inactive candidate(s)**" filter: The candidate becomes visible.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48235225766/original/-Z8c9AooNbD64Xj8JXkd_7_114-2TCe4Ww.png?1656411274)


- Including Job Status in Reports: In the **Recruitment Report**, accessible via **HR Lounge > Reports**, you can include the "**Active/Inactive**" status by following these steps:


1. Go to **HR Lounge > Reports > Recruitment Report.**
2. Select **Jobs & Application Date.**
3. In the **Fields & Filters** tool, click on the **Candidate Information** category.
4. Under the **Active **filter, click **Add filter**.
5. Choose the values: **Yes/No.**
6. Click **Apply changes** to include the filter before exporting your report.
