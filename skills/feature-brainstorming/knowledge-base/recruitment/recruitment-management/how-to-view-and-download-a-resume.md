# How to View and Download a Resume

*This guide explains how to view and download a candidate's resume from the BrioHR Recruitment module. Follow these simple steps to access and save resumes for recruitment purposes.*

1. Navigate to ** HR Lounge > Recruitment**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-05-2024-09-18-03-7524-AM.png?width=1375&height=1147&name=image-png-Dec-05-2024-09-18-03-7524-AM.png)
2. Click on** 'All Candidates.' **to access the list of applicants.
3. Click on** the Candidate’s Name **whose resume you wish to view and click on it. This will redirect you to their detailed profile.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-05-2024-09-19-42-1780-AM.png?width=1354&height=1111&name=image-png-Dec-05-2024-09-19-42-1780-AM.png)
4. Once in the candidate's profile, navigate to the **Resume** tab where the resume is displayed.
5. To download the resume, click the **Download** button. The file will save to your designated folder.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-05-2024-09-21-50-5734-AM.png?width=1365&height=984&name=image-png-Dec-05-2024-09-21-50-5734-AM.png)
