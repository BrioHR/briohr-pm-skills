# How to View Candidate Profile

*Learn how to access and view detailed candidate profiles in BrioHR's Recruitment module. This guide provides simple steps for navigating to a candidate's profile and reviewing their information effectively.*

### Steps to View a Candidate Profile


1. Go to** HR Lounge** >** Recruitment.**
2. Click on** 'All Candidates.'**
3. Click on the **Candidate’s Name.**
Locate the candidate whose profile you want to view and click on their name. You will be redirected to their detailed profile page.
