# How to View Job Application Dates

*This article explains how HR Admins and Managers can view candidates' job application dates in the Recruitment module.*

**NOTE:**


**This guide is for HR Admins with access to the Recruitment module from the HR Lounge dashboard.**


**Managers can use the same instructions to view candidate job application dates by accessing the Recruitment module from the Team dashboard.**


**1. Accessing the Recruitment Module**


- In the **HR Lounge**, click on **Recruitment**.


**2. Viewing Job Application Dates**


- HR Admins can view job application dates from the following sections:

  - **Open Jobs**
  - **All Candidates**
  - **Candidate Profile**


### A. Viewing Application Dates From "Open Jobs"


- Go to the **Recruitment Dashboard** and select **Open Jobs**.


![0pQze4OaloahuBqOUbDBFrnCLbs_ngVxxw.png?1654138775](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/0pQze4OaloahuBqOUbDBFrnCLbs_ngVxxw.png%3F1654138775.png?width=688&height=216&name=0pQze4OaloahuBqOUbDBFrnCLbs_ngVxxw.png%3F1654138775.png)


- Click on the job title to open the job details.
- Scroll down to the **Candidates **section to view the application dates in the **APPLIED DATE** column.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48229876839/original/SPGqS8tWEV7HacGGF3dAfGr1wvSRbgSuZw.png?1654138979)


### B. Viewing Application Dates From "All Candidates"


- Navigate to the **Recruitment Dashboard** and click on **All Candidates**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48229877239/original/KBoQ1j-NQslcQNa286Q4tJBsC2mCSWU1RQ.png?1654139395)


- In the **JOBS **column, you can see the application dates for candidates across various job titles.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48229877804/original/SdLHQyMVbcVezAcKWr798MS8Ko3K_73xcg.png?1654139759)


### C. Viewing Application Dates From The "Candidate Profile"


- On the **Recruitment Dashboard**, click on **All Candidates**.
- Click on the "..." icon next to the candidate's name and select **See candidate profile**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48229898258/original/SvuU1wHeMdGoIfU8v3pZ6M3BCUuRbhjSVg.png?1654151424)


- The application dates will be displayed in the **JOBS APPLIED** column on the candidate's profile.
