# Enhanced HR Statistics: New Filters, Graphs, and Insights

*This article explains the enhanced HR Statistics page with new filters, graphs, and insights for data analysis.*

We are pleased to announce updates to our **Statistics** page, bringing new features and enhanced functionality to improve your data analysis experience.


**Key Enhancements:**


- **Filter Functionality**: Filter data by department, office, job grade, and employment type to customize graph views based on your selections.
- **New Metrics Added**:
a. Total number of active employees
b. Average workforce age
c. Average years of service
- **Comprehensive Graphs**: Visual insights for Gender, Nationality, Age Group, Service Tenure, Department, Office, Office Country Location, Job Grade, Job Title, and Employment Type.





---


**How to Access the Statistics page:**


1. Go to **HR Lounge** and select **Reports**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-18-2024-07-05-44-6591-AM.png?width=688&height=238&name=image-png-Dec-18-2024-07-05-44-6591-AM.png)





2. Navigate to **Statistics** > **General**


     a. Use **filters** to refine the displayed data.


     b. Review metrics, such as **total employees**, **average workforce age**, and **average years of service**.


     c. Explore detailed **graphs** for key organizational and demographic insights.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-18-2024-07-23-46-9762-AM.png?width=688&height=250&name=image-png-Dec-18-2024-07-23-46-9762-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-18-2024-07-22-49-2275-AM.png?width=688&height=287&name=image-png-Dec-18-2024-07-22-49-2275-AM.png)








**Note:**





**The total number of employees displayed reflects only those who are actively employed as of the date you access the Statistics Page.**

**For instance:**
**1. Employees added to the system with a join date after the current date will not be included.
**
**2. Similarly, employees whose employment end date is prior to the date you are viewing the Statistics Page will also be excluded from the data.**
