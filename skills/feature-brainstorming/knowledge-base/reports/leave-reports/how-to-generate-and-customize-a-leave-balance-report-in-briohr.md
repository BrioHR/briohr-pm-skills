# How to Generate and Customize a Leave Balance Report in BrioHR

*Learn how HR Admins can easily generate, customize, and export employee leave balance reports in BrioHR using the Analytics Report Builder.*

### Introduction


HR Admins can easily view and analyze employees’ leave balance records by generating a **Leave Balance Report** from the **Reports** module.


You may choose to generate a report for a **single leave type** (e.g., Annual Leave) or for **multiple leave types** (e.g., Annual and Medical Leave).


This report can be accessed from the **Analytics** menu in BrioHR.


---


### Step-by-Step: Download Leave Balance Report


#### Step 1 - Log In to BrioHR Account


Log in to your BrioHR account using your registered credentials.


#### Step 2 - Access to the Analytics Report Builder Page


1. Go to **Analytics** > **Report Builder.**


2. Search for **Leave Balances.**


3. Click **Create **to start generating the report.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-13-2025-07-11-37-0426-AM.png?width=670&height=279&name=image-png-Nov-13-2025-07-11-37-0426-AM.png)


---


#### Step 3 - Generate a Leave Balance Report


1. Select the desired **Year** and** Date **range.


2. Enable or disable** Rounding Rules.**


**TIP:** Disable if you want to show the exact amounts without rounding.


3. Choose one or multiple Leave Types (e.g., Medical, Annual, Emergency Leave).


4. At the Generated Report, click** Expand to Fullscreen **to preview before downloading.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-13-2025-07-22-22-3481-AM.png?width=670&height=276&name=image-png-Nov-13-2025-07-22-22-3481-AM.png)


---


#### Step 4 - To Customize the Report Format via Fields & Filters Function


Use **Fields & Filters** to display only the relevant information.


1. Click **Fields & Filters. **


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-13-2025-07-23-41-6380-AM.png?width=670&height=240&name=image-png-Nov-13-2025-07-23-41-6380-AM.png)
**


2. Expand any category (e.g, Personal Details)


3. Use the following actions:


- **Clear** – Unselect all fields to omit them from the report.
- **Select All** – Include all available fields.
- **Manually Select/Unselect Checkboxes** – Include or exclude specific data fields.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-13-2025-07-24-41-1120-AM.png?width=385&height=536&name=image-png-Nov-13-2025-07-24-41-1120-AM.png)


4. To apply filters:


- Click **+Add filter**.
- Choose the desired filter criteria.


5. Click **Apply Changes **to save.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-13-2025-07-26-04-7774-AM.png?width=375&height=561&name=image-png-Nov-13-2025-07-26-04-7774-AM.png)


The system will automatically refresh the report to show only the selected fields and filtered data.


---


#### Step 5 - Save the Report Format For Future Use


Save your customized report layout for future use.


1. Click **Save As.**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-13-2025-07-34-30-3148-AM.png?width=2000&height=749&name=image-png-Nov-13-2025-07-34-30-3148-AM.png)
**


2. Enter a name for your report format.


3. Click **Save.**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-13-2025-07-34-38-4799-AM.png?width=2000&height=625&name=image-png-Nov-13-2025-07-34-38-4799-AM.png)
**


Next time, simply select your saved report format from the drop-down list to generate a report with the same layout

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-13-2025-07-36-13-0301-AM.png?width=2000&height=645&name=image-png-Nov-13-2025-07-36-13-0301-AM.png)

#### Step 6 - Export the Leave Balance Report


When your report is ready:


1. Click **Export Report**.


2. Choose your preferred format:


- **Export as CSV - **To download the report in CSV format.
- **Export as Excel - **To export the report in Excel format.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**1. Can non-HR users generate leave reports?**
No. Only users with HR Admin permissions can generate leave balance reports.


**2. What does “Disable Rounding Rules” mean?**
It allows you to show the exact leave balance without applying rounding adjustments.


**3. Can I include custom fields in the report?**
Yes. You can add or remove data fields using the **Fields & Filters** section.


**4. Will deleting a saved report format affect my data?**
No. It only deletes the layout configuration, not your leave records.


**5. Which export formats are supported?**
Reports can be exported as **CSV** or **Excel (.xlsx)** files.
