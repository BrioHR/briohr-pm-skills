# Company Payroll Report & Payroll Variance Report: How to View the Total Amount

*This article explains how to view the total amount in the Company Payroll Report and Payroll Variance Report.*

### A. Accessing The Reports


1.   Go to **HR Lounge** and click on "**Reports**."


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246808394/original/Iq7hzAdexmA5kUYw22CvYwK8OcUv-7BFFg.png?1661242324)


2.   Click on "**Report Builder**" and select:


a. **Company Payroll Report > Create** (to view the Company Payroll Report)


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246809609/original/pRiL6wCM9ZWc9rDSNL5Tgg8TzVHs3sYtBA.png?1661242587)


b.  **Company Payroll Variance Report > Create** (to view the Company Payroll Variance Report)


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246810046/original/kxbvi2fOvNzNALnWzFLVPPKE-5ZLvoNr1Q.png?1661242691)


### B. Viewing The "Total Amount"


**NOTE:**


**The following steps apply to both the Company Payroll Report and the Company Payroll Variance Report. By default, employees are listed according to their Employee ID in the generated reports.**


1. Select the **Payroll Center.**
2. Select the **Mode**.
3. Select the **Granularity > "Show Employees only**."
4. To download the report, click on the "**Export Report**" button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246822199/original/SPWGqmdvw_ZBXnXZviTUEvysyu5xzZPRVA.png?1661245115)


**The "Total" amount will be displayed at the bottom of the table/exported report, as shown in the sample screenshots below.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246816721/original/XtNlgaOcwau8DVl2cP2ObzMulsKYNhJC9g.png?1661244148)


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246816797/original/xALZAN3DTslCNrk6LePAqXg8XN6xh9w4uQ.png?1661244166)


### C. Viewing The "Total Amount (By Department)"


**NOTE:**


**The following steps apply to both the Company Payroll Report and the Company Payroll Variance Report.**


1. Select the **Payroll Center**.
2. Select the **Mode**.
3. Select the **Granularity > "Show Employees and Departments only**."
4. To download the report, click on the "**Export Report**" button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246821903/original/lbffMd_YwttiLgOLmaApVQwjLIDwBJTFUw.png?1661245052)


**The "Total (By Department)" amount will be categorized according to individual departments and shown at the bottom of each respective department, as displayed in the sample screenshots below.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246823538/original/mDb5hWdnlxUn4uq9mgA7LZNOCkMlesuIRg.png?1661245415)


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246824455/original/qmoDpE9arrPTonJIHfVbmaWk9av1BBaCMQ.png?1661245722)


### D.  Viewing The "Grand Total Amount"


**NOTE:**


**The following steps apply to both the Company Payroll Report and the Company Payroll Variance Report.**


1. Select the **Payroll Center**.
2. Select the **Mode**.
3. Select the **Granularity > "Show Employees and Departments only.**"
4. To download the report, click on the "**Export Report**" button.

**The "Grand Total" amount will be shown at the bottom of the table/exported report, as illustrated in the sample screenshots below.**
