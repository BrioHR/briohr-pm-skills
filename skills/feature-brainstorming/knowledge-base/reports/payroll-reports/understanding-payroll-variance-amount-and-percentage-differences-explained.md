# Understanding Payroll Variance: Amount and Percentage Differences Explained

*This article explains how to read the amount and percentage differences in the Company Payroll Variance Report.*

### A.  Accessing The Reports


- In the** HR Lounge**, click on **Reports**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246808394/original/Iq7hzAdexmA5kUYw22CvYwK8OcUv-7BFFg.png?1661242324)


- Click on **Report builder > Company Payroll Variance Report > Create**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246810046/original/kxbvi2fOvNzNALnWzFLVPPKE-5ZLvoNr1Q.png?1661242691)


### B.  Viewing The Amount & % Difference


- Select the **Country (only one country can be selected at a time).**
- Select the **Payroll Center (you can select multiple payroll centers within the same country).**
- Select the **Mode: **(Select the two months you wish to compare values)
- Select the **Granularity: "Show Employees Only**" or "**Show Employees and Departments Only**".
- To download the report, click the "**Export Report**" button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48250050617/original/I-JACgZeBXrR5KdEyqPOdjONLJsxhfTkng.png?1662709460)


After applying the filters, the table will display the following **amount and percentage differences**:


- **Basic Salary**
- **Gross Deductions & Additions**
- **Tax Relief**
- **Gross Earnings**
- **Employee and Employer Contributions**
- **Total Employee and Employer Deductions**
- **Net Deductions and Additions**
- **Other Taxable Items**
- **Net Pay**


Amounts that have decreased will be displayed in **red**, while amounts that have increased will be displayed in **green**.
