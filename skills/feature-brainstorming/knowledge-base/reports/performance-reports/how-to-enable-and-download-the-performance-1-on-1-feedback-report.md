# How to Enable and Download the Performance 1-on-1 Feedback Report

*This article explains how to enable permissions and download the Performance 1-on-1 Feedback Report.*

### Enabling User Group Permission Settings


To view and download the report, the HR/Admin must first enable access under "**Settings**" by following these steps:


- Go to **Settings **and click on **User Groups**.
- Find the relevant user group and click **Edit**.
- Under the "**HR Modules Permission" > "Reports Module**" category, check the "**Allow access to performance one-on-one report**" box.
- Click **Save**.

### Viewing & Downloading The Report:


- In the **HR Lounge**, click on **Reports**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48235764920/original/fpZFfsv6o93zNuLDO_soxiaAnXny21weaA.png?1656583213)


- In **Report Builder**, click on **Performance 1-on-1 Feedback > Create**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48239816136/original/oWX7Kd4s-UP9J6a-c781UBCwC2LixQTg7g.png?1658310190)


- Select one or more cycles to include in your report.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48239816752/original/8j4gyOj8zu-na9clNgRnSbyZavSaxOT0Yw.png?1658310300)


- In the "**Fields & Filters**" tool, click on **Cycle Name** to deselect filters you do not want in your customized report.


**NOTE:**


**By default, all filters are selected. To remove a filter, uncheck the box and click the "Apply changes" button.**


**If more than one cycle is selected, each cycle will have a dedicated category under the "Fields & Filters" tool.**
