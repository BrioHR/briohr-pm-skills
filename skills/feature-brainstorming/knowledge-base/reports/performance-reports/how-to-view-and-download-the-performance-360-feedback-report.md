# How to View and Download the Performance 360 Feedback Report

*This article explains how to enable permissions and download the Performance 360 Feedback Report.*

### Enabling User Group Permission Settings


To view and download the Performance 360 Feedback Report, the Admin user must enable access under "**Settings**" by following these steps:


1. In **Settings**, click on **User Groups**.
2. For the relevant user group, click **Edit**. (The example shown below is for the Admin user group.)
3. Under the "**HR Modules Permission" > "Reports Module**" category, check the "**Allow access to performance 360 report**" checkbox.
4. Click the **Save **button to complete the action.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246777914/original/3PAqgeBGRE9qVBXeQWSY57SuHGMLV7cy-A.png?1661231328)


### Viewing & Downloading The Report


1.   In** HR Lounge**, click on **Reports**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48235764920/original/fpZFfsv6o93zNuLDO_soxiaAnXny21weaA.png?1656583213)


2.   In the **Report Builder**, click on **Performance 360 Feedback > Create.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246778284/original/IR9khG5qEAAwtAsM4yRc8PN9y_BCk0vpBg.png?1661231586)


3.   Select one or several cycles to include in your report.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246779584/original/WhLGjuZrpNRHI5zI2KpdUCbCgsiYcWTJaA.png?1661232393)


4.   In the "**Fields & Filters**" tool, click on the **Cycle Name** to deselect filters you do not wish to appear in your customized report.


**NOTE:**


**a. By default, all filters are selected. To remove a filter, uncheck the checkbox and click on the Apply Changes button.**


**b. If more than one cycle is selected, each selected cycle will have a dedicated category under the "Fields & Filters" tool.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246779756/original/_7sPtMBlMSuleXGH2V8JEzxhCqydNqkO3Q.png?1661232520)

5.   Click the **Export Report** button and choose your preferred format to download your customized report.
