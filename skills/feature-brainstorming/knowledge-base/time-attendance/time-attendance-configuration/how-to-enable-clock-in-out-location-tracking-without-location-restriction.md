# How to Enable Clock In/Out Location Tracking Without Location Restriction

*Learn how to set up location tracking for employee clock-in/out in BrioHR’s Time Attendance Policy without restricting location. Step-by-step guide included.*

### Introduction


Location tracking in BrioHR allows employers to capture employees’ clock-in/out coordinates without restricting them to a specific address. This feature provides flexibility for employees while ensuring employers retain accurate attendance records.


When location tracking is enabled, employees must clock in/out using the **BrioHR mobile app**, which **captures GPS coordinates** automatically.


---


### Benefits of Location Tracking in Time Attendance


- **Flexibility for Employees: **Employees can clock in/out from any location without restrictions.
- **Accurate Attendance Date: **Employers can view the location coordinates where clock in/out occured.
- **Improved Compliance: **Helps maintain accurate timekeeping records for HR purposes.





---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Setting Up Location Configuration in Time Attendance Policy


#### Step 1 - Access the Time Attendance Module


Log in to **BrioHR account.**


1. Navigate to** HR Lounge > Time Attendance**

**


---


#### Step 2 - Access Time Attendance Policy & Location Configuration


1. Go to **Policy **> Select any **Time Attendance Policy** to modify.


2. In the** Time Attendance Policy, **select **Other Configuration.**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-29-2025-05-28-40-2460-AM.png?width=670&height=234&name=image-png-Sep-29-2025-05-28-40-2460-AM.png)
**


####


---


#### Step 3 - Enable Clock In/Out with Location Tracking


1. Find the **Geolocation** section (**Clock In/Out with location tracking**)


2. Enable the **toggle button.**


3. Click **Save.**


**NOTE:**


Once this setting is enabled, employees must use the** BrioHR mobile app **to clock in and out. Their clock in and out location will be captured through the mobile app and **without **area restriction.


They also need to [grant the app permission to access their location](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/enable-location-detection-for-accurate-clock-ins-briohr-mobile-app?hsLang=en) first.


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-29-2025-05-29-50-0264-AM.png?width=670&height=304&name=image-png-Sep-29-2025-05-29-50-0264-AM.png)
**


**CAUTION:**


Do **not **select "Restrict Clock-in Location" as this will restrict employees to a specific area.


To track location without restrictions, enable only "Clock In/Out with location tracking".

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**Q1: Can employees clock in/out from any location when location tracking is enabled?**


A: Yes, employees can clock in/out from any location. The system records their GPS coordinates without restricting them to a specific address.


**Q2: Do employees need to use the BrioHR mobile app for location tracking?**


A: Yes, location tracking works only through the BrioHR mobile app to capture GPS data accurately.


**Q3: What is the difference between "Restrict Clock-in Location" and "Clock-in/Out with Location Tracking"?**


A: "Clock-in/Out with Location Tracking" is to enable geolocation only in the Time Attendance policy, while "Restrict Clock-in Location" restricts clock-in/out to specific areas (geofencing).
