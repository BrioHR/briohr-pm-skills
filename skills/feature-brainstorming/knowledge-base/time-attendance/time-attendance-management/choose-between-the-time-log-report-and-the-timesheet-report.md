# Choose Between the Time Log Report and the Timesheet Report

*The Time Attendance Time Log Report only shows records with clock-in data; use the Timesheet Report to include employees without clock-ins (absent, on leave) and overtime fields.*

** Audience & Scope**

- Audience: HR Admin
- Module: Time & Attendance / Analytics > Reports
- When to use this article: Your attendance download is missing employees who did not clock in or were on leave; you need an overtime (OT) report.


[](#which-report-to-use)**Which report to use**

- Time Log Report — shows only records with clock-in data. Employees who did not clock in (absent, on leave) will not appear.
- Timesheet Report — shows the record even without clock-in data; use this for complete department lists and for overtime.


[](#download-an-overtime-report)**Download an overtime report**

1. Go to Analytics > Reports > Time Attendance Timesheet Report.
2. Select the overtime fields you need.
3. Export in CSV or Excel.
