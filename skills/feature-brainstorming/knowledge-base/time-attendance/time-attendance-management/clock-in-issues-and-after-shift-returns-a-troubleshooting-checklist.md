# Clock-In Issues and After-Shift Returns: a Troubleshooting Checklist

*Why clock-ins fail (final clock-out done, no active shift) and the two supported ways to handle ad-hoc returns to work after the shift.*

 **"I clocked out and can't clock back in"**

Two likely reasons:


- **Final Clock-Out was already done** — once an employee taps Final Clock-Out, the workday is officially closed and further clock-ins that day are blocked.
- **No active shift covers that period** — the system needs an active assigned shift to allow a clock-in, so ad-hoc work outside scheduled hours is not recognised.


**Assigning additional shifts (ad-hoc scenarios)**

**Method 1 — Manager assigns a second shift:**


Go to **Team > Time Attendance > Rostering**, hover over the day, click **+**, select a shift covering the ad-hoc period, then **Publish Rostering**.


**Method 2 — HR assigns a second shift:**


Go to **HR Lounge > Time Attendance > Rostering**, search the employee, hover over the day, click **+**, add a shift for the ad-hoc period, then **Publish Rostering**.


**Quick fix: Clock In button not responding at all**

If tapping Clock In does nothing:


1. **Reinstall the BrioHR app first** — this resolves most unresponsive-button cases.
2. If the issue persists after reinstalling, contact support so the team can investigate the account.


**FAQ**

**Q: Can we capture an employee's location when they clock in/out during field visits?**


Yes — this can be enabled through the Geolocation feature: **HR Lounge > Time Attendance > Policy >** select the policy **> Other Configuration > Geolocation >** enable the toggle. If the employees are not required to be at a fixed location, ensure **No Restriction** is selected there.
