# How HR Admin Can Mark an Employee as Absent in BrioHR

*Learn how HR Admin can easily mark an Employee's attendance as absent in BrioHR. Follow our step-by-step guide to update attendance records accurately and ensure compliance.*

### Introduction


An HR Admin with access to the **HR Lounge** and **Time Attendance** module can mark an employee as **"absent"** if they have not clocked in for the day.


HR Admins can also perform this task on behalf of a manager.


**NOTE:
**
If you are a** manager **and you want to mark your team member's attendance as absent, please refer to** [[this guide](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/mark-direct-report-as-absent-for-managers?hsLang=en)].**


### Why HR Admin Need to Mark Employees as Absent


This ensures that the employee’s attendance is accurately recorded in their **daily timesheet**. The company can then assess whether further action is needed for employees who frequently miss work without valid reasons.


### Where Can HR Admin Update Absentee Status?


HR Admin can mark employees as absent from two locations:


1. From the **Daily Attendance** page.
2. From an **employee's timesheet** page.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide to Marking Absences in BrioHR


Login to your BrioHR account.


1. Navigate to the **HR Lounge** tab.


2. Click on the **Team Attendance** menu.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-27-2025-09-21-59-9350-AM.png?width=2004&height=897&name=image-png-Oct-27-2025-09-21-59-9350-AM.png)


---


### Method 1: Via the Daily Attendance Page


1. Click on the **Daily Attendance** tab. By default, you will see today's date.


2. Click on the status option** "Not in"** in to filter for employees with no clock in record. The **number** indicates how many employees have not clocked in for the day.


3. The employee's status will display as **"Not-in"** with an **"NCI"** tag (Not Clocked In).


4. Click the **"Mark As Absent"** button.


5. A confirmation message will appear. The status will remain **"Not-in"**, but the **"NCI"** tag will be removed.


6. A remark under the **Action** column will indicate the absence.


**Undo an Absence Mark:**


Click the **"↺" (undo) icon** to undo absent.

### Method 2: Via  the Timesheet Page


1. Click on the **Timesheet **tab. By default, you will see the current month’s timesheet.


2. Search for the team member's name in the** Search** bar.


3. Click on the team member’s name or select **"Open in New Browser"** to open their timesheet.


4. Locate the specific date and click **"Mark as Absent"**.


5. A confirmation message will appear. The** "NCI" **tag will be removed.


6. The employee's absence will be recorded under the **Action** column.


**Undo an Absence Mark:**


Click the **"↺" (undo) icon** to undo absent.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


**1. Can I mark multiple employees as absent at once?
**No, you can only mark employees as absent **one at a time** from either the **Daily Attendance** page or the **Timesheet** page.
On the **Daily Attendance** page, you can filter employees who haven't clocked in by selecting the red **"Not In"** status. This will show all employees who have not clocked in for the day.


**2. Can I undo the "absent" status for multiple employees at once?
**No, just like marking an employee as absent, you must undo it **one at a time**. This can be done manually by clicking the **"undo"** icon in the **Actions** column.
