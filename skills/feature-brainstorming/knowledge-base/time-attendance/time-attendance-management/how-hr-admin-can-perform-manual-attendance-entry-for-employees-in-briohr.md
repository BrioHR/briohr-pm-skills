# How HR Admin Can Perform Manual Attendance Entry for Employees in BrioHR

*Learn how to manually clock in and clock out employees in BrioHR as HR Admin. This step-by-step HR guide ensures accurate time tracking and timesheet adjustments.*

### Introduction


HR Admins can manually enter attendance records to edit clock-in (first in), clock-out (last out), and break durations, ensuring accurate daily timesheets for employees.


### What Is Manual Daily Attendance Entry?


Manual daily attendance entry allows HR Admin to add or modify attendance data for their employees including clock-in, clock-out, and break durations.


There are two (2) places where an HR Admin can manually enter daily attendance for an employee:


1. From the **Daily Attendance** Section
2. From the **Timesheet **Section


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Manual Attendance Entry for Employees


Login to your BrioHR account using a web browser.


1. Navigate to the **HR Lounge** page.


2. Go to **Time Attendance.**


### Method 1: From the Daily Attendance Page


1. Click the **Daily Attendance** tab.


2. By default, you will see today's date.


3. Search for the employee's name in the search bar.


4. Click the pencil icon to start manual attendance entry:


1. 1. First In
  2. Last Out
  3. Break

### Method 2: From the Employee's Timesheet


#### Accessing an employee's timesheet provides HR Admins with a comprehensive view of their attendance for the entire month.


1. Click the **Timesheet** tab.
2. By default, you will see the current month.
3. Search for the employee's name in the search bar.
4. Click the employee's name, or the **"open in new browser" **to open the employee's timesheet'
5. Find the date for manual attendance entry and click the pencil icon to start editing.

  1. First In
  2. Last Out
  3. Break

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


**1. Can HR Admins edit past attendance records in BrioHR?**

Yes, HR Admins can adjust past attendance records by opening the employee’s timesheet. Follow the steps under **"Manual Attendance Entry from Employee's Timesheet"** above.


**2. Can reporting managers edit their team's daily attendance?**


Yes, reporting managers can edit their team's daily attendance if they have access to **Team Page > Time Attendance**. To enable this permission, refer to [[this guide](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-enable-the-time-attendance-module-in-team-manager?hsLang=en)].


**NOTE:**
**Once the employee's timesheet has been approved, neither HR nor managers are allowed to modify the employee's attendance records.**
