# How to Create and Manage a Budget Center in the Training Module

*Budget center allows you to monitor the initial budget, amount utilized on training, and remaining balance for each budget allocation*

### Step-by-Step Guide to Setting Up Budget Center


Login to your BrioHR account


1. Click on **HR Lounge.**
2. Select the** Training **tab.
3. Navigate to **Budget Center.**


#### To create a new budget center:


1. Click on the **+Create budget center** button.
2. A pop out window will appear to create budget center


  1. **Name: **provide a name for your budget center.
  2. **Budget amount:** select a currency and input amount.
3. Click** Save.**

### Step-by-Step Guide to Managing Budget Center


Once the budget center is created, you can manage and update it easily. Here is how:


- **Change Budget Center Statu**s:


  - **Active:** allows you to select this budget center when creating a training program.
  - **Inactive**: prevents you from selecting this budget center during a training program creation.
- **Track Budget Allocation: **The** Budget** column shows the originally allocated budget for each budget center.
- **Monitor Utilization: **The **Utilized **column tracks the amount used from all created training programs.
- **Check Remaining Balance:** The **Balance **column displays the remaining amount available for future training programs.


Additional actions:


- Click the **pencil icon** to edit an existing budget center.
- Click the **bin icon **to delete an existing budget center.


**Deleting an existing budget center cannot be undone. Please proceed with caution!**
