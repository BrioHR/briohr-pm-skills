# How to Create Training Categories

*Training categories help in organizing courses and modules, making it easier to manage your training programs.*

Training programs can be categorized, although it is not compulsory. You can set different approval workflows for categorized and non-categorized training programs. For more information on setting up training approval workflows, please refer to [this guide](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/set-training-managers-approval-workflow?hsLang=en).


**NOTE:**

**If you don't have access to this module in HR Lounge, please ensure the access has been granted in your user group. Please refer to this article for more information:**

[How to enable the Training Module for HR Admin, Managers and Employees in BrioH](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/enable-training-module-hr-managers-employees-briohr?hsLang=en)


---


### To create training categories:


Login to your BrioHR account


1. In the** HR Lounge**, click on **Training.**
2. Go to **Settings**, then click on **Categories**.
3. Click on **+ Create categories** to start creating a new category
4. A pop out window will appear. Enter the category name and click **Save**
5. The newly created category is now available for use.
