# Manage Company Training Catalog

*Managing your company's training catalog efficiently ensures employees can access relevant learning opportunities. This guide covers creating, managing, and publishing training programs to employees with step-by-step instructions.*

#### How to Manage the Company Training Catalog


### Accessing the Training Catalog


1. Go to **HR Lounge** > **Training** > **Training Catalog**.


### Creating a New Training


1. Click on the **+ Create Training** button.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-20-2025-03-46-17-8902-AM.png?width=1132&height=556&name=image-png-Mar-20-2025-03-46-17-8902-AM.png)
2. Under the **Details** section, fill in the required fields based on the training content.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-20-2025-03-47-10-4107-AM.png?width=1333&height=924&name=image-png-Mar-20-2025-03-47-10-4107-AM.png)
3. Upload any **training materials** under the **Attachment** field.
4. Add a **training video link** under the **Video Link** field (if applicable).


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-20-2025-03-47-29-8571-AM.png?width=688&height=176&name=image-png-Mar-20-2025-03-47-29-8571-AM.png)
5. In the **Trainer section**, enter:

  - **Trainer’s name, phone number, email, profile photo, and bio.
**
6. Click **Save** to create the training.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-20-2025-03-50-17-0563-AM.png?width=972&height=549&name=image-png-Mar-20-2025-03-50-17-0563-AM.png)
7. The new training will be saved in **Draft** status.

#### Managing the Training Catalog


### Editing & Publishing Training


Before publishing, click on the **Edit** button to manage the setup.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-20-2025-03-51-13-2381-AM.png?width=1138&height=484&name=image-png-Mar-20-2025-03-51-13-2381-AM.png)


#### Setting Eligibility


Decide who can register for the training:


- **Eligible to all employees** – All employees can see and enroll.
- **Restricted to certain groups** – Only employees meeting specific criteria can enroll.
- **Select trainees manually** – Only selected trainees can enroll; assign them in the **Trainees tab** (training will not be visible in the catalog).

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-20-2025-03-51-38-2164-AM.png?width=1345&height=510&name=image-png-Mar-20-2025-03-51-38-2164-AM.png)


#### Managing Training Budget


- Under the **Budget** section, link the **training cost** to a **budget center** and update the total cost.


#### Setting Enrollment Approval


- Under the **Approval** section, enable the **approval requirement** for enrollment.
- If enabled, trainees’ requests will follow the **approval workflow** defined in the **Training Settings**.


#### Assigning Trainees


- If the **Select Trainees Manually** option is enabled, go to the **Trainees page** to enroll selected participants.


### Publishing the Training


Once all settings are updated, click **Publish Training** to make it available for **eligible employees** to register.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-20-2025-03-52-43-9479-AM.png?width=1144&height=439&name=image-png-Mar-20-2025-03-52-43-9479-AM.png)
