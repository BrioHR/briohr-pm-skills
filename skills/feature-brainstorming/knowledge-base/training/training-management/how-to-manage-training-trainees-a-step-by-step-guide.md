# How to Manage Training Trainees: : A Step-by-Step Guide

*Managing training trainees efficiently ensures that employees receive the development they need, and the company maintains accurate records of participation.*

### Why Is It Important to Manage Training Trainees?


Proper trainee management helps HR professionals:


- Ensure that employees are enrolled in the most relevant training sessions.
- Track attendance for future certifications or performance reviews.
- Manually enroll employees when necessary.
- Maintain an accurate record of training events for compliance and development purposes.


---


### Steps by Steps to Open a Training Catalog


To begin managing training trainees in BrioHR, follow these steps:


Login to BrioHR account


1. Go to **HR Lounge.**
2. Click the **Training** tab.
3. Navigate to the **Training Catalog.**

  1. Here, you will find a list of training events and their statuses.
4. Select the training event for which you want to manage trainees.
5. Go to the **Trainees** tab.

### How to Manage Trainees in a Training Event


In the **Trainees** tab, HR managers can:


- **Check enrollment status** of employees.
- **Mark attendance** for those who have participated.
- **Manually add** or **unenroll trainees** when necessary.


---


#### A) Check the Enrollment Status


There are different statuses to track when managing trainees:


1. **Status : Enrolled**


Employees have either self-enrolled or have been added by HR.
2. **Status: Submitted**
The employee's enrollment is pending approval.
3. **Status: Unenrolled**
The employee was previously enrolled but has now been unenrolled from the training event.

#### B) Mark Employees as 'Attended'


To track employee attendance in a training event:


- Toggle the** "Attended"** to mark their attendance.


Marking attendance is essential for keeping accurate records of training participation and ensuring that employees receive any necessary certifications or recognition.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2024-07-59-04-1445-AM.png?width=670&height=204&name=image-png-Oct-02-2024-07-59-04-1445-AM.png)


---


#### C) Unenroll and Re-enroll Trainees


If you need to manage trainee enrollments, here's how to unenroll and re-enroll participants:


1. **Unenroll Trainees :**
You can unenroll trainees from the training event if their status is '**Enrolled'.**

  1. Click the** '-Unenroll' **button to unenrolled trainees. The status will change to** 'Unenrolled'. **
2. **Re-enroll Trainees: **

  1. To re-enroll them, click the **+Enroll **button. The status will change from** 'Unenrolled' **to** 'Enrolled'**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2024-08-03-27-6160-AM.png?width=670&height=164&name=image-png-Oct-02-2024-08-03-27-6160-AM.png)
**


**NOTE:**
**Employees cannot be completely removed from the trainee list.**


---


#### D) Manually Add/Enroll Trainees


To manually add or enroll trainees in a training event:


1. Click the **+Add trainees** button. A pop out window will appear.
2. Select the employee.

  1. Find the employees in the search box.
  2. You can filter by department and office.
3. Click **'Add to training'** to enroll the employees into the training event.

  1. Their names will be added and status automatically becomes 'Enrolled'


Manually adding trainees allows HR to control who participates in specific training sessions, ensuring that relevant employees get the training they need.
