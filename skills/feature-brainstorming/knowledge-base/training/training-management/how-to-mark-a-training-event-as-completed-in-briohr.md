# How to Mark a Training Event as Completed in BrioHR

*Learn how to mark a training event as completed in BrioHR to prevent future enrollments and remove it from the active training catalog. Step-by-step guide included.*

### Introduction


In **BrioHR**, you can mark training events as **Completed** once they are no longer needed in the training catalog. This ensures that employees cannot enroll in outdated training sessions, keeping your catalog clean and relevant.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Marking a Training Event as Completed


#### Step 1 - Accessing the Training Catalog in HR Lounge


1. Go to **HR Lounge** > **Training**.


2. Click **Training Catalog**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-26-2025-08-20-45-7446-AM.png?width=688&height=314&name=image-png-Aug-26-2025-08-20-45-7446-AM.png)


---


#### Step 2 - Select and Complete the Training Event


1. Choose the **training event name** you want to update.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-26-2025-08-21-30-0120-AM.png?width=688&height=295&name=image-png-Aug-26-2025-08-21-30-0120-AM.png)


2. In the **Details** section, locate the** Complete **button in the top-right corner.


3. Click **Complete.**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-26-2025-08-22-25-5250-AM.png?width=688&height=311&name=image-png-Aug-26-2025-08-22-25-5250-AM.png)
**


4. A pop-out confirmation message will appear and confirm your action.


**IMPORTANT:**


Once a training event is marked as** Completed,** employees can no longer enroll or provide feedback. This process cannot be undone.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-26-2025-08-23-54-8918-AM.png?width=486&height=287&name=image-png-Aug-26-2025-08-23-54-8918-AM.png)


5. After confirmation, you will se a toast success message.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-26-2025-08-24-13-0282-AM.png?width=349&height=117&name=image-png-Aug-26-2025-08-24-13-0282-AM.png)


---


#### Step 3 - Verify Training Event Status


1. Return to the **Training Catalog** page.


2. Search for the **training event name**.


3. The status will now show as **Completed** (previously *Published*).


4. Employees can no longer enroll in the completed training event.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-26-2025-08-25-47-7476-AM.png?width=688&height=315&name=image-png-Aug-26-2025-08-25-47-7476-AM.png)


---


#### Why Mark a Training Event as Completed?


- Prevents employees from enrolling in outdated sessions
- Keeps your training catalog clean and organized
- Ensures employees only access active and relevant training programs


---


#### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**Q1: Can I undo marking a training event as completed?**


**A:** No. Once a training event is completed, the action is final and cannot be reversed.


**Q2: Will employees receive a notification when a training event is marked as completed?**


**A:** No. The event will simply be removed from active enrollement options in the catalog.


**Q3: What happens to employees already enrolled before completed?**


**A:** Their existing enrollment record remains, but no new enrollements are allowed.
