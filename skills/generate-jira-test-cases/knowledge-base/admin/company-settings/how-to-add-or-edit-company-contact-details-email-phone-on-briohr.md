# How to Add or Edit Company Contact Details (Email/Phone) on BrioHR

*This article explains how to add or edit your company's contact email and phone number under Settings > Company.*

Once you have logged in to your BrioHR account, click on the **Settings **icon located at the top right corner of the page.


Next, navigate to the **Company **tab where you can view the **Company Information** section.


To add or edit the Company Contact Details, follow these steps:


1. Click on the **Edit **icon.
2. In the **Contacts **column, click on the **Add Contact** icon.
3. Insert the **Name, Email** address, and **Phone **number.
(To delete the information added, click on the **Bin **icon.)
4. Click **Save **to complete the action. If you want to cancel any changes, click **Cancel**.
