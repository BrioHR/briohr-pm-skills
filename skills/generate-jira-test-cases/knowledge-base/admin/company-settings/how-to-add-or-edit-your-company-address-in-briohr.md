# How to Add or Edit Your Company Address in BrioHR

*This article explains how to add or edit your company address under Settings > Company.*

Once you have logged in to your BrioHR account, click on the **Settings **icon located at the top right corner of the page.


Next, navigate to the **Company **tab where you will find the **Company Information** section.


To add or edit the company address, follow these steps:


1. Click on the **Edit **icon.
2. Enter the company address in the **Address **column.
3. Click **Save **to complete the action. If you wish to discard any changes, click **Cancel.**
