# How to Manage Job Titles in BrioHR: Add, Edit, or Delete Job Titles

*Learn how to manage job titles in BrioHR as an Admin by adding, editing, or deleting job titles easily. Maintain accurate employee records and streamline HR data management.*

### Introduction


Accurate management of job titles ensures HR teams maintain consistent and up-to-date employee records. In **BrioHR**, HR Administrators can add, edit, or delete job titles to maintain organized and accurate employment details.


You can modify an employee’s job title directly from their individual profile or update multiple records in bulk. However, job titles must first exist in the system before they can be assigned to employees.

### Why Managing Job Titles Matters


A standardized list of job titles helps HR teams:


- Maintain consistent data across departments.
- Simplify employee record management.
- Prevents errors during payroll, reporting, and appraisals.

### Step-by-Step Guide: Manage Job Titles in BrioHR


#### A) Add a New Job Title


Log in to your BrioHR account,


1. Navigate to **Settings** > **Job Titles**.


2. Click **+Add Job Title** to create a new title.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-10-2025-01-39-03-3217-AM.png?width=670&height=309&name=image-png-Oct-10-2025-01-39-03-3217-AM.png)


3. Enter the desired job title > Click **Save**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-10-2025-01-39-38-7476-AM.png?width=670&height=356&name=image-png-Oct-10-2025-01-39-38-7476-AM.png)


**TIP:**

Always** search for the job title **before creating one to avoid duplicates.


---


#### B) Edit an Existing Job Title


1. On the **Job Titles** page, locate the title you want to edit.


2. Click the **pencil (edit) **icon next to it.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-10-2025-01-41-13-7557-AM.png?width=670&height=264&name=image-png-Oct-10-2025-01-41-13-7557-AM.png)


3. Modify the job title name and click** Save** to confirm new changes.


---


#### C) Delete an Existing Job Title


**IMPORTANT:**

You **cannot delete** a job title if it is currently** assigned to any employee**.


The system will show a warning message with the employee's name. You'll first need to unassign the job title before deleting it.


###


1. On the **Job Titles** page, click the **bin (delete)** icon next to the title.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-10-2025-01-43-13-8481-AM.png?width=670&height=265&name=image-png-Oct-10-2025-01-43-13-8481-AM.png)


2. A confirmation pop-up will appear.


3. Click **Confirm** to proceed with deletion.


**NOTE:**

If the job title is **not assigned to any employee**, you are **allowed to delete** it.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-10-2025-01-43-26-1781-AM.png?width=670&height=357&name=image-png-Oct-10-2025-01-43-26-1781-AM.png)

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**Q1: Can I assign multiple job titles to one employee?**
A: No, each employee can only have one active job title at a time in BrioHR profile.


**Q2: What happens if I delete a job title assigned to an employee?**
A: You will be prompted to unassign the employee before the job title can be deleted.

**Q3: Can job titles be imported in bulk?**
A: Currently, job titles must be added manually, but employee job title assignments can be updated via bulk in Excel upload.


**Q4: Is there a limit to the number of job titles I can create?**
A: No, there is no set limit. You can create as many job titles as your organization requires.
