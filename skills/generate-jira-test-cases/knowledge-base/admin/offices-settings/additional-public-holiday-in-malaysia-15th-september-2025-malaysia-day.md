# Additional Public Holiday in Malaysia : 15th September 2025 (Malaysia Day)

*With BrioHR, no action is needed in line with this announcement as we’ve updated our system to reflect the additional public holiday!*

Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim has announced that **Monday, September 15th, 2025**
will be declared an additional public holiday in conjunction with the 2025 Malaysia Day celebration. (Source: [The New Straits Times Publication](https://www.nst.com.my/news/nation/2025/07/1248979/updated-extra-public-holiday-sept-15-malaysia-day-year-watch))


In line with this announcement, we've updated our system with a new public holiday, "**Malaysia Day Additional Holiday**," that has been added for all offices based in Malaysia.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-23-2025-10-09-56-4361-AM.png?width=468&height=868&name=image-png-Jul-23-2025-10-09-56-4361-AM.png)


**NOTE:**


**This public holiday is automatically added to the system. If you prefer to disable it, please follow these steps:**





1. Go to **Settings** > **Offices**.
2. Select the relevant office and click **Edit** (green pencil icon).
3. In the "Edit an office" window, navigate to the **Public Holidays** tab.
4. Toggle off ** "Malaysia Day Additional Holiday"**
5. Click **Submit** to save your changes.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-23-2025-10-11-04-6965-AM.png?width=625&height=732&name=image-png-Jul-23-2025-10-11-04-6965-AM.png)
