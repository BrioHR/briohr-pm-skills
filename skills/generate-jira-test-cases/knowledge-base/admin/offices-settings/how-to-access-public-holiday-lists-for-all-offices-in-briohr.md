# How to Access Public Holiday Lists for All Offices in BrioHR

*Learn how to view public holiday lists for all offices in BrioHR. A quick guide for HR admins to access holiday information across multiple locations.*

### Introduction


In BrioHR, **HR Admins** can easily access public holidays relevant to their assigned office. The displayed list helps ensure accurate leave planning and employee scheduling, minimizing confusion about office-specific holidays.


---


### Viewing Public Holidays as an HR Admin


#### 1. Public Holidays Based on Assigned Office


The list of public holidays shown on the **Home page (under “Who’s Away”)** and in the **Leave module** is specific to the office you are assigned to.


For example:
If you are assigned to the **Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia** office, you will only see the **Malaysian public holidays**.


This ensures you view only the holidays applicable to your office’s region.


#### 2. Viewing Public Holidays for Other Offices


If you have access to the **main Settings**, you can view the complete list of public holidays created for **all company offices** in your BrioHR account.


You can also check public holiday details for other countries or branches, which helps in managing multinational teams and coordinating company-wide leave policies.
