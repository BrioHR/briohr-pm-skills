# How to Add or Customize Public Holidays in BrioHR

*This article explains how to add or customize public holidays for your offices beyond the default national and regional ones.*

**NOTE:**


**By default, BrioHR includes national and regional public holidays based on the individual states and countries where they are applicable.**


If you wish to add or customize **additional public holidays**, follow these steps:

1.    Click on **Settings**.


2.    Click on **Offices**.


3.    Select the Office where you want to make the customization and click on **Edit **under the **ACTIONS **column.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48294282782/original/UXUR_2SQW6fWyn6uQpX3zlVyiGsSG5QQkQ.png?1682411971)4.    In the **Edit an Office** pop-up window, click on the **Customize Public Holidays** section.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48294284189/original/jBerNArwB3pqItWLbbrfphThlLB_rdATxA.png?1682412334)


5.    Scroll down and click on the **+Add Da**y icon.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48294285007/original/lgtGrBXmYf7s_8MQnavns-toB1da2MfqJA.png?1682412550)


6.    Enter the **Name **and **Date** of the holiday.


∗ **You can use the bin icon to discard any changes made.**


**∗ To add more than one public holiday, click on the +Add Day icon again and repeat the process.**


7.    Click **Submit **to complete the action. Click **Cancel **to discard any changes made.
