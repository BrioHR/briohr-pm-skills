# How to Allow Employees Working on Weekends to Apply for Weekend Leave

*This article explains how to let employees who work weekends apply for weekend leave by assigning them the right office schedule.*

**Example:**


**All employees in the office work 5 days a week (Monday to Friday).**


**Only employees A, B, and C work 6 days a week (Monday to Saturday).**


**The system does not allow employees A, B, and C to apply for leave on Saturdays.**


- Employees A, B, and C must be assigned to an office with 6 working days (including Saturday).
- In other words, you need to create a separate office for 6 working days and assign employees A, B, and C to this office specifically.
- Once this process is completed, employees A, B, and C will be able to apply for leave on Saturdays accordingly.


#### Step 1:


To create an office, follow these steps:


**1.    Click on Settings.**


**2.    Click on Offices.**


**3.    Click on Add new office.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48166199888/original/T_FhADiOl0H3j1bVve8P_9wTBiVYLICFWw.png?1638411978)


**4.    Input the relevant details in the 'Add new office' pop-up.**


**∗Remember to enable Customize working days and select the checkbox next to the applicable days.**


**5.    Click on Submit to record your changes in the system.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48166200392/original/s4fh0vHNQI5gjIIwnMDImMMTWPEW-QrrKQ.png?1638412508)


#### Step 2:


Once you have completed the above steps, you may assign the employee to the newly created office by following these steps:


- **At the employee's profile, click on Edit.**
- **In the Edit Profile pop-up, select the new office from the 'Office' dropdown.**
- **Click on Save to record your changes in the system.**
