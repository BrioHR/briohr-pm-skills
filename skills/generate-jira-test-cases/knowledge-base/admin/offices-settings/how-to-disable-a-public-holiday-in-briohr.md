# How to Disable a Public Holiday in BrioHR

*Learn how to disable public holidays in BrioHR with this simple step-by-step guide. Customize your public holiday settings effortlessly*

### Introduction


If you're looking to disable public holidays in the BrioHR system, this guide will walk you through the process step-by-step. Whether you need to remove a specific holiday or customize your holiday settings, BrioHR makes it simple.


---


### Why Disable a Public Holiday in BrioHR ?


There may be instances where a public holiday does not apply to your company or certain office locations.
By disabling it, you allow employees to apply for leave using their regular entitlement on that date instead of treating it as an official holiday.


**NOTE:**

Once a public holiday is disabled, the system treats that day as a **normal working day**. Employees can use their **leave entitlement** to apply for leave on that date.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Disable a Public Holiday


#### Step 1 – Log In to BrioHR


Log in to your **BrioHR account** using your admin credentials.


#### Step 2 – Go to Settings


From your main dashboard, navigate to: **Settings > Offices**


#### Step 3 – Select the Office


1. Find the office where you want to disable a public holiday.


Example: *Kuala Lumpur Office*


2. Click the **pencil icon** to edit the office details.


#### Step 4 – Disable the Public Holiday


1. Locate the specific public holiday you wish to disable.


For example: *16 September 2025*


2. Toggle the switch **off** to disable it.


#### Step 5 – Save Changes


1. Click **Save** to confirm your changes.


The selected date is **no longer designated as a public holiday **for employees assigned to that office.


It will also **no longer appear on the Home page Who's Away calendar** as a public holiday
