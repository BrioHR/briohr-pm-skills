# How to Enable Leave Applications for Employees on Friday/Saturday in Kedah, Kelantan & Terengganu Offices

*This article explains how to enable Friday/Saturday leave applications for offices in Kedah, Kelantan, and Terengganu.*

Most states and federal territories in Malaysia observe a Saturday-Sunday weekend. However, states such as Kedah, Kelantan, and Terengganu observe a Friday-Saturday weekend.


**As a result, for offices located in Kedah, Kelantan, and Terengganu, BrioHR's default weekend settings are Friday-Saturday. Therefore, employees working at any of these office locations will not be able to apply for leave on Fridays or Saturdays by default.**


As shown in the screenshot below, under the Leave module, Fridays and Saturdays are greyed out in the calendar:


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48150694540/original/CuLHA85FPUSK7Gov3HNq8-427oPrZnJuHQ.jpg?1634802145)


You can **customize the working days** by following these steps:


- Click on **Settings**.
- Click on **Offices**.
- Click **Edit **at the office branch you wish to customize.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48150703019/original/0unuEfS5Zn63sOlu97MAU2mMk_3mc8RzSA.png?1634803908)


- In the **Edit an Office** pop-up window, enable the **Customize Working Days** option.


![nOXkuVILfXjaYuU2-ercUlOrgfZyr-kBTQ.png?1634804072](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/nOXkuVILfXjaYuU2-ercUlOrgfZyr-kBTQ.png%3F1634804072.png?width=429&height=549&name=nOXkuVILfXjaYuU2-ercUlOrgfZyr-kBTQ.png%3F1634804072.png)


- Select the applicable days (Friday/Saturday) from the drop-down list.
- Click **Submit **to complete the action. Otherwise, click **Cancel **to discard the changes.


![Tl9fH12x40-wYlknFcvBZVM20bvjBsHnIw.png?1634804305](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/Tl9fH12x40-wYlknFcvBZVM20bvjBsHnIw.png%3F1634804305.png?width=427&height=549&name=Tl9fH12x40-wYlknFcvBZVM20bvjBsHnIw.png%3F1634804305.png)


- Once the customization is complete, employees can apply for leave on Fridays and Saturdays, and these days will no longer be greyed-out in the calendar, as shown in the screenshot below:


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48150706177/original/Kt6z85_9Dwt3pp6HVz9N_3beeTBslo9qaw.jpg?1634804442)
