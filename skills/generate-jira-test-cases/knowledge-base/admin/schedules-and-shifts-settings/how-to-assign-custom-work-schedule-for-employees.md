# How to Assign Custom Work Schedule for Employees

*Learn how to assign custom work schedules to employees in BrioHR. Follow our step-by-step guide for individual and bulk schedule assignments*

### Introduction


By default, employees register in BrioHR system follow the work schedules assigned to their respective offices. However, in some cases, certain employees may require a different work schedule than their department or office. This feature is known as **custom-assigning work schedules for employees.**


This guide will walk you through the process of assigning custom schedules to individuals or multiple employees at once.





**Learn more:**

### Step-by-Step Guide: Custom Assign Work Schedules by Employees

** **


**Step 1 - Login to BrioHR**


Sign in to your BrioHR account to get started.


**Step 2 - Navigate to Work Schedules**


1. Go to **Settings** > **Schedules & Shifts.**


2. A list of existing work schedules will appear.


**A) Assign Work Schedule to Individual Employees**


1. Click **Assign Schedule**


2. By default, every employee's schedule shows** "Follow office schedule"**


3. Locate the employee's name.


4. From the drop-down menu under **Schedules, **assign the relevant schedule.


5. The change will be saved automatically.


**B) Bulk Assign Work Schedules for Multiple Employees**


1. Select the checkboxes next to multiple employees (or click **Select All**).


2. A bottom menu will appear.


3. Under** Assign Schedule,** choose the relevant schedule from the drop-down menu.


4. Click** Apply **to save changes.


Tip: Use the search bar and filters at the top. You can filter by:-


- Department
- Office
- Employee Status: Active or Inactive

### Benefits of Custom Assigning Work Schedules


- Flexibility for employees with different working patterns.
- Accurate tracking of clock-in/out times.
- Improved leave management and payroll accuracy.
- Allows HR teams to adapt schedules quickly without affecting the whole office.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**Q1: What happens if I don't assign a custom work schedule to an employee?**


A: The employee will automatically follow the default schedule assigned to their office.


**Q2: Can I assign different schedules to the same employee for different weeks?
**


A: Currently, each employee can follow one active schedule at a time. However, you (as HR) or the manager can update the employee's schedule anytime in his Rostering in the Time Attendance module.


**Q3: Will bulk assignment override existing custom schedules?**


A: Yes, applying a new schedule in bulk will replace the employee's previous custom schedule.
