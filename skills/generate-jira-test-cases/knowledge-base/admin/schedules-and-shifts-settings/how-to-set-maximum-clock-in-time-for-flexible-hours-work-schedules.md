# How to Set Maximum Clock-In Time for Flexible Hours Work Schedules

*Flexible work schedules are becoming increasingly popular, offering employees freedom while maintaining productivity. This guide explains how to set a maximum clock-in time to ensure balance and accountability for flexible-hour employees.*

### Introduction


Flexible-hour work schedules allow employees to manage their working hours without fixed clock-in and clock-out times. However, some organizations may need to enforce a **boundary for the latest permitted clock-in time**.


With BrioHR, employers can configure the **Maximum Clock-In Time** within work schedules. This helps track punctuality while still giving employees flexibility.


---


### What is the Maximum Clock-In Time


The **Maximum Clock-In Time** defines the latest time employees on a flexible-hour arrangement are allowed to clock in.


- If an employee clocks in **after this set time**, they will be marked as **late**.
- This setting is **optional** and can be customized per schedule.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Setting Maximum Clock-in Time for Flexible Hours


#### Step 1 - Login to BrioHR


Login to your BrioHR account using your registered email and password.


#### Step 2 - Find the Flexible Hours Work Schedule


1. Nvigate to** Settings** > **Schedules & Shifts**


2. Go to the **Work Schedules** section.


3. Filter by **'Select Arrangement'** and choose **Flexible Hours**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-01-2025-08-32-41-0426-AM.png?width=2000&height=911&name=image-png-Oct-01-2025-08-32-41-0426-AM.png)


#### Step 3 - Configure the Maximum Clock-In Time


1. Click **pencil (edit) icon **next to the flexible hours work schedule.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-01-2025-08-37-12-8584-AM.png?width=2000&height=702&name=image-png-Oct-01-2025-08-37-12-8584-AM.png)


2. Locate the **Maximum Clock-in Time. **Enter the desired time in hours and minutes (e.g., 10:30)


3. Click **Save** to apply changes.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**Q1: Is the maximum clock-in time mandatory for flexible schedules?
**


A: No. This setting is optional and can be applied only if your organization requires it.


**Q2: What happens if an employee clocks in after the maximum time?
**


A: The system will mark the employee as **late**, which will reflect in their attendance timesheet records.


**Q3: Can different offices or teams have different maximum clock-in times?
**


A: Yes. Since maximum clock-in is tied to **work schedules**, you can configure it differently for each office or team. Learn more[how to assign work schedules by offices](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/assign-work-schedules-by-office-briohr?hsLang=en).


**Q4: Can I change the maximum clock-in time later?**


A: Yes. You can edit the work schedule and update the maximum clock-in time at any point.
