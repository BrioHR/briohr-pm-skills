# How to Enable Finance Department to Approve Claims

*Learn how to grant the Finance team access and permissions to approve employee expense claims in BrioHR. Step-by-step guide for Admins*

You have assigned several employees as Claim Assignees in the Finance department, but they are unable to approve claims when accessing tasks under the 'Pending Actions' panel.


This issue arises because they currently lack the necessary access permissions to approve claims. Regardless of whether the assignee is from HR or Finance, they must have access to HR Lounge > Claims > Dashboard to approve employees' submitted claims.


This article is divided into two parts:


### Step A: Setting The Approver For Claims


1.    Navigate to **HR Lounge > Expense Claims**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-07-2025-08-41-59-9854-AM.png?width=1826&height=671&name=image-png-Sep-07-2025-08-41-59-9854-AM.png)

**(Choose individuals from the HR & Finance departments responsible for approving claims.)**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-07-2025-08-44-21-9175-AM.png?width=1895&height=736&name=image-png-Sep-07-2025-08-44-21-9175-AM.png)

### Step B: Enabling Access To The Claims Module


1.    Go to **Settings > User Groups**.


2.    Select **Edit **(green pencil icon) for the **Finance **user group.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-07-2025-08-49-09-4185-AM.png?width=1901&height=719&name=image-png-Sep-07-2025-08-49-09-4185-AM.png)
[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48153501957/original/hwSdmNjn9757f1hRKei4szZGT9LpeafZfw.png?1635746771)


3.    Navigate to **Pages Access > HR Lounge to enable access. **


4.    Under **HR Modules Permission, enable access to the Claim Module**.


5.    For '**Allow dashboard access**,' select **Allowed for all employees **from the dropdown.


6.    Review the rest of the access and enable if required.


7.    **Save** to apply the changes.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-07-2025-08-56-09-7960-AM.png?width=859&height=806&name=image-png-Sep-07-2025-08-56-09-7960-AM.png)


#### Important Notes


- Finance approvers will now have visibility of all employee claims.
- They can approve only the claims they are assigned to, ensuring proper accountability.
- This setup provides **a controlled yet efficient claim approval process**.
