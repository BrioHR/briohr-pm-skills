# How to Enable the Training Module for HR Admin, Managers, and Employees in BrioHR

*Learn how to enable the Training Module in BrioHR for HR Admins, Managers, and Employees. Step-by-step guide to assigning permissions and managing training access.*

To ensure effective training management, enabling the **Training Module** in BrioHR is crucial for HR Admin, Managers, and Employees. Each user group requires specific permissions to manage and access training information. This guide provides a step-by-step approach to configure these permissions.





### Enabling Training Module for HR/Admin (HR Lounge > Training)


1.    Login to BrioHR and click into **Settings** >** User Groups**


2.   At the **Admin User Group**, click on the** Edit **icon


###


3.   Ensure HR Lounge Modules Permission is enabled.


1. 1. Go to **Pages Access** > Tick the checkbox for** HR Lounge**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-06-28-10-1107-AM.png?width=670&height=629&name=image-png-Sep-24-2025-06-28-10-1107-AM.png)
**


4.   Click on** HR Modules Permission > Enable the Reports Module**


5.   Tick the checkboxes :


1. 1. **Allow access to training event report**
  2. **Allow access to employee training report**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-06-39-34-6932-AM.png?width=670&height=632&name=image-png-Sep-24-2025-06-39-34-6932-AM.png)





6.   Within HR Modules Permission, look for the **Training Module **and** enable the toggle**


1. 1. Tick the checkbox **'Allow to access module settings' - **This allows HR to access the settings and configurations of the Training Module.


7.   Click **Save **to apply the changes.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-06-50-23-7187-AM.png?width=670&height=636&name=image-png-Sep-24-2025-06-50-23-7187-AM.png)


**NOTE:**


**Enabling** 'Allow to access module settings' will allow HR/Admin **to access **the settings and configurations of the Training Module.


**Disabling** this permission will limit HR/Admin **to viewing only** the 'Training Catalog' and 'Training Request' tabs under HR Lounge > Training.


---


### Enabling Training Module Access to Managers (Team > Training)


1.    Login to BrioHR and click into **Settings** >** User Groups**


2.   At the **Manager User Group**, click on the** Edit **icon


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-06-48-11-9302-AM.png?width=670&height=283&name=image-png-Sep-24-2025-06-48-11-9302-AM.png)

3.   Within the Manager User Group, go to **Manager Modules Permission**.

4.   Search for the** Training Module** > **Enable the toggle button**


1. 1. **Team Access:** Set employees the manager has access to:

    1. **Direct Reports Only**
    2. **2 levels below** (Direct reports and their direct reports
    3. **Direct reports and all levels below**
  2. (Optional) Tick the checkbox of **View Budget Center balance and Training Cost of Training Requests** - This allows approvers to view the Budget Center balance and Training Cost of Training Event Creation Requests that are pending their approval


5. Click **Save**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-06-56-04-2213-AM.png?width=670&height=636&name=image-png-Sep-24-2025-06-56-04-2213-AM.png)





---


### Enabling Employee Permissions for the Training Module (Home > My Training)


1.    Login to BrioHR and click into **Settings** >** User Groups**


2.   At the **Employee User Group**, click on the** Edit **icon


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-06-58-29-2249-AM.png?width=670&height=258&name=image-png-Sep-24-2025-06-58-29-2249-AM.png)


3.   Within the Employee User Group, go to **Employee Modules Permission**.

4.   Search for the** Training Module** > **Enable the toggle button**


5.   Click **Save**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2025-07-00-51-9550-AM.png?width=670&height=634&name=image-png-Sep-24-2025-07-00-51-9550-AM.png)
