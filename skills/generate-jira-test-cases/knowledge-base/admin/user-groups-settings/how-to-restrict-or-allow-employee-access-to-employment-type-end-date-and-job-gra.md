# How to Restrict or Allow Employee Access to Employment Type, End Date & Job Grade

*Learn how to enable or disable employee access to Employment Type, Employment End Date, and Job Grade in BrioHR. Step-by-step guide for Admins.*

In BrioHR, Admins can control whether employees have permission to **view or edit sensitive profile details** such as **Employment Type, Employment End Date, and Job Grade**. This feature ensures sensitive HR information is managed according to your company’s policies.


This article provides a step-by-step guide to restrict or allow employee access to these profile fields using **User Group settings**.


### Steps to Restrict or Allow Access


1.   Login to BrioHR and click into **Settings >** **User Groups**.


2.   At the **Employee User Group**, click on the **Edit **icon.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-07-2025-09-35-13-8321-AM.png?width=670&height=252&name=image-png-Sep-07-2025-09-35-13-8321-AM.png)

3.   Click on **Profile Sensitive Data**:


#### A. Employment End Date And Job Grade


- Configure employees' access to **view or edit **the Employment End Date and Job Grade by clicking on the **Change **icon.
- Select or unselect the settings you wish to configure.
- Click **Save **to complete the action.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48210734444/original/TIf9TIO5VhHrax9F1ArINF21gt03Rt9KzQ.png?1648984820)


#### B. Employment Type


- Configure employees' access to **view or edit** the Employment Type by clicking on the **Change **icon.
- Select or unselect the settings you wish to configure.
- Click **Save **to complete the action.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**1. Can I apply these settings to specific employees instead of groups?**
No, permissions are controlled at the **User Group level**. To customize access, assign employees to different User Groups.


**2. What happens if I restrict access to Employment End Date?**
Employees will not be able to see or edit their own **End Date** field in their profile.


**3. Can managers still see these details if employee access is restricted?**
Yes, managers or HR users with higher access permissions can still view/edit these fields.


**4. Is it possible to give view-only access without edit rights?**
Yes. BrioHR allows you to configure **view-only** or **edit** access separately.
