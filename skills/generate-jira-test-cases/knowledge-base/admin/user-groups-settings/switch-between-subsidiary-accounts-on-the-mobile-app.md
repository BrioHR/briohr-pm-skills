# Switch Between Subsidiary Accounts on the Mobile App

*Switch between subsidiary accounts directly from the brioHR mobile app. Easily access your assigned subsidiaries and return to your main branch without logging in again.*

##### Audience & Scope


Audience: Admin / Managers


Country: Global


Pre-requisites:  User must have access to one or more subsidiary accounts.





#### Overview


If you have been granted access to multiple subsidiary accounts, you can now switch between your accessible companies directly from the **Account** tab in the mobile app.


This enhancement allows you to:


- Switch between subsidiary accounts without signing in again.
- Quickly access the companies assigned to you.
- Return to your main branch at any time with a single tap.


**Note:** Only subsidiary accounts that you have been granted access to will be displayed. Your main branch will not appear in the list of subsidiaries.


**IMPORTANT: Please ensure that your brioHR mobile app is updated to Version 2.4.0.**


####


#### How to switch to a subsidiary account


1. Open the **brioHR Mobile App**.
2. Navigate to the **Account** tab.
3. Under the **Subsidiary** section, tap **Subsidiaries**.
4. Select the subsidiary you would like to access.
5. The app will automatically switch your session and redirect you to the selected subsidiary's **Account** screen.





#### How to return to your main branch


When you are logged into a subsidiary account:


1. Go to the **Account** tab.
2. Under the **Subsidiary** section, tap **Back to Main Branch**.
3. Your session will switch back to your main branch and you will be redirected to the main branch **Account** screen.

#### Important Notes


- Only subsidiaries you have permission to access will be displayed.
- Your **main branch** is not included in the **Subsidiaries** list.
- After logging out from a subsidiary account, your next login will always take you to your **main branch** instead of the last subsidiary you accessed.





#### Benefits


Switching between subsidiary accounts on the mobile app allows you to:


- Access multiple company accounts without repeatedly logging in and out.
- Save time when managing multiple subsidiaries.
- Move seamlessly between your main branch and subsidiary accounts.
- Improve productivity while working on the go.
