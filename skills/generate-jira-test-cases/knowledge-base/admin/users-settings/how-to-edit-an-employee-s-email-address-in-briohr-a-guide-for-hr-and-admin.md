# How to Edit an Employee's Email Address in BrioHR: A Guide for HR and Admin

*Learn how HR and admin users can update or edit an employee’s email address in BrioHR. Step-by-step instructions shared for smooth account management.*

In **BrioHR**, each employee’s email address is important for accessing the system and receiving notifications. As an HR or Admin user, you may need to **update or correct an employee’s email address** if they change their work email or if there were any errors during email setup for the employee.


This guide provides step-by-step instructions to edit an employee’s email address in BrioHR.


---


### Steps to Edit an Employee’s Email Address in BrioHR


1. **Log in** to your BrioHR account.
2. Navigate to **Settings > Users** to view the employee list.
3. Search for the employee’s name using the search bar.
4. Under the **ACTIONS** column, click on the **Edit icon** (pencil).
5. In the **EMAIL** column, enter the employee’s **new email address.**
6. Click the **Save icon** (tick ✅) to confirm changes.


  - To discard, click the **Cancel icon** (❌).

### Best Practices for Updating Employee Email Addresses


- Double-check for typos before saving.
- Ensure the new email address is **active and valid.**
- Inform the employee that their **login credentials and notifications** will now be tied to the updated email address.
