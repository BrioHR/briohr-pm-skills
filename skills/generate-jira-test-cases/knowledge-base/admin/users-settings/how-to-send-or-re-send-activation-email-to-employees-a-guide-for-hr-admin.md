# How to Send or Re-Send Activation Email to Employees: A Guide for HR/Admin

*Learn how HR or Admin users can send or re-send activation emails to employees in BrioHR. Step-by-step guide to ensure employees receive login credentials.*

When a new employee account is created in **BrioHR**, the system does not automatically send an activation email. Instead, HR or Admin users must manually trigger the activation email. This email provides the employee with their **username and temporary password** for first-time login.


This guide explains how to **send or re-send activation emails** to employees, especially new joiners, so they can access their BrioHR accounts smoothly.


####


#### Steps to Send or Re-Send Activation Email


1. Log in to your **BrioHR account.**
2. Go to **Settings > Users.**
3. Select the **checkbox** next to the employee’s name.
4. Click on the **Send Activation Email** icon.
5. A confirmation prompt will appear: *“Send activation email?”*


  - Click **Yes** to proceed.
  - Click **No** to cancel.
6. Once confirmed, the system will send the **activation email** to the employee.

#### What Does the Activation Email Contain?


The activation email sent by BrioHR includes:


- Employee’s **username**
- A **temporary password** for first-time login
- Instructions on how to access their BrioHR account

#### Best Practices for Activation Emails


- Ensure the employee’s **email address is correct** in the system before sending.
- Inform new joiners to check their **spam/junk folder** if they do not see the activation email.
- Encourage employees to **change their temporary password** immediately after logging in.





**NOTE:**


The credentials sent via activation email can only be used from the employee's join date onwards. Employees **cannot log in before their official start date.**


**Exception:** If the employee receives a welcome email via the Onboarding module (not the standard activation email), they **CAN** log in before their join date to complete onboarding tasks.





#### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**1. Why didn’t my employee receive the activation email?**
Check that their email address in **Settings > Users** is correct and remind them to check their **spam/junk folder.**


**2. Can I send activation emails to multiple employees at once?**
Yes. You can select multiple employees using the checkboxes and send activation emails in bulk.


**3. What happens if I re-send an activation email?**
The employee will receive another activation email with their username and temporary password.


**4. Does the activation email expire?**
The activation email is valid until the employee logs in for the first time using the provided credentials.


**5. What should I do if the employee’s email address is wrong?**
Update their email address first under **Settings > Users** before sending the activation email.
