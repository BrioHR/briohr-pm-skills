# How to View Profiles of Inactive or Resigned Employees

*Learn how HR and Admin users can view inactive or resigned employee profiles in BrioHR via Settings or HR Lounge.*

To access the profile of an inactive or resigned employee, there are two places you can go:


**A. From Settings (Admin) > Users**
**B. From HR Lounge > Employee List**


- For instructions on setting the end of employment date for an employee, please refer to [this article](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-set-an-employees-end-date?hsLang=en) for HR and [this article](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-deactivate-or-remove-a-resigned-employees-account-in-briohr?hsLang=en) for account Admin.
- For guidance on restoring the account status of a resigned employee, please refer to [this article](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-restore-a-resigned-employees-account-status?hsLang=en).


---


### A. From Settings (Admin)


1.  Log in to your BrioHR account and click on the **Settings **icon.


2.  Click on **Users**.


3.  Search for the employee's name in the **Search **column.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48151560842/original/-HoNZpfOn-KzcyXI417Tpc6ktLEDTN3qKw.png?1635150994)


4.  Click on the Ellipsis ('...') icon in the **ACTIONS **column.


5.  Select "**View profile**" to be directed to the inactive employee's **profile**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48154283094/original/JC1TngBZF1DDdYkPWzv5GqFN-8EJ28Bt0A.png?1635920416)


On the inactive employee's profile (depending on the HR/Admin user module access), you can view/edit the following information:


1. Profile Information
2. Leave Policies & Balances
3. Payroll Information


---


### B. From HR Lounge > Employee List


1.  Log in to your BrioHR account and click on **HR Lounge **and then into **Employee Management**


2.  Go to **Employee List** and change the status from **Active **to **Inactive**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48295586611/original/n-PNH7_HXKX9GqLLGtLr0JrtTbLP4QbjOQ.png?1683103196)


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48295586746/original/-j4w4-seXn6nu387DbtG3ui6SsYhEqGA2Q.png?1683103230)


3.  The system will display all resigned employees. Find the employee you're looking for and click the Ellipsis ('...') icon to open more actions.


4.  Click "**View Profile**" to open the resigned employee's profile.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48295588070/original/LkKMsA_o7AWIC87JBM7gSEGGNFe5hpRZoQ.png?1683103614)
