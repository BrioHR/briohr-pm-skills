# How to Change Your Password in BrioHR

*This article explains how to change your BrioHR password, including after first login with the temporary password.*

You can reset your password when logging into your BrioHR account. This is particularly common when logging in for the first time and using the default password provided in the account activation email.


To change your password in BrioHR, follow these steps:


Log in to your BrioHR account.


- Click on **Me **(next to your profile picture at the top right corner).
- Select **User Settings** from the drop-down menu.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-10-2025-09-17-56-4353-AM.png?width=2015&height=965&name=image-png-Jun-10-2025-09-17-56-4353-AM.png)


- Go to Change My Password and click on **Change**.
- Enter your **current password **and then your **new password**. Your new password must meet the following criteria:

  - At least 8 characters long
  - Contains both lowercase and uppercase letters
  - Includes at least one number


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-10-2025-09-18-03-2699-AM.png?width=2000&height=623&name=image-png-Jun-10-2025-09-18-03-2699-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-10-2025-09-18-15-6771-AM.png?width=2000&height=949&name=image-png-Jun-10-2025-09-18-15-6771-AM.png)


- Click **Save **once you’ve entered your new password.


**NOTE:**


- **After saving your new password, you will remain logged in. When you log out, ensure you use your newly reset password the next time you log in. Remember, both your password and username are case-**SENSITIVE**.**
