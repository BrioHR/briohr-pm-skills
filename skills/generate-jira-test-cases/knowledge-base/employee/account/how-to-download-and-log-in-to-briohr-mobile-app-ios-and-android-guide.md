# How to Download and Log in to BrioHR Mobile App (iOS & Android Guide)

*Learn how to download the BrioHR mobile app on iOS or Android and log in using Single Sign-On (SSO) or email/password. Step-by-step instructions included.*

### Introduction


As a registered BrioHR user, downloading the **BrioHR mobile app** makes it easier to manage daily HR tasks directly from your phone. With the app, you can:


- Apply for leave
- Submit claims
- View payslips
- Clock in and clock out for attendance
- Approve direct report's leave application, claim submission and approver attendance overtime (for managers)


---


### Where to Download BrioHR Mobile App


The BrioHR mobile app is available for both iOS and Android devices. Simply search for **“BrioHR”** in the following app stores:


- **Apple App Store** (for iPhone users)
- **Google Play Store** (for Android users)


Related article: **[First Time Login via Activation Email an Downloading the BrioHR App](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/first-time-login-via-activation-email-and-downloading-the-briohr-app?hsLang=en)**


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Logging in to BrioHR Mobile App


#### Step 1 - Open the App


Launch the **BrioHR mobile app **on your device.


1. You have two options to log in:

  1. Using **Single-Sign On (SSO).**
  2. Using regular **Email and Password.**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-18-2025-08-32-31-9853-AM.png?width=284&height=565&name=image-png-Aug-18-2025-08-32-31-9853-AM.png)
**


**Option 1 (a) - Login Using Single-Sign On (SSO)**


- If your registered email is linked to an SSO provider, you can log in using:


  - **Google**
  - **Microsoft**
  - **Okta**


**Simply enter your email and use the same password you use for your Google/Microsoft/Okta account.**


**Learn more: [BrioHR Single-Sign On (SSO): Streamline Your Login with Google, Microsoft, and Okta](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/briohr-single-sign-on-sso-streamline-your-login-with-google-microsoft-and-okta?hsLang=en)**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-18-2025-08-32-49-9453-AM.png?width=688&height=462&name=image-png-Aug-18-2025-08-32-49-9453-AM.png)


**Option 1 (b) - Login Using Email and Password**


If you do not use SSO, log in with:


- **Email address** (as your username)
- **Password** (the one you set specifically for your BrioHR account)


Related article: **[How to Change Your Password in BrioHR](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-change-your-password-in-briohr?hsLang=en)**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-18-2025-08-33-19-1353-AM.png?width=290&height=623&name=image-png-Aug-18-2025-08-33-19-1353-AM.png)


---


#### Step 2 - Confirm Login


1. Tap **Login** to access your BrioHR account.
2. You are now successfully logged into the BrioHR mobile app.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-18-2025-08-36-10-2886-AM.png?width=651&height=581&name=image-png-Aug-18-2025-08-36-10-2886-AM.png)


---


#### Troubleshooting Login Issues


- If you cannot log in using SSO, confirm that your organization has enabled this option in BrioHR settings.
- If you forgot your password, follow this guide: [How to Resolve Login Issues](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-resolve-login-issues?hsLang=en)
- If you did not receive your activation email (for first time user), please contact your HR Admin for assistance.
