# How to Reset Your BrioHR Password and Fix Login Issues

*Having trouble logging into your BrioHR account? Learn the step-by-step process to reset your password, common login mistakes to avoid, and troubleshooting tips.*

### Introduction


If you’re unable to log in to your **BrioHR account**, don’t worry! Login issues are common and can usually be resolved quickly. Below, we cover the most common reasons you may be unable to sign in and provide a step-by-step guide to resetting your password.


###


### Common Reasons Why You Can't Log In


1. **Using a different email address:** Ensure you’re signing in with the correct registered email.
2. **Mistyped email address:** Double-check for typing errors.
3. **Mistyped password**: Confirm correct use of uppercase/lowercase letters, numbers, special characters, and spacing.


###


### How to Reset Your BrioHR Password Through 'Forgot Password'


If you’ve forgotten your password or are still unable to log in, follow these steps to reset it:


#### Step 1 - Visit the BrioHR Login Portal


Go to [BrioHR Login](https://app.briohr.com/signin?redirect=%2Fhome) and click on the** "Forgot Password".**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-02-2026-02-28-09-2648-AM.png?width=1577&height=708&name=image-png-Jul-02-2026-02-28-09-2648-AM.png)


#### Step 2 - Request a Password Reset


Enter your registered email address and click **"Request Password"**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-02-2026-02-29-45-1113-AM.png?width=1615&height=896&name=image-png-Jul-02-2026-02-29-45-1113-AM.png)
**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-22-2025-01-38-59-0963-AM.png?width=373&height=111&name=image-png-Aug-22-2025-01-38-59-0963-AM.png)


#### Step 3 - Check Your Email Inbox


Look for an email titled **"Your Password Request"**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-22-2025-01-39-26-6758-AM.png?width=670&height=164&name=image-png-Aug-22-2025-01-39-26-6758-AM.png)


#### Step 4 - Reset Your Password


Click the **"RESET NOW"** button inside the email.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-22-2025-01-40-00-3305-AM.png?width=670&height=700&name=image-png-Aug-22-2025-01-40-00-3305-AM.png)


####


#### Step 5 - Create a New Password


Enter your new password. Make sure it meets these requirements:


- At least **8 characters**
- Includes a **lowercase and uppercase letters**
- Contains **at least one number**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-02-2026-02-33-53-2174-AM.png?width=1620&height=895&name=image-png-Jul-02-2026-02-33-53-2174-AM.png)
**


#### Step 6 - Finalize the Reset


Click the **"Reset Password"** button to save your new password.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-02-2026-02-34-09-2443-AM.png?width=1620&height=895&name=image-png-Jul-02-2026-02-34-09-2443-AM.png)


#### You will see a success message shown once the password reset was completed. Click "Log in via Web" to login to the system.


####


#### Step 7 - Log In With Your New Password


You can now log in to your BrioHR account with your new credentials.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-02-2026-02-36-03-4499-AM.png?width=1600&height=892&name=image-png-Jul-02-2026-02-36-03-4499-AM.png)


###


### Troubleshooting Login Issues


If you still cannot access the **BrioHR login page**, the issue might be caused by:


- Internal IT restrictions
- Firewall or security measures enforced by your company


In this case, please contact your **IT support team** and request them to unblock access to the site. Once access is enabled, follow the steps above to reset your password.
