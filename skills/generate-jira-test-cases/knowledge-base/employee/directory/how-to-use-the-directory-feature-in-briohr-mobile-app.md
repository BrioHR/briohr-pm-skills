# How to Use the Directory Feature in BrioHR Mobile App

*Learn how to find your colleagues easily using the Directory feature in the BrioHR Mobile App. Discover how to search, filter by office or department, and ensure your app is up to date.*

### Introduction


Finding your colleague's profile is simple and convenient with the **Directory Feature** in the **BrioHR Mobile App**.


This function allows you to** search and filter employees** quickly - helping you locate team members across different offices or departments with just a few taps.


###


### How to Use the Directory in BrioHR Mobile App


#### Search and Filter Employees


You can use the **Search** and **Filter** tools to refine your employee list based on:


- **Offices: **View employees under a specific office location.
- **Departments:** Filter by department for easy navigation.
- **Sorting Options: **Sort the list by **Employee ID** or **Alphabetical Order** to organize results efficiently.


This makes it easier to connect with the right people without scrolling through the entire company directory.


**IMPORTANT:**


The information you are able to view is based on the access configured for your user group permissions. For further access, reach out to your HR/Admin respectively.

### Ensure You Are Using the Latest App Version


To fully experience the Directory feature and other updates, make sure your **BrioHR Mobile App** is updated to the **latest version.**


The app may prompt you automatically for updates, but if **auto-update is not enabled,** follow the steps below:


#### For iOS Users:


1. Open the App Store
2. Navigate to the **Updates** section
3. Locate and **manually update the BrioHR Mobile App**


#### For Android Users:


- Launch the Google Play Store
- Go to **My Apps & Games**
- Find **BrioHR Mobile App** and tap **Update**


Keeping your app updated ensures that all new features, including the improved directory functions, run smoothly.


###


### Why You Cannot Access the Directory Page


If you can see the **Directory** menu in your mobile app but receive an **“Access Denied”** pop-up message, it means you do not have permission to view this section.


Please contact your **HR Admin** for further assistance.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**1. What is the Directory feature in BrioHR Mobile App?**
The Directory feature helps you search, filter, and find colleagues easily across offices and departments.


**2. Why can’t I find a colleague in the Directory?**
Ensure the colleague’s profile is active and that you have access permissions to view their information.


**3. How often should I update the BrioHR Mobile App?**
It’s recommended to keep automatic updates enabled so you always have access to the latest features and improvements.


**4. Can I view contact details in the Directory?**
Yes, depending on your company’s privacy settings, you can view contact details like email or phone number directly from the Directory.
