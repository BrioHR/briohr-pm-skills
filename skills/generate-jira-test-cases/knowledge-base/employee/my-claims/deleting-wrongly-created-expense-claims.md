# Deleting Wrongly Created Expense Claims

*This is a step-by-step guide on how to cancel/delete open claim if wrongly created the claim, and unable to cancel by employee.*

Login to your BrioHR account.


1. At **Home**, click on **My Claims **menu.


2. At screen Expense Claims, choose the respective claims that need to be delete and click on **trash bin** icon.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-11-2024-05-33-36-2672-AM.png?width=688&height=325&name=image-png-Oct-11-2024-05-33-36-2672-AM.png)


3. Click on **Confirm button** to confirm delete the respective claim, or click on **Cancel button** to cancel the confirmation.
