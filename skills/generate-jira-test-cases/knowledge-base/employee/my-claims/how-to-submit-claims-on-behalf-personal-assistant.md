# How to Submit Claims on Behalf (Personal Assistant)

*This article explains how a designated Personal Assistant can view, create, and submit claims on behalf of a manager.*

Introducing our latest feature: the Personal Assistant, which allows you to designate an employee as a 'Personal Assistant' to a manager or CEO.


This feature enables the assigned personal assistant to view, create, and submit claims on behalf of the manager or CEO directly from the personal assistant’s Home page. A new menu, "Claims on Behalf," will appear on the Home page.


If you are an HR professional looking to set up Personal Assistant module permissions, please refer to this article:


[Streamline Claims with the New 'Claims on Behalf' Personal Assistant Feature](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/streamline-claims-with-the-new-claims-on-behalf-personal-assistant-feature?hsLang=en)





[Embedded content](https://www.loom.com/embed/d38f48ead13d4b0d830c4605986b1822?sid=9e3c0d65-8423-454d-8427-bae2e137d791)


1. Login to BrioHR.
2. Go to the Home page.
3. Look for the menu "**Claims on Behalf.**"
