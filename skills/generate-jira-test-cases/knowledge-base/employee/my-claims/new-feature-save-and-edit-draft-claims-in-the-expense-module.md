# New Feature: Save and Edit Draft Claims in the Expense Module

*This article explains how to save a claim as a draft and edit it later in the Expense Claims module.*

Discover the latest enhancement to our Expense Claims module. We are excited to introduce the "**Save as Draft**" feature, designed to improve your experience by allowing you to save a claim as a draft. This ensures that you can return to it later without losing any progress, even in unexpected situations.


The "**Save as Draft**" feature allows you to revisit and revise your claim when you have more time. This feature is available on both web browsers and mobile apps.


**NOTES:**


- **Before saving a claim as a draft, you must provide a Report Name (mandatory). Other details like the Transaction Date, Claim Type, and Total Amount can be added later.**
- **Draft claims cannot be submitted until all mandatory details are provided. Edit your draft claim to include these details, enabling submission.**
- **Once all mandatory details are provided, the claim will automatically transition from draft status to a regular claim, ready for submission.**
- **Draft claims can seamlessly integrate with regular claims that have an open status within a claim report.**
- **The "Save as Draft" feature is available for all claim types except Cash Advance claims.**


---


### Steps to Save a Claim as Draft:


- Navigate to **Home > My Claims**.
- Click on **+ New Claim**.
- Fill in the **Report Name**.
- Click on **Save as Draft or Save & Add Another Draft.**

### Steps to Edit Draft Claims:


- Navigate to **Home > My Claims**.
- Locate the claim report, noting that the **Submit **button will be unavailable.
- Click on the **arrow **button beside the report name.
- If it's a draft claim, it will display **DRAFT** on the left-hand side.
- Click the **Edit **button to add all mandatory details.


![7nlFyV7XyCAle1hF8ijC_3wu9AFYb72quA.png?1715329726](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/7nlFyV7XyCAle1hF8ijC_3wu9AFYb72quA.png%3F1715329726.png?width=688&height=247&name=7nlFyV7XyCAle1hF8ijC_3wu9AFYb72quA.png%3F1715329726.png)


- Once complete, click Save or Save & Add Another.

- The edited claim, now containing all mandatory details, will no longer be in **DRAFT** status.
