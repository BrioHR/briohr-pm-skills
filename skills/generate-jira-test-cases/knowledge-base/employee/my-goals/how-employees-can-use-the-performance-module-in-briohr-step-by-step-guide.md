# How Employees Can Use the Performance Module in BrioHR: Step-by-Step Guide

*Learn how to navigate BrioHR’s Performance module — from setting objectives to completing appraisals and 360 feedback. Follow this simple step-by-step guide for employees.*

### 1. Accessing the Performance Dashboard


To begin, employees need to navigate to **My HR > Goals** within the BrioHR platform. This section displays the **Performance Dashboard**, where all performance-related activities are managed.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-19-2025-07-57-53-2405-AM.png?width=670&height=295&name=image-png-Nov-19-2025-07-57-53-2405-AM.png)


### 2. Reviewing and Creating Objectives


The first task is to **review the company objectives**. Employees can also add their own objectives if needed.


When creating a new objective, employees must decide whether the objective should be part of the **appraisal form** or set as a **personal goal**.


- If the objective is **not** part of the appraisal process, select **“This is a non-rated objective.”**


Once all objectives are listed, employees should **review them with their manager**. The manager will then **confirm the objectives**.


Depending on system access, employees may still be able to **edit objectives after manager confirmation**.
It’s important for employees to **update the progress** regularly to reflect ongoing achievements and milestones.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-19-2025-08-01-23-4215-AM.png?width=670&height=291&name=image-png-Nov-19-2025-08-01-23-4215-AM.png)


NOTE*:* Keeping objectives updated helps managers track employee performance more effectively and ensures accurate appraisal results.


### 3. Completing the Appraisal Form


Once the **Appraisal Form** becomes available on the **Performance** page, employees can proceed to fill it out.


The appraisal form typically includes the following sections:


1. **Questionnaire / Self Appraisal**
2. **Objectives Rating**
3. **Competencies Rating**
4. **Summary**


Employees should:


- Provide feedback and ratings for each **objective**, **competency**, and **question**.
- Write an overall **summary** reflecting their performance.


After completing the appraisal, click **Submit**. Once submitted, the form will be **pending manager review (Appraiser feedback)**.


###


### 4. Engaging in 1-on-1 Feedback


Some appraisal processes may include **1-on-1 Feedback** sessions.
These are periodic discussions — daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly — between employees and managers.


Both parties can use the **comment section** to document discussion notes. This feature is particularly helpful when referring back to previous conversations and tracking performance discussions over time.


###


### 5. Participating in 360 Feedback


Another optional task in the Performance module is **360 Feedback**.
In this process, employees may be assigned to give feedback on:


- Their **colleagues**
- Their **superior**
- Their **direct reports**


When assigned as a **360 feedback giver**, employees might be required to provide constructive feedback and ratings to complete the task.


####


#### Conclusion


BrioHR’s Performance module empowers employees to take an active role in managing their performance journey — from setting goals to completing appraisals and participating in feedback processes.


By regularly updating objectives, providing thoughtful appraisals, and engaging in feedback discussions, employees can gain valuable insights and contribute to a transparent performance culture.


####


#### Suggested FAQs


**1. Where can I find my performance dashboard in BrioHR?**
You can access it by navigating to **My HR > Goals** on your dashboard.


**2. Can I edit my objectives after the manager confirms them?**
Yes, depending on the access permissions set by your organization, you may still be able to edit your objectives after confirmation.


**3. What is a non-rated objective?**
A non-rated objective is a personal goal that is **not included** in the official appraisal rating.


**4. When will the appraisal form become available?**
The availability of the appraisal form depends on your company’s performance cycle setup. You will receive a notification once it is open.


**5. What is 360 feedback and how does it work?**
360 feedback involves providing feedback on peers, managers, or direct reports. If assigned, you will need to give ratings and comments as part of your performance tasks.
