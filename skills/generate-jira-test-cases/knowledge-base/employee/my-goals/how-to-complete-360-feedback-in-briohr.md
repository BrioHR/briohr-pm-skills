# How to Complete 360 Feedback in BrioHR

*Learn how to give and submit 360 feedback in BrioHR. Follow this step-by-step guide to access tasks, provide constructive feedback, and support your colleagues’ performance improvement.*

### Introduction


In the BrioHR performance management system, managers may assign you as a feedback giver to evaluate a colleague's performance.


When you are selected, you will:


- Receive an **email notification**
- See a new task under your **Pending Actions panel.**


Providing feedback is an essential step in the **360 Feedback progress**, helping managers make better decisions and enabling your colleagues to grow professionally.


Managers also benefit by having a **holistic view of employee performance**, making it easier to provide fair evaluations.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Give and Complete 360 Feedback


If you have been assigned to give 360 feedback to a colleague, you will receive an email notification with the following instructions:


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48320188322/original/wQFvtKCmh7ayiEXzPOgzJYThA_2DTOFTgQ.png?1702282493)


#### Step 1 - Log in to BrioHR Account


Log in to your BrioHR account using your registered credentials.


#### Step 2 - Access 360 Feedback Task in Pending Actions


1. On the Home page, click on **Pending Actions**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-02-59-25-1223-AM.png?width=670&height=220&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-02-59-25-1223-AM.png)
**


2. Search for** '360 Feedback'** to filter specific tasks


3. Click the task to open it


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-03-00-10-1191-AM.png?width=670&height=218&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-03-00-10-1191-AM.png)


#### Step 3 - Complete the 360 Feedback Task


1. A pop out window will appear with list of **questions and/or competencies **to review


2. Provide written feedback in the text fields


3. Rate each feedback area using the star rating system


4. Choose one of the following:


- **Save as Draft **- if you plan to complete it later
- **Submit **- if you are ready to finalize your feedback


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-03-01-53-4181-AM.png?width=670&height=313&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-03-01-53-4181-AM.png)

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**1. Can I edit my feedback after submitting it?**


Not directly — you cannot edit it yourself once submitted. If a revision is needed, ask the employee's manager to reopen or send back your feedback — see [How Managers Can Reopen or Send Back 360 Feedback for Revisions](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-managers-can-reopen-or-send-back-360-feedback-for-revisions?hsLang=en). Do still review your answers before submitting.


**2. What happens if I save my feedback as draft?**


Your progress will be saved, and you can return later to complete and submit it.


**3. Will my colleagues know if anyone has given them 360 Feedback?**


No, your colleagues won't know. Only their managers and HR can view the submitting 360 Feedback.
