# How to Apply Leave via BrioHR Web Browser (Desktop & Laptop)

*Learn how to apply leave using a browser login through desktop or laptop effectively. This guide covers step-by-step instructions.*

### Introduction


Applying for leave in BrioHR is easy and flexible. You can apply using either the BrioHR web browser on your desktop or laptop, or through the mobile app. This guide focuses on how to apply for leave using the BrioHR web browser.

If you would like to request for credit leave (eg, request for Replacement credit), please refer to [[this guide](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-request-for-replacement-leave-credit-video?hsLang=en)]

### Step-by-Step Guide: Applying Leave Using a Desktop or Laptop


#### Step 1: Log In and Access Your Leave Dashboard


Log in to your BrioHR account.


1. On the navigation bar, select **My HR > Leave**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-22-2025-06-55-56-4513-AM.png?width=670&height=298&name=image-png-Sep-22-2025-06-55-56-4513-AM.png)


On the **Leave Dashboard**, you will see your Leave entitlement along with the status and history of your leave requests.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-22-2025-06-56-24-8925-AM.png?width=670&height=306&name=image-png-Sep-22-2025-06-56-24-8925-AM.png)


---


#### Step 2: Submit Your Leave Application


1. Click the **+ Apply Leave** button on the Leave Dashboard


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-22-2025-06-56-46-0731-AM.png?width=670&height=306&name=image-png-Sep-22-2025-06-56-46-0731-AM.png)


2. A pop-out window will appear for you to enter your leave details:


- Select the correct **year **(e.g., 2025).
- Choose your **leave type **(e.g., Annual).
- Select your leave start date and end date.


  - For half-day leave, tick the **Half-day AM** (morning) or **Half-day PM **(afternoon) checkbox under the date.


**NOTE:**


The system automatically calculates the total leave days based on your selected dates and times.


- Optionally, add a reason or comment in the **Justification **field.
- Optionally**, attach** any supporting documents.


3. Click **Apply** button to submit your leave application.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-22-2025-07-01-16-0901-AM.png?width=670&height=335&name=image-png-Sep-22-2025-07-01-16-0901-AM.png)





---


#### Step 3: Confirm Submission and Await Approval


After submitting, a confirmation notification will appear in the top-right corner, confirming that your leave application was created successfully.


Your approver(s) will receive an email notification for further processing.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-27-2024-03-12-30-4458-AM.png?width=388&height=142&name=image-png-Dec-27-2024-03-12-30-4458-AM.png)
