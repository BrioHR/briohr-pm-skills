# How to Apply Past Year Leave in the New Year

*Learn how to apply past year leave (such as medical or emergency leave) in the new year using your BrioHR leave dashboard.*

### Introduction


There are situations where employees need to apply for leave taken in the previous year after the new year has already begun. This commonly happens when you return to work after year-end and need to update your leave records in the system.


This guide explains how to apply **past year leave using your current year leave dashboard** in BrioHR.


---


### Common Scenarios for Applying Past Year Leave


### 1. Medical Leave:


You were on medical leave on December 28 and 29, but only returned to work on January 2, and need to submit the leave application afterward.


### 2. Emergency Leave:


You took emergency leave on December 29 and need to apply for it in the system once you resume work in January.


In these cases, you can submit a backdated leave application by switching the leave year in your dashboard.


**DISCLAIMER:**

For illustration purposes:


- 2025 is used as the *previous year*
- 2026 is used as the *current (new) year*


If you are reading this in later years, the applicable years may differ.


---


### Step-by Step Guide: Apply Past Year Leave in the Current Year


#### Step 1 - Log In to Your BrioHR Account


Log in to your BrioHR account using your registered credentials.


#### Step 2 - Access the Leave Dashboard


1.  Go to **My HR **> **Leave**. You will be directed to the current year leave dashboard.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-31-2025-04-17-04-5731-AM.png?width=670&height=298&name=image-png-Dec-31-2025-04-17-04-5731-AM.png)

2.   Click **+Apply Leave.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-31-2025-04-17-12-6217-AM.png?width=670&height=309&name=image-png-Dec-31-2025-04-17-12-6217-AM.png)





---


#### Step 3 - Select the Past Year


1.   In the Apply Leave pop-up window, locate the Year dropdown.


2.  Select the previous year (e.g, 2025)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-31-2025-04-18-03-3240-AM.png?width=670&height=313&name=image-png-Dec-31-2025-04-18-03-3240-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-04-2024-05-50-35-7303-AM.png?width=670&height=315&name=image-png-Oct-04-2024-05-50-35-7303-AM.png)





**NOTE:**


You can only apply for** leave types that do not carry forward**, such as:


*Medical leave, Emergency leave, Unpaid leave*


If you need to apply for a leave type that is not listed (e.g, Annual leave), please contact your HR for assistance.


---


#### Step 4 - Apply for Past Year Leave:


1.   Select the relevant leave type (e.g, Medical leave).


2. Choose the applicable date(s) from the previous year.


3. Click **Submit**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-31-2025-04-21-03-4092-AM.png?width=670&height=359&name=image-png-Dec-31-2025-04-21-03-4092-AM.png)


---


#### Step 5 - Confirm Your Leave Application:


1.    Check the **Leave Requests Status & History** table for the previous year (e.g, 2025)


2.   Ensure that your leave application has been successfully submitted.
