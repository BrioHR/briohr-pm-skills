# How to View Leave Request Status and History in BrioHR

*Learn how employees can check their leave history and request status in BrioHR. Avoid duplicate applications and track leave timelines with ease.*

### Introduction


BrioHR provides employees with a personal leave dashboard where they can view their leave history records for the current year.


This feature helps employees:


- Avoid submitting duplicate leave applications for the same date.
- Track their leave application status.
- Monitor activity timelines for better visibility.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: View Leave Request Status and History


#### Step 1 - Login and MyHR > Leave


Login to your **BrioHR account.**


1. Navigate to **MyHR > Leave.**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-12-2025-07-00-45-1534-AM.png?width=378&height=433&name=image-png-Sep-12-2025-07-00-45-1534-AM.png)
**


---


#### Step 2 - Find Leave Request Status and History Table


1. Scroll to the bottom of the page until you see the table titled **'Leave Request Status and History'**


2. This table displays both **past and upcoming leave records.**


3. From here, you can:


- Check the **status** of your leave applications.
- View the **leave type** taken and **corresponding dates.**


4. Click **See Details** to open more information, including leave type details and an **activity timeline.**

### Why This Matters


By regularly checking the leave request status and history table, employees can ensure their records are accurate, reduce duplicate leave submissions, and stay updated on HR approvals and rejections.
