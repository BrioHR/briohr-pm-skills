# How to View and Download Payslips from BrioHR Mobile App

*Learn how to access and download your payslips using the BrioHR mobile app. Step-by-step instructions for employees.*

### Introduction


The BrioHR Mobile App offers a seamless solution for employees to effortlessly access and download their payslips directly from their mobile devices.


This article provides a step-by-step guide on navigating the app to retrieve payslips.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Accessing Payslips


#### Step 1 - Navigate to the  Home Page


1. - Login to **BrioHR Mobile App**.
  - You will be directed to the Home Page.


Learn more: [How to Download and Login to BrioHR Mobile App (iOS & Android Guide)](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/download-and-login-to-briohr-mobile-app-ios-android-guide?hsLang=en)


---


#### Step 2 - Tap on "Payslips"


1. - From the Home Page, tap the **Payslips** option.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-18-2025-10-03-38-2139-AM.png?width=281&height=603&name=image-png-Aug-18-2025-10-03-38-2139-AM.png)


---


#### Step 3 - Select the Desired Month:


1. - In the **Payslips section**, you can select the specific **month and year** of the payslip you want to view.
  - Payslips are organized in **reverse chronological order** (latest month at the top).


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-18-2025-10-04-17-9520-AM.png?width=574&height=545&name=image-png-Aug-18-2025-10-04-17-9520-AM.png)


---


#### Step 4 - Download Payslips:


1. - After selecting the desired month, tap the **Download** button in the top-right corner of the screen.
  - The file will download instantly to your mobile device.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-18-2025-10-04-49-2467-AM.png?width=288&height=618&name=image-png-Aug-18-2025-10-04-49-2467-AM.png)


---


#### Important Notes for HR/Admin


For employees to view their payslips in the mobile app:


- The **Payroll Module** must be enabled.
- Navigate to:
**Admin Settings → User Group Settings → Employee Modules Permission → Payroll Module**
- Once enabled, employees will see the **Payslips** icon on their app’s Home Page.
