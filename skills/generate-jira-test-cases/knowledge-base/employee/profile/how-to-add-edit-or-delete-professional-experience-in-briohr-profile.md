# How to Add, Edit, or Delete Professional Experience in BrioHR Profile

*Learn how to add, modify, or delete your professional experience in your BrioHR profile. Step-by-step guide with instructions and troubleshooting tips for employees.*

### Introduction


Keeping your BrioHR profile updated with accurate professional experience helps HR and managers maintain complete employee records. This guide walks you through the steps to add, edit, or delete your professional experience in BrioHR.


Related guide: [How to Edit Your Profile on BrioHR](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-edit-your-profile-on-briohr?hsLang=en)


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Add, Modify and Edit Your Professional Experience in Profile


#### Step 1 - Access My Profile


Login to your BrioHR account.


 1.   Go to **Home page **> Click **View my profile.**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-19-2025-07-53-46-0276-AM.png?width=688&height=193&name=image-png-Aug-19-2025-07-53-46-0276-AM.png)
**


---


#### Step 2 - Add a New Professional Experience


Scroll to the **Professional Experience** section.


1. Click **+ (plus) icon** to add a new entry.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-19-2025-07-59-35-8269-AM.png?width=688&height=181&name=image-png-Aug-19-2025-07-59-35-8269-AM.png)


2. Fill in the required details in the **Add Professional Experience** form (e.g., company name, role, duration).


3. Click **Save** to confirm.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-19-2025-07-59-48-0106-AM.png?width=688&height=370&name=image-png-Aug-19-2025-07-59-48-0106-AM.png)


---


#### Step 3 - Modify or Delete Existing Professional Experience


**1. Edit Experience:**


- Click the **pencil icon** next to the entry you want to update.
- Make the necessary changes and save.


**2. Delete Experience:**


- Click the **bin (trash) icon** to remove an entry.
- Confirm deletion when prompted.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-19-2025-08-00-20-6718-AM.png?width=688&height=205&name=image-png-Aug-19-2025-08-00-20-6718-AM.png)


---


#### Important Notes


- If the **add/edit/delete buttons** are not available in your account, it means you do not have permission to make changes.
- In this case, please contact your **Admin or HR personnel** for assistance.


---


#### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


**Q1: Why can't I add or edit my professional experience?**


A: This usually happens if your HR Admin has not granted permission for you to edit your profile. Please reach out to them for access.


**Q2: Can HR Admin edit my professional experience for me?**


A: Yes, if you don't have access, your HR or Admin can update your profile details on your behalf.


**Q3: Can I add multiple job experiences at once?**


A: Yes. You can add multiple entries to reflect different roles, companies, or career stages.


**Q4: Where else can I update my personal details?**


A: In addition to professional experience, you can also update your education details, profile, photo, bio, and gallery.
