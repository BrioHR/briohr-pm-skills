# How to Edit Your Profile in BrioHR

*Learn how to edit your profile in BrioHR with this step-by-step guide. Update your profile photo, personal details, professional experience, and education section effortlessly. Contact Admin or HR if you need additional permissions.*

If you have **editing permission**s in BrioHR, updating your personal profile is fast and intuitive. This guide walks you through each step such as editing your profile photo, bio, gallery, work experience, and education.


**NOTE:**


**If you do not have the permission, please contact your Admin or Human Resources personnel to make the necessary changes to your personal details.**


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Edit Your Personal Profile


#### Step 1 - Access My Profile


Login to your BrioHR account.


 1.   Go to **Home page **> Click **View my profile.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48291391706/original/hvGnqij6pUu87Ciw6HCe4Oif9_qSM3HpKw.png?1680769550)


---


#### Step 2 - Update Your Profile Photo


1. Hover your cursor over the avatar,


2. Click on "**Change**" next to the pencil icon to update your profile photo.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48291391843/original/X67rJSBq74TxLfQe6S05F32Q_n4u36bN8Q.png?1680769593)


**Learn more: [How to Add or Edit Your Profile Photo](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-add-or-edit-your-profile-picture?hsLang=en)**


---


#### Step 3 - Update the About (Bio) Section & Photo Gallery


1. Click the pencil icon in your About section to open the editor.


- Update your personal introduction or description.


2. Click the **+Add Photo** button to upload images to your gallery section.


**NOTE:**
**Maximum of 10 photos can be uploaded in the Photo Gallery**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48291392195/original/dcAXIwP7pSJW562jzwwy9YMoSR4YYBl3ug.png?1680769710)


---


#### Step 4 - Update Your Personal Details


1. Click the pencil icon to update your **personal details** such as home address, date of birth and others.


**NOTE:**
**If you do not see this 'Personal Details' section or unable to edit, it is because of your user group access permission. Please contact your HR for assistance.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-15-2025-08-40-57-2413-AM.png?width=670&height=156&name=image-png-Aug-15-2025-08-40-57-2413-AM.png)


---


#### Step 5 - Add or Update Professional Experience and Education Details


1. Click the **plus (+) icon** beside **"Professional Experience"**


- Fill in the provided template (pop-out window) with your previous job details
- To edit existing record, click the** pencil icon**.


2. Click the **plus (+) icon** in the** Education **section to add your academic background


- To edit existing record, click the **pencil icon**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-15-2025-08-42-39-3438-AM.png?width=670&height=241&name=image-png-Aug-15-2025-08-42-39-3438-AM.png)

**Troubleshooting & Permissions**


If any edit options (Add/Edit/Delete) are unavailable, you likely lack the necessary permissions. Please contact your **Admin or HR personnel** for access.


For troubleshooting profile image issues—such as uploads failing due to large file size—refer to the article [Unable to Upload Your Profile Picture? Here’s What to Do](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/unable-to-upload-your-profile-picture-heres-what-to-do?hsLang=en) for recommended file sizes and solutions
