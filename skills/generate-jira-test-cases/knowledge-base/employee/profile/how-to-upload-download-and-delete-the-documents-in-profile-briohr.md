# How to Upload, Download and Delete the Documents in Profile BrioHR?

*Learn how to upload, download, and delete documents in BrioHR. Step-by-step guide with tips on file size limits and document management for employees.*

###


**NOTE:**


**If you do not have the permission, please contact your Admin or Human Resources personnel to make the necessary changes to your personal details.**


**Note for Admin or HR personnel:**


- **For Non-Admin User Groups, please ensure that the permissions “View Own” and “Modify Own” are enabled for the 'Documents' section under Profile Sensitive Data if you want users to view and edit their own Documents under their profile. **
**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-05-2026-01-57-13-7010-AM.png?width=670&height=491&name=image-png-Mar-05-2026-01-57-13-7010-AM.png)
**


### Introduction


The **Documents **section in BrioHR allows employees to upload, download, and manage important files directly from their profile.


This feature ensures that personal and work-related documents are securely stored and easily accessible.


In this guide, you will learn how to:


- Upload documents to your profile
- Download documents for personal use.
- Delete documents you no longer need.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Managing Documents in Profile BrioHR


#### Step 1 - Accessing the Documents Section


1. Log in to your BrioHR account.


2. From the **Home page**, click on** My Profile**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-36-26-7973-AM.png?width=670&height=217&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-36-26-7973-AM.png)
**


3. Navigate to the **Documents** section


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-37-10-4646-AM.png?width=670&height=243&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-37-10-4646-AM.png)


---


#### A) Uploading Documents


1. Click on the **Upload file**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-41-03-8566-AM.png?width=670&height=243&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-41-03-8566-AM.png)
**


2. Select a file from your folder and click **Open**


3. The file will appear in the Documents section once uploaded.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-42-45-9504-AM.png?width=670&height=282&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-42-45-9504-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-42-43-1886-AM.png?width=670&height=246&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-42-43-1886-AM.png)


**NOTE: **


The maximum file size allowed is **10MB.**


---


#### B) Downloading Documents


1. In the **Documents section**, click on the **three-dot (⋮) icon** next to the file.


2. Select **Download**.


3. A confirmation message will appear: *“Download Successful”*.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-45-04-6746-AM.png?width=670&height=244&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-45-04-6746-AM.png)


---


#### C) Deleting Documents


1. Click on the **Delete** option next to the document.


2. Confirm by clicking the **Confirm button**.


3. The document will be permanently removed from your profile.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-45-19-4754-AM.png?width=670&height=244&name=image-png-Oct-02-2025-09-45-19-4754-AM.png)

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**Q1: What is the maximum file size I can upload in BrioHR? **


A: You can upload documents up to **10MB** in size.


**Q2: Can I upload multiple documents at once?**


A: Currently, documents must be uploaded one at a time.


**Q3: Can deleted documents be recovered?**


A: No, once deleted, documents cannot be recovered. Please ensure you have backups before deletion.
