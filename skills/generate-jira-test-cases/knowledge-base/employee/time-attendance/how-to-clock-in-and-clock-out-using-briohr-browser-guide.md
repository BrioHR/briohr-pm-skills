# How to Clock In and Clock Out Using BrioHR (Browser Guide)

*Learn how to perform first clock in and final clock out using BrioHR’s browser-based system. Step-by-step guide for employees to track their working hours effectively.*

### Introduction


Clocking in and out is an essential part of **managing your work hours** in BrioHR. With the **browser-based system**, employees can easily record their start and end times.


The system automatically tracks:


- **Work duration**
- **Overtime**
- **Lateness**


This guide explains the step-by-step process of clocking in and clocking out using BrioHR’s web platform.


---


### Step-by-Step to Clock In and Clock Out through Web Browser


#### Step 1 - Find the Clock In Section on the Home Page


Login to your BrioHR account.


1. From the **Home page,** look for the **Clock In** section.
2. This section may display:


  - Work duration hours
  - Maximum clock-in time (if applicable)
  - Shift name (if applicable)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-20-2025-06-09-15-4502-AM.png?width=688&height=207&name=image-png-Aug-20-2025-06-09-15-4502-AM.png)


---


#### Step 2 - Clocking In At the Start of Your Workday


1. To begin your workday, click the **Clock-In** button.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-20-2025-06-10-32-6408-AM.png?width=688&height=207&name=image-png-Aug-20-2025-06-10-32-6408-AM.png)


2.** **A** “Confirm Clock In” **sidebar will appear, displaying the following details:


- **Time:** Your current clock-in time.
- **When:** The corresponding clock-in date.
- **Note:** An optional field where you can add a note or comment before clocking in.


3. Click **Clock In** to confirm and proceed.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-20-2025-06-16-32-1220-AM.png?width=425&height=433&name=image-png-Aug-20-2025-06-16-32-1220-AM.png)


####


---


#### Step 3 - Clocking out at the End of Your Workday


1. At the end of your workday, go back to the **Clock In section**.
2. Click the **Clock-Out** button.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-20-2025-06-28-38-4210-AM.png?width=688&height=201&name=image-png-Aug-20-2025-06-28-38-4210-AM.png)


3. A** “Confirm Clock Out” **sidebar will appear, displaying the following details:


- **Time:** Your current clock-out time.


- **When:** The corresponding clock-out date.
- **Note:** An optional field where you can add a note or comment before clocking out


**NOTE:**


The system will record your total hours worked and track any overtime or lateness (if applicable)

**Q2: Can I edit my clock-in or clock-out time in BrioHR?**


**A: **No, employees cannot edit attendance records directly. If there is a mistake, please contact your HR Admin.


**Q3: What happens if I forgot to clock out?**


**A:** If you forget to clock out, your HR Admin or Manager may adjust your timesheet based on company policies.


**Q4: Where can I view my attendance records?**


**A: **You can check your monthly attendance Timesheets from **My HR > Time Attendance > Timesheets.**
