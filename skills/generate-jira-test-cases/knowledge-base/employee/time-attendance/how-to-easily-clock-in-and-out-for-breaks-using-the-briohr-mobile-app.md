# How to Easily Clock In & Out for Breaks Using the BrioHR Mobile App

*Learn how to clock in and out for breaks using the BrioHR Mobile App. Ensure accurate time tracking and avoid overtime miscalculations with this step-by-step guide.*

Clocking out and back in for break time is now easier with the BrioHR Mobile App.

The duration recorded between clocking out for a break and clocking back in will be reflected in the employee's daily timesheet, ensuring accurate calculation of working hours for any given day.


---


### Clocking Out Before Break


Login to your BrioHR mobile app


1. Go to the main page
2. Look for the** Time Attendance** box
3. To clock out before taking a break, tap the **Clock-Out** button.
4. A confirmation pop-up will appear.
5. Click **Take Break** to proceed.
6. You are now clocked out before going on break.

### Clocking Back In After Break


Login to your BrioHR mobile app


1. Go to the main page
2. Look for the** Time Attendance** box
3. To clock back in after your break, tap the **Clock-In** button.
4. A confirmation pop-up will appear.
5. Click **Confirm Clock In** to proceed.
6. You are now clocked back in after your break.

#### What if I Forgot to Clock Out and Clock In for My Break?


If you forget to clock out and clock in for your break, the system will not calculate the break duration in your daily timesheet. This may result in additional hours being counted as **overtime**.


In such cases, please contact your Manager or HR for assistance. They can manually adjust your break time in the system.


#### Can HR/Manager Adjust My Break Time Manually in BrioHR?


Yes, your HR and Manager has access permission to manually key in the break time duration to ensure accurate working hours recorded in your daily timesheet.
