# Requesting Pre-Approved Overtime as an Employee

*This article explains how Employees can request for pre-approved overtime function in the system using the Time Attendance module.*

#### Audience & Scope


Audience: Employee


Module: Time Attendance


Country: Global


Pre-requisites: Access to Time Attendance module in MyHR





#### Step-by-Step Instructions for Employees to request Pre-Approved Overtime


1. Hover over **MyHR** and click into the **Time Attendance** module


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-27-2026-02-28-18-9952-AM.png?width=670&height=177&name=image-png-Feb-27-2026-02-28-18-9952-AM.png)
2. Proceed to click into the **Work Schedule** tab and into the **date** which you would like to assign the Overtime in advance


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-27-2026-02-30-56-4301-AM.png?width=670&height=280&name=image-png-Feb-27-2026-02-30-56-4301-AM.png)
3. Click on the **Request OT** dropdown, then click on **Create OT** request


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-27-2026-02-31-35-0345-AM.png?width=670&height=273&name=image-png-Feb-27-2026-02-31-35-0345-AM.png)
4. Fill in the required information such as the **Date** and** Duration** of the Overtime, if you have any **Reasons/Remark** and **Attachments**, be sure to have this information filled in as well


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-27-2026-02-33-06-5143-AM.png?width=650&height=527&name=image-png-Feb-27-2026-02-33-06-5143-AM.png)
5. Now that all information has been filled in, click on **Submit** and the Overtime is now pending for approval
6. You will be able to refer to the **Request List** of all Overtime submissions , click on the Request OT dropdown and choose **View All Requests**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-27-2026-02-37-51-1579-AM.png?width=670&height=265&name=image-png-Feb-27-2026-02-37-51-1579-AM.png)
7. You will be able to **view all Overtime requests** submitted as well as the **status** the request is in.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-27-2026-02-39-22-4789-AM.png?width=670&height=207&name=image-png-Feb-27-2026-02-39-22-4789-AM.png)
8. Once the Overtime submission has been approved, there will be an email notification received in your inbox
