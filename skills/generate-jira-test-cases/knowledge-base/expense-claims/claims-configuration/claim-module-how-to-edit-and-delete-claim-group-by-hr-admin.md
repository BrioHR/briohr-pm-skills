# Claim Module - How to edit and delete claim group (by HR/Admin)

*This is a step-by-step guide on how to edit and delete claim group by HR or Admin*

Login to your BrioHR account.


1. At H**R Lounge**, click on **Expense Claims** menu.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-28-2024-09-13-10-4643-AM.png?width=688&height=282&name=image-png-Aug-28-2024-09-13-10-4643-AM.png)





2. Click on **Claim Groups tab.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-28-2024-09-13-22-1813-AM.png?width=688&height=319&name=image-png-Aug-28-2024-09-13-22-1813-AM.png)


**To edit Claim Groups**


3. Click on **pencil icon** to edit respective claim group.


4. You can edit **Group name** to rename the name, or you can tick the checkbox in **Claim type** to choose the same item that would be in the same group.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-28-2024-09-17-02-0907-AM.png?width=688&height=466&name=image-png-Aug-28-2024-09-17-02-0907-AM.png)


5. Click on **Save button** to save the setting.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-28-2024-09-17-19-3373-AM.png?width=688&height=296&name=image-png-Aug-28-2024-09-17-19-3373-AM.png)





**To delete Claim Groups**


6.  Click on the **trash bin icon** to delete the respective claim group.


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-28-2024-09-18-36-5251-AM.png?width=688&height=319&name=image-png-Aug-28-2024-09-18-36-5251-AM.png)
**


7.  Click on **Delete button** to confirm delete the respective claim group, or click on **Cancel button** to cancel the deletion.


**

**
