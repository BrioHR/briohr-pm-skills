# Custom Payroll Integration for Cash Advance Requests & Settlements

*This article explains how to customize payroll integration so cash advance claims appear as a distinct pay item during the payroll cycle.*

This tutorial explains the steps to customize the payroll integration for cash advance claims. To ensure that "cash advance claim" appears as a distinct pay item during the payroll cycle, it's essential to follow these steps:


**Step 1:** Create a custom pay item and categorize it under "Reimbursement." For detailed instructions on creating a custom pay item, please refer [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-create-a-custom-pay-item-in-briohr?hsLang=en).


**Step 2:** Customize the payroll connection settings by navigating to the "**Edit Claim Types**" section.
