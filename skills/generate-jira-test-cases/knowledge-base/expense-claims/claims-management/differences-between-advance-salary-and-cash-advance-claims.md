# Differences between Advance Salary and Cash Advance Claims

*Learn the difference between advance salary and cash advance claims, including when to use the Claims or Payroll module for accurate processing and reporting.*

**Audience & Scope**


Audience: HR Admin


Module: Payroll & Expense Claims


Country: Global


Pre-requisites: HR Lounge access for Payroll and Expense Claim module





Understanding the distinction between advance salary and cash advance claims helps ensure accurate processing and compliance within your HR and Payroll workflows.


#### a. Cash Advance (Expense Claims Module)


A **cash advance** refers to funds provided to employees for business-related expenses, such as travel or project costs. These are managed within the Expense Claims module via **HR Lounge > Expense Claims**, where employees can request and reconcile expenses accordingly.





#### b. Advance Salary (Payroll Module)


Advance salary, on the other hand, is when an employee receives a portion of their salary before the official payday. This is not handled in the Claims module, but instead processed through the *Payroll* module. There are two common ways to manage this:


- Run an **ad-hoc payroll cycle** to process the early payment, or
- Add a **custom pay item** within the current payroll cycle


By using the correct module for each scenario, organizations can maintain clear records, streamline approvals, and ensure accurate financial reporting.
