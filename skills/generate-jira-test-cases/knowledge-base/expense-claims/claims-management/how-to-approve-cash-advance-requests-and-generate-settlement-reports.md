# How to Approve Cash Advance Requests and Generate Settlement Reports

*This article explains how to view and approve cash advance request claims and generate settlement reports for approved advances.*

In this tutorial, we'll cover the following steps:


1. How to View and Approve Cash Advance Request Claims

  1. Learn how to access and approve submitted cash advance request claims. (See Image 1)
2. How to Send Approved Cash Advance Requests to Payroll

  1. Understand the process for forwarding approved cash advance requests to the payroll system.
3. Payroll Cycle View of Approved Cash Advance Claims

  1. See how the approved cash advance claims appear in the payroll cycle, specifically for custom pay items. (See Image 2)
  2. For instructions on customizing pay items for specific cash advance claim types, click [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/custom-payroll-integration-for-cash-advance-requests-settlements?hsLang=en).
  3. For general payroll connection configuration, click here.
4. Cash Advance Settlement Claim Report Generation

  1. Once a claim is reimbursed, a new report labeled "Cash Advance Settlement" will appear on the employee's My Claims page. (See Image 3)
