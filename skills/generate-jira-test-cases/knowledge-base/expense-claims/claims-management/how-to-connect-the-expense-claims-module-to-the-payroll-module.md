# How to Connect the Expense Claims Module to the Payroll Module

*This article explains how HR Admins can connect the Expense Claims module to the Payroll module so approved claims flow into payroll.*

**Audience & Scope**


Audience: HR Admin


Module: Expense Claims & Payroll


Country: Malaysia, Singapore


Pre-requisites: HR Module Permission for Expense Claims and Payroll modules


---


There are two ways to connect the Expense Claims module to the Payroll module. Follow the steps below for detailed instructions.


**For more information on how to transfer approved claims to payroll, click [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-transfer-approved-expense-claims-to-payroll?hsLang=en).**


**For more information on how to manage approved claims in the Payroll module, click [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/managing-approved-claims-in-the-payroll-module?hsLang=en).**


---


### A. Default-Linked Payroll Items (General Settings)


**NOTE:**


**These general settings apply to all claim types.**


**To customize individual claim types linked to specific Payroll Center(s) under Claim Types settings, refer to the steps under "B. CUSTOM-LINKED PAYROLL ITEMS (CUSTOM SETTINGS)". **


1.   In **HR Lounge**, go to **Expense Claims > Settings.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-08-2026-04-43-57-0513-AM.png?width=670&height=323&name=image-png-Jul-08-2026-04-43-57-0513-AM.png)
[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48233419824/original/0tGuOXGU-HVdwfzodIy9OXNm8abztsPfqA.png?1655691237)


2.   Scroll down to the **Payroll Connection** column. **Here, link the payroll center to the payroll item.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-08-2026-04-46-24-5462-AM.png?width=670&height=300&name=image-png-Jul-08-2026-04-46-24-5462-AM.png)


**NOTE:**


**This is the default-linked payroll item for every claim type. Under the General settings, the "Expense Claims" payroll item and "Cash Advance Settlement" will be linked to the Payroll Center(s) by default.**


- **The "Expense Claims" payroll item is a default item under the "Reimbursement" category, which can be found in HR Lounge > Payroll > Settings > Payroll Items.**
- **The "Cash Advance Settlement" payroll item is a default item under the "Cash-Advance-Settlement" category.**





![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-08-2026-04-50-39-1155-AM.png?width=670&height=310&name=image-png-Jul-08-2026-04-50-39-1155-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-08-2026-04-53-19-1241-AM.png?width=670&height=203&name=image-png-Jul-08-2026-04-53-19-1241-AM.png)


**NOTE:**


- **You may also add custom payroll items in Payroll > Settings > Payroll items > '+Create custom item', which can later be selected in Expense Claims General Settings as a payroll item to be linked to the Payroll Center. When creating a custom payroll item:**

  - **For "Payroll Expense Items," select the Type as "Addition" and the Category as "Reimbursement."**
  - **For "Payroll Deduction Items," select the Type as "Deduction" and the Category as "Cash Advance Settlement."**


**For more information on how to create a custom pay item, please refer [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-create-a-custom-pay-item-in-briohr?hsLang=en).**

### B. Custom-Linked Payroll Items (Based on Claim Type)


**NOTE:**


**These custom settings apply only to specific claim types, linking a specific claim type to a specific payroll item.**


1.   In **HR Lounge**, go to **Expense Claims > Claim Types**.
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-08-2026-06-11-48-3555-AM.png?width=670&height=320&name=image-png-Jul-08-2026-06-11-48-3555-AM.png)


2.   Click on the **Edit **icon for the specific claim type you wish to link to a payroll item.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48327507807/original/dyZ51DrX-D9crzxXDTK_oN64caeoDR_4GQ.png?1708314742)


4.   Click on **Payroll Connection** and select the payroll item to which the claim type should be linked, according to the Payroll Center(s). Click on the **Save **icon to complete the action.


**NOTE:**


**The items in this list correspond to all payroll items under the "Reimbursement" category in the Payroll module.**
