# How to View Claim History for Submitted and Approved Requests

*This article explains how to view a claim's history from the point of submission in the Team or HR Lounge.*

**NOTE:**


**The claim history begins from the point of submission, not from the creation of the claim request.**


### Steps to View Claim History:


1.   In the **Team/HR Lounge**, navigate to **Expense Claims** and select **Claim Request**.


2.   To view a summary of the claim history, hover your mouse over the **See History** icon in the **ACTIONS **column.
