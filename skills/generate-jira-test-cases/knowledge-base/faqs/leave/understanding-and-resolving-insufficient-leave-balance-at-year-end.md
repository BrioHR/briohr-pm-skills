# Understanding and Resolving Insufficient Leave Balance at Year-End

*This article explains why employees may see insufficient leave balance errors at year-end and how to resolve them.*

As the year draws to a close, numerous employees are in the process of applying for leave in January 2025. Many of them are utilizing either their 2025 leave entitlement balance or their carried-over balance from 2024.


You might encounter instances where an employee attempting to apply for leave in the current year sees an error message: "**You do not have sufficient annual entitlement to book this leave.**"


#### Why Does This Happen?


This tutorial will guide you through a systematic, step-by-step process to identify the reasons behind the insufficient leave balance issue.
