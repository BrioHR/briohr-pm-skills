# How to Resolve "Unable to Compute Payslip" Warning

*This article explains how to resolve the 'Unable to compute payslip' warning by enabling EPF for Malaysian payroll centers.*

When running payroll for the first time in BrioHR, particularly for a new payroll center, you may encounter the following warning message:


**"Unable to compute payslip: EPF should be enabled for Malaysian payroll centers to ensure accurate PCB calculation."**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48323793835/original/o7a8C-Dk33cAUJOQNhJwarRogf-2xK7BDA.png?1705467617)


### Why Does This Happen?


This error occurs because the EPF (Employees Provident Fund) has not been selected for your payroll center. To prevent this, you must ensure that EPF is enabled in the [Organizations] section, regardless of whether an individual employee is contributing to the EPF.


Important: You are enabling EPF at the company/payroll center level, not at the employee level. For information on enabling an individual employee's EPF, please refer to [this article](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-update-employee-payroll-details?hsLang=en).


### How to Resolve This Warning:


To resolve this issue, follow these steps to enable EPF for your payroll center. For more information, please refer to [this article](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-set-up-company-payroll-general-settings-in-malaysia?hsLang=en)


Log in to BrioHR.


- Navigate to the **HR Lounge**.
- Click on **Payroll** to access the payroll dashboard.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48344584003/original/ydpqTxklgGT_kuggdSHDTABk8LZGtTI4hg.png?1721807232)


- Select **Settings**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48344584042/original/hFFiq6_XcAPeWQJz5T9diXPCyK98f8f8LQ.png?1721807248)


- Go to the **General **tab.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48344584062/original/HULCLDjtBcNY-TWh4KQGuBQ9PqQw4aI8Zw.png?1721807261)


- In the **Registration Information** section, locate the Organizations section.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48344584068/original/v6mRz4ZRlOOnaoD6WYM8V4SnS_FJjs-Q6g.png?1721807270)


- Locate the section for **Organizations**.


**NOTE:**


**Check the boxes for EPF and any other relevant statutory bodies your company contributes to.**


- Click the **Save** button to apply the changes.


Once you’ve completed these steps, you can proceed with the payroll cycle without encountering the warning message.
