# Troubleshooting Profile Picture Upload Issues in BrioHR

*This article explains common reasons profile picture uploads fail in BrioHR and how to resolve them.*

### Introduction


If you’re experiencing difficulties uploading your **profile picture in BrioHR**, don’t worry—it’s usually due to simple, fixable reasons. By identifying the cause, you can quickly resolve the issue and successfully upload your photo.


---


### Common Reasons Why Profile Picture Upload May Fail


#### 1. File Size Is Too Large


BrioHR has a **recommended file size limit between 1MB and 10MB**.


- If your photo exceeds this size, the upload may fail.
- To fix this, **resize or compress the photo** before re-uploading.


#### 2. File Format Compatibility


Ensure your profile picture is in a **supported format**, such as:


- **.jpg**
- **.jpeg**
- **.png**


Other formats may not upload successfully.


#### 3. Browser or Access Permission Restrictions


- Sometimes, your **HR/Admin may disable the profile picture upload option** for employees.
- If this is the case, you will need to contact your **HR or Admin team** for assistance.


---


### Recommended Next Steps


1. Check your **file size and format**.
2. Try uploading from a **computer web browser**, as the mobile app currently does not support profile picture uploads.
3. If the issue persists, contact your **HR/Admin** or reach out to the **BrioHR Support Team**.


For detailed instructions on uploading or editing you profile picture, refer to our guide: [How to Upload or Change Your Profile Picture in BrioHR](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-add-or-edit-your-profile-picture?hsLang=en)
