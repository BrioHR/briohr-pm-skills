# BrioHR Single Sign-On (SSO): Streamline Your Login with Google, Microsoft, and Okta

*Learn how to log in to BrioHR using Single Sign-On (SSO) with Google, Microsoft, or Okta. Step-by-step guide for easier and more secure login.*

### What is Single Sign-On (SSO)?


Single Sign-On (SSO) is a secure authentication method that allows you to log in to BrioHR using your existing email and password from trusted providers. With SSO, you only need to log in once, eliminating the need to remember multiple passwords.


Currently, BrioHR supports SSO through Google, Microsoft and Okta.


### Benefits of Using SSO with BrioHR


- **Convenience **- Log in quickly without entering multiple credentials.
- **Security **- Reduce password fatigue and strengthen access control,
- **Efficiency** - One-click login saves time for employees.


---


### How to Use SSO with BrioHR


#### Step 1 - Open the BrioHR Login Page


Visit the official [BrioHR Login Page](https://app.briohr.com/signin?redirect=%2Fhome).


#### Step 2 - Locate the "Login to Your Account" Section


At the top-right corner of the page, you will see the login options.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-01-2025-02-37-03-5397-AM.png?width=2000&height=839&name=image-png-Oct-01-2025-02-37-03-5397-AM.png)


#### Step 3 - Select Your Email Provider


Depending on your registered email provider, choose one of the following:


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-01-2025-02-37-17-5334-AM.png?width=670&height=281&name=image-png-Oct-01-2025-02-37-17-5334-AM.png)


- ##### Login with Google


  - Click **"Google"**
  - **Username: **Your registered BrioHR email (Google account)
  - **Password: **Your Google account password


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-01-2025-02-37-56-2235-AM.png?width=337&height=426&name=image-png-Oct-01-2025-02-37-56-2235-AM.png)


- ##### Login with Microsoft


  - Click **"Microsoft"**
  - **Username: **Your registered BrioHR email (Microsoft account)
  - **Password: **Your Microsoft account password


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-01-2025-02-38-19-1728-AM.png?width=372&height=464&name=image-png-Oct-01-2025-02-38-19-1728-AM.png)


- ##### Login with Okta


  - Click** "Okta"**
  - **Username: **Your registered BrioHR email (Okta account)
  - **Password: **Your Okta account password


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-01-2025-02-39-52-4181-AM.png?width=487&height=301&name=image-png-Oct-01-2025-02-39-52-4181-AM.png)





Once verified, you will be redirected to your BrioHR Home page.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**Q1: Can I use any email provider to log in with SSO?
**
A: No. BrioHR currently supports SSO only for Google, Microsoft, and Okta accounts.


**Q2: Do I need to set up SSO in my BrioHR account first?
**
A: No setup is required if your registered email is from Google, Microsoft, or Okta. You can directly log in using the relevant button.


**Q3: What if my company uses a different email provider (other than Google, Microsoft and Okta) ?
**
A: You will need to log in using the standard BrioHR username and password.


**Q4: Is SSO more secure than the standard login method?
**
A: Yes, SSO reduces the need for multiple passwords and leverages the advanced security features of trusted providers like Google, Microsoft, and Okta.
