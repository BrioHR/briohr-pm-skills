# Thai Language is Now Available in brioHR

*Learn how to switch your brioHR interface to Thai. Follow a few simple steps in User Settings to enable the new Thai language option, with changes applied instantly.*

**Audience & Scope**


Audience: All users


Country: Global


Pre-requisites: Reach out to Chat Support to enable this feature within the account.





We are excited to announce that **Thai** is now available as a language option in brioHR. Users can now switch their system language to Thai for a more localized and user-friendly experience.


**IMPORTANT:**


Please reach out to brioHR Chat Support or send us an email at support@briohr.com to **request for this language to be enabled for your account first**.


#### How to Change Your Language to Thai


Follow these simple steps to enable the Thai language:


1. Click on your **Profile** icon in the top-right corner of the screen.
2. Select **User Settings**.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-02-2026-12-29-04-8569-AM.png?width=373&height=238&name=image-png-Jul-02-2026-12-29-04-8569-AM.png)
3. Under **Change Language**, click the dropdown menu.
4. Select **Thai** from the list of available languages.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-01-2026-02-38-32-3685-AM.png?width=670&height=292&name=image-png-Jul-01-2026-02-38-32-3685-AM.png)
5. The language change will be **applied automatically**—no additional steps are required.





#### IMPORTANT:


- The language preference is applied at the **user level**, allowing each user to choose their preferred display language independently.
- Once selected, the brioHR interface will immediately display in Thai.
