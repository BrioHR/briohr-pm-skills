# Understanding the Different Log In Methods in brioHR

*Learn the different ways employees can log in to brioHR, including activation email, new joiner account login, and Single Sign-On (SSO) using Google, Microsoft, or Okta. Understand the steps for each login method and how to change your password after your first login.*

#### Audience & Scope


Audience: HR Admin / Manager / Employee


Country: Global





brioHR provides multiple login methods to help employees access their accounts easily and securely. Depending on how the employee account was created and configured, employees may log in using one of the following methods:


1. **New Joiner Account Login**
2. **Activation Email Login**
3. **Single Sign-On (SSO) Login**
**
**


This article explains how each login method works and the steps employees need to follow to access their brioHR account.





| Login Method | When It Is Used | Login Credentials Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New Joiner Account Login | Employee account is created during onboarding | Credentials provided in the account creation email |
| Activation Email | Employee receives an activation invitation from the admin | Registered email + system-generated password (first login) |
| Single Sign-On (SSO) | Employee uses Google, Microsoft, or Okta authentication | Registered email + SSO provider credentials |





####


#### 1. Login via New Joiner Account Creation


For employees who have been onboarded through brioHR, their account may already be created by the company as part of the onboarding process.


##### Step 1: Receive the Account Notification Email


When the employer creates an account for a new joiner, the employee will receive an email notification informing them that their brioHR account has been created.


The email will include their login credentials required to access the system, example shown below.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-17-2026-10-00-43-0592-AM.png?width=670&height=393&name=image-png-Jul-17-2026-10-00-43-0592-AM.png)





##### Step 2: Access BrioHR


Employees can click on the **Go To BrioHR** button within the email.


They will then be redirected to the brioHR login page, where they can enter the provided credentials to access their account.


After logging in successfully, employees may update their password through their user settings if required.


####


#### 2. Login via Activation Email


The activation email method is commonly used when an admin creates an employee account and sends an invitation for the employee to activate their brioHR access.


##### Step 1: Receive the Activation Email


Once the admin sends the activation email, the employee will receive an email containing a link to activate their brioHR account.


Within the email, the employee will also receive a **system-generated password**, which is required for their first login. Example shown below:


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-17-2026-10-02-14-8136-AM.png?width=670&height=374&name=image-png-Jul-17-2026-10-02-14-8136-AM.png)


##### Step 2: Activate the Account and Log In


After clicking on the **Complete Activation** button, employees can proceed to the brioHR login page.


To log in for the first time, employees will need to enter:


- **Username : Email address** registered with brioHR
- **System-generated password** provided in the activation email


Once the credentials are entered successfully, the employee will be granted access to their brioHR account.


##### Step 3: Change the Password


After successfully logging in, employees can update their password to a preferred password for future access.


To change the password:


1. Click on the **Profile icon**.
2. Select **User Settings**.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-17-2026-10-07-46-6453-AM.png?width=670&height=169&name=image-png-Jul-17-2026-10-07-46-6453-AM.png)
3. Click **Change Password**.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-17-2026-10-08-26-1872-AM.png?width=670&height=310&name=image-png-Jul-17-2026-10-08-26-1872-AM.png)
4. Enter the new password and save the changes.


Once the password has been updated, employees can log out and log in again using their new password.





#### 3. Login via Single Sign-On (SSO)


Employees can also access brioHR using Single Sign-On (SSO) if their registered email address is supported by one of the available identity providers:


- Google
- Microsoft
- Okta


##### How SSO Login Works


When using SSO, employees do not need to enter a separate brioHR password.


Instead, they can:


1. Select the preferred SSO login option (Google, Microsoft, or Okta)

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-17-2026-10-09-23-3783-AM.png?width=670&height=312&name=image-png-Jul-17-2026-10-09-23-3783-AM.png)
2. Enter their email address and password for the selected provider

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-17-2026-10-12-06-2391-AM.png?width=274&height=366&name=image-png-Jul-17-2026-10-12-06-2391-AM.png)

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-17-2026-10-11-14-7902-AM.png?width=276&height=368&name=image-png-Jul-17-2026-10-11-14-7902-AM.png)
3. Complete the authentication process


Once verified, employees will be automatically signed in to their brioHR account.


####


**NOTE**:


The email address used for SSO login must match the email address registered in brioHR. If a different email address is used, the employee will not be able to access their brioHR account through SSO.
