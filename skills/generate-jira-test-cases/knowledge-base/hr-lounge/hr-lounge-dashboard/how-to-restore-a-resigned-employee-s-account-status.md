# How to Restore a Resigned Employee's Account Status

*This article explains how to restore a resigned employee's account status after their end date has passed.*

When an employee's end date is set, their account status automatically changes from 'Active' to 'Inactive' after the resignation date passes.


- For instructions on setting an employee's resignation date in their profile, please refer to [this article](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-deactivate-or-remove-a-resigned-employees-account-in-briohr?hsLang=en).
- For information on finding or viewing a resigned employee's profile, please refer to [this article](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-view-profiles-of-inactive-or-resigned-employees?hsLang=en).


---


### A) For Admin: Restore Account Status from Settings


1.    Go to **Settings**.


2.    Navigate to **Users**.


3.    Search for the employee's name.


4.    Click the **ellipsis (three dots)** icon.


5.    Select **Restore Employee**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48305922204/original/8Oo71MKrQRPuudzqKOExufnQMI4Xaq091A.png?1690487174)


6.    The account status changes from 'Inactive' to 'Active'.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48305923547/original/T6NXZe2RFKvEpdh-LQ5BuzAaDATBZQDUSA.png?1690487985)


### B) For HR: Restore Account Status from HR Lounge


1.    Go to **HR Lounge**.


2.    Search for the employee's name in the Search box.


3.    Filter the status to '**Inactive**'.


4.    Click the **ellipsis (three dots)** icon.


5.    Select **Restore Employee.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48305923310/original/CWBxd6gcRTqa-fUXVj3tp6ISMtbBkabg1w.png?1690487852)


6.    The account status changes from 'Inactive' to 'Active'.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48305923485/original/1NTX570QVknCWDoHlHnRWvar0wFOCNUp-g.png?1690487952)
