# How the Enable Tolerance Amount Works in the Leave Module

*The Enable Tolerance Amount feature in the leave module allows employees to take leave even with a negative balance, within a predefined limit.*

The **Enable Tolerance Amount** feature in the leave module allows employees to take leave even with a negative balance, provided the negative balance is within a set limit. This feature helps companies offer more flexibility in leave management.


### How to Set Up the Enable Tolerance Amount in Leave Policy


Login to BrioHR account


1. Go to **HR Loung**e > **Leave** > **Leave Policies**
2. Look for **Annual leave** and click  on **View leave details**
3. Select the relevant leave policy under **Annual Leave**
4. Go to **General Configuration **and scroll down to** Entitlement** section
5. Tick the checkbox of** Enable Tolerance Amount **and the desired number in the **Set Tolerance Amount** field.
6. Scroll to the bottom of the policy and click **Save Settings.**

### What is the Common Use Case for This Function


In the example below, Company Z’s annual leave policy is set as follows:


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-03-2024-07-42-49-2144-AM.png?width=688&height=216&name=image-png-Sep-03-2024-07-42-49-2144-AM.png)


- **Yearly Entitlement:** 12 Days
- **Earned Leave:** Enabled (Accrual Type: Monthly)


Employees of Company Z earn 1 day of leave for every month worked (12 days / 12 months).


- **Tolerance Amount:** Enabled
- **Tolerance Limit : **2 Days


In January, half of Company Z's employees want to apply for 3 days of Chinese New Year leave. Normally, they wouldn’t be able to do this, as they only accrue 1 day of leave in January.

However, by enabling the **Tolerance Amount** feature and setting a limit of 2 days, the system allows employees to take more leave days than they currently have in their balance, with a balance limit to -2 days.


- **Earned Balance:** 1 Day
- **Borrowed Balance:** 2 Days
- **Number of Leave Days Applied :** 3 Days
- **Final Annual Leave Balance (in January): **-2 Days


As a result, Company Z's employees can apply for 3 days of leave in January. Their balance will be -2 days, which will be offset by the leave they earn in February (1 day) and March (1 day). By the end of March, their balance will return to 0 day.
