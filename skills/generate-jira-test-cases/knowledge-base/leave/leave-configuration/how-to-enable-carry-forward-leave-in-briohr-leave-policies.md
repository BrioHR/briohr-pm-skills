# How to Enable Carry Forward Leave in BrioHR Leave Policies

*Learn how to configure carry-forward leave settings in BrioHR to automatically transfer unused leave days into the next year. Step-by-step guide for HR Admins.*

### Introduction


The **carry-forward leave** feature allows employees to bring unused leave from the previous year into the new year. This ensures employees can reserve a portion of their unused leave instead of using their entire entitlement within the same year.


As an HR Admin, you must enable the carry-forward setting **before December 31st each year** so the system can automatically move all eligible unused leave balances at **midnight on December 31st**.

Once enabled, the configuration remains active for future leave policy years unless you make changes manually.





---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Enabling Carry Forward Setting


#### Step 1 - Log In to BrioHR Account


Log in to your BrioHR account using your registered credentials.


#### Step 2 - Access a Leave Policy in the Leave Module


1. Go to **HR Lounge** > **Leave**.


2. Click **Leave Policies**.


3. Search for the relevant leave type (e.g., *Annual Leave*).


4. Click **'View Leave Details'** to open the selected leave type.


#### Step 3 - Configure the Carry Forward Setting


1. Select any leave policy listed under the leave type.


2. In the policy, open **General Configuration** and locate the **Entitlement** section.


3. Look for **Carry Forward 202X** (the year automatically reflects the next calendar year)


**Example:**
If you are viewing the 2025 leave policy, the system will show Carry Forward 2026.


4. Toggle **ON** the carry-forward setting, then configure one of the following rules:


- **Add number of days** – set a limit for the maximum number of days that can be carried forward.
- **Select All** – allow employees to carry forward *all* unused leave balance.
- **Set Expiry** – define an expiry date for the carried-forward days in the following year.


5. Scroll down and click **Save Settings** to confirm the changes.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**1. When will the carry-forward leave be applied?**
The system automatically transfers eligible unused leave on midnight** of December 31st**. Please ensure you have **enabled** the Carry-Forward setting in the leave policy before this date.


**2. Do I need to update carry-forward settings every year?**
No. The setting applies automatically each year unless you manually change or disable it.


**3. Can I set a limit on how many days employees can carry forward?**
Yes, you can specify the number of days allowed under the carry-forward rule.


**4. What happens if employees do not use their carried-forward leave before the expiry date?**
If you set an expiry, any unused carried-forward leave will be forfeited after the defined period.


**5. Does carry-forward apply to all leave types?**
Carry-forward is available only for leave types for which HR Admins enable the setting.
