# How to Enable/Disable Prorate Leave

*Learn how to enable or disable prorated leave in BrioHR's Leave Module. Understand the different prorate options available, such as Yearly Basis, and how leave entitlements are calculated for employees joining or leaving mid-year.*

### How to Enable/Disable Prorated Leave


To enable or disable **prorated leave** for specific policies, follow these steps:


1. Go to **HR Lounge** > **Leave**.
2. Select the applicable **leave type** and **policy**.
3. Under **Entitlement**, locate the **Prorated Leave** section.
4. Toggle the **Prorate Leave** option **ON** or **OFF**.


---


In BrioHR, there are two types of prorated leave options:


### 1) Yearly Basis


This applies to employees who join or leave in the middle of the year. You can choose from three options:


- **Started Month**: Count the full month for employees who join during the month.
- **Completed Month**: Exclude the month if the employee leaves before it ends.
- **Partial Month**: Prorate leave based on the number of days worked in the month.


### Example: Prorated Leave Calculation


**Scenario**:


- Annual Leave Entitlement: **14 days**.
- Employee joins on **March 15**.


**Options**:


- **Started Month**: The full month of March is counted. The employee receives **14 days** of annual leave.
- **Completed Month**: March is excluded, and leave is prorated for the remaining months of the year.
- **Partial Month**: Leave is prorated based on days worked in March. For instance, working **16 out of 31 days** would calculate leave proportionally.


For more information, you can refer to [[this guide](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/leave-proration-formula-yearly-basis?hsLang=en)] about yearly basis proration formula.
