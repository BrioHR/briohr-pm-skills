# How to Link Emergency Leave with Annual Leave Balance in BrioHR

*This article explains how to link emergency leave to the annual leave balance so emergency leave days deduct from annual leave.*

In BrioHR, you can link an emergency leave to an annual leave balance.


This means that if an employee takes one (1) day of emergency leave, it will deduct one (1) day from their annual leave balance.


**NOTE:**


- **To link emergency leave with an annual leave balance, you need to set it up from the Annual Leave policy settings page only.**
- **Linking emergency leave will deduct from the annual leave's current year entitlement only. It will NOT deduct from the carry-forward annual leave balance.**


---


#### Steps to Link Emergency Leave with Annual Leave Balance:


Log in to your BrioHR account and click on HR Lounge.


1.   Navigate to the **Leave **module.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48293567165/original/vFWwfwwik9PTFUuZMeKIKFFCI9AbLF_gVQ.png?1681955836)


2.   Go to **Leave Policies**.


3.   Look for "**Annual**" and click on **View leave details** to open the Annual Leave policy settings page.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48293567197/original/_P9y0hV-6UvERFY1PsthjKULdFMrwIxkUg.png?1681955880)


**NOTE: **


**If you have more than one (1) policy type under your annual leave, choose the one you want to link with emergency leave.**


4.   Choose a leave policy to configure it for linking with emergency leave.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48293567243/original/J31yhozGnZadkGLk4nsGm3sa-SF-_mgC9A.png?1681955939)


5.   Look for the section called **ENTITLEMENT**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48293567302/original/65hKLFbkhKVFD8gfNwaD2UVPm_-sx-OseA.png?1681956037)


6.   Scroll down until you find **link to another leave type**.


7.   Search for "**Emergency**" and tick the checkbox to select it.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48293567354/original/DH8vPPtoXt3oq34BfXrPez3LA6Thv3HdHw.png?1681956081)


8.   Click **Save **to update this leave policy setting.


**NOTE:**


**If you want to link all available annual leave policies, you need to repeat the above steps for each policy.**
