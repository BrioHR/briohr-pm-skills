# Childcare Leave Policies and Entitlements in Singapore Explained

*This article explains childcare leave policies and entitlements in Singapore and how they are supported in BrioHR.*

### Introducing Childcare Leave on BrioHR!


Childcare leave in Singapore is a statutory benefit provided to working parents, designed to help them balance work responsibilities with caring for their young children. Eligible working parents of Singapore citizen children are entitled to six days of paid childcare leave per year.


For more information, visit the Ministry of Manpower (MOM) website.


Types of Childcare Leave Available:


1. Government-Paid Childcare Leave (GPCL) for Singapore citizen children
2. Government-Paid Childcare Leave (GPCL) for non-Singapore citizen children **(Newly available from 10 May 2024)**
3. Extended Childcare Leave (ECL) for Singapore citizen children **(Newly available from 10 May 2024)**

#### A) Updating Children’s Information in BrioHR


Ensure that **employees' children's information is accurately added or updated in their Profile >** **Family Details** before creating and assigning the childcare leave type.


Refer to this [guide](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-set-up-childrens-information-in-family-details?hs_preview=sMnHzHWa-174736573208&hsLang=en) on updating the children's information for employees eligible for this leave.

#### B) Creating a Childcare Leave Policy


1.   Navigate to: **HR Lounge > Leave > Leave Policies > Add new leave type > Fill in details > Leave type template > Select Singapore Childcare Leave**


**NOTE:**


**This leave type cannot be set up as a credit-based leave.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48336455468/original/02tMjCyF_2UyzuytbEfFN9JCG7GnZ0S_Tg.png?1715324825)


2.   The leave type created is **pre-filled with recommended settings according to MOM** regulations, such as annual entitlement, prorated entitlement percentage, maximum entitlement, and rounding options.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48311481156/original/ma--a7E2K0horB09DIoPx5ukeTcEyoggrg.png?1695199694)


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48311481428/original/SDux0xj6oEnpVfeaHdBludIwiOckNqmKzw.png?1695199771)


**NOTE:**


**Some settings are disabled due to MOM regulations, including Carry Forward, Earned Leave, Seniority, and Linking of leave types. A warning message will appear if these settings are enabled.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48311482122/original/c6ZSVuLikEHMaena8l6NMWsg1VBHe1rK2w.png?1695200047)


3.   After selecting the appropriate settings, scroll up to **TOOLS**.


Click on **Assign Policy > Select employees > Assign the policy accordingly > Apply.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48311633884/original/rH0Xy_XG4p5bZzQWtn7Uvx-kqVWGewXLJQ.png?1695289543)


4.   Click on **Childcare Limit** to ensure that employees with eligible children appear in the list along with their entitlement.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48322965390/original/EosxGAPNrYre_z-nWTbGVAHglUM4cXej3g.png?1704849322)


**NOTE:**


**Add “UTILIZED AMOUNT” for employees previously employed at different companies or those transitioning from different HR software to account for previously utilized childcare leave.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48322965437/original/2gZaNLHpgQz9tLl1ooHTbOl4JwCGwYeT8Q.png?1704849393)


**NOTE:**


**The Annual Limit is the maximum number of days provided for the current year, affected by the Utilized Amount and Maximum Entitlement set in the policy. This limit is displayed before proration (if applicable) and current year leave utilization. **


**The actual number of days available will be reflected in the leave entitlement dashboard.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48311604805/original/Nb0hub-6n77GS52qAVIo8mzPAFLpVZ5w5w.png?1695274399)


5.   Employees can apply for childcare leave as usual.


Navigate to: **Home > My Leave > Apply Leave > Select leave type > Fill in the dates > Submit.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48311485370/original/xwJsDY8hVbf23-AMhElZbyNfjqo9jsmM8A.png?1695201432)


**NOTE:**


**Childcare leave entitlement can only be utilized in the current year and cannot be applied to previous or future years.**


**An error message will appear if the leave is applied for outside the current year.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48311635408/original/F_TF2sW191lRk1RBUT3eXJUvvaBmqSuUIA.png?1695290144)
