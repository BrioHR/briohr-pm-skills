# Download Leave Utilizations Report & Leave Balance Report from Leave Dashboard

*Learn how to download the leave utilization and leave balance reports from your leave dashboard for better tracking and management.*

To manage employee leave effectively, it's essential to have access to detailed reports such as the Leave Utilizations Report and Leave Balance Report. This guide will walk you through the steps to download both reports from your leave dashboard.


Steps to Download the Leave Utilizations & Leave Balance Report


1. In **HR Lounge** / **Leave Module** / **Dashboard**
2. Click on **Reports**
3. Download either **Leave Utilizations** or **Leave Balance **

  1. **Leave Utilizations**: Choose the Leave Utilizations report, and select whether you want to download it for the full year or by month, based on your preferences.
  2. **Leave Balance**: Choose the Leave Balance report and select the "As of" date to download the balance as of that specific date.

Downloading the Leave Utilizations and Leave Balance Reports is a simple process that can help you track and manage employee leave more effectively. By following these steps, you ensure your reports are accurate and up-to-date.
