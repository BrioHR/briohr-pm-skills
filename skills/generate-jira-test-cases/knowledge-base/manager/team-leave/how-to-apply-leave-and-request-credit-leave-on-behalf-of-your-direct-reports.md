# How to Apply Leave and Request Credit Leave on Behalf of Your Direct Reports

*Learn how managers can apply leave or request credit leave on behalf of direct reports in BrioHR. Step-by-step guide to managing team leave applications.*

In BrioHR, managers have the ability to **apply for leave and request leave credits** on behalf of their direct reports. This feature ensures smoother workforce management, especially when employees are unable to submit their own requests.


However, managers must have the appropriate **permissions enabled**. If you are unable to access this feature, please contact your **account admin** to grant the necessary permissions.





### Steps to Apply Leave or Request Credit Leave for Direct Reports


1.   Login to BrioHR and click into **Team >**** Leave**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-08-2025-04-50-56-3903-AM.png?width=1797&height=704&name=image-png-Sep-08-2025-04-50-56-3903-AM.png)


2.   On the Team Leave dashboard, choose the **direct report** for whom you want to apply leave or request credit leave.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-08-2025-04-52-03-4822-AM.png?width=1800&height=801&name=image-png-Sep-08-2025-04-52-03-4822-AM.png)
[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48239193653/original/6SJRE2OkiXwVATFkJ0pY1kfcPgvo1LExpQ.png?1658127855)


3.   You will be directed to the leave page for the selected direct report.


- Click on the '**+ Apply Leave**' or '**Request Leave Credit**' button to begin the application process.


The leave or credit leave request will follow the **standard approval workflow**, ensuring consistency with company policies.
