# How Managers Can Reopen or Send Back 360 Feedback for Revisions

*This article explains how managers can reopen or send back submitted 360 feedback so givers can revise their ratings.*

Sometimes, as a manager, you may need 360 feedback givers to **modify their submitted feedback and ratings**, or you may receive requests from feedback givers to **edit their submissions**.


As a manager or appraiser, you can send back feedback directly from the appraisee's personal performance page. Please watch this video for a step-by-step guide on how to send back feedback.
