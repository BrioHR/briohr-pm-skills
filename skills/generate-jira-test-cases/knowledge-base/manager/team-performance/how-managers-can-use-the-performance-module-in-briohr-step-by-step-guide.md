# How Managers Can Use the Performance Module in BrioHR: Step-by-Step Guide

*Learn how to navigate BrioHR’s Performance module — from setting and confirming objectives to completing appraisals and 360 feedback. Follow this simple step-by-step guide for managers.*

### 1. Accessing the Performance Dashboard


To begin reviewing your team’s performance activities, navigate to **Team > Performance** within the BrioHR platform. This section provides access to the **Performance Dashboard**, where you can oversee and manage all performance-related tasks for your team members.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-20-2025-06-45-47-2031-AM.png?width=670&height=270&name=image-png-Nov-20-2025-06-45-47-2031-AM.png)


### 2. Reviewing and Managing Objectives


Your first responsibility is to **review the objectives submitted by your employees**. Employees may also create and propose additional objectives when necessary.


When an employee creates a new objective, they must determine whether it should be included in the appraisal form or treated as a personal (non-rated) goal. If the objective is not part of the appraisal, it should be marked as **“This is a non-rated objective.”**


Once employees have prepared their objectives, you will review and **confirm** them. Depending on your organization’s settings, employees may still have permission to edit their objectives even after your confirmation.


Employees are expected to **update their progress regularly**, and as a manager, you should encourage this habit to ensure accurate tracking and meaningful discussions during the appraisal process.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-20-2025-06-54-04-4335-AM.png?width=670&height=257&name=image-png-Nov-20-2025-06-54-04-4335-AM.png)


### 3. Reviewing the Appraisal Form


Once the appraisal form has been submitted by the employee, as a manager, it will appear in your queue for **manager (appraiser) review.**You will then need to evaluate their responses and provide your feedback.


The appraisal form typically includes:


- **Questionnaire / Manager** **Appraisal**
- **Objectives Rating**
- **Competencies Rating**
- **Summary**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-20-2025-06-59-53-8401-AM.png?width=670&height=275&name=image-png-Nov-20-2025-06-59-53-8401-AM.png)


### 4. Conducting 1-on-1 Feedback Sessions


Some appraisal processes include **1-on-1 Feedback sessions**. These may be scheduled daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly depending on your workflow.


As a manager, this is your opportunity to:


- Discuss performance progress
- Address challenges
- Provide guidance and support


Both you and your employees can use the **comment section** to record notes from each discussion. These notes serve as a helpful reference for future conversations and ongoing performance monitoring.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-20-2025-07-01-07-5346-AM.png?width=670&height=271&name=image-png-Nov-20-2025-07-01-07-5346-AM.png)


### 5. Overseeing 360 Feedback Participation


If your organization utilizes the **360 Feedback**, employees may need to be assigned to provide feedback on colleagues, their superior, or their direct reports.


As a manager, you will **need to assign the respective feedback givers** for the employees and should ensure that employees **complete their assigned feedback tasks **thoughtfully and on time. Constructive feedback and ratings provided in this section contribute to a well-rounded evaluation for everyone involved.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-20-2025-07-02-46-3453-AM.png?width=670&height=291&name=image-png-Nov-20-2025-07-02-46-3453-AM.png)


### Conclusion


BrioHR’s Performance module equips managers with the tools needed to guide, support, and evaluate their teams effectively. From reviewing objectives to assessing appraisals and facilitating feedback discussions, managers play a crucial role in shaping a transparent and growth-oriented performance culture.


By encouraging regular updates, meaningful self-appraisal, and active participation in feedback processes, managers can help employees gain valuable insights while maintaining alignment with organizational goals.





### Suggested FAQs


1. **Where can I view my team’s performance dashboard?**
Go to **Team > Performance** from your dashboard.
2. **Can employees edit their objectives after I confirm them?**
Yes, in some cases. This depends on the access permissions set by your organization.
3. **What is a non-rated objective?**
A non-rated objective is a personal goal employees set that is not included in the official appraisal scoring.
4. **When will the appraisal form be available to my team?**
The availability follows your company’s performance cycle setup. Employees will receive a notification when the form is open.
5. **How does 360 feedback work for employees?**
Employees may be assigned to provide feedback on peers, managers, or direct reports. They must complete any assigned ratings and comments as part of their performance tasks.
