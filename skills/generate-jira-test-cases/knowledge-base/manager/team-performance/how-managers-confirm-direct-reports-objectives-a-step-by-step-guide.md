# How Managers Confirm Direct Reports' Objectives: A Step-by-Step Guide

*This article explains how managers confirm their direct reports' objectives after employees submit them.*

Managers can confirm their direct reports' objectives after the performance cycle has begun and employees have submitted their objectives following discussions with their managers.

To confirm the objectives, please follow the steps below:

1. Navigate to **Team**.
2. Select **Team Performance**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-13-2024-03-08-49-5257-AM.png?width=688&height=271&name=image-png-Sep-13-2024-03-08-49-5257-AM.png)

3. Open the performance page of a direct report by clicking on **See Details**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-13-2024-03-09-09-2679-AM.png?width=688&height=278&name=image-png-Sep-13-2024-03-09-09-2679-AM.png)

4. In the **Objectives** section, click the **Confirm Objectives** button.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-13-2024-03-08-18-9055-AM.png?width=688&height=331&name=image-png-Sep-13-2024-03-08-18-9055-AM.png)
