# Manager: How to Release Appraisal Ratings for Direct Reports

*This article explains how managers release appraisal ratings to direct reports once the appraisal form is ready.*

Managers or appraisers will be notified whenever the appraisal form is ready to be released in two ways:


  ♦  Email Notification


  ♦  Pending Actions List in BrioHR


**Best Practice:** Before releasing the appraisal form to team members or appraisees, it's recommended to have a one-on-one discussion.


---


### Step 1: Email Notification


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48165234090/original/AlIuhz_ETG7nuIpPvUMjpqD_3sJqpZlPHw.png?1638260492)


You will receive an email notification when the appraisal form is ready to be released.


---


### Step 2: Pending Actions Panel Notification


Log in to your **BrioHR **account.


- Click on the "**Pending Actions**" button in the top-right corner.
- Look for a notification titled "**Release Appraisal**" with a due date.
- Click on the notification, and a pop-up message will appear. Select "**See Appraisal**."


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48165236689/original/h9q8bPogudTqM3uEkSO_chGMCIq4MV9mjA.png?1638260614)


- Review the **Appraisal Form**.
- Once you are satisfied with the content and have had a one-on-one discussion with your team member, click "**Release Appraisal**."


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48165237320/original/oplwUqQ0ChCcg-fU8y0xsAq4ZK5vE13qqQ.png?1638260711)


- A confirmation message will pop up. Click "**Continue**" to finalize the release.


The appraisal will then be released to the appraisee. They will be notified via email and the Pending Actions list in BrioHR to acknowledge receipt of the appraisal.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48165240578/original/MMZSIpqqJeZf7CWZ-Njyqms8nezvE8cIOA.png?1638260843)
