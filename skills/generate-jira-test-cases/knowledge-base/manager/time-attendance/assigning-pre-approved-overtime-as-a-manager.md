# Assigning Pre-Approved Overtime as a Manager

*This article explains how Managers can assign the pre-approved overtime for employees with flexible and shift schedule using the Time Attendance module.*

#### Audience & Scope


Audience: Manager


Module: Time Attendance


Country: Global





#### Step-by-Step Instructions for Flexible schedule employees


1. Click into **Team** and into the **Time Attendance** module
2. Proceed to click into the **Rostering** page and search for the **Employee** required


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-09-2026-09-22-32-2171-AM.png?width=670&height=129&name=image-png-Feb-09-2026-09-22-32-2171-AM.png)
3. Click on the '**Add**' button and click into the **Add Pre-Approved overtime (OT)**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-09-2026-09-24-24-8694-AM.png?width=670&height=160&name=image-png-Feb-09-2026-09-24-24-8694-AM.png)
4. Fill in the number of hours/minutes for the Pre-Approved overtime and click **Add OT**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-09-2026-09-26-43-2296-AM.png?width=416&height=239&name=image-png-Feb-09-2026-09-26-43-2296-AM.png)
5. You will then be able to see the **Pre Approved OT** showing for the employee's roster


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-09-2026-09-28-01-5215-AM.png?width=670&height=170&name=image-png-Feb-09-2026-09-28-01-5215-AM.png)

**NOTE: **
The Pre-Approved Overtime can still be edited even after the Roster has been published. You will need to hover over the Overtime added and click to modify the hours accordingly.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-09-2026-02-21-57-0036-PM.png?width=670&height=207&name=image-png-Feb-09-2026-02-21-57-0036-PM.png)


**NOTE:**


- Pre-approved OT (assigned by manager) is visible in:


  - HR Lounge > Time Attendance > Rostering (for HR Admin)
  - HR Lounge > Time Attendance > Timesheets (for HR Admin)
  - Team > Time Attendance > Rostering (for manager)
  - Team > Time Attendance > Timesheets (for manager)
- Calculated OT (from actual clock-in/out) is auto-calculated when employee works beyond scheduled hours and **IF** 'Calculate Overtime' is enabled in the employee's TA policy. The overtime hours can be checked at HR Lounge > Time Attendance > Timesheet (not in Roster).





#### Common Issues/FAQ


**Q: Who can assign pre-approved overtime in the system?**
**A**: Pre-approved overtime can be assigned by managers with access to the Team and Time Attendance modules.


**Q: Can I assign pre-approved overtime for both flexible and shift schedule employees?**
**A**: Yes. Pre-approved overtime can be added for both flexible schedule and shift schedule employees by accessing the Rostering page in the Time Attendance module.


**Q: Will the pre-approved overtime appear on the employee’s roster immediately?**
**A**: Yes. Once added, the pre-approved overtime will be displayed directly on the employee’s roster.


**Q: Can pre-approved overtime be added as an additional shift?**
A: Yes. For shift schedule employees, you can select **Add time slot instead** to create the overtime as an additional shift by filling in the required details and clicking **Create**.


**Q. Can pre-approved overtime be edited after the roster is published?**
A: Yes. Pre-approved overtime remains editable even after the roster has been published. Simply hover over the overtime entry on the roster and click to modify the hours.
