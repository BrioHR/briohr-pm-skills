# Employee Attendance Timesheet Overview for Managers in BrioHR

*Learn how managers can review and approve an employee’s attendance timesheet in BrioHR. Track work hours, overtime, and absenteeism with ease. This guide suitable for HR Admin too.*

**Audience & Scope**


Audience: Manager


Module: Time Attendance


Country: Global


Pre-requisites: Access to Time Attendance module within Team dashboard





Understanding how to read, navigate, and review a direct report's attendance timesheet is crucial to ensuring that work and overtime hours are recorded accurately.


In addition to making **manual attendance entries** on behalf of direct reports, managers can also **approve timesheets** and **send them to payroll** for overtime payment processing.


This video explains how managers can:


- Access the **Timesheet** tab in the **Team** page > **Time Attendance** module.
- Filter a direct report’s name by **status**.
- Review the **timesheet summary** for a selected month, including:

  - **Total overtime hours** for Normal Days, Rest Days, and Public Holidays.
  - **Total late hours** and **absenteeism** for the month.
