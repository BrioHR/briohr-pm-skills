# How Managers Approve Direct Report's Overtime via BrioHR Mobile App

*Learn how managers can approve or reject employee overtime requests directly from the BrioHR Mobile App. Step-by-step guide with tips and related resources.*

### Introduction


Managers can easily approve their direct reports’ overtime requests directly from the **BrioHR Mobile App**, allowing approvals to be done **anytime, anywhere**.


From the app, managers can **modify the approved overtime hours**, **add comments**, or **reject overtime** with a reason.


The system automatically determines whether the overtime falls under:


**✔ Normal day overtime**
✔ **Rest day overtime**
✔ **Public holiday overtime**


Managers will receive an **email notification** to review and approve the direct report’s overtime hours.

### Step-by-Step Guide: Approve Overtime via BrioHR Mobile App


#### Step 1 - Open the Team Task in BrioHR Mobile App


Login to **BrioHR Mobile App.**


1. Tap** Team Task**.


2. In the **Team Task List**, select the task titled **'Approve Overtime'**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-15-2025-07-56-52-7314-AM.png?width=611&height=550&name=image-png-Aug-15-2025-07-56-52-7314-AM.png)


---


#### Step 2 - Approve or Reject Overtime


#### 2.1: Approve Overtime


1. Tap **Review OT.**


2. A pop out will appear with the following options:


a) **Edit the hours to approve**.


b)** Add comment or notes** by tapping **+Tap to add notes**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-15-2025-07-57-18-2118-AM.png?width=611&height=555&name=image-png-Aug-15-2025-07-57-18-2118-AM.png)


3. Tap **Approve** to continue approval process.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-15-2025-07-57-43-6785-AM.png?width=611&height=561&name=image-png-Aug-15-2025-07-57-43-6785-AM.png)


#### 2.2: Reject Overtime


1. Tap **Reject OT**.


2. A pop out will appear. Enter the **reason for rejection** in the text field.


3. Tap **Reject** to confirm the rejection.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-15-2025-07-58-08-2813-AM.png?width=611&height=535&name=image-png-Aug-15-2025-07-58-08-2813-AM.png)





---


#### Best Practices for Overtime Approval


- Always review the overtime request details before approving or rejecting.
- Include comments to give employees feedback or clarification.
- Approve overtime promptly to avoid payroll delays.
