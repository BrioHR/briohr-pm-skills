# How Managers Can Assign Shifts on Public Holiday in BrioHR Rostering

*Learn how managers can assign shifts on public holidays in BrioHR to ensure employees can clock in and receive the correct overtime public holiday rate.*

### Introduction


Managers can modify their team members' **weekly and monthly rostering schedules** as needed. This includes assigning shifts on** rest days** to:


- Allow team members on a shift basis **to clock in** on their public holidays.
- Ensure the system applies the **correct overtime public holiday rate** automatically.


**IMPORTANT:**

If **no shift is assigned **on a public holiday, the employees are unable to clock in on that day.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Assign Shifts on


#### Step 1 - Access the Rostering Page


Log in to your BrioHR account.


1. Go to **Team > Time Attendance.**


2. Click on** Rostering.**

#### Step 2 - Select the Team Member and Date


1. Use the** search box** to filter by the team member's name.


2. Locate the** public holiday **you want to assign the shift to (e.g., Deepavali, 20th October 2025)


3. Hover over the box and click the **plus (+) icon**.

#### Step 3 - Create or Select a Shift


1. Choose one of the following options:


**a) + Create new shift** – Create a new shift if the desired shift period is not available.
**b) + Choose from existing shifts** – Select from an existing shift list.

### Identifying Public Holiday Shifts in BrioHR


- A shift on a public holiday will have a **blue label** at the top-right corner of the shift box.
- This indicates that **different pay rates** may apply.
- If a team member clocks in and out on a public holiday, the system will calculate overtime hours using the **public holiday overtime rate**.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**1. Can I assign multiple shifts on a public holiday for the same team member?**
Yes, you can assign multiple shifts to the same direct report on a public holiday. However, the shifts cannot overlap in time. Each shift must cover a separate time period.


For example:


- **Shift 1: **09:00 - 13:00
- **Shift 2: **17:00 - 20:00


**2. What happens if I delete a public holiday shift?**
The employee will no longer be able to clock in on that public holiday.


**4. Can I assign a shift to multiple direct reports at once?**
No, you cannot assign a shift to multiple team members in bulk. Currently, assigning shifts can only be done one person at a time on the Rostering page.
