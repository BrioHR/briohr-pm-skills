# How Managers Can Mark a Direct Report as Absent in BrioHR

*Learn how managers can easily mark a direct report's attendance as absent in BrioHR. Follow our step-by-step guide to update attendance records accurately and ensure compliance.*

### Introduction


If a team member is absent from work, their reporting manager can easily mark their attendance as **absent** from the **Team** page.


Accurately recording absences ensures the company maintains correct attendance data and can assess whether further action is needed for employees who frequently miss work without valid reasons.


### Why Managers Need to Mark Employees as Absent


- Ensures employee attendance is accurately recorded in daily timesheets.
- Helps HR track absenteeism trends.
- Allows the company to take appropriate actions for repeated absences.


### Where Can Managers Update Absentee Status?


Managers can mark a team member as absent from two locations:


1. From the **Daily Attendance** page.
2. From an **employee's timesheet** page.


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Marking Absences in BrioHR


Login to your BrioHR account.


1. Navigate to the **Team** tab.


2. Click **Team Attendance**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-08-2025-06-40-43-1064-AM.png?width=411&height=348&name=image-png-Sep-08-2025-06-40-43-1064-AM.png)


---


### Method 1: Mark Absence via the Daily Attendance Page


1. Click the **Daily Attendance** tab. By default, you will see today's date. Search for the team member's name in the Search bar.


2. The team member’s status will display as **"Not-in"** with an **"NCI"** tag (Not Clocked In).


3. Click the **"Mark As Absent"** button.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-08-2025-07-13-00-8369-AM.png?width=2000&height=695&name=image-png-Sep-08-2025-07-13-00-8369-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-08-2025-07-13-10-4478-AM.png?width=2000&height=604&name=image-png-Sep-08-2025-07-13-10-4478-AM.png)


4. A confirmation message will appear. The status will remain **"Not-in"**, but the **"NCI"** tag will be removed.


5. A remark under the **Action** column will indicate the absence.


**Undo an Absence Mark:**


Click the **"↺" (undo) icon** to reverse the absence mark.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-08-2025-07-14-05-8798-AM.png?width=2000&height=603&name=image-png-Sep-08-2025-07-14-05-8798-AM.png)


---


### Method 2: Mark Absence via the Timesheet Page


1. Click the **Daily Attendance** tab. By default, you will see the current month’s timesheet.


2. Search for the team member's name in the** Search** bar.


3. Click on the team member’s name or select **"Open in New Tab"** to open their timesheet.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-08-2025-07-15-11-0874-AM.png?width=2000&height=693&name=image-png-Sep-08-2025-07-15-11-0874-AM.png)


4. Locate the specific date and click **"Mark as Absent"**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-08-2025-07-15-22-8416-AM.png?width=2000&height=858&name=image-png-Sep-08-2025-07-15-22-8416-AM.png)


5. A confirmation message will appear. The** "NCI" **tag will be removed.


6. The team member's absence will be recorded under the **Action** column.


**Undo an Absence Mark:**


Click the **"↺" (undo) icon** to reverse the absence mark.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-15-2025-03-16-17-7306-PM.png?width=670&height=167&name=image-png-Feb-15-2025-03-16-17-7306-PM.png)

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


**Q1: Why can't I access the Team page or the Time Attendance section?

**A: This is likely due to user access permissions. Please contact your HR Admin to grant you the necessary permission. You can share [[this guide](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-enable-the-time-attendance-module-in-team-manager?hsLang=en)] to your HR Admin.


**Q2: Can I adjust my team member’s clock-in, clock-out, and break durations instead of marking them absent?

**A: Yes! You can modify attendance records manually. Refer to [[this guide](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/manual-daily-attendance-entry-for-direct-reports?hsLang=en)] for detailed instructions on editing daily attendance.


**Q3: Can I mark absences for employees in advance?**


A: Currently, BrioHR allows marking absences on recorded dates only. For future absences, employees should submit leave application or use your company's leave module.
