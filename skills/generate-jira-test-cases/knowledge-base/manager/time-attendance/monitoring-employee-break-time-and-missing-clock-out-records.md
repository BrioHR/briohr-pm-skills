# Monitoring Employee Break Time and Missing Clock Out Records

*This article explains how Managers can monitor break time and missing clock-out records in the Time Attendance module.*

**Audience & Scope**


Audience: Manager


Module: Time Attendance


Country: Global


Pre-requisites: Team dashboard access for Time Attendance module enabled





#### Step-by-Step Instructions for Managers to Monitor Break Duration


**Note: **


This feature will only apply to employees on a shift-based work schedule. Employees under a flexible work schedule are not included.


An employee is considered 'Exceeding Break' if:
1. The employee has clocked out for a break.
2. The employee **has not clocked back in from break**.
3. The current time is later than the expected clock-in time from break.


1. Go to** Team > Time Attendance**
2. Under the **Daily Attendance** tab, find the '**Exceeded Break**' button. You should see a list of employees who have exceeded their break duration for the day.

#### Step-by-Step Instructions for Managers to Monitor Missing Clock Out


**Note:**


An employee qualifies as Not Clock-Out (NCO) if:
1. The employee has a valid clock-in record for the selected date.
2. The employee has not made a final clock-out.
3. The current time is later than the employee’s expected clock-out time.


1. Go to** Team > Time Attendance**
2. Under the **Daily Attendance** tab, find the '**Not clock out**' button. You should see a list of employees who are missing clock-out records for the day.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-17-2026-06-41-41-2956-AM.png?width=670&height=277&name=image-png-Apr-17-2026-06-41-41-2956-AM.png)

4. Use the filters at the top if needed.
