# How Managers Approve Training Event Enrollment and Creation Requests

*Learn how managers can approve or reject training event enrollment and creation requests in BrioHR using the Team page or Pending Actions section. Step-by-step guide included.*

### Introduction


As a manager, you can easily approve or reject Training Event Enrollment and Training Event Creation requests submitted by your team members. This can be done from either the Team Page or the Pending Actions section in BrioHR.


Properly reviewing and responding to these requests makes sure your team members can only participate in relevant training events and keep your organization's training catalog organized.


There are two methods of approving the training enrollment and training creation requests:


1. Approve from the **Team Page > Training.**


2. Approve from the **Pending Actions Task.**


---


### Step-by-Step Guide: Approve or Reject the Training Enrollment and Training Creation Requests


#### Method 1: Approve or Reject Requests from the Team Page


1. Navigate to **Team > Training.**


2. Review the requests submitted by your team members.


- You can view the training event details by clicking on the training name.
- Additional information, such as status and requester, will also be displayed.


3. Select the **Approve** or **Reject** button to finalize your decision.

---


#### Method 2: Approve or Reject Requests from the My To-Do List


1. On the Home Page, go to **My To-Do List**.


2. Locate and click the **Training** option.


3. Click **Training Details **to review.


4. Select the **Approve** or **Reject** button to complete the action.

#### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**Q1: Can I approve multiple training requests at once?**


A: Currently, training requests need to be approved or rejected individually.


**Q2: Where can I see all previously approved or rejected requests?**


A: You can view the status of all training requests in the Training section under the Team tab.


**Q3: Can employees see the status of their training creation requests?**


A: Yes, employees can view the approval status in their own Training section.
