# How to Enable Onboarding Sections and Include Necessary Information

*This article explains how to enable or disable onboarding sections so new joiners provide the information your company requires.*

When setting up an **Onboarding Process** in BrioHR, you can enable or disable specific sections to match your company’s requirements. Each section represents a key onboarding step, ensuring that new joiners provide the necessary information before completing their onboarding. Follow the steps below to configure onboarding sections properly.


For a complete guide on setting up onboarding sections, refer to [this article](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-set-up-the-onboarding-section-in-briohr?hsLang=en).


#### Steps to Enable Onboarding Sections


1. Login to** BrioHR**
2. Navigate to** HR Lounge > Onboarding**
3. Select **Process**
4. **Create a New Process or Select an Existing Process** – Click **See Details** to modify an existing process.
5. **Configure the Onboarding Section** – In the left panel, you will find a list of onboarding steps that can be enabled or disabled according to your company’s requirements.
6. **Enable the Required Sections** –

  1. Sections that are **enabled** will display a ✅ (green tick).
  2. If a section is **disabled**, click on it to enable and include it in the process.
7. **Review Necessary Information** – Once a section is enabled, check the fields that need to be completed for that step.
8. Click **Save**
