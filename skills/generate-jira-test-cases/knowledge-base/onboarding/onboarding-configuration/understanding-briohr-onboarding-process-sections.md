# Understanding BrioHR Onboarding Process (Sections)

*Ensure a smooth and structured onboarding process by setting up the Onboarding Section in BrioHR. Follow the steps below to configure onboarding sections as part of your process.*

### Steps to Set Up the Onboarding Section


1. Login to** BrioHR**
2. Navigate to** HR Lounge > Onboarding**
3. Select **Process**
4. **Create a New Process or Select an Existing Process** – Click **See Details** to modify an existing process.
5. **Configure the Onboarding Section** – In the left panel, you will find a list of onboarding steps that can be enabled or disabled according to your company’s requirements:


  - **Personal Information** – Collect basic employee details such as **full name, contact information, and emergency contacts**.
  - **Payroll Information** – Gather **banking details, salary structure, and statutory information** needed for payroll processing.
  - **Qualification** – Request **educational background, certifications, and relevant work experience**.
  - **Contract** – Upload and share **employment contracts** that employees can review and acknowledge.
  - **Day 1 Preparation** – Provide necessary details such as **workstation setup, assigned mentor, and company policies**.
  - **Orientation Meetings** – Schedule **introductory meetings** with key stakeholders and department heads.
  - **Orientation Trainings** – Assign required **training sessions** to help employees understand company policies and workflows.
  - **Orientation Objectives** – Set expectations and objectives for the **first 30, 60, or 90 days** to track employee progress.
  - **Probation –** **Configure probation notifications** to remind HR/admin to decide on the probation result **after a set number of days**. Additionally, **update the probation status** to indicate whether the new hire has passed or failed.

### Finalizing the Setup


Once all necessary sections are configured, click **Save**. The system will validate that all required fields are completed before saving. If any mandatory fields are missing, BrioHR will prompt a message indicating which sections need attention.


By following these steps, you can ensure a seamless and well-structured onboarding experience for new employees.
