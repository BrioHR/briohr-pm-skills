# How HR Can Complete Onboarding Tasks in BrioHR

*This article explains how HR completes onboarding tasks such as verifying employee details and assigning onboarding processes.*

HR plays a crucial role in managing and overseeing the **onboarding process** to ensure a smooth transition for new joiners. This includes tasks such as **verifying employee details, assigning onboarding processes, and ensuring all required documents are completed**. Follow the steps below to complete HR onboarding tasks efficiently in BrioHR.

Steps to Complete Onboarding Tasks


### Method 1: Using Pending Actions


1. Login to** BrioHR**
2. Click on** 'Pending Actions'** – Located at the top right corner of the dashboard.


  - Use the **filter function** to search by keywords such as "onboarding" or the employee’s name.
3. **Fill in the necessary task details** – Enter required information, such as objectives or assigned tasks.
4. **Click Submit** – Finalize and submit the completed tasks.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-17-2025-08-15-25-2157-AM.png?width=688&height=404&name=image-png-Mar-17-2025-08-15-25-2157-AM.png)


Method 2: Accessing the Employee's Onboarding Process


1. Login to **BrioHR**
2. Navigate to** Team > Onboarding**
3. Select and view the **employee’s onboarding details**
4. Click on** 'My Task'**
5. **Complete the required tasks** – Provide the necessary information for each onboarding step, such as payroll configuration, contract preparation, and system access setup, etc.
6. **Click Submit**

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-17-2025-08-16-34-8779-AM.png?width=932&height=942&name=image-png-Mar-17-2025-08-16-34-8779-AM.png)


By following these steps, HR can efficiently track and complete onboarding responsibilities, ensuring that new employees have everything they need for a seamless transition.
