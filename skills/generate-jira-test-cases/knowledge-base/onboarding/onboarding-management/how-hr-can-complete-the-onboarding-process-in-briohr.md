# How HR Can Complete the Onboarding Process in BrioHR

*This article explains how HR marks the onboarding process complete once all stakeholders have finished their tasks.*

Once all assigned stakeholders—including new joiners, managers, HR, and department heads—have completed their respective tasks, the onboarding process is considered complete. However, if HR determines that sufficient information has been provided for the new joiner, they can also manually mark the onboarding process as completed. Follow the steps below to finalize the onboarding process in BrioHR.


#### Steps to Complete the Onboarding Process


1. Login to **BrioHR**
2. Navigate to **HR Lounge > Onboarding > Dashboard**
3. **View Details of the Employee’s Onboarding Process** – Review all completed tasks to ensure everything is in order.
4. Return to **Dashboard** – Once verified, go back to the main dashboard.
5. **Click on the '...' Button** – Located next to the employee’s onboarding status.
6. **Select 'Complete Onboarding'** – Finalize the onboarding process for the employee.

By completing these steps, HR ensures that the onboarding process is successfully closed, either based on task completion by all stakeholders or by HR’s assessment of the provided information.
