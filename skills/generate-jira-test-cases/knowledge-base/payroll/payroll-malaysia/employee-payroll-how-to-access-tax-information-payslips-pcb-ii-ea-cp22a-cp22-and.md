# Employee Payroll: How to Access Tax Information, Payslips, PCB II, EA, CP22A, CP22, and CP21 Forms

*This article explains how HR can download employee payslips, PCB II, EA, CP22A, CP22, and CP21 forms and view tax information from the payroll center.*

As an HR professional, you can download various employee payroll files such as payslips, PCB II forms, EA forms, and CP22 forms. Additionally, you can view an employee's tax information directly from the payroll center dashboard.


**NOTE:**


**If you need to download payroll documents for resigned employees or those who have changed payroll centers, please refer to "How to find resigned or inactive employees in the payroll center" section.**


---


To get started: Log in to your BrioHR account and navigate to the HR Lounge.


1.   Select **Payroll**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48292169910/original/w24baSSsVWI9387Nd5nQq9BYknmHZGppTw.png?1681270994)


2.   You will be directed to the **Payroll **dashboard.


3.   Scroll to the bottom of the page to view the **Payroll Employees list**. In this section, you can search for an employee by their Name or ID.


4.   **Select an action** from the **Actions **dropdown menu.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48292169963/original/aba-UaQz1u_-pQDJb2638wzLdrGOPFOlvw.png?1681271008)

### How to Find Resigned or Inactive Employees in the Payroll Center


To find **resigned employees or those who have changed payroll centers** in the Payroll Employees list section, filter the status to "Inactive."


The system will display only those employees who have resigned or changed payroll centers.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48294292684/original/3qBLJOOuUYxQZhBSEhsGgmXownf8ATolmA.png?1682414653)


---


### A) To download an employee's Payslip:


1.   Select **Payslips **from the **Actions **dropdown list.


2.   Choose the **payroll period** and click **Download **to obtain a PDF copy of the employee's payslip, or click **Send Payslip** to email it to the employee.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48292170370/original/juARh3bZFiVi35AE2pDqHtRptYSXVpvG9A.png?1681271414)


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48292170284/original/DxiHDgvwEs0sJSn2SP-l6uwTzoqX-RKnig.png?1681271304)


---


### B) To view an employee's Tax Information:


1.   Select **Tax Information** from the **Actions **dropdown list.


2.   You will be directed to the **employee's profile**, where you can view their **payroll information.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48292170378/original/IwC8xs9Zfnmml2JIbP2WTGnr5AicHy4kSA.png?1681271426)


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48292170448/original/LQm6764JZ0U5tahWG-5oaUzxn3T4W3oiDQ.png?1681271496)


---


### C) To download an employee's PCB II Form:


1.   Select **PCB II** from the **Actions **dropdown list.


2.   Choose the **year**, select the **PCB II form date**, and click **Generate **to download a PDF copy of the employee's PCB II Form.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48292170516/original/QTy5AkgDRK1dA2EQi1LxnoyWGq-eW-skSg.png?1681271552)


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48292170593/original/VPzvRUnXbrH6wQOXK8lNieoNwygsKKVAog.png?1681271617)


---


### D) To download an employee's EA Form:


1.   Select **EA** from the **Actions **dropdown list.


2.   Choose the **year**, **EA form date**, and preferred **language**, then click **Generate **to download a PDF copy of the employee's EA Form.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48292170638/original/1NI5TWiBaTCaXH5Ps6E7IIdczxB3C9cKxQ.png?1681271670)


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48292170666/original/RPszHObH6Z0H6-FjMW0H4QeldwGqA7U0BA.png?1681271718)


---


### E) To download an employee's CP22 Form:


1.   Select **CP22 **from the **Actions **dropdown list.


2.   Fill in the required fields in the pop-up window, then click **Generate **to download a PDF copy of the employee's CP22 Form.
