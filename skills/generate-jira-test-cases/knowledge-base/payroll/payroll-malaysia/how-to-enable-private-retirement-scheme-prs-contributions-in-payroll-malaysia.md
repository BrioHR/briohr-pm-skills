# How to Enable Private Retirement Scheme (PRS) Contributions in Payroll Malaysia

*This article explains how to enable Private Retirement Scheme (PRS) contributions at company and employee levels in Malaysia payroll.*

**NOTE:**


**The PRS contribution is not enabled by default. The HR Admin must select the organization at both the company and employee levels before processing payroll.**


### A. Company Level


To enable PRS contributions at the company level, follow these steps:


- In the **HR Lounge**, go to **Payroll > Settings**.
- In the **REGISTRATION INFORMATION** section, scroll down to the "**Organizations**" column and select "**PRS**" from the dropdown menu.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48223653364/original/W4l5E5U7DNAh78Uh5euLJCjcK-KaD5cZfQ.png?1651737588)


- In the **Calculation **section, scroll down to the "**Income Basis for PRS Calculation**" column and choose the applicable payroll items.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48223658771/original/xg2Xr3IP_KwSIHF_s0VWGrom3eTl2hhzdA.png?1651738120)


- In the same **Calculation **section, scroll down to the "**HPRS Percentage of Income**" column and enter the percentage for the employer's contribution, as shown in the sample screenshot below.


![-LHNbmkOeKp4ETrlSrg_5RkuWtrdD7Trsg.jpg?1651738246](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/-LHNbmkOeKp4ETrlSrg_5RkuWtrdD7Trsg.jpg%3F1651738246.jpg?width=559&height=39&name=-LHNbmkOeKp4ETrlSrg_5RkuWtrdD7Trsg.jpg%3F1651738246.jpg)


- Click the **Save **icon to complete the action.


### B. Employee Level


To enable PRS contributions at the employee level, follow these steps:


- Go to **People > Employee List**, search for the employee, and click on their name to navigate to their **profile**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48223663124/original/rbarM4h8vb7g8bs7LUvZymgUIOdcuIhQQw.png?1651738692)


- In the employee's **profile**, click the **Payroll **tab. In the "**Statutory Contributions**" column, click the **Edit **icon.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48223664619/original/Hq-DkuADVhuP5r1lOxxwE33GO42IgLuwJg.png?1651738960)


- In the "**PRS**" field, select the appropriate option from the dropdown menu.


![w71Bg6-Ri5XabeErXKKRwiZtNoJ6zW_aIQ.png?1651739276](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/w71Bg6-Ri5XabeErXKKRwiZtNoJ6zW_aIQ.png%3F1651739276.png?width=566&height=154&name=w71Bg6-Ri5XabeErXKKRwiZtNoJ6zW_aIQ.png%3F1651739276.png)


- Click the **Save **icon to complete the action.


Once PRS contributions are enabled, they will appear on the Payroll dashboard and the employee's payslip during payroll processing, as shown in the sample screenshots below.
