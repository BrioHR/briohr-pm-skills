# How to Enter Receipt Number and Date in Malaysia’s PCB II Tax Form (BrioHR Guide)

*Learn how to enter receipt number and date in Malaysia’s PCB II Form using BrioHR. Follow this step-by-step guide for accurate tax payroll reporting.*

You can enter the receipt number and receipt date for the PCB II form within the **monthly payroll cycle**.


### Step-by-Step: How to Enter Receipt Details in BrioHR


1. Go to **HR Lounge** > Navigate to the** Payroll module.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-09-2025-07-26-47-8609-AM.png?width=688&height=254&name=image-png-Apr-09-2025-07-26-47-8609-AM.png)


2. On the Payroll Dashboard, click on the payroll month in which the PCB payment was made. *For example, click on January 2025*


**NOTE:**


**
** You can only add the receipt number and receipt date for payroll months with the status **"Completed"**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-09-2025-07-27-02-3685-AM.png?width=2000&height=851&name=image-png-Apr-09-2025-07-27-02-3685-AM.png)


3. Locate the **Statutory Reporting** table.


4. Find **PCB** and click on the **Add Receipt** button.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-09-2025-07-28-11-4994-AM.png?width=2000&height=756&name=image-png-Apr-09-2025-07-28-11-4994-AM.png)


5. A pop-up window will appear, allowing you to enter the receipt details for the PCB II form (*only applicable for January 2025*).


There are 4 fields to fill in:


- **Receipt/Bank Slip/Transaction Number (PCB)**
- **Receipt/Transaction Date (PCB)**
- **Receipt/ Bank Slip/Transaction Number (CP38)**
- **Receipt/Transaction Date (CP38)**


6. Fill in the necessary details and click **Save**.


If the information is saved successfully, a confirmation message will appear.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-09-2025-09-37-08-7104-AM.png?width=2000&height=771&name=image-png-Apr-09-2025-09-37-08-7104-AM.png)
