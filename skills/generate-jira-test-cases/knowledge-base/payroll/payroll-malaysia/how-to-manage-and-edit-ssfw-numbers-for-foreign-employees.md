# How to Manage and Edit SSFW Numbers for Foreign Employees

*This article explains what the SSFW number is and how to manage and edit SSFW numbers for foreign employees registered with SOCSO.*

### What is the SSFW Number?


According to the Employee's Social Security Act 1969 (Act 4), employers who hire foreign workers must register their employees with SOCSO and contribute to the Employment Injury Scheme, effective from January 1, 2019. This **contribution is the employer's** responsibility and must be paid monthly.


The SSFW Number, or **Social Security Foreign Worker number**, a 12-digit number is created when employers register their foreign employees via the PERKESO ASSIST portal or at PERKESO counters for the first time.


### Where to Edit the SSFW Number for Foreign Employees?


#### Part 1: Edit Individually


For foreign employees, it's mandatory for the Admin/Payroll Processor to update the SSFW Number in their profiles.


1.   Navigate to the employee's **profile **and select the **Payroll **section.


2.   In the **General Payroll Information** section, click the **pencil icon** to enter Edit mode.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48278263990/original/jN1DjXS_IeUFQLDQnfYfb9YRLwyGiehHhQ.png?1674884457)


3.   Locate the **SSFW Number** (under PCB Number) and update the number in the text field.


4.   Click **Save**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48278264022/original/YTluuQh77oQfh4hSu4upTfWn48COyI1AJA.png?1674884519)


#### Part 2: Edit in Bulk


1.   Go to **HR Lounge.**


2.   Click on **Payroll**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48278264393/original/LAVVB6g75iQgJSiRPHbCen1pzxlW9o8XGg.png?1674884995)


Scroll down to the **Payroll employees list.**


3.   Click on the **Bulk Download/Upload** button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48278264405/original/hamytW4bSoLpuAplIJTiC_nJuPgyWmUtNA.png?1674885045)


4.   A pop-up window will appear; click on the **Download **button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48278264542/original/fowGimk1tpdr3onT0ZQGUh1YW6AzzTn8GA.png?1674885255)


An **Excel template** titled '**Employee Payroll Data**' will be downloaded to your computer.


5.   Open the '**Employee Payroll Data**' **worksheet** to make edits.


**NOTE:**


**Do not delete any worksheets or columns, and do not rearrange them in this Excel template.**


6.   Find **column [Y] **with the header title **SSFW Number** and update it. Click **Save**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48278264634/original/kdiCJrcoJh0TJYKrV-fshlT5xJl4S8drxg.png?1674885440)


Return to the pop-up window Bulk Download/Upload.


7.   Click **Choose File** and upload your saved Excel template.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48278264785/original/jdtLkfg4x1m31oC1sQDJoLmd13KfReQCHg.png?1674885588)
