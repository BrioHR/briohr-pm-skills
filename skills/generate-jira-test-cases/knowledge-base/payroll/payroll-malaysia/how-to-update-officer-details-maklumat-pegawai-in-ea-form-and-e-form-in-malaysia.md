# How to Update Officer Details (Maklumat Pegawai) in EA Form and E Form in Malaysia Payroll

*This article explains how to update officer details (maklumat pegawai) shown in the EA Form and E Form from the payroll registration settings.*

To update officer details (maklumat pegawai) in the EA Form and E Form using BrioHR, follow these steps:


Log in to Your BrioHR Account


- Navigate to HR Lounge >** Payroll > Settings > General**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-30-2026-08-37-55-9140-AM.png?width=670&height=297&name=image-png-Apr-30-2026-08-37-55-9140-AM.png)
**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48329093394/original/_UGkV6JBi4LxR82-qog4GLhg0u3inJV4FQ.png?1709519257)


- Locate the **[Registration Information]** Section and Update Officer Details

  - **Phone Number**: Nombor telefon pegawai
  - **Officer Name:** Nama pegawai
  - **Officer IC/Passport:** IC/Passport pegawai
  - **Officer Position:** Pangkat pegawai
  - **Officer Email Address**: Alamat emel pegawai
- After Updating the Fields, Don't Forget to **Save **Your Changes


####


#### E Form: Check the updated officer details in the 'Akaun Bahagian B' section.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48329094498/original/Iu8BULvMRyXNbPMhtSJCyrxL2MCQQ0U6JA.png?1709520356)


**NOTE:**


To change **Kategori Majikan (Employer Category) in Form E**, do not change company settings. Select the correct category at download time:


**HR Lounge > Payroll > Dashboard > Download (Form E/CP8D) > select the 'Status of Employer' in the pop-up window.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-30-2026-08-33-22-7324-AM.png?width=670&height=333&name=image-png-Apr-30-2026-08-33-22-7324-AM.png)


####


#### EA Form: Check the updated officer details in the 'Part F' section.
