# Malaysia Payroll Centre: Supported Banks for Payroll, Statutory and Zakat Bank Files in BrioHR

*Learn which banks support payroll salary files, statutory payment files (PCB, EPF, SOCSO, EIS), and zakat bank files in BrioHR. Ensure correct bank setup for smooth payroll processing.*

**Audience & Scope**


Audience: HR Admin & Payroll Manager


Module: Payroll


Country:  Malaysia


Pre-requisites: Access to HR Lounge > Payroll > Module & Module Settings


---


### Introduction


After completing a monthly payroll run, you can generate a bank file directly from BrioHR and upload it to your bank's portal for salary crediting and statutory payments.


Each company bank selected in the Payroll Center Settings will generate a specific bank file format. Therefore, it is essential to ensure that:


- The **correct company bank** is selected, and
- The **bank file version** matches your bank's requirements


This ensures successful uploads and prevents payment rejections.


**Learn More:** [How to Set Up a Company Bank Account in Payroll Center Settings](/knowledge/how-to-set-up-company-payroll-general-settings-in-malaysia#bank-account)


To download the bank files for salary payroll and statutory payment, you can download after a payroll cycle has been completed.


**Learn More:** [How to Download a Bank File for Bank Portal Upload and Payment](/knowledge/which-file-to-download-for-bank-portal-upload-and-payment?hsLang=en)


---


If the chosen company's bank does not support bank files for statutory payments in BrioHR, you can download the text file for each statutory contribution and upload it directly to the respective statutory portal.


The payment can then be completed through the statutory organization's portal.


**Learn More:**

[Step-by-Step Guide to Download Statutory Contribution Text Files (EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB)](/knowledge/payroll-malaysia-how-to-download-statutory-contributions-text-files?hsLang=en)
