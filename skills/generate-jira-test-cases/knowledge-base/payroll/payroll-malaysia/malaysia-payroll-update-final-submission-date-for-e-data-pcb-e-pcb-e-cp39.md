# Malaysia Payroll Update: Final Submission Date for e-Data PCB/e-PCB/e-CP39

*This article explains the final submission date for e-Data PCB, e-PCB, and e-CP39 Monthly Tax Deduction data and payments in Malaysia.*

**Important Deadline: Final Submission Date for Monthly Tax Deduction (MTD)**


The final submission date for the e-Data PCB, e-PCB, and e-CP39 Monthly Tax Deduction (MTD) data and payments in Malaysia is **15th January 2025**.


#### Key Points:


- Employers are required to complete all MTD submissions and payments by **15th January 2025**.
- After this date, MTD submission functions will be deactivated, and employers will only have limited access to the system for review purposes.


#### Transition to e-PCB Plus:


The **e-PCB Plus** system is** mandatory** for all employers, and you must transition to this new system to continue making accurate and timely submissions.


- **Launch Date:** The e-PCB Plus system is expected to launch by the **end of January 2025**, pending final testing by LHDN (Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia).
- **Action Required:** Employers must ensure they transition to e-PCB Plus to facilitate smooth future submissions.


**Important Note for Payroll Bank Application Users !**

Employers who are using a payroll bank application to make payments to LHDN do not need to register in the e-PCB Plus system.


#### For Employers:


- **Mandatory Transition:** All employers are required to adopt the e-PCB Plus system.
- **Questions or Assistance:** If you have any inquiries or require assistance with the transition to e-PCB Plus, please contact **LHDN** directly.


#### Next Steps for Employers:


1. Complete all **e-Data PCB, e-PCB, and e-CP39 submissions and payments** by **15th January 2025**.
2. Prepare for the **transition to e-PCB Plus** by the end of January 2025.
3. After the final submission date, e-Data PCB, e-PCB, and e-CP39 will be **fully deactivated**.
