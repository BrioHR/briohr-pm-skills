# Setting Statutory Contributions for Employees Aged 60 and Above (Malaysia Payroll)

*Ensure accurate statutory contribution settings for employees aged 60 and above, complying with Malaysia’s EPF, EIS and SOCSO regulations.*

- **EPF (Employees’ Provident Fund) Contribution**


  - Category C, D & E (Refer [here](https://briohr.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/48000986524-types-of-epf-category))
  - Employer contribution: **4%** (Malaysians)
  - Employer contribution: To refer to the following table for Non-Malaysians

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-13-2025-08-02-24-3305-AM.png?width=688&height=425&name=image-png-Feb-13-2025-08-02-24-3305-AM.png)
  - Employee contribution: **Optional** (default 0%, unless the employee opts in).
  - System allows employees to opt-in for contributions and reflects this in payroll calculations, able to customize by Rate (%) or Value ($)

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-13-2025-06-52-03-9434-AM.png?width=688&height=348&name=image-png-Feb-13-2025-06-52-03-9434-AM.png)
- **EIS (Employment Insurance System) Exemption**


  - Employees aged **60 and above are exempt** from EIS contributions.
  - To select [non related] under the Employee Payroll Profile to disable EIS deductions


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-13-2025-06-57-44-1222-AM.png?width=613&height=151&name=image-png-Feb-13-2025-06-57-44-1222-AM.png)


- **SOCSO (Social Security Organization) Contribution**


  - Employees aged **60 and above are covered under the Employment Injury Scheme only**.
  - To select [**Insured only against employment injury**] apply the correct reduced rate for SOCSO contributions.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-13-2025-06-59-54-4638-AM.png?width=676&height=250&name=image-png-Feb-13-2025-06-59-54-4638-AM.png)
