# Tax Resident Marital Status Categories

*This article explains the updated tax resident marital status categories aligned with the LHDN calculator, including the new fourth category.*

The Marital Status categories have been updated to align with those in the LHDN calculator.


Previously, the available options were: Single, Married (spouse not working), and Married (spouse working). We have now added a fourth category: Divorced, Widowed, or Single with an Adopted Child.


It is crucial for employees to update this information in their profiles as necessary. To edit your Marital Status, please follow the steps below:


1.   Go to the Employee Profile and click on the Payroll tab.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48259278626/original/BELS7iu4F8Cg5xQvnTYNDEq0iDuS3Sc4EQ.png?1666780646)


2.   Click on the Edit button in the General Payroll Information section.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48259278821/original/LTLeicsLO_vO7HAu3O5ivS4lpNRbxGVoFA.png?1666780715)


3.   In the Marital Status dropdown menu, select the appropriate category. After making the necessary changes, click Save to finalize the update.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48259279433/original/9lGgoNGzz4Falmi0ghFXmxE2hU81HAHv5g.png?1666780880)


**NOTE:**


**These categories are derived from the LHDN calculator.**
