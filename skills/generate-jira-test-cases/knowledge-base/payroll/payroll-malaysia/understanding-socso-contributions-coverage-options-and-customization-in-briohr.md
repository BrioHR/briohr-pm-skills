# Understanding SOCSO Contributions: Coverage Options and Customization in BrioHR

*SOCSO (Social Security Organization), also known as PERKESO (Pertubuhan Keselamatan Sosial), is a Malaysian government agency that provides social security protection to employees through insurance schemes.*

SOCSO operates under the **Employees' Social Security Act 1969** and covers employees in the private sector, except for domestic workers, civil servants, and those who are self-employed (unless they contribute voluntarily under specific schemes).





In BrioHR, there are 4 options to set up for SOCSO;


1. Insured against invalidity & employment injury
2. Insured against employment injury
3. Custom Rate
4. Custom Value


#### 1. Insured Against Invalidity & Employment Injury


- Provides financial protection to employees in cases of invalidity (permanent inability to work due to illness or disability) and employment injury (work-related accidents or occupational diseases).


- First Category (Employment Injury & Invalidity Scheme)


- If an employee experiences a permanent disability due to a medical condition, he/she is eligible for Invalidity Pension


- If the disability resulted from a workplace accident, he/she can claim benefits under the Employment Injury Scheme for medical expenses and rehabilitation.

#### 2. Insured Against Employment Injury


- Protects employees solely against injuries sustained during work-related activities, including accidents at the workplace, while commuting to work, or due to occupational diseases.


- Second Category (Employment Injury Scheme Only)


- A factory worker sustains an injury while operating machinery. SOCSO covers their medical expenses, rehabilitation costs, and compensates for temporary loss of income through the Temporary Disablement Benefit.


- If the injury results in long-term disability, they may receive the Permanent Disablement Benefit.

#### 3. Custom Rate


- A specific contribution **percentage** by both the employee & employer


- Rates are determined based on an agreed percentage rather than standard SOCSO tables.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-13-2025-02-35-34-7969-AM.png?width=623&height=155&name=image-png-Feb-13-2025-02-35-34-7969-AM.png)


---


#### 4. Custom Value


- A fixed contribution **amount** determined by the employer, instead of following SOCSO’s standard calculation based on wages.


- This structure allows businesses to remain flexible while still providing social security coverage for their workforce.
