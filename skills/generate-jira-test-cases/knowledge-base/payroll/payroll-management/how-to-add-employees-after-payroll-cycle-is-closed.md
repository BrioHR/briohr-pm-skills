# How to Add Employees After Payroll Cycle is Closed

*Learn how to add new employees to a closed payroll cycle in BrioHR. Follow this guide to ensure timely salary processing for late hires*

Sometimes, a new employee joins the company after the monthly payroll has been processed, yet their salary still needs to be paid within that same payroll month.


With BrioHR, users have the flexibility to **create additional payroll cycles** as needed.


In this article, you will learn how to:


- Set up a new payroll cycle for the new hire
- Allocate salary percentage for the new hire
- Preparing for future payroll cycles *(to ensure the new hire is included in the main/default cycle in future months*)


**IMPORTANT**


**Reopening a closed payroll cycle and adding a new hire to it can carry certain risks. For example, original payroll amounts may automatically change if any modifications are made to the employee's payroll profile or the payroll center settings after the payroll has been closed.**





**Hence, we recommend creating a new payroll cycle instead of reopening and modifying a closed payroll cycle.**


**NOTE**
**You can process a new payroll cycle if the status of the payroll month is "Ongoing". If the status is "Completed", you need to reopen the payroll cycle first to change the payroll month status to "Ongoing". **





**For assistance, please contact BrioHR Live Chat, or email to support@briohr.com.**

#### Setting Up a New Payroll Cycle for the New Hire



4. Click on **Payroll Cycle**
**
![3-Aug-23-2024-05-47-08-5812-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/3-Aug-23-2024-05-47-08-5812-AM.png?width=688&height=305&name=3-Aug-23-2024-05-47-08-5812-AM.png)
**
5. Click on **New Cycle**
**
![4-Aug-23-2024-05-48-40-8300-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/4-Aug-23-2024-05-48-40-8300-AM.png?width=688&height=295&name=4-Aug-23-2024-05-48-40-8300-AM.png)
**
6. In the **General** tab, enter the *Cycle Name*,* Pay Day*, *Start Date*, and *End Date.*

![5-Aug-23-2024-05-50-09-4201-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/5-Aug-23-2024-05-50-09-4201-AM.png?width=483&height=442&name=5-Aug-23-2024-05-50-09-4201-AM.png)

7. Next, click on the **Employees** tab, and check the box next to the new hire’s name.

8. Click **Save** once the new hire’s box is checked.

![6-Aug-23-2024-05-51-53-7022-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/6-Aug-23-2024-05-51-53-7022-AM.png?width=660&height=407&name=6-Aug-23-2024-05-51-53-7022-AM.png)


---


#### Allocating Salary Percentage for the New Hire



9. On the same **Payroll Cycle** setting page, go to **Set Allocation For Employee Salary & Remuneration.**
**
![7-Aug-23-2024-05-53-29-3848-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/7-Aug-23-2024-05-53-29-3848-AM.png?width=688&height=299&name=7-Aug-23-2024-05-53-29-3848-AM.png)
**
10. Click on **Set Salary Percentage Allocation.**
**
![8-4](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/8-4.png?width=688&height=243&name=8-4.png)
**

11. In the **Default** cycle column, set the percentage as **0%**

12. At the **New Hire** cycle column (*the new cycle created*), set the percentage as **100%**

13. Click the **Save & Close** button.

![9-2](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/9-2.png?width=688&height=307&name=9-2.png)


Click the "ongoing" payroll month.
You can now process the salary for the new hire by following the same steps as for the main payroll run. The new hire cycle will be visible once you click the **Run Payroll** button.

![10-2](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/10-2.png?width=585&height=401&name=10-2.png)

### Preparing for Future Payroll Cycles



After closing the New Hire cycle and preparing to process the next month’s payroll, it’s crucial to follow these steps** to ensure the previous month’s new hire is included in the main payroll run**:


**IMPORTANT STEP BEFORE PROCESSING PAYROLL FOR FUTURE MONTHS**





1. In** HR Lounge**, search for the new hire included in the New Hire cycle from the previous month


![11-Aug-23-2024-06-00-08-3507-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/11-Aug-23-2024-06-00-08-3507-AM.png?width=688&height=286&name=11-Aug-23-2024-06-00-08-3507-AM.png)


2. Navigate to employee’s **Payroll profile**


3. Click the **pencil icon** under **Payroll** section to edit the salary allocation percentage


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-23-2024-06-01-40-8189-AM.png?width=688&height=472&name=image-png-Aug-23-2024-06-01-40-8189-AM.png)


*Before editing, you can see the allocation is set as follows :*


![12-4](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/12-4.png?width=688&height=264&name=12-4.png)


4. Update the percentage to 100% in the Default (or main) cycle, and uncheck the New Hire cycle box

5. Click **Save**  once the update is complete.


The employee will now be included in your main (default) payroll run.


![13-1](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/13-1.png?width=688&height=296&name=13-1.png)
