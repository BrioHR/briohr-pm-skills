# How to Download & Customize Payroll Reports for a Specific Date Range

*This guide provides step-by-step instructions on selecting payroll centers, date ranges, and data filters to generate accurate payroll records.*

### Steps to Download Payroll Reports for a Custom Date Range


1. **Access the Reports Section**


  - Navigate to **HR Lounge** > **Reports**.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-27-2025-07-44-42-7777-AM.png?width=688&height=329&name=image-png-Feb-27-2025-07-44-42-7777-AM.png)
  - Search for **Company Payroll Report** and click **Create**.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-27-2025-07-45-47-4650-AM.png?width=688&height=452&name=image-png-Feb-27-2025-07-45-47-4650-AM.png)
2. **Select Payroll Report Settings**


  - Choose the **Payroll Center** (if multiple centers exist).
  - Select **Mode**:

    - Choose **Range** and select the desired **month range**.
  - Pick a **Cycle** (individual or combined payroll cycles).
  - Set **Granularity** (whether to display employees only or include departments).

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-28-2025-03-02-09-2146-AM.png?width=688&height=386&name=image-png-Feb-28-2025-03-02-09-2146-AM.png)
3. **Customize the Report Using Fields & Filters**


  - Go to **Fields & Filters** to select the data to include.
  - **Example Customization Options:**


    - **Include Employee EPF**: Tick the checkbox under **Payroll Employee Contributions**.
    - **Exclude Employee EPF**: Untick the checkbox.
    - **Filter by Office**:

      - Go to **Employment Details** > Click **+ Add Filter** under **Office**.
      - Select the specific office name to include in the report.
*
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-27-2025-09-31-49-8068-AM.png?width=688&height=876&name=image-png-Feb-27-2025-09-31-49-8068-AM.png)

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-27-2025-09-36-13-0482-AM.png?width=688&height=889&name=image-png-Feb-27-2025-09-36-13-0482-AM.png)
*
4. **Export the Payroll Report**


  - Click **Export Report** to generate the report.
  - Available formats: **CSV, Excel, PDF**.
