# How to send payslip and download payslip in bulk for a specific payroll month?

*Efficiently send and download payslips in bulk for a payroll month with BrioHR. Follow our step-by-step guide to streamline employee payslip distribution.*

Managing payroll efficiently involves ensuring all employees receive their payslips accurately and on time. Here's a simple guide to help you **send and download payslips in bulk** for a specific **payroll month;**


1. Navigate to the HR Lounge and select the Payroll module


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-27-2024-08-42-13-8864-AM.png?width=688&height=412&name=image-png-Dec-27-2024-08-42-13-8864-AM.png)


2. Find [Quick Actions] and click into [***Payroll Documents***]


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-27-2024-08-47-24-2443-AM.png?width=688&height=378&name=image-png-Dec-27-2024-08-47-24-2443-AM.png)


3. Make sure that the Document type is [***Payslip***]


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-27-2024-08-54-19-5187-AM.png?width=688&height=168&name=image-png-Dec-27-2024-08-54-19-5187-AM.png)





There are 2 options, either to send payslip in bulk or to download payslip in bulk


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-27-2024-08-55-49-6269-AM.png?width=688&height=162&name=image-png-Dec-27-2024-08-55-49-6269-AM.png)





4. To send payslip in bulk, click into ***Send Payslip in Bulk. ***Select the year, month and payroll cycle you wish to send, choose the employee and click ***Send payslip***. You may also filter the status if the payslip has been sent for particular employee


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-27-2024-08-59-27-4561-AM.png?width=499&height=559&name=image-png-Dec-27-2024-08-59-27-4561-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-27-2024-09-00-53-3926-AM.png?width=498&height=567&name=image-png-Dec-27-2024-09-00-53-3926-AM.png)





5. To download payslip in bulk, click into ***Payslip Bulk Download***. Select the year, month and payroll cycle you wish to download, choose the employee and click ***Download payslip***


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-27-2024-09-04-46-7077-AM.png?width=491&height=516&name=image-png-Dec-27-2024-09-04-46-7077-AM.png)
