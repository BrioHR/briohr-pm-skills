# How to Bulk Download Payslips (Singapore/ SG)

*This guide explains how to bulk download payslips in BrioHR for employees in Singapore. Follow these steps to easily select payroll cycles, employees, and download payslips in bulk.*

### Steps to Bulk Download Payslips


#### 1. Login to Your BrioHR Account


- Use your credentials to log in and access the BrioHR dashboard.


#### 2. Navigate to the Payroll Module


- Click on **HR Lounge** from the main menu.
- Go to the **Payroll module**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-22-2024-04-53-28-4168-PM.png?width=688&height=295&name=image-png-Dec-22-2024-04-53-28-4168-PM.png)


#### 3. Access Payroll Documents


- In the **Quick Actions box**, click on the **Payroll Documents button**.
- Navigate to **Payslip Bulk Download**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-22-2024-04-54-36-0001-PM.png?width=688&height=297&name=image-png-Dec-22-2024-04-54-36-0001-PM.png)


#### 4. Select Payroll Details


- Choose the **year**, **month**, and **payroll cycle** of the payslips you want to download.


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-22-2024-04-55-14-1592-PM.png?width=688&height=306&name=image-png-Dec-22-2024-04-55-14-1592-PM.png)
**


#### 5. Select Employees


- Select the specific **employee names** from the list.
- To select all employees, check the box next to the **search field**.


#### 6. Download Payslips


- Click on the **Download Payslip button**.
- A confirmation message will appear once the process begins.


#### 7. Receive Payslips in Email


- A **zip file** containing the payslips will be sent to your **registered email** address.
