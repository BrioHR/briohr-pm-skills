# How to Enable Employer Contributions (Singapore)

*Step-by-Step Guide to Enable Employer Contributions for Singapore Payroll in BrioHR.*

Login to the BrioHR account


1. Click on HR Lounge


2. Navigate to Payroll


![1-Sep-17-2024-09-05-49-4743-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/1-Sep-17-2024-09-05-49-4743-AM.png?width=688&height=299&name=1-Sep-17-2024-09-05-49-4743-AM.png)


 You'll be directed to the Payroll dashboard page.


3. Click on Settings


![2-Sep-17-2024-09-08-32-6732-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/2-Sep-17-2024-09-08-32-6732-AM.png?width=688&height=293&name=2-Sep-17-2024-09-08-32-6732-AM.png)


4. Click on "General"


![3-Sep-17-2024-09-09-43-3014-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/3-Sep-17-2024-09-09-43-3014-AM.png?width=688&height=303&name=3-Sep-17-2024-09-09-43-3014-AM.png)


5. Click on dropdown at the Organization


![4-Sep-17-2024-09-10-59-5664-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/4-Sep-17-2024-09-10-59-5664-AM.png?width=688&height=356&name=4-Sep-17-2024-09-10-59-5664-AM.png)


6. Select the statutory bodies to which your company contributes. There are seven (7) options available (SDL, CPF, ECF, FWL, SINDA, CDAC and MBMF).


Example : Your company does have Chinese employee but the CDAC is not selected in this setting. The CDAC deduction will not be calculated in the payroll run.


If you don't select the organization, it will NOT be included during a payroll run.


![5-Sep-17-2024-09-12-46-4441-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/5-Sep-17-2024-09-12-46-4441-AM.png?width=688&height=374&name=5-Sep-17-2024-09-12-46-4441-AM.png)


7. Click the "Save" button once you have done with the update.


![6-Sep-17-2024-09-15-21-3596-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/6-Sep-17-2024-09-15-21-3596-AM.png?width=688&height=281&name=6-Sep-17-2024-09-15-21-3596-AM.png)
