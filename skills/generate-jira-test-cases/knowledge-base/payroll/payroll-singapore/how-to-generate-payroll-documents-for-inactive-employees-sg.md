# How to Generate Payroll Documents for Inactive Employees (SG)

*Even after employees resign, HR professionals may need to generate payroll documents for inactive employees for record-keeping, audits, or employee requests. This guide will walk you through the steps to efficiently retrieve the documents.*

### Steps to Generate Payroll Documents for Inactive Employees


1. **Access the Payroll Module**


  - Navigate to **HR Lounge** > **Payroll**.
2. **Locate the Payroll Employee List**


  - On the **Payroll Dashboard**, scroll down to **Payroll Employee List**.
3. **Filter for Inactive Employees**


  - Click on the filter option and select **Inactive** to display resigned employees.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-27-2025-04-07-10-4593-AM.png?width=1063&height=487&name=image-png-Mar-27-2025-04-07-10-4593-AM.png)
4. **Generate Payroll Documents**


  - Click on the **Select an Action** button.
  - Choose the **payroll document** you want to download for the selected employee.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-27-2025-04-08-04-0182-AM.png?width=1084&height=451&name=image-png-Mar-27-2025-04-08-04-0182-AM.png)
