# Payroll Singapore - Add Overtime for Day Off with Customizable Multiplier

*This article guides you through setting up Day Off Overtime (OT) calculations with custom multipliers and applying them to your payroll processing.*

#### We've added Overtime (Day Off) as another default overtime payroll item. Now, Singapore payroll centers can configure different rates for four distinct OT types:


- #### Normal workdays
- #### Rest days
- #### Days off
- #### Holidays


#### You can easily assign a specific multiplier to each category to match your company's policy.


---


**Audience & Scope**


Audience: HR Admin & Payroll Manager


Module: Payroll


Country: Singapore


Pre-requisites: HR Modules Permission in User group settings





**Step-by-Step Instructions**


PART 1: Setting Up the multiplier for the Day Off


1. Go to **HR Lounge > Payroll > Settings > General**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-01-2026-06-51-41-2221-AM.png?width=2266&height=1111&name=image-png-Jul-01-2026-06-51-41-2221-AM.png)


2. Under the **Calculation** section, type in your multiplier for **Day Off**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-01-2026-06-52-49-9379-AM.png?width=1579&height=1011&name=image-png-Jul-01-2026-06-52-49-9379-AM.png)


3. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click **Save**





PART 2: Adding Day Off Overtime during Payroll Run


1. In the payroll **dashboard**, **run the payroll cycle** that you want to process the Day Off OT in


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-01-2026-06-54-59-2218-AM.png?width=1747&height=994&name=image-png-Jul-01-2026-06-54-59-2218-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-01-2026-06-55-26-0268-AM.png?width=584&height=273&name=image-png-Jul-01-2026-06-55-26-0268-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-01-2026-06-55-55-0112-AM.png?width=580&height=253&name=image-png-Jul-01-2026-06-55-55-0112-AM.png)


2. Choose **Overtime (Day Off)** as an **addition** and enter the Day Off OT **hours**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-01-2026-06-57-18-6367-AM.png?width=2407&height=1069&name=image-png-Jul-01-2026-06-57-18-6367-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-01-2026-06-57-55-5790-AM.png?width=2299&height=1075&name=image-png-Jul-01-2026-06-57-55-5790-AM.png)


3. You can also add in the OT hours via the **Overtime multi rates table**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-01-2026-07-00-03-1511-AM.png?width=2377&height=888&name=image-png-Jul-01-2026-07-00-03-1511-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-01-2026-07-00-50-1467-AM.png?width=2377&height=990&name=image-png-Jul-01-2026-07-00-50-1467-AM.png)

4. The **amount** to be paid should reflect correctly based on the **multiplier** you set


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-01-2026-07-01-47-9625-AM.png?width=2377&height=973&name=image-png-Jul-01-2026-07-01-47-9625-AM.png)


5. **Complete the payroll cycle** as usual and check that **payslips** reflect accordingly

**Common Issues/FAQ**


Q1: My company doesn't have 'Day Off' in our work schedule. Do we have to set up the Overtime multiplier?


A1: If you do not use 'Day Off' in your company practice, you do not have to make any changes. We have created this option for clients who want the flexibility to choose another type of 'Rest Day' with a different OT rate (multiplier) calculation.
