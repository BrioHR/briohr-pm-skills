# Payroll Singapore: How to Access the Statutory Report (CPF) After Completing Payroll?

*Guide to Accessing and Downloading the CPF Statutory Report in BrioHR After Completing Payroll*

Login to the BrioHR account


1. Click on **HR Lounge**


2. Navigate to **Payroll**
![1-Sep-17-2024-09-05-49-4743-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/1-Sep-17-2024-09-05-49-4743-AM.png?width=688&height=299&name=1-Sep-17-2024-09-05-49-4743-AM.png)


3. Go to your **Completed** payroll month.


To download the statutory file, the payroll month’s status must be **Completed**


![1-Sep-18-2024-04-00-17-2163-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/1-Sep-18-2024-04-00-17-2163-AM.png?width=688&height=305&name=1-Sep-18-2024-04-00-17-2163-AM.png)


4. In the **Statutory Reporting**’s box, click the **Text File** button.
![1-Sep-18-2024-04-39-01-4088-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/1-Sep-18-2024-04-39-01-4088-AM.png?width=688&height=378&name=1-Sep-18-2024-04-39-01-4088-AM.png)


5. The report will be downloaded into your designated folder.
![1-Sep-18-2024-04-44-59-3407-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/1-Sep-18-2024-04-44-59-3407-AM.png?width=688&height=141&name=1-Sep-18-2024-04-44-59-3407-AM.png)
