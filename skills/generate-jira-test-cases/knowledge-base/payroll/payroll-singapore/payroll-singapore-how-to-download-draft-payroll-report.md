# Payroll Singapore: How to Download Draft Payroll Report

*In BrioHR, users can download the draft monthly payroll report for verification before finalizing the payroll.*

Login to the BrioHR account


1. Click on **HR Lounge**


2. Navigate to **Payroll**
![1-Sep-17-2024-09-05-49-4743-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/1-Sep-17-2024-09-05-49-4743-AM.png?width=688&height=299&name=1-Sep-17-2024-09-05-49-4743-AM.png)


3. Go to your **Ongoing** payroll month
![1-Sep-18-2024-03-30-37-5333-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/1-Sep-18-2024-03-30-37-5333-AM.png?width=688&height=320&name=1-Sep-18-2024-03-30-37-5333-AM.png)


4. Click the **Continue** button


![2-Sep-18-2024-03-32-00-3122-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/2-Sep-18-2024-03-32-00-3122-AM.png?width=688&height=534&name=2-Sep-18-2024-03-32-00-3122-AM.png)


5. Click the **Payroll Report** button
![3-Sep-18-2024-03-33-48-6577-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/3-Sep-18-2024-03-33-48-6577-AM.png?width=688&height=535&name=3-Sep-18-2024-03-33-48-6577-AM.png)


6. The report will be downloaded into your designated folder.
