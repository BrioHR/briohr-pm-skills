# Payroll Singapore: How to Generate Bank File

*How to Generate and Download Bank Files for Singapore Payroll in BrioHR.*

Login to the BrioHR account


1. Click on **HR Lounge**


2. Navigate to **Payroll**
![1-Sep-17-2024-09-05-49-4743-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/1-Sep-17-2024-09-05-49-4743-AM.png?width=688&height=299&name=1-Sep-17-2024-09-05-49-4743-AM.png)


3. Go to your **current month** payroll cycle


![3-Sep-18-2024-04-52-47-3810-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/3-Sep-18-2024-04-52-47-3810-AM.png?width=688&height=387&name=3-Sep-18-2024-04-52-47-3810-AM.png)


4. At the **Completed** payroll cycle, click the **See Details** button


To download the bank file, the payroll cycle’s status must be **Completed**


![4-Sep-18-2024-04-56-42-8685-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/4-Sep-18-2024-04-56-42-8685-AM.png?width=688&height=493&name=4-Sep-18-2024-04-56-42-8685-AM.png)


5. In the **Bank File**’s box, click the **Download** button.


![5-Sep-18-2024-04-58-50-8690-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/5-Sep-18-2024-04-58-50-8690-AM.png?width=688&height=383&name=5-Sep-18-2024-04-58-50-8690-AM.png)


6. You will have an option to exclude the employee from the bank file or download the bank file for all employees. Click the **Generate** button.
![3-Sep-18-2024-04-03-12-3858-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/3-Sep-18-2024-04-03-12-3858-AM.png?width=578&height=498&name=3-Sep-18-2024-04-03-12-3858-AM.png)


![6-Sep-18-2024-05-00-08-5683-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/6-Sep-18-2024-05-00-08-5683-AM.png?width=578&height=282&name=6-Sep-18-2024-05-00-08-5683-AM.png)


7. The report will be downloaded into your designated folder.


![7-Sep-18-2024-05-01-52-5518-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/7-Sep-18-2024-05-01-52-5518-AM.png?width=688&height=176&name=7-Sep-18-2024-05-01-52-5518-AM.png)


![6-Sep-18-2024-04-05-28-7065-AM](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/6-Sep-18-2024-04-05-28-7065-AM.png?width=688&height=260&name=6-Sep-18-2024-04-05-28-7065-AM.png)
