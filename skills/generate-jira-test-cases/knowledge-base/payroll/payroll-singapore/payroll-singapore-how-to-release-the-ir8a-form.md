# Payroll Singapore: How to Release the IR8A Form

*This article explains how HR can release the IR8A form in Singapore payroll so employees can download it from their Payroll Documents section.*

We are excited to introduce the new "**Release IR8A Form**" feature in our Singapore payroll module.


Employees can now conveniently download their IR8A form directly from the Payroll Documents section on their Home page. However, before the form becomes available for download, HR must release it to all employees.


The release of the IR8A Form typically occurs **after HR has completed the IRAS AIS submission**.


To release the IR8A form, follow these steps:





[Embedded content](https://www.loom.com/embed/aab28097bc4449b189484610ff8515f9?sid=924823fa-a4f9-4e14-a308-2da263433383)





Login to your BrioHR account. Navigate to HR Lounge > Payroll.


- Access **Payroll Documents**: On the payroll dashboard, go to **Quick Actions > Click the Payroll Documents **button**.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48327198810/original/HDPVdkru4IuqZYkRKub04x6q74BlChTT3A.png?1707984814)

- Select **IRAS**:


Ensure you choose the **correct Year of Assessment (YA)**. For instance, 2024 corresponds to employee remuneration for the year ending December 31, 2023.


- Release the IR8A Form: Click the "**Release IR8A Form**" button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48327199485/original/y_62t7GGwWpAXxo-QgCnu3Kek1aUq7k1Cw.png?1707985210)


- The button will change status to "**RELEASED**."


Once the IR8A Form is released, employees can retrieve a PDF copy from their Home page under Payroll Documents.


For Employees: To download your personal IR8A form, refer to this guide:


[How to Download Personal IR8A Form?](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-download-your-personal-ir8a-form-from-briohr?hsLang=en)

### Undoing the Release of the IR8A Form:


If you need to undo the release of the IR8A form, click the **ellipsis icon (three dots)** and select "**Undo Release.**"
