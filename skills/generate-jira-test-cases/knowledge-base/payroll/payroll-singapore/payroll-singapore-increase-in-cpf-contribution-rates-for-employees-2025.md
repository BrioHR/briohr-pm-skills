# Payroll Singapore: Increase in CPF Contribution Rates for Employees (2025)

*This article explains the January 2025 CPF contribution rate increase for employees aged 55 to 65 and what it means for payroll.*

Starting January 1, 2025, CPF contribution rates will increase for certain groups of employees.


**Who Will Be Affected?**
This update applies to employees **aged 55 to 65 **and will take effect for wages earned from January 1, 2025.





![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-20-2024-03-17-55-9523-AM.png?width=688&height=389&name=image-png-Dec-20-2024-03-17-55-9523-AM.png)


**For more detailed information, please visit the [CPF Board’s official website.](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/employer/infohub/news/cpf-related-announcements/new-contribution-rates)**





**What Does This Mean for Payroll?**
All **payroll cycles in 2025** will **automatically** reflect the updated CPF contribution rates. The updated rates can be found in Tables 1 to 5 of the [CPF contribution rate tables](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/content/dam/web/employer/employer-obligations/documents/CPF_contribution_rates_from_1_Jan_2025.pdf), effective from January 1, 2025.


For payroll cycles in 2024, the current CPF contribution rates will remain unchanged. The adjustments apply only to payroll cycles beginning in 2025.
