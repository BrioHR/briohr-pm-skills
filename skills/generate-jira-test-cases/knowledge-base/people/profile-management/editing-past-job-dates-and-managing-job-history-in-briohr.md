# Editing Past Job Dates and Managing Job History in BrioHR

*Learn how to edit job dates and add job history in BrioHR. This guide ensures accurate employee records, helping you correct errors or update missing job details quickly and efficiently.*

### Editing Dates for Past Jobs


Follow these steps to make corrections to past job dates:


1. Login to** BrioHR**
2. Navigate to the** Employee Page**
3. Select the** Job to Edit**
4. Make the Necessary Changes
5. Review the Information


---


### Adding an Item to the Job History


To add a new entry to an employee's job history, follow these steps:


1. Login to** BrioHR**
2. Go to the** Employee Page**
3. **Add a New Job Entry**
4. Fill in the Job Details
5. Review the Information
