# How to add/edit/delete Job Grades?

*Step-by-Step Guide to Adding, Editing, and Deleting Job Grades in BrioHR*

This feature is for you to include all the different job grades that are available your company into the system.


To do this, go to Settings > Job Grades.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-23-2024-05-30-02-9366-AM.png?width=688&height=292&name=image-png-Aug-23-2024-05-30-02-9366-AM.png)


From here, you can click "+ Add job grade" to insert all job grades that your company has.


You will be able to include the name of the Job grade into the text field available.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-23-2024-05-31-25-7856-AM.png?width=430&height=184&name=image-png-Aug-23-2024-05-31-25-7856-AM.png)


Once that is done, you will be able to see it in the employee's profile, as shown in the screenshot below.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-23-2024-05-34-12-7377-AM.png?width=688&height=274&name=image-png-Aug-23-2024-05-34-12-7377-AM.png)


To make adjust your employee's job grade, you can go to their profile > Edit > Job grade.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-23-2024-05-34-55-3846-AM.png?width=688&height=462&name=image-png-Aug-23-2024-05-34-55-3846-AM.png)


To make any edits to the job grades, go back to Settings > Job grade> Click the green pencil icon beside the job grade that you want to edit.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-23-2024-05-36-08-9940-AM.png?width=688&height=353&name=image-png-Aug-23-2024-05-36-08-9940-AM.png)


To delete any of the custom fields, you will be able to do so by clicking the red dustbin icon.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-23-2024-05-36-35-3683-AM.png?width=688&height=353&name=image-png-Aug-23-2024-05-36-35-3683-AM.png)
