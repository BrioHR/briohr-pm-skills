# How to add/edit/delete/mark as returned equipment

*This article provides a step-by-step guide to managing equipment in BrioHR, including adding, editing, deleting, and marking equipment as returned. Streamline your equipment tracking process by following these easy instructions.*

Login to your BrioHR account:


1. At Home, go to **My Profile.**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-26-2024-07-19-49-7754-AM.png?width=688&height=358&name=image-png-Nov-26-2024-07-19-49-7754-AM.png)
**


2. Navigate to **Equipment** section.


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-26-2024-07-32-00-9986-AM.png?width=688&height=376&name=image-png-Nov-26-2024-07-32-00-9986-AM.png)
**


---


### To add new Equipment


3. Click on **'+' icon** to add new Equipment.


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-26-2024-07-33-49-7757-AM.png?width=688&height=376&name=image-png-Nov-26-2024-07-33-49-7757-AM.png)
**


4. Fill the **Equipment type, Equipment Details**, tick the **checkbox if item required to be returned**, and choose the **Date Received.**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-26-2024-07-35-16-9598-AM.png?width=497&height=537&name=image-png-Nov-26-2024-07-35-16-9598-AM.png)
**


5. Click on **Save** button to save the details.


---


### To edit the Equipment


6. Click on **Edit pencil icon** to edit the **Equipment.**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-26-2024-07-45-56-6133-AM.png?width=688&height=376&name=image-png-Nov-26-2024-07-45-56-6133-AM.png)
**


7. Edit the required fields.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-26-2024-12-12-11-8483-PM.png?width=491&height=534&name=image-png-Nov-26-2024-12-12-11-8483-PM.png)


8. Click on **Save** button to save the details.


---


### To delete the Equipment


9. Click on **trash bin icon** to delete the **Equipment.**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-26-2024-12-14-24-5131-PM.png?width=688&height=387&name=image-png-Nov-26-2024-12-14-24-5131-PM.png)
**


10. Click on **Confirm button** to confirm deletion.


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-26-2024-12-15-09-4742-PM.png?width=332&height=231&name=image-png-Nov-26-2024-12-15-09-4742-PM.png)
**


---


### To mark Equipment as returned


11. Click on **right icon** to mark the Equipment as Returned


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-26-2024-12-17-32-3965-PM.png?width=688&height=387&name=image-png-Nov-26-2024-12-17-32-3965-PM.png)


12. Choose **Date Return.**


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-26-2024-12-19-02-4976-PM.png?width=491&height=448&name=image-png-Nov-26-2024-12-19-02-4976-PM.png)


13. Click on **Confirm return button** to confirm the action
