# How to add/edit/delete spouse information?

*Manage Employee's Spouse Information: Add, Edit, or Delete Details Easily.*

This feature assists you in adding/editing/deleting the employee's spouse information in the system.


To find the spouse information, you can go to the employee's profile > Family details.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-27-2024-05-09-47-3266-PM.png?width=688&height=467&name=image-png-Aug-27-2024-05-09-47-3266-PM.png)


To make any changes to the spouse information, you can click on the green pencil icon on the right of the page.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-27-2024-05-10-05-4415-PM.png?width=688&height=467&name=image-png-Aug-27-2024-05-10-05-4415-PM.png)


Once you have click on the green pencil icon, you will be able to edit the following:


- Name
- Mobile phone
- Email
- Date of birth
- Nationality
- Documentation information


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-27-2024-05-18-47-9670-PM.png?width=688&height=651&name=image-png-Aug-27-2024-05-18-47-9670-PM.png)


Once you have completed the updates, do remember to click "Save".


Lastly, to delete the spouse information, you can click on the red dustbin icon on the right of the page.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-27-2024-05-10-40-6635-PM.png?width=688&height=467&name=image-png-Aug-27-2024-05-10-40-6635-PM.png)
