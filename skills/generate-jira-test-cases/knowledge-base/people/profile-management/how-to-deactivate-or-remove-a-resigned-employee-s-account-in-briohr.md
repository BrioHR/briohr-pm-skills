# How to Deactivate or Remove a Resigned Employee's Account in BrioHR

*This article explains how to end a resigned employee's employment to revoke their access, since profiles cannot be fully deleted.*

**NOTE:**


- **You cannot completely remove an employee or their profile from the system.**
- **Instead, you can select 'End Employment' on the resigned employee's profile to revoke their user access.**
- **The employee will be automatically categorized as 'Inactive' and will no longer appear in the list of active employees.**
- **Only an Admin User with the necessary access can perform this action.**


---


### A) Changing Account Status from 'Active' to 'Inactive'


Follow these steps to '**End Employment**':


- Go to **HR Lounge > Employee Management**.
- Search for the employee's Name or ID in the **search **column.
- Click on the employee's name to navigate to their **profile**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-15-2026-02-26-57-3997-AM.png?width=2320&height=1033&name=image-png-Jul-15-2026-02-26-57-3997-AM.png)


- On the resigned employee's **profile**, click the **ellipsis ('...')** icon next to the Edit button.
- Select '**End Employment**' from the dropdown menu.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-15-2026-02-28-17-1280-AM.png?width=1699&height=616&name=image-png-Jul-15-2026-02-28-17-1280-AM.png)


- Input the **Employment End Date** to revoke the resigned employee's user access.
- Click **Save **to complete the action or **Cancel **to abort.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48163466585/original/HnXhquM5fWyeaFZ5FK9WVRmYv7hKx3OJzg.png?1637829928)


**NOTE:**


1. **An employee listed as 'Inactive' will be automatically excluded from Payroll. The system will prorate the payroll amount according to the selected Employment End Date.**
2. **If you do not see the ellipsis button, you do not have permission to edit an employee's end date. Please contact your Account Admin to enable this permission.**
3. **An employee's profile will change from 'Active' to 'Inactive' once their employment end date has passed.**


After completing these steps, you can double-check the status of the resigned employee by following these steps:


- Click on **Settings**.
- Click on **Users**.
- Search for the name of the resigned employee to view their '**Inactive**' status and **Employment End Date.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48163473328/original/dtFwIwkk_G9XC3EH4RefqrldL2JybR9B2w.png?1637830851)


**NOTE: **


**INACTIVE status will only appear ONE DAY AFTER THE EMPLOYMENT END DATE. **
