# How to Set Up Children's Information in Family Details

*This article explains how to fill in children's information, including nationality, in the Family Details section.*

Our system now includes a convenient feature that allows you to assign the nationality of employees' children in the Family Details section.


**Step-by-Step Instructions to Fill in Children's Information**


- Go to **Employee Profile > Family Details > Children Information > +Add Children**.


**NOTE:**


**If you cannot see the "+Add Children" button, your access might be disabled. Please contact your account Admin to enable this feature for you.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48304111071/original/Q-TsihSUmeLtQ1QhQ4kkbC7XwaQGFpQdtQ.png?1689067012)


- Under **Nationality**, click on the dropdown menu to select the relevant nationality.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48304241170/original/5Mq08XctQkk2DhgW1h45hXGFTUfXTBm73g.png?1689148714)


- Click on the **Add Document** button if you need to upload any documents for the child's information.
- Fill in all the necessary details under Identity Documents and click **Save **to capture the information in the system.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48304241871/original/Dxn4CNdS11ngDu8EHDU-rr6nW44nBxXGLw.png?1689149077)


Once completed, the child's information will be saved as shown in the example screenshot below.
