# How to Enable 360 Feedback During an Ongoing Performance Cycle

*This article explains how to enable 360 feedback during an ongoing performance cycle if it was initially disabled.*

**NOTE:**


**The 360 feedback feature can only be enabled during an ongoing cycle if it was initially disabled for all employees at the beginning of the cycle.**


To enable 360 feedback, follow these steps:


- In the **HR Lounge,** click on **Performance**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48242445515/original/34P_dpU8tSS1RozqQUk_Oh-G6vChtRikSA.png?1659406627)


- In the **Cycle Overview **dashboard, click on the "<" icon to access the settings for the ongoing (active) cycle.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48242445787/original/cZfBnbXy5JLjkLA1QrvGFswennnKNXlzLw.png?1659406982)


- In the active cycle, click on the **Edit **icon.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48242446027/original/ONMAl4oOzm0pD0MV7fy6-s_M6LCdDk9qEQ.png?1659407178)


- Scroll down and click on Column 5 **(Select your add-ons: 1-on-1 and 360 feedback).**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48242446277/original/4n2AERFd5-A62kpU9Qlu-PsQwChhBMAjsg.png?1659407386)


- Toggle the 360 feedback switch to enable it.


**NOTE:**


**This is a one-time action. Once 360 feedback is enabled for the active cycle, it cannot be disabled later.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48242446529/original/u_zcJ9FQbD1J0_eJoIQ3UMY7zBn2NxJgfQ.png?1659407578)


- Input the **questions**, configure the **feedback ratings**, and select the **start and end dates for the 360 feedback**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48242447029/original/ga0IHLTsCp-1M-oBnVF6jx8tz-T61pqjLA.jpg?1659408008)


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48242447037/original/8lHKioYB_wm8goFRRaJ_FFndDh_BAP4YiQ.jpg?1659408021)


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48242447056/original/Q0hcGLZEHryE7aKPhWSJHuh8R0epTLKHvg.jpg?1659408035)


- Scroll to the top of the page and click the **Save **button to complete the action.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48242447301/original/EVX_21PHSnRSIlJ1jYrRpuDLau8UVm1cUQ.png?1659408211)


Once 360 feedback has been enabled, the assignee will receive a notification under the **Pending Actions List** to perform the task accordingly.
