# How to Refresh Competencies and Appraisal Form in BrioHR Performance Cycle

*This article explains how to refresh an employee's competencies when they change departments during an active performance cycle.*

Introduction


From time to time, you may need to refresh employees' competencies and appraisal form, especially when employees transfer to another department and need to be evaluated with a set of new department competencies. Also, you might need to refresh the appraisal form to capture the latest competencies.


In this article, we are going to  show




















If an employee changes departments during an active performance cycle, you can refresh their competencies to reflect the new department in the current performance cycle.


To refresh the competencies, follow these instructions:


1.   In the **HR Lounge**, click on **Employee Performance**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48177126501/original/jzp0QWzIxNR1NPC_veKz3cI8T-mtl99n9Q.png?1641781008)


2.   Click on **Employees **to manage your employees in the current cycle. Search for the employee's name and click on the **Refresh **icon to update their competencies and appraisal form.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48177127372/original/q4gFBFR81liKRnpDkzhPqe6e-QyVHX1lzQ.png?1641781735)


**NOTE:**
**Once you click the refresh icon, the competencies and appraisal form will be updated according to the employee's new department. All previous information in the appraisal form will be deleted.**
