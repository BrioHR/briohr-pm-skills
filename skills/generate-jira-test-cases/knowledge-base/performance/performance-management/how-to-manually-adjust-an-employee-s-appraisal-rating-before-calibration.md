# How to Manually Adjust an Employee's Appraisal Rating Before Calibration

*HR professionals may need to adjust employee appraisal form ratings before calibration to ensure accuracy. This guide provides a step-by-step process to manually update appraisal ratings in the HR system.*

1. **Access the Performance Module**


  - Navigate to **HR Lounge > Performance** in your HR system.
2. **Locate the Employee’s Appraisal Form**


  - Under **Dashboard**, use the dropdown to find the relevant field.
  - Search for the **employee’s name** whose appraisal rating needs adjustment.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-20-2025-02-21-26-1366-AM.png?width=1221&height=661&name=image-png-Mar-20-2025-02-21-26-1366-AM.png)
3. **Click on the Appraisal Form**


  - Select the **Appraisal Form** for the chosen employee.
4. **Adjust the Appraisal Form Rating**


  - **To adjust a specific item:**

    - Click on the **three-dot menu** next to the item.
    - Select **Edit**, make changes, and click **Save** to confirm.
  - **To adjust the overall rating:**

    - Modify the **scoring or star rating** under the **Overall Rating** field.
    - Changes will be **auto-saved**.
