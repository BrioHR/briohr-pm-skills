# How to Nudge an Appraiser to Complete the Appraisal Form

*As a Performance admin, ensure timely completion of appraisal forms by nudging appraisers through the HR Lounge Performance module. Follow these steps to send an automated reminder email to pending appraisers.*

### Steps to Nudge an Appraiser


1. Access the **Performance module** via **HR Lounge**.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-18-2024-02-54-09-1326-AM.png?width=688&height=375&name=image-png-Oct-18-2024-02-54-09-1326-AM.png)
2. On the **Dashboard page**, scroll down to locate **Appraisal Overview**.
3. Click **See Details** under **Appraiser Appraisal** to view pending appraisers.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-18-2024-02-55-30-9090-AM.png?width=688&height=419&name=image-png-Oct-18-2024-02-55-30-9090-AM.png)
4. Identify appraisers who have not completed the appraisal form.
5. Click **Nudge** to send an email reminder.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-18-2024-02-59-08-7770-AM.png?width=688&height=498&name=image-png-Oct-18-2024-02-59-08-7770-AM.png)


Once the reminder is sent, the appraiser will receive an automated email notification prompting them to complete the appraisal form.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-18-2024-03-00-48-5105-AM.png?width=688&height=495&name=image-png-Oct-18-2024-03-00-48-5105-AM.png)
