# How to Remind Employees to Complete Self Appraisal Forms: A Guide for HR Teams

*This article explains how HR can use the Nudge function to remind employees to complete their self-appraisal forms.*

Sometimes, employees may overlook submitting their appraisal forms. In such cases, HR or Admin can use the "Nudge" function to remind employees to complete their forms.

Follow these steps to send a reminder:


1. Access the **HR Lounge**.


2. Navigate to the **Performance** section.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-19-2024-08-58-42-2436-AM.png?width=688&height=348&name=image-png-Sep-19-2024-08-58-42-2436-AM.png)


3. In the **Dashboard**, click on "**See details**" under **Self Appraisal**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-19-2024-09-00-15-0184-AM.png?width=688&height=292&name=image-png-Sep-19-2024-09-00-15-0184-AM.png)


4. Select the "**Nudge**" button.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-19-2024-09-01-07-0848-AM.png?width=688&height=263&name=image-png-Sep-19-2024-09-01-07-0848-AM.png)


5. A confirmation window will appear. Click "**Nudge**" to proceed.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-19-2024-09-01-58-6539-AM.png?width=688&height=159&name=image-png-Sep-19-2024-09-01-58-6539-AM.png)


6. The employee will receive an email reminder, as shown in the screenshot below.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-19-2024-09-03-38-0233-AM.png?width=688&height=367&name=image-png-Sep-19-2024-09-03-38-0233-AM.png)
