# How to Remove Employees from an Ongoing Performance Cycle in BrioHR

*Learn how HR and Admin users can remove or include employees in an ongoing or upcoming BrioHR performance cycle. Step-by-step instructions, important notes, and FAQs included.*

### Introduction


In certain situations, HR or Admin users may need to remove an employee from a performance cycle that is already in progress. This may happen when:


- An employee resigns during the cycle
- An employee was mistakenly added to the appraisal cycle
- A restructuring or reassignment occurs


BrioHR allows Admin or HR users to easily manage employee participation in both the current and upcoming performance cycles.

### Step-by-Step Guide: Removing Employees From The Current Performance Cycle


#### Step 1 - Log In to BrioHR


Log in to BrioHR account using your registered credentials.


#### Step 2 - Access the Employees Page in the Performance Management Module.


1. On the main navigation bar, go to **HR Lounge** > **Performance Management**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-18-2025-03-48-04-9216-AM.png?width=2004&height=915&name=image-png-Nov-18-2025-03-48-04-9216-AM.png)


2. From the Performance Dashboard, click **Employees.**


3. This section allows you to manage employees in the **current cycle** and prepare participants for the next cycle.


4. Use the **search bar** to find the employee you want to update.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-18-2025-03-49-02-0683-AM.png?width=2000&height=962&name=image-png-Nov-18-2025-03-49-02-0683-AM.png)

### Managing Employee Inclusion and Exclusion


#### A - How to Exclude Employees From the Current or Next Cycle


1. Go to the **Current Cycle **or **Next Cycle** Column


2. Click on the **"-"** **(Remove)** icon to exclude the employee from the selected cycle.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-18-2025-03-50-34-4213-AM.png?width=2000&height=753&name=image-png-Nov-18-2025-03-50-34-4213-AM.png)


3. A pop-up confirmation message will appear. Click **Yes** to proceed.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-18-2025-03-51-49-5417-AM.png?width=552&height=316&name=image-png-Nov-18-2025-03-51-49-5417-AM.png)


4. The employee's status will update from **INCLUDED **to **EXCLUDED.**


**
![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-18-2025-03-52-49-2959-AM.png?width=2000&height=744&name=image-png-Nov-18-2025-03-52-49-2959-AM.png)
**


#### 2(b) How to Include Employees From the Current or Next Cycle


1. Go to the **Current Cycle** or **Next Cycle** Column.


2. Click on the **"+" (Add) **icon to include an employee in the selected cycle.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-18-2025-04-07-48-0285-AM.png?width=2000&height=863&name=image-png-Nov-18-2025-04-07-48-0285-AM.png)


3. The employee's status will update from **EXCLUDED **to **INCLUDED**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-18-2025-04-09-55-9448-AM.png?width=2022&height=733&name=image-png-Nov-18-2025-04-09-55-9448-AM.png)





**IMPORTANT NOTES:**


1. If you remove an employee and later include them again, they must** restart all tasks** from the beginning of the cycle.

  1. However, data such as **Objectives** and **Appraisals** will remain saved.
2. No notifications or pending action alerts are triggered when an employee is excluded; HR/Admin users solely manage this action.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


**1. Can I remove an employee from a performance cycle after it has started?**
Yes. HR Admin can remove employees at any point during the ongoing performance cycle.

**2. Will previous appraisal data be deleted if I exclude an employee?**
No. Objectives and Competency appraisals remain intact, but the employee will need to restart their tasks if re-included.


**3. Can employees see if they have been removed from the cycle?**
No. The notifications won't be sent. The action is internal to HR/Admin. From the employee's side, he cannot access his "My Goals" page anymore.

**4. Can I exclude an employee from the next cycle only?**
Yes. You can choose to exclude an employee from either the** current **or** upcoming** cycle.

**5. What if the employee was removed by mistake?**
Simply click the "+" (Add) icon to add them back. They will rejoin the cycle but must start their tasks.
