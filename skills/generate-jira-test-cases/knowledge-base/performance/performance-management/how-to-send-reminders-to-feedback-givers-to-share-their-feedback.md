# How to Send Reminders to Feedback Givers to Share Their Feedback

*Ensure timely completion of the 360-degree feedback process by sending reminders to feedback givers. Follow these steps in HR Lounge to send automated nudges and improve feedback collection.*

### Steps to Nudge Feedback Givers to Share Feedback


To ensure all employees receive necessary feedback, HR can send reminders to pending feedback givers. Follow these steps:


#### 1. Access the Performance Module


- Navigate to **HR Lounge > Performance Module

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-18-2024-02-54-09-1326-AM.png?width=688&height=375&name=image-png-Oct-18-2024-02-54-09-1326-AM.png)

**


#### 2. Open the 360 Feedback Progress Section


- On the **Dashboard**, locate the **360 Feedbacks Progress** section.
- Click **See Details** under **Upwards, Peer, or Downwards Feedback** to view pending feedback submissions.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-14-2024-10-51-17-9571-AM.png?width=688&height=410&name=image-png-Nov-14-2024-10-51-17-9571-AM.png)


#### 3. Identify Pending Feedback Givers


- Review the **feedback status** of each employee.
- Click **Nudge** next to those who have not yet shared their feedback.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-14-2024-10-53-38-2854-AM.png?width=688&height=288&name=image-png-Nov-14-2024-10-53-38-2854-AM.png)


#### 4. Send Reminder Notification


- Click **Nudge** again to trigger an automated email reminder.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-14-2024-10-55-23-6674-AM.png?width=688&height=288&name=image-png-Nov-14-2024-10-55-23-6674-AM.png)


#### 5. Employee Notification


- Feedback givers will receive an **email reminder** to submit their feedback promptly.


By following these steps, HR can ensure all necessary feedback is provided on time, helping maintain a smooth and effective performance evaluation process.
