# Releasing an Appraisal Form on Behalf of an Appraiser

*Ensures the performance review process stays on schedule, even if the appraiser is unavailable or delayed. This helps prevent disruptions in the overall appraisal timeline and prevents bottlenecks in the workflow.*

### Steps to Release an Appraisal Form on Behalf of an Appraiser


#### 1. Access the Performance Module


- Navigate to **HR Lounge > Performance Module**
- On the **Dashboard** page, locate the **Appraisal Overview** section to check appraisal statuses, due dates, and completion percentages.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-18-2024-02-54-09-1326-AM.png?width=688&height=375&name=image-png-Oct-18-2024-02-54-09-1326-AM.png)


#### 2. Find 'Release Appraisal'


- Click **See Details** under **Release Appraisal** to access pending appraisal forms.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-14-2024-06-42-38-2692-AM.png?width=688&height=281&name=image-png-Nov-14-2024-06-42-38-2692-AM.png)


#### 3. Select Employees


- On the **Release Appraisal** page, find employees whose appraisal forms have not been released.
- Select the employees for whom you want to release the appraisal forms.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-14-2024-07-33-35-6596-AM.png?width=688&height=456&name=image-png-Nov-14-2024-07-33-35-6596-AM.png)


#### 4. Release the Appraisal Form


- Click **Release Appraisals**, then confirm by selecting **Release**.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-14-2024-07-35-41-0216-AM.png?width=688&height=551&name=image-png-Nov-14-2024-07-35-41-0216-AM.png)
- Once released, employees will receive an email notification informing them that their appraisal forms are available.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-14-2024-07-38-08-2422-AM.png?width=688&height=325&name=image-png-Nov-14-2024-07-38-08-2422-AM.png)
