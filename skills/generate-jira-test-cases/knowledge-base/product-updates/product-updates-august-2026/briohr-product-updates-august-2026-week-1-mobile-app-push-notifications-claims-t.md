### Payroll Update


#### Streamline Reimbursements by Syncing Expenses Directly to Payroll via API


HR and Admins can now push pre-approved expense claim reports from third-party systems directly into brioHR’s payroll queue using our new Expense Claim Import API.


**Key benefits:**


- **Bypass repetitive approvals:** Pre-approved external claims land directly in the "To process" queue.
- **Import anytime:** Park claims safely in brioHR even without an open payroll cycle.
- **Keep payslips clean:** Claims mapped to the same item are aggregated into a single line item.


👉 Read more about importing external expense claims via API [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/import-external-expense-claims-to-briohr-payroll-via-api-integration?utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-8Z3cK_1C0eIe2XXvJ2RiARJZb_OcL2h4qE1jIQtNmZRBkO-8-MAMV8EchuwAWMdoG-ncaX&hsLang=en)!

#### (MY) Ensure Accurate EPF Contributions with Automatic Category Selection


Let the system determine the correct statutory contribution category instantly, reducing manual admin work and preventing calculation errors during employee age milestones.


**Key benefits:**


- **Automated classification:** Categories are determined automatically based on nationality and age.
- **Milestone protection:** Prevents contribution errors when an employee reaches age milestones (e.g., turning 60).


*Note: To ensure compliance, Permanent Resident (PR) status still requires a quick manual assignment to Category A or C.*


👉 Read more about the steps to update EPF contribution options [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/types-of-epf-category?utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-8Z3cK_1C0eIe2XXvJ2RiARJZb_OcL2h4qE1jIQtNmZRBkO-8-MAMV8EchuwAWMdoG-ncaX&hsLang=en)!

You can now view the exact SKBBK contribution amount breakdown within both the Payroll Report and Company Payroll Report, making month-end verification and compliance audits faster and more transparent.

#### Effortlessly Process Backdated Overtime and Unpaid Leave


Avoid manual math when handling retro adjustments. brioHR now lets you pull previous months' timesheet and leave data straight into your current payroll run.


**What you can do:**


- **Prior Month Overtime:** Automatically pull past overtime hours and calculation formulas directly from Time Attendance.
- **Prior Month Unpaid Leave:** Select the relevant past month to calculate unpaid leave deductions using that specific month's formula.
- **Clear Audit Trail:** Clear indicator badges show the source month and formula used for full transparency.


👉 Read more about retro payroll adjustments [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-send-approved-employees-timesheets-to-payroll-in-briohr?utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-8Z3cK_1C0eIe2XXvJ2RiARJZb_OcL2h4qE1jIQtNmZRBkO-8-MAMV8EchuwAWMdoG-ncaX&hsLang=en)!

### Time Attendance Update


#### Ensure Accurate Overtime with Flexible Daily Schedules


Configure custom work hours for specific days of the week under an employee's schedule to accurately reflect real-world working patterns.


**Key benefits:**


- **Flexible daily setup:** Set varying work hours per day under a schedule's Default Pattern.
- **Precise overtime calculations:** The system automatically compares worked hours against that specific day's scheduled target, ensuring accurate payouts for shift and flexible workers.


👉 Read more about customizing work hours [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/customize-work-hours-by-day-for-work-schedules?utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-8Z3cK_1C0eIe2XXvJ2RiARJZb_OcL2h4qE1jIQtNmZRBkO-8-MAMV8EchuwAWMdoG-ncaX&hsLang=en)!

### Recruitment Update


#### Re-Engage Qualified Talent by Allowing Candidates to Reapply


Maximize your talent pool by enabling rejected candidates to apply for other open roles across your organization.


**Key benefits:**


- **Expand candidate pipelines:** Allow previously rejected applicants to submit applications for new roles (placed directly under "SCREENING").
- **Full visibility:** Track a candidate's entire application journey—including past outcomes—directly on their profile timeline.
- **Automatic updates:** Ensure candidate records stay fresh with auto-updated resumes upon new application submissions.


👉 Read more about configuring candidate re-application settings [here](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/allow-rejected-candidates-to-apply-for-other-open-jobs?utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-8Z3cK_1C0eIe2XXvJ2RiARJZb_OcL2h4qE1jIQtNmZRBkO-8-MAMV8EchuwAWMdoG-ncaX&hsLang=en)!
