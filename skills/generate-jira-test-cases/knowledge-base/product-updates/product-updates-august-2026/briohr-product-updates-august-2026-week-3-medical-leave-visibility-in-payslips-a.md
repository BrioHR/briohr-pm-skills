### Payroll Module Update


### Take Control of Employee Medical Leave Summary Visibility


You can now choose whether to display an employee's Medical Leave summary directly in their payslip.


By default, the **Payslip Medical Leave Summary** feature is disabled, giving your organisation full control over whether this information is visible to employees.


Key benefits:


- 👁️ **Greater visibility:** Enable the feature to allow employees to conveniently view their Medical Leave summary directly in their payslips.
- ⚙️ **Flexible configuration:** Choose whether to display the Medical Leave summary based on your organisation's preferences and requirements.
- 🔒 **Controlled by default:** The feature is disabled by default, so you can decide when and if it should be made visible.

### (MY) Ensure Accurate EIS Contributions with Automatic Category Selection


Just like EPF, employee EIS contributions can now be set to resolve automatically — no more manually updating an employee's EIS status when they turn 60.


Key benefits:


- 🤖 **Automatic resolution:** brioHR determines whether EIS applies based on the employee's nationality and date of birth at each payroll run.
- 🎂 **Handles age transitions for you:** Contributions automatically switch to Non related the month after an employee turns 60 — no manual tracking required.
- 🚩 **Built-in safeguards:** Payroll flags any Automatic employee with missing nationality or date of birth before the cycle can proceed, so nothing slips through unnoticed.
- 🔍 **Full visibility:** Hover over the tooltip during payroll run to see the resolved EIS status for each employee.

### Time Attendance Module Update


### Smarter Overnight Shift Detection with Automatic Clock-Out Attribution


When an admin or manager edits an entry for an overnight shift, brioHR now automatically recognizes whether the Clock-Out belongs to the same day or the next day, keeping shift durations accurate for every cross-day correction.


Key benefits:


- 🔔 **Clear workday attribution:** A banner confirms when a past-midnight or past-split-shift-time entry still counts toward the previous workday, so there's no second-guessing.
- 🧮 **Accurate durations automatically:** Shift duration, OT, and shift premiums/deductions recalculate correctly the moment the entry is corrected.
- 📱 **Available everywhere:** Works the same way on Timesheet Detail, Daily Attendance, and the mobile app.


👉 **[Read more about editing overnight Clock-Out entries here.](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/manual-daily-attendance-entry-for-employees-as-hr-admin?utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-97ZUG8RUtBSKMCnRSUYaINy30YFLH3-fe5xrizNJBazcBHMtr8jhfSne3o7TCQfldDlUzW&hsLang=en)**





### Send Clock Data with Smarter SFTP & API Matching


Clients using SFTP or API integration can now choose to have brioHR intelligently determine each employee's actual first Clock-In and last Clock-Out from raw, unsorted attendance records — no need to pre-clean or pre-sort your data before sending it.


Key benefits:


- 🗂️ **Smart record matching:** brioHR sorts unordered, unlabelled attendance records and automatically identifies each employee's true first Clock-In and last Clock-Out per day.
- ⚡ **Less prep work:** Send data straight from your source system, in any order, and let brioHR handle the rest.
- 🎛️ **Your choice, your setup:** Choose between your Full Action Log (Default) or our new First In & Last Out strategy — whichever fits your data source best.


Reach out to our support team if you would like to change your integration strategy or enable SFTP/API clock integration for your company.
