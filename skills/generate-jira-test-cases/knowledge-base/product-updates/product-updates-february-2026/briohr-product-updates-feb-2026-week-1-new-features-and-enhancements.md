### Time Attendance Module New FeatureManager Pre-Approved Overtime


Managers and admins in charge of employee rosters can now assign pre-approved overtime to employees. This allows employee to see any OT scheduled for them in their work schedule. This feature can be enabled in the Time Attendance module, based on policy settings. Please refer to the articles below for a step-by-step guide.


- [Enable Manager Pre-Approve Overtime Setting as HR Admin](/knowledge/enable-manager-pre-approved-overtime-setting-as-hr-admin?hsLang=en)
