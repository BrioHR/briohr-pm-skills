### Replacement Public Holiday for Thaipusam (2026)


Since the Federal Territory public holiday falls on the same day as Thaipusam in 2026, a **replacement public holiday** has been automatically added for offices located in the **Federal Territory – Kuala Lumpur and Putrajaya**. The replacement holiday for Thaipusam is scheduled on:


**📌 3 February 2026**


**Please Note:**


- This public holiday is automatically applied for offices with the **State** set as the Federal Territory of Kuala Lumpur or the Federal Territory of Putrajaya.
- Offices in the **Federal Territory of Labuan** are not affected; their public holiday settings remain unchanged.


Read our article below:


- [Public Holiday Update for Thaipusam (2026)](/knowledge/public-holiday-update-for-thaipusam-2026?hsLang=en)


Want to remove this public holiday from your calendar?
Follow this [step-by-step guide](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/public-holiday-update-for-thaipusam-federal-territory-day-2026?utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz--wgsb35b2q7dIkBUkbbCgzEmgloIJn5HxkvD6OHDs6QMpKqOmjTvI3UkIN1em9Zt7688Z5&hsLang=en) to disable the public holiday in your settings.
