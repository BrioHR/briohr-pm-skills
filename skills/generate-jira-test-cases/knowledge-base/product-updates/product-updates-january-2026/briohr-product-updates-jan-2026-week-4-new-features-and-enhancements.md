### Payroll (SG) Enhancements & Guides


- [Foreign Worker Levy (FWL) Proration in BrioHR](/knowledge/foreign-worker-levy-proration?hsLang=en)

  - We have introduced enhancements to Singapore statutory calculations to improve the accuracy of Foreign Worker Levy (FWL) deductions, particularly for employees who join or leave employment partway through a month. These enhancements ensure FWL is prorated correctly in accordance with regulatory requirements.[](/knowledge/foreign-worker-levy-proration?hsLang=en)
- [Self-Help Group (SHG) Proration in BrioHR](/knowledge/self-help-group-shg-proration-singapore?hsLang=en)


  - Read the article above for detailed explanation on how employee salaries should be prorated for Singapore Self-Help Group (SHG) contributions when Permanent Resident status takes effect mid-month, including CPF and SHG compliance requirements.
