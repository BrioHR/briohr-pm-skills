# BrioHR Product Updates - July 2026, Week 1 |  Years of Service Tracking & Payroll YTD Summary Settings

*Discover BrioHR’s July 2026 product updates, designed to improve your HR experience. Stay informed on the latest innovations from BrioHR.*

### Introduction


At BrioHR, we are committed to continuously enhancing the user experience. We listen to valuable feedback from our clients to deliver new features, improvements, and system enhancements.


This article provides a summary of all BrioHR product updates released on Week 1 of July 2026.





### Global HR Update


#### View Years of Service in Employee Profiles & Reports


**Years of Service is now available** in both the **Employee Profile** and **Employee Information Report**, making it easier to view an employee's length of service at a glance.


**What's new?**


- View an employee's Years of Service directly from the **Job History** tab in the Employee Profile.
- Include Years of Service as part of the **Employee Information Report** for easier reporting.
- The value is calculated automatically based on the employee's **Joined Date**, ensuring it is always up to date for active employees and accurately maintained for inactive employees.


👉 [Refer to this article to learn more about this feature](/knowledge/years-of-service-now-available-in-employee-profile-and-reports?hsLang=en)





### Payroll Module Update


#### Take Control of Employee YTD Summary Visibility


You can now choose to disable the '**Payslip YTD Summary**' which appears in the payslips generated.


By **default**, this feature will be **enabled**, allowing employees to conveniently view their Year-to-Date summary directly within their payslips. However, we understand that different organisations have different preferences, so you now have the flexibility to turn off this feature if it does not align with your needs.
