### Payroll Payslip Updates


#### Leave Summary Visibility in Payslips


By default, this feature will be **enabled**, allowing employees to conveniently view their leave balances directly within their payslips. However, we understand that different organisations have different preferences, so you now have the flexibility to **turn off this feature** if it does not align with your needs.


Once configured, your selected setting will be consistently applied across all payslips, ensuring a seamless experience for employees accessing their payslips via both the **web browser and mobile app**.

#### Identity Number Standardization





To improve the utility of your payslips as formal company documents, we have standardized how Identity Numbers are displayed at the top section of the payslip:


- For Malaysia (MY): Displays** MyKad** or **Passport** Number.
- For Singapore (SG): Displays **NRIC** or **FIN**.


This update ensures your payslips carry the necessary data for official use, providing more value to your employees.
