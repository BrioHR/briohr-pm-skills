# brioHR Product Updates - May 2026, Week 4 | Document Management & New Overtime (Off Day) Pay Item for Payroll Malaysia!

### Document Management


Our newest Document Management module helps your team securely generate, store, and manage employee documents in one centralized space within brioHR.


Managing employee documents should be simple, secure, and accessible. With Document Management, your HR team can:


- Generate employee documents directly within brioHR
- Store and organize documents securely
- Access documents anytime, anywhere
- Reduce manual paperwork and scattered storage
- Keep employee records centralized and easy to manage


You have **complimentary access** to Document Management **until 31st December 2026**, so your team can explore and experience the feature.


👉 **[Read more about Document Management here](/knowledge/document-management?hsLang=en)**

### Payroll Malaysia


We’ve introduced a new default pay item: **“Overtime (Off Day)”** for Malaysia payroll users. This pay item is pre-configured with a **1.5x overtime multiplier**, making it easier to process overtime payments for employees working on their designated off days in accordance with common Malaysia payroll practices.


What this means for you:


• Faster payroll setup with a ready-to-use default pay item
• Reduced manual configuration for off day overtime calculations
• Better alignment with Malaysia payroll terminology and practices


👉 [Read more about the payitem configurations here](/knowledge/find-default-overtime-payroll-items-and-add-to-ot-multi-rates-table?hsLang=en)
