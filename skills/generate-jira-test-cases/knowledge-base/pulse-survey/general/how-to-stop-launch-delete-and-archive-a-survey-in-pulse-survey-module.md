# How to Stop, Launch, Delete, and Archive a Survey in Pulse Survey Module

*This article explains how to stop, relaunch, delete, and archive surveys in the Pulse Survey module.*

When a survey is ongoing, you can '**Stop**' the survey to prevent employees from responding to it. You can always **re-launch** a stopped survey whenever you want.


However, to **delete** and **archive** a survey, you must first stop it.


Firstly, open the Pulse Survey Module:


1.  Go to the **HR Lounge**.


2.  Click on **Pulse** to access the Pulse Survey dashboard.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48302089433/original/Zj2I4s3iUJIjhkIaAoOVjSVzSH3Gc501sQ.png?1687314216)


---


### A) To Stop an Ongoing Survey


1.  Click on the **Stop** button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48302089575/original/ZYRIxhHfr7YhbWdCvBUT7fSdGdpHnESxnw.png?1687314463)


2.  A pop-up message will appear to confirm stopping the survey. Click **Stop** to proceed.


**NOTE:**


**Once the survey is stopped, the campaign will be closed, and respondents will no longer be able to provide answers.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48302089949/original/Eu6m51sSxCDiry3uuYKqrdZOlSuh4Mvbgg.png?1687315197)


---


### B) To Launch a Stopped Survey


1.  Click on the **Launch** button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48302089954/original/W8Wie6phuEytykhu4O3A0a47609B7cJw0A.png?1687315209)


2.  A pop-up message will appear to confirm launching the survey. Click **Launch** to proceed.


**NOTE:**


**1.  Once you launch a stopped survey, a link to access the survey will be sent to the respondents' emails.**


**2.  Previous responses will be reset and kept as the first campaign result. Respondents will need to answer the survey again.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48302089964/original/VsY5IlQn2aXF2j5KCe1F8dFPtEi-LRNh1Q.png?1687315239)


---


### C) To Delete and Archive a Stopped Survey


To delete or archive a survey, ensure it is stopped.


1.  Click on the **ellipsis** (...) button. A list of options will appear:


- Archive
- Delete


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48302089675/original/qKosKpxCDXRoIHePNoPBnudsdpxwGltWng.png?1687314652)


**C (i) - Archive**


When you click **Archive**, a pop-up confirmation message will appear.


2.  Click Archive to confirm.


**NOTE:**


**You cannot launch or edit the settings of archived surveys, but you will still be able to access the results.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48302094956/original/w0hjgXMULCgBLl7FyYy1VDpo-R6G0Zuilg.png?1687322481)


**C (ii) - Delete**


When you click **Delete**, a pop-up confirmation message will appear.


2.  Click **Delete** to confirm.


**Note:**


**Deleting will permanently remove the survey, including the results. This process cannot be undone.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48302095069/original/rlbacdAxrJs76iiHMuLmHPLlGB_4oMrOxA.png?1687322611)


---


Alternatively, you can stop, launch, and delete a survey by clicking on the survey title itself.


1.  Click on an existing survey's title, e.g., Workplace Culture Survey.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48302263439/original/5xVu2aLaa55WlVMKhlBWh6_o1UyFf8vDHQ.png?1687426859)


2.  At the right corner of the survey table, there is:


- A **Stop** button (which will change to a **Launch **button once the survey is stopped).
- An **ellipsis **(...) button to open the **Delete **button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48302263748/original/dddn6vO-LDsuNCDTLQZT2UrUjem-IqvDuQ.png?1687426966)
