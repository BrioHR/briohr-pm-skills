# How to View and Analyze Your Pulse Survey Results

*This article explains how to view and analyze Pulse Survey results from the Pulse dashboard.*

Step-by-Step Guide to Accessing Pulse Survey Results:


- Navigate to the **HR Lounge.**
- Click on **Pulse.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48302268711/original/woaL_XOUJ7JdpNFt2cO-m_4I1tRYNqH4DA.png?1687429366)
On the Pulse Survey dashboard:


- Locate any **'In-Progress'** survey and click **'See Results.'**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48304073614/original/3cw_e__w8RiE-7QHfWkPPS_QbXI4o-sMjA.png?1689036949)
Depending on the number of survey campaigns conducted, results will appear for each run. If it's the first survey, only its results will show.


Select the desired result period and use filters for Offices, Departments, and Job Titles.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48304073616/original/BjW_-xyvF5FeP9KHfUqdsmD170bkgwNXjw.png?1689036958)
