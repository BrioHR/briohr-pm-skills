# How to Enable Reject Candidate & Candidate Application Notifications

*Managing candidate notifications in BrioHR allows you to streamline the recruitment process and stay updated. Follow these steps to enable or disable notifications for rejecting candidates and receiving candidate applications.*

1. Login to **BrioHR**
2. Navigate to ***HR Lounge / Recruitment***
3. Go to ***Settings***
4. Under ***Notifications***:

  - Toggle on or off for **Reject Candidate**
  - Toggle on or off for **Candidate Application**
