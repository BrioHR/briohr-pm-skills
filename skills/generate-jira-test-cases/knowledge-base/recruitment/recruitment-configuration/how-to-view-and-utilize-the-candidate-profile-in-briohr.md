# How to View and Utilize the Candidate Profile in BrioHR

*In BrioHR, accessing and managing candidate profiles is essential for organizing your recruitment efforts. This guide explains how to navigate to a candidate’s profile, what details are available, and the various actions you can take.*

### A. How to Access a Candidate Profile


Follow these steps to locate and view candidate profiles:


1. **Navigate to Recruitment**:


  - Go to **HR Lounge** in the main navigation menu.
  - Select **Recruitment** to view all recruitment-related activities.
2. **Access Candidate List**:


  - Under **All Candidates**, use the search or filter options to find a specific candidate.
  - Click on the candidate's name to open their profile.

### B. Features and Actions in the Candidate Profile


In the candidate profile, you can review information, update hiring statuses, and keep track of interactions with each candidate. Here are the options available:


#### 1. Add to Favorites


- Click **Add to Favorites** to bookmark candidates you want to follow up on or review frequently.


#### 2. Mark as Viewed


- When you view a candidate profile, you can mark it as “Viewed” to keep track of profiles you’ve already assessed.


#### 3. Manage Job Applications


- You can manually **Add or Edit Jobs Applied** by the candidate, allowing flexibility in tracking candidates across multiple job openings.


#### 4. Edit Hiring Details


- In the **Hiring Details** section, you can edit information specific to the candidate's application, such as assigned recruiter, manager, or specific notes relevant to the role.


#### 5. Attached Files


- Access **Attached Files** to review any documents submitted by the candidate, such as portfolios, cover letters, or certificates.


#### 6. Profile Links


- View linked external profiles, such as **LinkedIn** or **GitHub**, if the candidate has provided them.


#### 7. Update Hiring Process Statuses


- You can update the candidate’s current status in the recruitment pipeline:

  - **Screening**: The candidate is under initial review.
  - **Interview**: The candidate is actively in the interview process.
  - **Offer**: An offer has been extended.
  - **Keep in View**: The candidate is under consideration for future roles.


#### 8. View Resume and Experience


- Access the candidate’s **Resume** and view details about their **Professional Background and Experience** to evaluate fit.


#### 9. Add Internal Notes


- Use **Internal Notes** to document any feedback or observations regarding the candidate, which is visible only to the recruitment team.

### Additional Tips


- **Filter Regularly**: Use filters in the **All Candidates** section to easily locate candidates marked as Favorites or in specific hiring stages.
- **Update Regularly**: Keeping hiring statuses and notes current ensures a smooth and organized recruitment process for the whole team.


By effectively using the candidate profile features in BrioHR, you can better track, manage, and streamline your recruitment efforts.
