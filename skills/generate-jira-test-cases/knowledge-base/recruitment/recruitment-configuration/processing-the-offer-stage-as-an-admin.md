# Processing the Offer Stage as an Admin

*Learn how to generate, send, and approve candidate offers in the Recruitment module. Follow this step-by-step guide to streamline your hiring process with flexible salary options and approval workflows.*

#### Audience & Scope


Audience: HR Admin


Module: Recruitment


Country: Global


Pre-requisites:  Admin User group settings enabled


###


#### Introduction


Once a candidate has successfully completed the Interview stage and your company is ready to proceed, the next step is to move them into the **Offer stage**. This is where you can formally generate and send an employment offer while ensuring proper approval workflows are followed.


This guide walks you through the entire process of moving a candidate to the Offer stage, generating the offer, and managing approvals efficiently.


####


#### Moving a Candidate to the Offer Stage


To begin the offer generation process, you will first need to update the candidate’s status:


1. Go to **HR Lounge** and click into the **Recruitment module**
2. At the Dashboard, scroll down and click on the **All Candidates** tab
3. Search for the candidate
4. Click on the **Status dropdown**
5. Select **Offer** and click **Continue**

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-27-2026-07-08-33-2660-AM.png?width=670&height=268&name=image-png-Mar-27-2026-07-08-33-2660-AM.png)


The candidate will now be successfully moved into the **Offer stage** of the recruitment process.





#### Generating the Offer


Once the candidate is in the Offer stage, you can proceed with creating the offer:


1. Click on the **Generate Offer** button

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-27-2026-07-09-50-0497-AM.png?width=670&height=233&name=image-png-Mar-27-2026-07-09-50-0497-AM.png)
2. Fill in the required details, including:

  - Job Title
  - Salary Type
  - Salary
  - Join Date
  - Upload Offer Document
3. Choose whether to send the offer for approval


###


#### Salary Type Options


You can select from multiple salary structures depending on your hiring needs:


- Monthly
- Daily
- Hourly


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-27-2026-07-11-08-7231-AM.png?width=439&height=314&name=image-png-Mar-27-2026-07-11-08-7231-AM.png)


#### Offer Approval Process


The system allows flexibility in managing approvals:


- You may **select multiple approvers** for the offer
- If approvers are selected, click **Send Offer**

  - Notifications will be sent to all selected approvers

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-27-2026-07-12-25-7053-AM.png?width=670&height=193&name=image-png-Mar-27-2026-07-12-25-7053-AM.png)
- If no approvers are selected, click **Generate & Approve Offer**

  - The offer will be **automatically approved**

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-27-2026-07-12-55-6301-AM.png?width=670&height=195&name=image-png-Mar-27-2026-07-12-55-6301-AM.png)





#### Tracking Offer Status


After submission:


- The offer will be marked as **Pending for Approval** (if approvers are assigned)
- The **Activities column** will display the current approval status

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-27-2026-07-15-33-2101-AM.png?width=670&height=185&name=image-png-Mar-27-2026-07-15-33-2101-AM.png)
- Selected approvers will receive tasks to review and approve the offer
- Once all approvals are completed, the Offer stage is finalized


You can also monitor the full timeline in the **Candidate Profile under Application Progress**, giving you complete visibility of the hiring workflow.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-27-2026-07-16-09-8677-AM.png?width=670&height=297&name=image-png-Mar-27-2026-07-16-09-8677-AM.png)





**The Approver will receive a Pending actions notification as per screenshot below: **


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-27-2026-07-17-19-5211-AM.png?width=670&height=370&name=image-png-Mar-27-2026-07-17-19-5211-AM.png)


The approver will be able to choose if they wish to **Reject** or **Approve **the offer.


**NOTE:**


**If any of the approver rejects the Offer, the entire Offer will be rejected, and the system will automatically reset to the Generate Offer stage. You will need to re-enter the required information.**


####


#### Common Issues/FAQ


**Q: When should a candidate be moved to the Offer stage?**


A: A candidate should be moved to the Offer stage after successfully completing the interview process and once the company has decided to proceed with hiring.


**Q: What salary types are available when generating an offer?**


A: You can choose from three salary types: **Monthly, Daily, and Hourly**, depending on the role and employment structure.


**Q: Can I assign more than one approver for an offer?**


A: Yes, the system allows you to select **multiple approvers** to ensure the offer goes through the necessary approval hierarchy.


**Q: What happens if no approver is selected?**


A: If no approver is assigned, the offer will be **automatically approved** once you click **Generate & Approve Offer**.


**Q:  How can I track the approval progress of an offer?**


A: You can track the status in the **Activities column** and view the full timeline in the **Candidate Profile under Application Progress**.
