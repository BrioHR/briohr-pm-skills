# How to Add a Candidate to a Specific Job

*Adding candidates directly into specific job openings helps streamline your recruitment process. Follow these steps to manually add candidates into your open job listings.*

### Step-by-Step Guide:


1. Login to **BrioHR**
2. Navigate to **HR Lounge > Recruitment**
3. Access **Open Jobs**

  - In the main dashboard, locate the section for** open jobs**.
4. Click on '**Add Candidate**'

  - Select the **specific job **you want to add a candidate to.
5. Fill in the Candidate Details

  - Enter the required candidate information and submit.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-24-2024-01-39-21-3062-AM.png?width=688&height=503&name=image-png-Oct-24-2024-01-39-21-3062-AM.png)


### Additional Tips:


- Ensure that all candidate details are accurately filled in for better tracking.
- Candidates can also apply directly through the job portal, reducing manual entry.


This simple process makes it easy to populate job openings with potential candidates directly from the system.
