# How to Assign a Pre-Assessment Form When Creating a New Job Posting

*This article explains how to assign a pre-assessment form from the dropdown when creating a new job posting.*

**(For HR/Recruiter Only)**


Navigate to **HR Lounge > Recruitment.**


You can assign a Pre-Assessment Form by selecting it from the dropdown menu while creating a new job posting.
