# How to Manually Add Candidates in BrioHR’s Recruitment Module

*This article explains how to manually add candidate records on the All Candidates page in the Recruitment module.*

In BrioHR's Recruitment module, there are two methods to add a candidate record:


Method 1: Manually add candidates on the 'All Candidates' page (explained in this article).


Method 2: Add candidates via the Job Board link (refer to the note below).


**NOTE:**


- **If you use the Job Board link, all submissions through the link will be automatically registered in the All Candidates List.**
- **Candidates who apply for a job via the Job Board link will have their applications automatically added to the All Candidates list.**


---


### How to Manually Add Candidates:


- Navigate to **HR Lounge > Recruitment**.
- Click on **Dashboard > All Candidates**.
- Click the **+Add Candidate **button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48321825254/original/Jvay5fDZhtKyDNF14L_jLZYBT_S8cg-saQ.png?1703744592)


- Enter all **relevant information** into the pop-up window.
- Once completed, click the **Submit **button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48321825444/original/-w39ziLrFyiOxyJ5ACFwBVm3K7kXVQiDtg.png?1703744770)


- You will now see the new candidate listed under the **All Candidates** section.
