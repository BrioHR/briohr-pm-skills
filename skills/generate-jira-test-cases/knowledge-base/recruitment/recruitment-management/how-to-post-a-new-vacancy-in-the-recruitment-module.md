# How to Post a New Vacancy in the Recruitment Module

*This article explains how to post a new vacancy in the Recruitment module and publish it on the career page.*

To post a new vacancy in the Recruitment Module, follow these steps:


Go to **HR Lounge** and select **Recruitment**.


- Click on the **Add New Job** [](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48042423101/original/MuHPWqS48NO3wjSLumejicGAp_OXQwaWEA.png?1591065779)button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48321626286/original/PAHtAVeF4HEGTxV08pnCiAqrzy6t4Ukk7w.png?1703493706)


- Enter all **relevant information** into the pop-up window.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48321626317/original/MsfdTyboIWGNJhSHoEBLkE38RLqej4L0cg.png?1703493778)


- Ensure you toggle on the **Publish on Career Page** option if you want the job to be posted on the **Job Portal**.
- Click on the **Submit [](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48042423234/original/LmfTEaOM8C8tUFsXoUrz5hWzjZ61lBjipQ.png?1591065928) **button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48321626347/original/Sc5ck0KVEUNqsbw3k1tkSpAUjxTsj0IPGA.png?1703493856)


- The new vacancy will now be listed under the** Job Title **column.
