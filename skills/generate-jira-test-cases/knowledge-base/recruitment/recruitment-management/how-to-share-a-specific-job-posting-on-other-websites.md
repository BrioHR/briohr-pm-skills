# How to Share a Specific Job Posting on Other Websites

*This article explains how to copy a job posting's URL from the Job Board to share it on other websites.*

Navigate to **HR Lounge** and select **Recruitment**.


1. Click on the **link **to access the **Job Board**.
2. Use the search function to locate the specific job position you're interested in.
3. Click on the job title to open the detailed** job posting**.
4. Copy the **URL **from the top of the page.
5. Paste this **link **into any page or website where you want to share the job posting.
6. Candidates who click on the link will be directed to the job posting and can submit their applications directly through the provided link.
