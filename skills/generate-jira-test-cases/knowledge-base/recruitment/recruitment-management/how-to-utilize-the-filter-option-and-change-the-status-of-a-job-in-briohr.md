# How to Utilize the Filter Option & Change the Status of a Job in BrioHR

*In BrioHR, the filter option and job status settings allow HR and recruiters to efficiently manage job openings. This guide provides step-by-step instructions for using the filter tool to locate specific jobs and details on changing the status.*

### A. How to Utilize the Filter Option


The filter tool helps narrow down job openings by their status, assigned manager, or recruiter, enabling easy access to relevant job postings.


#### 1. Accessing the Filter Tool


- Go to **HR Lounge / Recruitment**.
- Select the **Open Jobs** section to view all active, closed, or on-hold jobs.


#### 2. Applying Filters by Criteria


- In the **Open Jobs** section, locate the filter toolbar.
- You can filter jobs by:

  - **Status**: Choose to filter by “Open,” “Closed,” or “On Hold.”
  - **Manager**: Select the specific manager responsible for the position.
  - **Recruiter**: Filter by the recruiter assigned to the job posting.


### B. How to Change the Status of a Job


Changing the job status keeps your hiring records up to date and helps streamline recruitment. Each status—Open, Closed, or On Hold—reflects the position’s current hiring state.


#### 1. Accessing Job Status Settings


- In the **Open Jobs** section, locate the specific job you want to update.
- Click on the status option and select.


#### 2. Updating the Job Status


- Locate the **Status** dropdown.
- Choose the appropriate status:

  - **Open**: The job is actively hiring and visible to applicants.
  - **Closed**: The position has been filled or is no longer accepting applications.
  - **On Hold**: The hiring process is temporarily paused, and applicants cannot apply until re-opened.

### Additional Tips


- **Combine Filters**: You can combine filters (e.g., Status and Manager) for even more specific results.
- **Regular Updates**: Update job statuses promptly to maintain accurate records.


Using the filter and job status options effectively improves team coordination and keeps job postings well-organized.
