# How to View Job Details in BrioHR Recruitment Module

*This guide explains how to view job details in BrioHR once a job has been posted and is active. Learn where to find and access job information through the Recruitment module and Job Board.*

To view the **job details** of a posted and active job, follow these steps:


1. Go to the **Recruitment** module.
2. Locate the **Job Portal** section.
3. Click on the **icon** under the Job Portal section to access the BrioHR-generated **Job Board**.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-12-2024-06-00-09-0574-AM.png?width=1360&height=1029&name=image-png-Dec-12-2024-06-00-09-0574-AM.png)
4. You will see a **list of all jobs** created in the Recruitment module.
5. Click on the **job name** to view the job details.
