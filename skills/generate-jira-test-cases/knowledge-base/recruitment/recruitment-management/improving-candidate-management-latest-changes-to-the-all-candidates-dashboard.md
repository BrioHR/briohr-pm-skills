# Improving Candidate Management: Latest Changes to the All Candidates Dashboard

*This article explains the latest enhancements to the All Candidates dashboard for better candidate visibility and management.*

We are excited to unveil a series of enhancements to the **Recruitment module's 'All Candidates' dashboard**, designed to elevate your candidate management experience. Here’s a detailed overview of the latest updates:


#### 1.   Improved Candidate Visibility:


- Candidate names are now prominently displayed for a clearer, more accessible overview in **HR Lounge > Recruitment > Dashboard > All Candidates**.
- Clicking on a candidate’s name opens their profile in a new tab, streamlining the process of reviewing candidate information.


#### 2.   Efficient Note-Taking:


- A new notes column has been added, allowing users to leave comments for quick reference and enhancing collaboration and communication among team members.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48314072408/original/yuAUgGeQndyDpqDCqdzF006MX6DZPTdKyg.png?1697265764)


#### 3.   Swift Access to Contact Details:


- A convenient shortcut now displays candidates' contact details, including email addresses and phone numbers, directly on the dashboard for easy access.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48314072456/original/FvYXz_RN6fGlGbEOdW_P0-qtHP5xHVyWPA.png?1697265857)


#### 4.   Organized Job Application Information:


- The '**Applied Jobs**' column has been improved to offer a more organized view of a candidate's job applications. Users can access detailed information about multiple applications with a simple click on the '**+1 more**' button.


![_QDQnHOykRZe9d9O2qEtVslCU4KnKdFncw.png?1697266100](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/_QDQnHOykRZe9d9O2qEtVslCU4KnKdFncw.png%3F1697266100.png?width=688&height=283&name=_QDQnHOykRZe9d9O2qEtVslCU4KnKdFncw.png%3F1697266100.png)


#### 5.   Activities Tab for Ongoing Tasks:


- The '**Activities**' tab, prominently featured on the dashboard, provides a comprehensive list of ongoing activities scheduled for each candidate. Detailed activity information is available with just a click.

#### 6.   Advanced Filtering Options:


- The '**Advanced filters**' option allows users to refine their candidate searches. The default filter includes active candidates, but users can easily customize their search criteria to include rejected or pending candidates.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48314073143/original/N88XKqPa5uYywkFgt9UKQfY6O-aapa3fFA.png?1697267215)


#### 7.   New Filter Options for Interviews:


- Two new filter options, 'Completed Interview' and 'Interview With,' streamline the management of interviews:
- **Completed Interview**: Quickly locate candidates who have completed interviews.
- **Interview With**: Identify candidates scheduled for interviews with specific recruiters or HR representatives.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48314073198/original/_qjCL6Doj86V-MoAwQj1QEQBEK0Osl-RBQ.png?1697267287)


#### 8.   Consistent Features Across Dashboards:


- These enhancements also apply to the Open Jobs dashboard and Team dashboard for Managers, ensuring a seamless experience for all users.


These updates are designed to make the recruitment process more efficient, transparent, and user-friendly. We believe these enhancements will significantly improve the effectiveness of your candidate management within the Recruitment module.
