# How to Include Claim Type Custom Fields in the Expense Claims Report

*This article explains how to add claim type custom fields and include them in the Expense Claims Report.*

In the **Expense Claims **module, under **HR Lounge > Expense Claims > Settings > Claim Types > Edit Claim Type**, you can include custom fields by clicking on the "+Add Fields" icon. You can also set these custom fields as mandatory by selecting the "**This field is required**" checkbox.


These custom fields are additional fields that employees must fill in when they select a claim type. Examples include information such as hotel name, flight ticket number, etc.

**How Can The HR Admin Include These Custom Fields In The Expense Claims Report?**


- In **HR Lounge**, click on the **Reports **icon.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48228699582/original/HDVF-ZlfGxHqRWb8y92b6yEWnldwP3YOKg.png?1653620275)


- In **Report Builder**, click on **Expense Claims > Create**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48228699789/original/1QKvpNKTcVyRu3VW_dF45WyRBTdOVvdTTw.png?1653620437)


- Click on the **Fields & Filters** icon.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48228700179/original/1Gt6qZ5YlA7IOs7ooVPUOSyYExxVr1LY3Q.png?1653620775)


- Under **Claim - Custom Fields**:

  - i. Select your preferred filters by ticking the checkbox.
  - ii. Click on the "**+Add Filter**" icon, and select the values.
  - iii. Click on the "**+Apply Changes**" icon to apply the filters to your report.


![gKi8w3tPoAlHifuenCBpukWHgD9tt5MdtQ.png?1653621165](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/gKi8w3tPoAlHifuenCBpukWHgD9tt5MdtQ.png%3F1653621165.png?width=326&height=609&name=gKi8w3tPoAlHifuenCBpukWHgD9tt5MdtQ.png%3F1653621165.png)


- Lastly, click on the **Export Report** icon and select your preferred report format to download accordingly.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48228700760/original/Sz46P6SgBD3G13exQc5XC1Fuxmc5IWbJaQ.png?1653621346)
