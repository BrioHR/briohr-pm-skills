# Export Employee Data in Bulk (Profile, Bank Details, TIN, Past Employees)

*Where to export employee data in bulk: profile master file, payroll bank details, tax numbers (TIN), and listings that include resigned employees — plus what cannot be selected in-app.*

** Audience & Scope**

- Audience: HR Admin
- Module: Employment Management (Bulk Import/Export), Payroll, Analytics > Report Builder
- When to use this article: You need to extract employee bank account details, TIN numbers, a listing of employees (including past years / resigned staff), or the full profile data; or you want to download details for only some chosen employees.


[](#export-profile-data-master-file)**Export profile data (master file)**

1. Go to HR Lounge > Employment Management > Bulk Import / Export > Employee Information > Employee Information.
2. Download the template — it lists all profile information for all employees.


[](#export-bank-account-details)**Export bank account details**

- Quickest: HR Lounge > Payroll, scroll to the Payroll Employees list, click Bulk Download/Upload, then Download .xlsx — bank account information is included.
- Customizable: Analytics > Reports > Company Payroll Report > Create, open Fields & Filters, tick the employee bank-detail fields, Apply Changes, then Export Report (CSV, Excel or PDF).


[](#export-tin--tax-numbers)**Export TIN / tax numbers**

1. Go to Analytics > Report Builder > Company Payroll Report.
2. Customize the report fields to include the tax information field (under General Payroll Information).
3. Export — all employees' payroll details numbers in one report.


[](#employee-listing-including-past-years-resignedinactive)**Employee listing including past years (resigned/inactive)**

1. Go to Analytics > Reports, locate Employee Information Report, click Create.
2. Add the fields you need (Name, IC No., Join Date, Exit Date…).
3. Under Employment Details, use +Add Filter for Active/Inactive to include Inactive and Resigned employees.


- Filtering by department and office is also available in both the Employee Information Report and the Company Payroll Report.


[](#common-issues--faq)**Common Issues / FAQ**

- Q: Can I export only selected employees?
**A:** Reports can be narrowed with Fields & Filters by attributes (department, office, employment status…), but not by individual employee name. For a hand-picked list of employees, download the full file and filter or hide the others in Excel (a few clicks with Excel's filter).
- Q: Can I export the full compensation history with effective dates for all employees?
**A:** No single report exports all historical compensation changes. Use Employee Payroll Variance Report (compare two payroll periods), Company Payroll Report (records for a period), or check individually under Employee Profile > Compensation. You can use Employee Payroll Report and enter in the join month till present month for a specific employee. A bulk history export would be a feature request.
