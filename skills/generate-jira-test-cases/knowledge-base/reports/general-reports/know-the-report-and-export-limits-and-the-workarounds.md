# Know the Report and Export Limits (and the Workarounds)

*The export limits HR Admins hit most — the 5,000-row leave export cap, no export of hand-picked employees — and the recommended workarounds for each.*

** Audience & Scope**

- Audience: HR Admin
- Module: Leave, Reports
- When to use this article: You see "The number of leave for the selected year exceeds 5000. Please download monthly reports instead."; you want to export data for only some employees.


[](#leave-export-5000-row-cap)**Leave export: 5,000-row cap**

Annual leave-management exports are capped at 5,000 rows. Workaround: download monthly reports (and/or reduce the number of leave types per export), then combine the monthly files into one master spreadsheet in Excel.


[](#exporting-only-hand-picked-employees)**Exporting only hand-picked employees**

Reports can be filtered by attributes (department, office, status…) but not by individual employee name. Download the full file and filter in Excel. (See "Export Employee Data in Bulk".)
