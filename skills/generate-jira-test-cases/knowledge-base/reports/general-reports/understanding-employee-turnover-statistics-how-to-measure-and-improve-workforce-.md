# Understanding Employee Turnover Statistics: How to Measure and Improve Workforce Stability

*This article explains how to view employee turnover statistics and use them to measure workforce stability.*

**Employee turnover statistics** indicate the percentage of employees who resign each month. This metric is crucial for assessing workforce stability and the effectiveness of retention strategies.


To view the **employee turnover statistics** in BrioHR, follow these steps:


1. Go to **HR Lounge**.
2. Navigate to **Reports**.
3. Go to **Statistics**.
4. Navigate to **Attrition**.


You will be able to see the statistics as shown in the screenshot below.

### How is the Calculation Done?


Our system calculates the turnover rate percentage using the following formula:


**[ Total Employees Left This Month / [(Total Employee Beginning Of This Month + Total Employee End Of This Month) / 2)]] x 100.**


For example as per the statistic above, the employee turnover rate in March 2024 is 2.2%.


The calculation will be: [ 2/ [(92 + 90) /2]] x 100 = 2.2%.
