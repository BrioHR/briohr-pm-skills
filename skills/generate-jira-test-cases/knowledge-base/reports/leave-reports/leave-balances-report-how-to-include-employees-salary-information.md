# Leave Balances Report: How to Include Employees' Salary Information

*This article explains how to include employees' salary information in the Leave Balances Report using Fields & Filters.*

1.   Click on **Analytics **at the navigation bar.


2.   Click on **Report Builder**


3. Look for **Leave Balances **> Click on** Create** to start creating a report.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-16-2025-06-14-27-2738-AM.png?width=2009&height=1073&name=image-png-Jun-16-2025-06-14-27-2738-AM.png)


4.   Click the **Fields & Filters** icon to customize your Leave Balances Report.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48210736404/original/4Hg0OM_m9Iq9aCBecQbAwj3qpqfX9aPRzg.png?1648987078)


5**.   **Customize the Report:


i. Choose the **year **for the leave report you want to build.


ii. Select the **date **on which you want leave balances to be calculated.


iii. Choose one or more **leave types** to include in your report.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48210977912/original/nnfqOhWVuDv3nvumnxqldUxTAlaO04ROtw.png?1649065743)


6.   Include Employees' Salary Information:


i. Click **Employment Details**, select **Salary**, and then click **+ Add Filter**.


ii. Enter the desired **salary range**.


iii. Click **Apply Changes** to add the filter to your report.


![HY5jCOK4q40SH8Pi8lJTMhgTNxAvi__mig.png?1648987638](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/HY5jCOK4q40SH8Pi8lJTMhgTNxAvi__mig.png%3F1648987638.png?width=276&height=613&name=HY5jCOK4q40SH8Pi8lJTMhgTNxAvi__mig.png%3F1648987638.png)


**NOTE:**


**To delete the filter, click the "Bin" icon.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48210985829/original/BjWKcTSlo76VmNDsXLT0jWzV_xUWfOPVSA.png?1649066389)


**NOTE:**


**To save this customization as your preferred format, click the "Save As" icon. Enter the Report Name and click Save. Once saved, you can reload the report anytime for this specific category.**


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48210983140/original/XmAZ3pY2EbEK4YQziQIFHHiaEgwKKlkOrQ.png?1649066185)


7.   To download your customized report, click **Export Report** and choose your preferred **format**.
