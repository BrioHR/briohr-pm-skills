# How to Filter the Company Payroll Report and Payroll Variance Report by Country and Payroll Centers

*This article explains how to filter the Company Payroll Report and Payroll Variance Report by country and payroll centers.*

### A.  Accessing The Reports


1.   In the **HR Lounge**, click on "**Reports**".


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246808394/original/Iq7hzAdexmA5kUYw22CvYwK8OcUv-7BFFg.png?1661242324)


2.   Click on "**Report Builder**":


a.  **Company Payroll Report: Click "Create"** (to view the Company Payroll Report.)


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246809609/original/pRiL6wCM9ZWc9rDSNL5Tgg8TzVHs3sYtBA.png?1661242587)


b.  **Company Payroll Variance Report: Click "Create"** (to view the Company Payroll Variance Report.)


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48246810046/original/kxbvi2fOvNzNALnWzFLVPPKE-5ZLvoNr1Q.png?1661242691)


### B.  Viewing The Report By Country And Payroll Centers


**NOTE:**


**The following steps apply to both the Company Payroll Report and the Company Payroll Variance Report. By default, employees are ordered by their Employee ID in the generated reports.**


- Select the **Country (only one country can be selected at a time).**
- Select the **Payroll Center (you can select multiple payroll centers within the same country).**
- Select the **Mode: "Monthly" or "Range"**.
- Select the **Granularity: "Show Employees Only**" or "**Show Employees and Departments Only**".
- To download the report, click the "**Export Report**" button.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48248060867/original/QaKnlJudW2rYplH3QU-SLd_BM_UeAklE0A.png?1661850817)


The "**PAYROLL CENTER**" column will indicate the payroll center to which the employee belongs.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48248061910/original/0SAsncOOck-69_lWdcY4TYNI-CsP2oo2eQ.png?1661851040)


**NOTE:**


**If an employee has moved payroll centers during the selected time period, the report will display the relevant information for each payroll center.**


**Example:**


**January: Employee assigned to Payroll Center A.**


**February: Employee assigned to Payroll Center B.**


**When you select Mode > January to February, the employee will appear twice in the report, once under each assigned payroll center.**
