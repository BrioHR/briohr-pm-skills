# How to Customize Your Performance Management Report to Include Information on the Performance Cycle(s)

*This article explains how to customize the Performance Management Report to include information on performance cycles.*

Apart from **Employee Information, Personal Details**, and **Employment Details**, you can also customize your **Performance Management Report** by using the "**Fields & Filters**" tool to include additional information about the **Performance Cycle(s)** such as:


**a. Name(s) of the Appraiser(s): **


Tick the checkbox next to the name(s) to apply.


**b. Status of "Confirm Objective":**


Select Yes or No from the dropdown menu to apply.


**c. Status of "360 Appraiser Assigned":**


Select Yes or No from the dropdown menu to apply.


**d. Status of "Self Appraisal Completed":**


Select Yes or No from the dropdown menu to apply.


**e. Status of "Manager Appraisal Completed":**


Select Yes or No from the dropdown menu to apply.


**f. Status of "Appraisal Acknowledge":**


Select Yes or No from the dropdown menu to apply.


**g. Status of "Appraisal Released":**


Select Yes or No from the dropdown menu to apply.


**h. Information on "Overall Rating":**


Input the range in the columns provided to apply.


**i. Information on "Manager Competencies Rating":**


Input the range in the columns provided to apply.


**j. Information on "Manager Objectives Rating":**


Input the range in the columns provided to apply.


**k. Information on "Employee Competencies Rating":**


Input the range in the columns provided to apply.


**l. Information on "Employee Objectives Rating":**


Input the range in the columns provided to apply.


### Steps to Customize Your Performance Management Report:


- Access **HR Lounge**: Click on **Reports**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48231653062/original/VBJzOFTvq-Q2zZEVwL9stT_7GA8ntB-Z2g.png?1654846122)


- Create Report: At the **Report Builder** dashboard, select **Performance Management > Create**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48231653744/original/ksJxg5dpeCAGnHqCMdZ2Yt1rmt5kt35WMw.png?1654846301)


- Select **Performance Cycle(s)**: Choose the Performance Cycle(s) you wish to include in your report.


![1ZA_xH3ZYvRM-kB1Oo_nSkAeO6iIyqdyJA.png?1654846666](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/1ZA_xH3ZYvRM-kB1Oo_nSkAeO6iIyqdyJA.png%3F1654846666.png?width=688&height=201&name=1ZA_xH3ZYvRM-kB1Oo_nSkAeO6iIyqdyJA.png%3F1654846666.png)


- Include Performance Cycle Information:
- Click on the "**Fields & Filters**" icon.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48231656105/original/ySk5qr7joIF5aqdOeYWNom2hsDVvdexuOw.png?1654846832)


- Click on the **Performance Cycle name**.


![PVJV1ArhcZgPc3Y5v8EV-cB8GGdu_LhCHw.png?1654846934](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/PVJV1ArhcZgPc3Y5v8EV-cB8GGdu_LhCHw.png%3F1654846934.png?width=337&height=401&name=PVJV1ArhcZgPc3Y5v8EV-cB8GGdu_LhCHw.png%3F1654846934.png)


- Click on the "**+Add filter**" icon to add the filter.


![uSrK_xIcBCzp4dxskoJCmvGiMYXKi6NWWQ.png?1654847058](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/uSrK_xIcBCzp4dxskoJCmvGiMYXKi6NWWQ.png%3F1654847058.png?width=326&height=523&name=uSrK_xIcBCzp4dxskoJCmvGiMYXKi6NWWQ.png%3F1654847058.png)


- Select the **values **(Yes/No), input the **range**, or tick the **checkbox **to apply the filter.


![CxYSjpJ5N0BP2muhIAVz0leV5GL2D67v8g.png?1654847891](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/CxYSjpJ5N0BP2muhIAVz0leV5GL2D67v8g.png%3F1654847891.png?width=325&height=525&name=CxYSjpJ5N0BP2muhIAVz0leV5GL2D67v8g.png%3F1654847891.png)


- Click on the "**Apply changes**" icon to apply the selected filters to your report.
