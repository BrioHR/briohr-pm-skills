# How to View and Download the Performance Objective Report

*This article explains how to view and download the Performance Objective Report, including the show-all granularity option.*

### New Update:


1.  The Performance Objective Report now includes a new granularity option, "Show all (Objectives, Key Results, Qualitative Goals, and Contributing Goals)," allowing users to view contributing goals in the report.

### A. Enabling User Group Permission Settings


To view and download the report, the HR/Admin must first enable access under


"**Settings**" by following these steps:


1. In Settings, click on **User Groups**.
2. For the relevant user group, click **Edit**.
3. Under the "**HR Modules Permission" > "Reports Module**" category, check the "**Allow access to performance objective report**" box.
4. Click **Save**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48235762548/original/n2pMrWakUMTi8woD5eiQXw02QH0zkF2N3A.png?1656582791)


### B. Viewing & Downloading The Report


1.   In **HR Lounge**, click on **Reports**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48235764920/original/fpZFfsv6o93zNuLDO_soxiaAnXny21weaA.png?1656583213)


2.   In **Report Builder**, click on **Performance Objective > Create**.


[](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/48235765755/original/4ZjR-4kLMMiF24Bn98cVFn17CwBSdZFAeg.png?1656583406)


3.   Select the following information to appear in your report:


a. **Granularity of the report** (Objectives, Key Results, Qualitative Goals)


b. **One or several cycles**


**
![xCNKwSnT60FZYC8iI0EdgZ-VtvB8ecOFZA.png?1656583858](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Imported%20images/xCNKwSnT60FZYC8iI0EdgZ-VtvB8ecOFZA.png%3F1656583858.png?width=505&height=363&name=xCNKwSnT60FZYC8iI0EdgZ-VtvB8ecOFZA.png%3F1656583858.png)
**


4.   In the "**Fields & Filters**" tool, click on **Cycle Name** to unselect the filters you do not wish to include in your customized report.


**NOTE:**


**By default, all filters are selected. To remove a filter, uncheck the box and click on the "Apply changes" button.**


**If more than one cycle is selected, each cycle will have a dedicated category under the "Fields & Filters" tool.**
