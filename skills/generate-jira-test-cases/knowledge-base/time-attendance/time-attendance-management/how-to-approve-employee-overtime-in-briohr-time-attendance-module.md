# How to Approve Employee Overtime in BrioHR Time Attendance Module

*Learn step-by-step how HR Admins can approve employee overtime in BrioHR’s Time Attendance Module, including daily attendance and monthly timesheet methods.*

### Introduction


When an employee works beyond their designated daily or shift hours, **BrioHR automatically calculates overtime** based on their final clock-out time. The extra hours are displayed in the Time Attendance **Timesheet** as **Overtime.**


The system **automatically determines** whether the overtime falls under:
✔ **Normal day overtime**
✔ **Rest day overtime**
✔ **Public holiday overtime**


Managers will receive an **email notification** to review and approve the direct report’s overtime hours. However, HR Admins can also review and approve overtime on behalf of managers.


---


### Where HR Admins Can Approve Overtime


HR Admins can approve overtime using two methods:


1. **Approve overtime via Daily Attendance: **This method is ideal for approving today's attendance or yesterday's attendance.
2. **Approve overtime via Time Attendance Timesheet: **This method is useful for approving an employee's overtime for the entire month.


---


### Guide: HR Admins Approve Overtime


#### Step 1 - Access the Time Attendance Module


Open the Time Attendance Module.


1. Click on "HR Lounge" in the navigation bar.


2. Select Time Attendance.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-32-18-8713-AM.png?width=670&height=337&name=image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-32-18-8713-AM.png)


---


### Method 1:  Daily Attendance


#### Step 2 - Steps to Approve Overtime from Daily Attendance


1. Go to the **Daily Attendance** tab (default view is today’s date).


2. Select the checkbox **Show Overtime Only** to filter records with overtime hours.


3. Review the overtime details:


- Click the **"✔" (check) **to approve.
- Click the **"✖" (cancel) **to reject.


####


#### If Approving Overtime:


- Adjust overtime hours manually if needed [[How to Edit or Adjust Attendance Records](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/manual-daily-attendance-entry-for-employees-as-hr-admin?hsLang=en)]
- Add a comment.
- Click **Approve** to confirm.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-44-56-0622-AM.png?width=670&height=283&name=image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-44-56-0622-AM.png)


#### If Rejecting Overtime:


- Add a rejection reason in the comment box.
- Click **Reject** to proceed.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-45-03-4946-AM.png?width=670&height=232&name=image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-45-03-4946-AM.png)


#### Undo an Approval/Rejection:


- Click the **"↺" (undo) icon** to reopen the overtime record.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-47-33-5988-AM.png?width=670&height=172&name=image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-47-33-5988-AM.png)


---


### Method 2: TA Timesheets


#### Step 2 - Steps to Approve Overtime from Timesheets


1. Go to the **Timesheet** tab. Choose either **Month View** or **Week View**.


2. If there’s any overtime pending approval, a number will appear next to **OT Approval in Progress**. Click it to filter the pending requests.
3. Select an employee’s name or click the icon to open their timesheet in a new tab


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-20-40-4330-AM.png?width=670&height=202&name=image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-20-40-4330-AM.png)


4. Toggle **Show Overtime Only** to view only days with overtime.


5. Approve or reject overtime:


- Click the **"✔" (check) **to approve.
- Click the  **"✖" (cancel) **to reject.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-48-08-9533-AM.png?width=670&height=331&name=image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-48-08-9533-AM.png)


6. Blue/orange label indicate shifts on **public holidays** or **rest days**, which apply different overtime rates.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-49-07-9484-AM.png?width=670&height=206&name=image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-49-07-9484-AM.png)


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-49-44-2179-AM.png?width=339&height=124&name=image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-49-44-2179-AM.png)


7. View **total overtime per month** on the left side of the timesheet.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-49-31-5554-AM.png?width=670&height=316&name=image-png-Aug-13-2025-02-49-31-5554-AM.png)


**TIP:**
**Using the TA Timesheet method allows you to approve overtime in bulk for a given period, while the Daily Attendance method is best for quick day-to-day approvals.**


**NOTE:**


Calculated OT (from actual clock-in/out) is auto-calculated when employee works beyond scheduled hours and **IF** 'Calculate Overtime' is enabled in the employee's TA policy. The overtime hours can be checked at HR Lounge > Time Attendance > Timesheet (not in Roster).
