# How to Download Time Log, Timesheet, and Roster Reports in BrioHR Time & Attendance

*Learn how to download Time Log, Timesheet, and Roster reports in BrioHR’s Time & Attendance module with step-by-step instructions.*

**Audience & Scope**


Audience: HR Admin


Module: Time Attendance


Country: Global


Pre-requisites: User group settings - Access to Reports Module > Time Attendance Reports


---


The BrioHR Time & Attendance module lets HR generate three types of attendance reports:


- **Time Log Report **
- **Time Attendance Timesheet Report**
- **Time Attendance Roster Report**


These reports can be downloaded for a monthly period or any custom date range (up to 31 days), and can be customized to include only the fields you need.


---


### Step 1: Access the Reports Section


1. Go to **HR Lounge > Report Builder.**


2. Scroll down until you see:


- **Time Attendance Time Log Report, **
- **Time Attendance Timesheet Report and **
- **Time Attendance Roster Report**


3. Click** Create **to start creating your report format.


In this article, we will use the Time Attendance Time Log report as our example.

![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-11-2025-10-05-15-2732-AM.png?width=670&height=306&name=image-png-Aug-11-2025-10-05-15-2732-AM.png)


---


### Step 2: Select the Date Range


1. Select the** date range**, e.g., 1 July 2025 - 31 July 2025. Click** 'Expand to Fullscreen' **to expand the report table.


**Note: **
**Maximum date range for the report is 31 days. **


**E.g.: **


- **1st Jan - 31st Jan**
- **15th Jan - 14 Feb**


2. Click on the **Fields & Filters **button to customize the report format.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-11-2025-10-05-26-6163-AM.png?width=670&height=236&name=image-png-Aug-11-2025-10-05-26-6163-AM.png)


Below are the fields specific to each report:


**Time Log Report:**


- Clock-in related fields
- Clock-out related fields
- Break (log) related fields


**Timesheet Report:**


- First-in related fields
- Lateness (duration)
- Last-out related fields
- Early-out (duration)
- Break (duration)
- Scheduled work hours
- Total hours (Actual)
- Difference (Scheduled vs. Actual)
- Timesheet Status
- Overtime-related fields

  - Calculated Overtime
  - Overtime Status
  - Approved Overtime
  - Approved Date
  - Overtime Type (Normal Day, Rest Day, Public Holiday)
- Rest Day
- Absent


**Roster Report:**


- Scheduled hours
- Roster Status
- Rest Day


---


### Step 3: Customize Report Fields & Filters


1. Expand the category you would like to modify.


2. **Check/Uncheck** the boxes to include or remove the fields from your report.


3. To further filter your field, click on** +Add Filter** and choose the desired filter options.


4. Click **Apply Changes** to update your report format.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-11-2025-10-06-23-2237-AM.png?width=599&height=388&name=image-png-Aug-11-2025-10-06-23-2237-AM.png)


---


### Step 4: Download Your Report


Once your report is customized:


1. Click the **Export Report **and choose the report format either as **CSV** or an **Excel.**


2. To save the report format for future use, click on** Save As** and name the report format.


3. Next time, you can select the same custom report format from the **Select Saved Report** field.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-11-2025-10-06-42-8828-AM.png?width=670&height=286&name=image-png-Aug-11-2025-10-06-42-8828-AM.png)
