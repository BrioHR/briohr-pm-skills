# How to Create a Training Event: Step-by-Step Guide to Training Approval (Part 4)

*Managing training approvals is a critical step in ensuring that the right employees are enrolled in the appropriate training events. This Part 4 guide will help HR professionals streamline the approval process for employee training requests.*

### What is Training Approval and Why is it Important ?


Training approval allows HR professionals to regulate and manage employee enrollment in training events. With approval settings, you can ensure that training requests go through the proper channels and are aligned with organizational goals.


Depending on your preferences, you can enable approval workflows or allow automatic approval of enrollment requests.


---


### Steps by Steps to Open a Training Creation Page


Login to BrioHR account


1. Go to **HR Lounge**, and then click the **Training** tab
2. **Navigate to **Training Catalog

  1. In this section, you will see list of training events created and its statuses.
3. Click on** +Create Training** button to create a new training event.

#### Part 4: Enable Approval


After creating a new training event, navigate to the **Approval** tab to configure whether the training event requires approval.


**A) Enable Approval**


1. Select the checkbox of** 'Require Approval'**
2. The trainee will need to request enrollment, which will go through the approval workflow defined in the **Training Settings**.
3. Click **Save.**


**B) Disable Approval**


1. Unselect the** 'Require Approval' **checkbox**.**
2. Disabling approval for this training event means that any enrollment request made by the employee will be **automatically approved.**
3. Click **Save**.
