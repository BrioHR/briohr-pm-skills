# How to Create a Training Event: Step-by-Step Guide to Training Budget (Part 3)

*Managing the budget for training events is a crucial aspect of HR planning. In Part 3 of our step-by-step guide, we will explain how to set up the budget for a training event in BrioHR.*

### Why Is Setting a Training Budget Important?


Setting a training budget allows HR professionals to allocate resources effectively and track spending. By linking training costs to a specific budget center, you can easily monitor and control expenses while ensuring that your organization’s training initiatives are financially sustainable.


---


### Steps by Steps to Open a Training Creation Page


Login to BrioHR account


1. Go to **HR Lounge.**
2. Click the **Training** tab.
3. Navigate to the **Training Catalog.**
4. Click on** +Create Training** button to create a new training event.

#### Part 3: Budget


After creating a new training event, navigate to the **Budget** tab.


**A) Link Cost to Budget Center**


1. **Select the Checkbox**


To link the cost to a budget center, check the **Link cost to budget center** option.
2. **Choose the Budget Center**
Select the budget center associated with the training event. For how to create a budget center, please refer to this [guide](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/create-budget-center-in-training-module?hsLang=en).
3. **Enter Training Amount and Currency**
Input the total training amount and select the appropriate currency. The training cost will automatically be deducted from the chosen budget center.
4. **Click Save**
Once you have filled in all the details, click **Save** to finalize the budget settings.


![image](https://support.briohr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-27-2024-08-40-12-1855-AM.png?width=688&height=313&name=image-png-Sep-27-2024-08-40-12-1855-AM.png)


**B) Not Link Cost to Budget Center**


1. Unselect the checkbox
If you don’t want to link the training cost to a budget center, simply uncheck the **Link cost to budget center** option.
2. **Enter the Training Budget**
Manually input the training budget amount and select the appropriate currency.
3. **Click Save**
After entering all the required information, click **Save** to complete the process.
