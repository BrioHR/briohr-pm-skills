# How to Create a Training Event: Step-by-Step Guide to Training Trainees (Part 5)

*Managing trainees in a training event is the final crucial step in successfully organizing training programs for your employees. In Part 5 guide, we’ll walk you through how to manually add or unenroll trainees from the training event.*

### Steps by Steps to Open a Training Creation Page


Login to BrioHR account


1. Go to **HR Lounge**
2. Click the **Training** tab
3. **Navigate to **Training Catalog

  1. In this section, you will see list of training events created and its statuses.
4. Click on** +Create Training** button to create a new training event.

#### Part 5: Trainees


After creating a new training event, navigate to the **Trainees** tab to:


- Check who has registered for the training event.
- Review enrollment statuses.
- Record attendance.
- Manually add or unenroll trainees.


#### Manually Add/Enroll Trainees


1. Click the **+Add trainees** button. A pop out window will appear.
2. Select employees to add into the training.
3. Click **'Add to training'** to enroll the employees into the training event.

  1. The manually added trainees status will automatically be** 'Enrolled'.**
