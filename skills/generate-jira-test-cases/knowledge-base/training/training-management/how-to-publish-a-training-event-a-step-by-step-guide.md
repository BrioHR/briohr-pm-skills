# How to Publish a Training Event: A Step-by-Step Guide

*Learn how to publish a training event in BrioHR with this comprehensive, step-by-step guide. Manage your training catalog easily and make your training events visible to eligible employees.*

If you’re managing training events in BrioHR and want to make them available to your employees, this guide will walk you through the steps to publish a training event. Whether you're managing ongoing training or launching new sessions, this article covers how to publish a training event in just a few easy steps.


### Why Publishing a Training Event is Important


Publishing your training event allows employees to access it via the **Training Catalog**. This is essential for ensuring that relevant employees can enroll in or complete mandatory and optional training sessions.


If you are looking for how to create a training event, please refer to [[this guide]](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-create-a-training-part-1-setup-details?hsLang=en).


---


### Step-by-Step Guide to Publish a Training Event


**Follow these simple steps to publish your training event:**


Login to your BrioHR account


1. Click on **HR Lounge**
2. Select the** Training **tab
3. Navigate to **Training Catalog**
4. Find the draft/unpublished training event that you want to publish.


  1. Click the training name to open its details.
5. In the top right corner of the training details, look for button **"Publish Training" **and click it.
6. A success message will pop up, indicating that the training event has been published.
7. The training event status change from **[Draft]** to** [Published].**


**NOTE:**


**The published training event will now appear in the employees' Training Catalog. However, if you have restricted training eligibility, it will only appear for employees who meet the eligibility criteria.**

### FAQs


**Q: Why can't some employees see the published event in their Training Catalog?**
A: This might be due to eligibility restrictions. Ensure the eligibility criteria are set correctly during the publishing process. For how to setup a training eligibility, please refer to [[this guide]](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-create-a-training-part-2-setup-eligibility?hsLang=en).


**Q: Can I make changes to a training event after publishing?**
A: Yes, you can make updates to a published event. If necessary, you can unpublish it, make edits, and republish it.


**Q: How can I republish a previously unpublished training event?**
A: Simply follow the same steps mentioned in this guide to republish an event from draft mode.
