# How to View Training Creation Requests: A Step-by-Step Guide

*Discover how to view and review employee-submitted training creation requests in BrioHR. Learn how to monitor training events, check statuses, and evaluate request details.*

### Monitoring Training Creation Requests


Training creation requests in an organization help streamline employee development initiatives. As an HR professional, it's important to monitor and review these requests to ensure that training events align with business goals.


All training creation requests submitted by employees can be monitored under the **Training Request** tab in the Training module.


In this guide, we'll show you how to view and manage employee training creation requests using BrioHR’s platform.


---


### Steps by Steps to Open the Training Request page


Login to BrioHR account


1. Go to **HR Lounge.**
2. Click the **Training** tab.
3. Navigate to the **Training Request.**

### The Training Request Page


On this page, you can view all employee-submitted training event requests for review. The details you can see include:


- The **name of the training event.**
Click on the training name to view details such as the training description, date, time, venue, attachments, and trainer details.
- The** status** of the creation request.
- The** name of the requester.**
- The **date** the request was submitted.


**NOTE:**
**You cannot approve training event requests on behalf of the approver from the Training Request page. This page is only for reviewing event creation requests and their details.**
